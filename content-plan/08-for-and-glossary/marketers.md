# Vyra for marketers

**Marketing video is a volume problem with a consistency requirement. Vyra edits your real footage from a written brief, keeps one caption and title style across every cut, and turns a single shoot into a dozen platform-specific deliverables.**

- Product demos: [/formats/product-demo](/formats/product-demo)

## Your three most-used prompts

Demo cut:
```
Make a 40-second 9:16 product demo from this screen recording and the founder clips. Open on the founder saying "here is the part nobody shows you", then show the screen recording cropped to the area being clicked, back to the founder for the close. 3-4 word captions, white, lower third. Add our logo bottom right for the whole video.
```

Multi-platform set:
```
From this edit make three exports: 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 1:1 for the Instagram grid with the speaker centered, 16:9 for LinkedIn. Keep captions inside the safe zone on each.
```

Customer quote clip:
```
Find the moment the customer explains the result they got. Cut a 20-second clip around it, add a lower third with their name and company for the first 4 seconds, word-by-word captions, and end on a 2-second title card with our tagline.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the shoot: interviews, screen recordings, b-roll, logo files. Everything is transcribed and analyzed.
2. Write the deliverables list as prompts: one message per cut.
3. Review the first cut, lock the style (captions, titles, logo placement), then say "same style" for the rest.
4. Ask for platform variants from each locked cut.
5. Export and hand off.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Brand consistency by description: fonts, colors, logo position, caption style, once, reused across cuts.
- Transcript search across every interview: "every time a customer mentions onboarding."
- Platform reframing with masks, so 16:9 interviews become 9:16 and 1:1 without a second edit.
- Bring your own AI subscription through MCP if the team already standardized on Claude or ChatGPT.

## What it does not do

- It does not generate synthetic presenters, avatars, or stock footage. Shoot real material.
- It does not schedule or publish posts.

## FAQ

**Can it use our brand fonts and colors?**
Yes. Name the font and hex values in the prompt, or attach a reference video and say "match this text style."

**Can several people on the team use one project?**
Each person works in the browser against the same uploaded assets. Coordinate who owns which cut.

**Do we need an agency for the final polish?**
The full timeline editor is included, so an in-house person can finish any cut by hand.
