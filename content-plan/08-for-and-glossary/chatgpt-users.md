# Vyra for ChatGPT users

**ChatGPT can plan a video but cannot cut one. Vyra fixes that: connect through MCP and your ChatGPT account gets access to your footage and the editing tools, so "find the best take and build the edit" becomes something it can actually do.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Summarize then cut:
```
List what is in my Vyra project and describe the footage in a few lines. Then cut a 60-second 9:16 video: best takes, pauses over half a second removed, no captions yet. Read the timeline back when done.
```

Script to edit:
```
Here is the script I wrote with you earlier. Find the take of each line in my footage, assemble them in script order, and cut the rest. Where I stumbled, use the last clean take.
```

Style match:
```
Describe the attached reference video's cut rhythm, caption style, and music behavior in three lines. Then apply those three things to my footage. Where my instructions and the reference disagree, follow mine.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Connect ChatGPT to Vyra through MCP.
2. Upload footage in Vyra. Transcripts and scene analysis are built once.
3. Ask ChatGPT to summarize and propose a structure. Approve in text.
4. Execute step by step: rough cut, captions, music, overlays. Ask for frame captures to check.
5. Finish in the Vyra timeline if needed and export.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- The script you wrote in ChatGPT becomes the edit plan without switching tools.
- ChatGPT can search footage by what was said and what is on screen.
- Motion graphics, captions, and reference matching are all reachable from the chat.
- You pay for the editor and video understanding, not another model.

## What it does not do

- It does not make ChatGPT generate video. Everything comes from your uploads.
- It does not run without a Vyra plan. ChatGPT is the driver; Vyra is the car.

## FAQ

**Does this work with the free ChatGPT tier?**
It works with any ChatGPT surface that supports MCP connections. Check your plan's connector support.

**Can I still use Vyra's built-in AI?**
Yes. The built-in chat does not need ChatGPT at all. MCP is for people who prefer their own assistant.

**What if ChatGPT says it made a change it did not make?**
Ask it to read the timeline back with timestamps and compare. Then tell it exactly what to do.
