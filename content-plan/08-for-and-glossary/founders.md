# Vyra for founders

**You have ten minutes to record and none to edit. Vyra takes a raw phone video, removes the pauses and restarts, adds captions and a screen recording where you point at the product, and exports it vertical, all from a few sentences.**

## What you make

- Build-in-public updates: [/formats/founder-build-in-public](/formats/founder-build-in-public)
- Product walkthroughs with your face and screen: [/formats/app-walkthrough](/formats/app-walkthrough)
- Straight talking head takes: [/formats/talking-head](/formats/talking-head)
- Launch demos: [/formats/product-demo](/formats/product-demo)

## Your three most-used prompts

Weekly update:
```
Cut this 6-minute phone recording into a 60-second 9:16 update. Keep the parts about the new feature and the metric, cut everything about the office move. Remove pauses over half a second and restarts. Word-by-word captions, bold white. No music.
```

Face plus screen:
```
When I talk about the dashboard, show the screen recording as a picture-in-picture in the top right, about a third of the frame, for as long as I am on that topic. Then back to just me.
```

Launch demo:
```
Make a 45-second demo. Start on me saying the one-line pitch, then the screen recording cropped to what I am clicking, then me for the ask at the end. Add a title card with the product name for the first 2 seconds and a lower third with my handle.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Record on your phone, screen-record the product, upload both.
2. Ask for the cut with the length, what to keep, and what to drop.
3. Fix one thing per message. Ask for a timestamped list of cuts if you want to verify.
4. Add captions and the screen overlay.
5. Export 9:16 and post. Ask for a 16:9 version for LinkedIn if you need it.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Filler and restart removal with thresholds, so a rambling take becomes a tight one without sounding chopped.
- Screen recording overlays triggered by what you say, not by manual keyframes.
- One style reused across weekly updates: describe it once.
- Works through Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor via MCP if that is already your workflow.

## What it does not do

- It does not write your script or generate an avatar. You are on camera.
- It does not post to LinkedIn, X, or TikTok.

## FAQ

**I say "um" constantly. Can it fix that?**
Yes. "Remove every um, uh, and 'like' used as filler" is a normal prompt. Say "keep 'like' when I mean it" to avoid over-cutting.

**Can I drive it from Cursor or Claude Code?**
Yes, through the MCP connection. Same prompts, your own assistant.

**How long from upload to export?**
Analysis runs once on upload; after that a short cut is a few prompts and a review.
