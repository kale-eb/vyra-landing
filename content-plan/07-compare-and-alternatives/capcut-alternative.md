# CapCut alternatives

**The best CapCut alternative depends on why you are leaving: Vyra if you want an AI to edit long footage from a description, Descript if your content is talk, DaVinci Resolve if you want a free professional timeline, and Filmora or Kapwing if you want a similar editor with different trade-offs.**

## Why people look for a CapCut alternative

- Long footage. CapCut is built for short clips. Longer files can process slowly or fail; in one direct test a 35-minute file timed out after 4 minutes.
- Templates get repetitive. Every third Reel looks like the same CapCut template.
- No external AI. There is no way to connect Claude, ChatGPT, or any agent to CapCut.
- Watermarks, storage, and effect limits on the free tier push people to compare paid plans.

## The alternatives

1. **Vyra.** For creators with real footage who want an AI to cut it. Strengths: describe the edit in plain language and the agent builds it from transcribed, scene-indexed footage; custom motion graphics and reference-video matching instead of templates. Limitation: no template library, so you need to say what you want.

2. **Descript.** For podcasters and talking-head creators. Strengths: edit video by editing the transcript; strong audio cleanup. Limitation: no visual understanding of what is on screen.

3. **DaVinci Resolve.** For anyone willing to learn a professional tool. Strengths: free version is nearly complete; industry-best color. Limitation: steep learning curve, desktop only.

4. **Filmora.** For hobbyists who like effect packs. Strengths: friendly desktop timeline; large library of stylised transitions and titles. Limitation: you still make every cut.

5. **Kapwing.** For teams working in the browser. Strengths: many one-click AI utilities; shared workspaces. Limitation: no agent that plans a whole edit.

6. **VN Video Editor.** For mobile-first creators who want a free CapCut-like app. Strengths: capable phone timeline, no watermark on free exports (TODO verify). Limitation: manual editing, limited AI.

## How to switch

1. Export any CapCut project you want to keep as a finished video. Projects do not transfer between editors.
2. Upload your raw footage to Vyra. It transcribes speech and describes every scene while you wait.
3. Give the agent the brief you would have used to pick a template:

```
Make a 30-second 9:16 Reel from this footage in the style of a fast trend edit: cut on the beat of the attached sound, one clip per beat, word-by-word captions in bold white, a 3-word hook title on the first frame. No transitions.
```

## FAQ

**Is there a free CapCut alternative with no watermark?**
Vyra is free to start. DaVinci Resolve's base version is free. Check each tool's current export terms.

**Which alternative works with Claude or ChatGPT?**
Vyra, through MCP. Descript has a limited MCP that passes prompts to its own AI.

**Can any of these do CapCut-style templates?**
Filmora and Kapwing have template libraries. Vyra does not; it builds from your description or a reference video.
