# Vyra vs CapCut

**CapCut is the faster pick for template-driven 15-second clips; Vyra is the better pick when you have real footage and want an AI to search it, cut it, and build custom graphics from a description or a reference video.**

CapCut is a traditional editor with AI features sprinkled on top: auto-captions, background removal, a large template library. You still do the editing. Vyra is an agent-first editor. You describe the edit, and an AI that has already transcribed and visually indexed your footage does the cutting. CapCut gave AI a few buttons to press. Vyra gave AI eyes and hands.

## Where CapCut is stronger

- Template library. For a trending sound with a known cut pattern, picking a template beats describing it.
- Mobile-first. Shoot, edit, and post from one phone without touching a computer.
- Free tier is generous for basic cuts, captions, and transitions.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- Long footage. In a side-by-side test on the same 35-minute file, CapCut timed out after 4 minutes; Vyra indexed the full file in under 2 minutes with searchable scene descriptions.
- Custom motion graphics. Describe a lower third or an animated list and the agent builds it. No menu to scroll.
- Reference matching. Drop in a video you like and say "match this pacing and caption style." CapCut has no equivalent.

## Choose Vyra if

- You film long, then need short.
- You want to use the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already pay for.
- You need branded or one-off graphics, not a preset.

## Choose CapCut if

- You edit 15-second trend clips on your phone.
- A template already exists for the format you want.
- You do not want to describe anything, just tap.

## Try the same edit in both

Task: turn a 6-minute talking head into a 45-second Reel.

In CapCut: scrub the timeline, split at every pause and retake, delete the gaps, run auto-captions, pick a caption template, add a sound.

In Vyra:
```
Cut this 6-minute talking head into a 45-second vertical Reel. Keep the strongest take of each point, remove pauses, retakes, and filler words. Add 3-5 word captions, bold white, lower third. Put a calm lo-fi track under it at 25% while I'm speaking.
```

## FAQ

**Can I use CapCut with Claude or ChatGPT?**
No. CapCut has no MCP or agent integration. Only its built-in AI features are available.

**Does Vyra have templates like CapCut?**
No. Vyra builds each edit from your footage and your description, or from a reference video you attach. That is more personal and needs more direction.

**Is Vyra overkill for a 15-second clip?**
Often, yes. If a CapCut template already matches what you want, use it. Vyra earns its keep when the footage is long or the look is custom.

**What is MCP?**
Model Context Protocol, an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to external tools. It is how Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor can drive Vyra's editor directly.
