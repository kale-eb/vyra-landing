## Diagnosis in one paragraph

Vyra has a strong branded footprint and no unbranded surface area. The site has 10 URLs, one audited, no Organization schema, no pricing page, no use-case pages, no format or tutorial pages, and three comparison posts. AI engines cite competitor-owned ranked lists and how-to guides. The fix is not better copy on the homepage. It is a few hundred narrow, structured, first-party pages that each answer one commercial or how-to question, organised into segmented sitemaps so crawlers can find and revisit them. See 01-sitemap-architecture.md and 02-directory-content-plan.md.

## Immediate technical fixes (independent of content)

1. Add Organization, WebSite, and SoftwareApplication JSON-LD to the root layout.
2. Lengthen the homepage title to 50-60 characters. Suggested: "Vyra: AI Video Editor That Edits Your Real Footage by Chat".
3. Add canonical to the homepage (blog already has it).
4. Install the Searchable tracker or connect Vercel logs so crawler and AI-referral traffic is measurable. Right now we cannot see whether GPTBot, ClaudeBot, or PerplexityBot fetch the sitemap at all.
5. Add BreadcrumbList schema to every non-root page (comes free once the directory template exists).
6. Add a testimonials block and a "Made with Vyra" use-case block on the homepage with real creator names and links.
7. Decide on pricing exposure. The price-book experiment means per-user prices, so a canonical price page may not be possible. At minimum, update the $9.99 line in the AI-subscription blog post to "free to start" language so engines stop quoting a stale number.
8. Add title attributes to hero and feature images.
