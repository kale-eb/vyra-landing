# Testimonial

**A testimonial video is a customer describing a specific before, after, and result in their own words, cut so the strongest claim leads and every claim is backed by something shown on screen.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Landing pages, ads, sales decks, case studies |
| Hook style | The most specific result, spoken: "we went from 6 hours to 40 minutes" |
| Pacing | Calm, 8-12 seconds per point |
| Caption style | Phrase captions, name and role lower-third |
| Music | Soft, warm, no vocals |
| Shot list | Interview framing, slightly off-axis. B-roll of them using the product. Their workspace. Product screen. |
| Common mistakes | Generic praise ("it's great"). Interview questions left in. Talking head only for 90 seconds. |

## Structure

- 0-4s: the specific result
- 4-15s: who they are and the before
- 15-50s: what changed, 2-3 points, each with b-roll
- 50-70s: the result again, with proof on screen
- 70-80s: who they'd recommend it to

## How to shoot it

- Ask questions that force specifics: "what did Tuesday look like before?"
- Eye line just off the lens, interviewer next to the camera.
- Record for 15-20 minutes to get 60 usable seconds.
- Shoot b-roll of them actually using the product after the interview.
- Get a clean lower-third: name, role, company.

## How to edit it

- Find the single most specific sentence. Open on it.
- Cut out every question. Answers should stand alone.
- B-roll over every claim of usage or result.
- Lower-third for the first 4 seconds they appear.
- Show proof (a screen, a number, a before/after) when they say it.
- Keep their pauses that show thought; cut the ones that show searching.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Cut this 18-minute interview into a 75-second testimonial. Open on the line about "six hours to forty minutes". Remove my questions entirely, keep only her answers. Cover her mentions of "the dashboard" and "the team" with the matching b-roll clips. Add a lower-third with her name and role at the first cut. Soft music under.

> Make a 30-second vertical version with only the result and the recommendation, captions in phrases.

## Variations

- Quote card: 15-second single quote, big text.
- Case study: testimonial plus a numbers section.
- Multi-voice: three customers on one theme, cross-cut.
- Founder-to-customer: founder asks, customer answers, both visible.

## FAQ

**How long should the interview be?**
15-20 minutes. Specifics show up after the first five minutes when the person relaxes.

**Can Vyra remove the interviewer's questions?**
Yes. It transcribes both voices, so you can ask it to keep only the customer's answers and cut around the questions.

**Do testimonials need b-roll?**
For anything over 30 seconds, yes. A face alone for 90 seconds reads as low effort.
