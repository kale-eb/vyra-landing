# Lessons learned

**A lessons-learned video ties a specific outcome, such as a number, a failure, or a milestone, to two or three concrete takeaways, delivered to camera with the outcome shown as proof.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Founders, creators, freelancers, anyone with a result to share |
| Hook style | The result as a number: "I posted 100 videos. 3 things I'd change." |
| Pacing | Steady, one lesson per 15-20 seconds |
| Caption style | Phrase captions plus a card per lesson |
| Music | Light, neutral bed |
| Shot list | Talking head. Screenshot or clip of the proof (analytics, the product, the piece). One b-roll per lesson. |
| Common mistakes | Generic lessons ("be consistent"). No proof shown. Too many lessons. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the outcome and the number of lessons
- 3-8s: the proof on screen
- 8-70s: lessons, each: what I did, what happened, what I'd do now
- 70-80s: the one lesson that matters most
- 80-85s: what's next

## How to shoot it

- Pick a lesson only if you can name the specific moment you learned it.
- Record the proof screen first so you can reference it while talking.
- Record each lesson as a separate take.
- Keep the delivery reflective, not a lecture.
- Film a b-roll clip that represents each lesson, even loosely.

## How to edit it

- Show the proof within the first 5 seconds. Numbers on screen, not just spoken.
- Title card at the start of each lesson, numbered.
- Cut to b-roll for the "what I did" part, back to face for "what I'd do now".
- Punch in on the single most important lesson.
- Keep captions on throughout.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is a "3 things I learned from 100 posts" video. Cut it to 80 seconds. Put the analytics screenshot on screen when I say "a hundred videos", and add a numbered title card at the start of each lesson. Phrase captions throughout, and a light music bed that stays under my voice.

> Reorder the lessons so the one about "hooks" goes first, and cut the intro so the first thing I say is the number.

## Variations

- Failure post-mortem: one thing that went wrong, three causes.
- Milestone recap: "what got me to 10k".
- Year in review: 5 lessons, one per quarter.
- Client lessons: what a project taught you.

## FAQ

**How many lessons?**
Three. Two feels thin, four gets skipped.

**Do I need to show real numbers?**
Showing them is the whole reason the format works. Blur what's sensitive, keep the headline number.

**Can the agent build the title cards and place the screenshot?**
Yes. Describe the card style once and ask it to apply it to each lesson and drop the screenshot on the sentence where you say the number.
