# Before and after transformation

**A before-and-after transformation video shows a starting state, compresses the process, and reveals the result, with the reveal held as the payoff of the whole video.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Renovations, art, fitness, cleaning, design, edits of edits |
| Hook style | Flash the after for one frame, then show the before |
| Pacing | Slow before, very fast process, held after |
| Caption style | Minimal, "before" / "after" labels, one line of context |
| Music | Beat-driven, the reveal lands on a drop |
| Shot list | Locked-off before shot. 10-30 process clips. Locked-off after shot from the identical position. Detail shots of the result. |
| Common mistakes | Different framing for before and after. Process too long. Reveal without a held shot. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: one frame of the after (optional tease)
- 1-5s: the before, held, labeled
- 5-25s: the process, fast, on beats
- 25-30s: the reveal, match cut from before to after
- 30-40s: detail shots of the result
- 40-45s: one line of what it took

## How to shoot it

- Mark the tripod position with tape. Shoot before and after from the exact spot.
- Same lens, same height, same light if possible.
- Film the process in short bursts, 3-5 seconds each.
- Shoot the after in 3 framings: wide, medium, detail.
- Record a slow push-in on the final result.

## How to edit it

- Hold the before for 3-4 seconds, longer than feels right.
- Cut the process to the music, one clip per beat or half-beat.
- Match cut from before to after on the same frame position.
- Land the reveal on the music drop. Hold it for 3 seconds with no caption.
- Detail shots after the reveal, not before.
- Optional: a swipe or split showing both at once at the end.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 30-second transformation video. Hold the "before" clip for 4 seconds with a small "before" label, then cut the process clips fast on the beat of the music, then match cut to the "after" clip on the drop and hold it for 3 seconds. Add the detail shots after.

> Put a one-frame flash of the after at the very start as a tease, then go to the before.

> Add a split-screen comparison of before and after for the last 3 seconds, before on the left, after on the right.

## Variations

- Timelapse transformation: process is a single sped-up clip.
- Split-screen: before and after side by side the whole time.
- Multi-stage: three or four checkpoints with labels.
- Reverse reveal: start on the after, "here's how".

## FAQ

**How long should the before shot hold?**
3-4 seconds. The viewer needs to register it to feel the reveal.

**Does the process have to be on beat?**
It doesn't have to, but it raises watch time noticeably. Vyra can beat-sync the process section for you.

**What if I forgot to film the before?**
Use a photo, or a short shot of the earliest state you have, and label it honestly.
