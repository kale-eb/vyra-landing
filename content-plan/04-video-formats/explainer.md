# Explainer

**An explainer video breaks down one concept, system, or "why" question using a clear spoken argument supported by simple on-screen graphics, so the viewer understands something they didn't before.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Concepts, industry topics, "why does X happen", product philosophy |
| Hook style | The question or a surprising fact |
| Pacing | 10-20 seconds per idea, graphics change with each idea |
| Caption style | Phrase captions plus key terms as big text |
| Music | Neutral, curious, low |
| Shot list | Face or voiceover. A diagram or graphic per idea. Real-world b-roll where possible. A summary card. |
| Common mistakes | Too many ideas. Graphics that decorate instead of explain. No summary. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the question
- 3-10s: why it matters to the viewer
- 10-80s: the explanation, 3 ideas max, one graphic each
- 80-95s: the summary in one sentence
- 95-100s: the implication or what to do

## How to shoot it

- Write the explanation as three sentences before anything else.
- Record the voiceover close to the mic, calm and clear.
- Sketch a simple diagram per idea. Boxes and arrows are enough.
- Film b-roll of the real thing if it exists.
- Record a to-camera version of the summary line.

## How to edit it

- Graphic appears exactly when the idea is introduced.
- Key terms as large text the first time they're said.
- Build diagrams piece by piece, not all at once.
- Return to face or b-roll between graphics so it isn't a slideshow.
- Summary card with the three ideas at the end.
- Captions throughout.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is an explainer on why short videos get more reach. Cut it to 90 seconds. When I introduce each of the three reasons, create a simple motion graphic that shows it: a bar chart for "completion rate", a loop arrow for "replays", and a share icon multiplying for "shares". Make the phrase "completion rate" big text the first time I say it. Phrase captions throughout, summary card with the three reasons at the end.

> Keep the graphics minimal, black text and one blue accent, matching the attached reference.

## Variations

- Whiteboard explainer: drawings appear as you talk.
- Data explainer: charts are the main visual.
- Myth-buster: claim, then why it's wrong.
- Analogy explainer: one metaphor carried through.

## FAQ

**How many ideas can one explainer hold?**
Three. If you have five, make two videos.

**Do I need a designer for the graphics?**
No. Describe the graphic in plain words and Vyra creates the motion graphic. Keep it simple: shapes, arrows, one accent color.

**Face or voiceover?**
Face for trust and personality, voiceover for pure clarity. Many explainers use face for the hook and summary only.
