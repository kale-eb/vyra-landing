# Green screen commentary

**A green screen commentary video puts a creator in front of a screenshot, article, tweet, or clip and has them react to or explain it, with the source visible the whole time.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | News takes, hot takes, explaining a post, myth-busting |
| Hook style | Point at the source and state your position in 2 seconds |
| Pacing | Fast, 6-10 cuts per 30 seconds |
| Caption style | Word-by-word or short phrases, positioned away from the source |
| Music | None or a faint bed |
| Shot list | One take on a plain wall. The source image or clip at full resolution. Optional zoomed crops of the source. |
| Common mistakes | Source text too small to read. Creator covering the key line. Reading the whole source out loud. |

## Structure

- 0-2s: source appears, creator says the take
- 2-10s: what the source claims, in one sentence
- 10-40s: your points, zooming into the relevant part of the source for each
- 40-50s: the conclusion
- 50-55s: question to the comments

## How to shoot it

- Stand against a plain wall so the background removal is clean.
- Keep your body to one side and leave the other side for the source.
- Point and gesture toward where the source will be, even though it isn't there yet.
- Record in one take per point so each cut has a natural start.
- Export the source at native size, not a screenshot of a screenshot.

## How to edit it

- Remove the background and place the source full-bleed behind you.
- Zoom the source to the sentence you're reacting to on each point.
- Keep captions in the third of the frame that is not covered by you or the key text.
- Cut every pause. This format has no tolerance for dead air.
- Highlight the key line of the source with a box or underline.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Remove my background and put the attached screenshot behind me, full frame. Each time I say "here" or "this part", zoom the screenshot into the sentence I'm talking about. Add word-by-word captions on the side of the frame I'm not standing in.

> Cut my commentary to 45 seconds, tightest possible, no pauses. Keep the article behind me the entire time and highlight the quoted line in yellow when I read it.

## Variations

- Clip reaction: a video plays behind you instead of an image.
- Duet-style: split frame, source left, you right.
- Timeline scroll: the source scrolls as you move through it.
- Mini-explainer: the source is a chart you annotate.

## FAQ

**Do I need an actual green screen?**
No. A plain, evenly lit wall is enough for AI background removal. Avoid busy patterns and hair-colored backgrounds.

**Can I use someone else's post?**
Commentary on public posts is common, but keep the source attributed and add something the source doesn't say.

**Where do captions go?**
Opposite side from your body, below the key text of the source. Never over the line you're pointing at.
