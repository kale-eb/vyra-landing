# Story time

**A story time video is a creator telling a personal story directly to camera, usually while doing something with their hands, with the story's energy and the cut doing the work instead of b-roll.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Personal anecdotes, awkward moments, "you won't believe what happened" |
| Hook style | Verbal cliffhanger: "So I get a text from my landlord at 2am" |
| Pacing | Fast cuts, punch-ins on every reveal |
| Caption style | Word-by-word or short phrases, high contrast |
| Music | Faint bed or none |
| Shot list | One continuous take, usually with an activity (makeup, cooking, drawing). Punch-in framing. |
| Common mistakes | Explaining who everyone is for 20 seconds. Losing the thread. Flat delivery. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the cliffhanger line
- 3-10s: minimum context, two names max
- 10-90s: events in order, each with a small reveal
- 90-110s: the big reveal
- 110-120s: how it ended, one line

## How to shoot it

- Do an activity with your hands. It relaxes delivery and gives visual movement.
- Frame from chest up, activity visible in the bottom of the frame.
- Tell the story once out loud before recording to find the beats.
- Record in one take. Restarts kill momentum.
- Leave energy in your voice at the reveals. Don't flatten them.

## How to edit it

- Cut every pause and tangent. Story time lives on momentum.
- Punch in 15-20% on each reveal, back out on the next setup.
- Word-by-word captions if the delivery is fast, phrases if calm.
- No b-roll needed. A sound effect at the big reveal is enough.
- Keep the activity continuous across cuts so jump cuts feel natural.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is a 6-minute story time. Cut it to 2 minutes: remove pauses, tangents, and any part where I repeat myself. Zoom in slightly on every moment I say something surprising and zoom back out after. Captions word by word in bold white with a black outline.

> Open the video on the line "and then she said she knew" and then cut back to the start of the story.

## Variations

- Get-ready-with-me story: told during makeup or outfit.
- Cooking story: told while cooking, food is the b-roll.
- Drawing story: told while sketching, timelapse of the drawing.
- Car story: told while parked in the car, very casual.

## FAQ

**Should I show the people in the story?**
No. Names or nicknames are enough, and privacy matters. Keep it about what happened to you.

**Is it okay to be long?**
Yes if every 10 seconds has a small reveal. Cut anything that doesn't move the story.

**How does Vyra know where the reveals are?**
It transcribes the take, so you can point at the lines by quoting them, or ask it to find "the surprising moments".
