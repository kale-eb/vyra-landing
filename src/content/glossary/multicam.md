Multicam is editing footage from two or more cameras that recorded the same moment, cutting between angles on one synced timeline.
**Example.** a podcast with a wide two-shot and a close-up per host, switching to whoever is talking.
**In Vyra.** multi-camera sync aligns angles by matching their transcripts; then ask the agent to cut to the speaker.
