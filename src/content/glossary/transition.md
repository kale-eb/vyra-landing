A transition is the treatment between two shots, from a straight cut to a dissolve, wipe, or whip pan, and the straight cut is the default for a reason.
**Example.** a slow dissolve between a sunset and a night skyline signals time passing.
**In Vyra.** transitions are applied on request; the default creative direction favors clean cuts, so say "add a quick dissolve between the two locations" when you want one.
