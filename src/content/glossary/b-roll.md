B-roll is supplementary footage cut over or between primary footage to show what is being talked about or to hide an edit.
**Example.** a shot of the brushes while the speaker says "I use three brushes."
**In Vyra.** "when I mention the brushes, cut to the brush footage for 2-3 seconds, then back to me."
