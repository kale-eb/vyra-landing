Cadence is the rhythm of cuts and speech across a video, felt as how often the picture changes and how the pacing rises and falls.
**Example.** an intro that cuts every second, a middle that holds for five, and a fast finish.
**In Vyra.** give a rhythm in numbers ("average shot length three seconds, hold the talking clips") and the agent cuts to it.
