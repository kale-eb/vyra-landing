Voiceover is narration recorded separately and laid over the picture, common in faceless, tutorial, and recap formats.
**Example.** a calm narration over a montage of studio shots.
**In Vyra.** upload your own recording and duck the music under it, or try the experimental voice over panel that generates speech from a script.
