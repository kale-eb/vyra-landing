In Vyra, a template is structural guidance for the agent about how a kind of video is assembled, such as Short Talking Head, Long Podcast, or Vlog.
**Example.** the Vlog template favors music-driven pacing and location changes; Long Podcast favors speaker switching and chapter marks.
**In Vyra.** pick a template at project creation or let the agent set one in conversation; the template can be adapted with your own preferences.
