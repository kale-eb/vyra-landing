Shares are viewers sending a video to someone else, saves are viewers bookmarking it, and platforms weight both as strong intent signals beyond likes.
**Example.** a tutorial gets saves; a relatable skit gets shares.
**In Vyra.** choose the format for the signal you want, then prompt for it; the format pages list which formats earn which.
