A reference frame is a single still from a reference video used to show an agent the exact look, framing, or text treatment to match.
**Example.** a frame from a favorite creator's video with big yellow captions under the chin.
**In Vyra.** scrub the reference video and click "Add to chat" at any timestamp to attach that frame to your request.
