H.264 is the most widely supported video codec, playable on every phone, browser, and platform, which is why most social exports use it.
**Example.** TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube all ingest H.264 MP4 without re-encoding problems.
**In Vyra.** the MP4 export option is H.264 at your chosen resolution, from 480p up to 4K upscale.
