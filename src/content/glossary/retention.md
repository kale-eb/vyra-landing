Retention is the percentage of viewers still watching at each point in a video; platforms use it to decide how widely to distribute the video.
**Example.** a 30-second video where half the audience leaves by second 5 has a retention problem at the hook, not the ending.
**In Vyra.** ask for tighter pauses, an earlier hook, or a mid-video re-hook to address specific drop points.
