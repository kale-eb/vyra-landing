A keyframe is a point on the timeline where a property such as position, scale, opacity, or volume is fixed, with the editor interpolating values between keyframes.
**Example.** two volume keyframes take music from 100 percent to 20 percent over one second when the speaker starts.
**In Vyra.** volume, position, scale, and effect parameters can be keyframed; say "fade the music down over one second when I start talking."
