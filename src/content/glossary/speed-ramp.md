A speed ramp is a gradual change in playback speed inside one clip, usually fast to slow or slow to fast, timed to a beat or a gesture.
**Example.** a walk-in starts at 2x, slows to 0.5x as the person turns to camera, then returns to normal.
**In Vyra.** ask for a ramp with timestamps and the agent sets time remapping keyframes; 60 fps source keeps the slow part smooth.
