A match cut joins two shots that share a shape, motion, or framing so the edit feels continuous even though the subject changes.
**Example.** a coffee cup set down in a cafe cuts to a paint jar set down in the studio at the same spot in frame.
**In Vyra.** describe both moments and the shared element; the agent can align the cut so the motion carries across.
