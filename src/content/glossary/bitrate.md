Bitrate is the amount of data used per second of video, measured in megabits per second, and it sets the ceiling on how much detail an export can keep.
**Example.** the same 1080p clip at 5 Mbps looks soft in motion, while at 20 Mbps the grain and text stay crisp.
**In Vyra.** the export slider runs from 5 to 50 Mbps for MP4 and 5 to 20 Mbps for WebM; the Instagram and TikTok preset uses 20 Mbps.
