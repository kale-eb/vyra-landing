A hyperlapse is a timelapse where the camera moves through space between frames, producing a fast, traveling shot.
**Example.** walking through a city with the shot compressed to a few seconds.
**In Vyra.** "speed the walking clip up 8x and keep it stabilized-looking by trimming the shaky start."
