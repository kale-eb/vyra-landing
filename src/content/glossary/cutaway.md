A cutaway is a brief shot away from the main action, used to show what is being discussed or to hide an edit in the primary footage.
**Example.** a shot of the brushes while the speaker says "I only use three brushes."
**In Vyra.** "when I mention the brushes, cut to the brush footage for two seconds, then back to me" is a cutaway request.
