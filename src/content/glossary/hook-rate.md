Hook rate is the share of viewers who keep watching past the first few seconds, typically measured at three seconds.
**Example.** 70 percent hook rate means seven of ten people stayed past second three.
**In Vyra.** "open on the line where I say X" or "start on the reveal shot" moves the hook to the first frame.
