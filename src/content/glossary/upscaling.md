Upscaling is exporting at a higher resolution than the source, which enlarges the frame but cannot add detail the camera did not capture.
**Example.** a 1080p phone clip exported at 4K is smoother to scale on a big screen but no sharper than the original.
**In Vyra.** the export dialog offers 4K and 1440p as upscale options alongside Original, 1080p, 720p, and 480p.
