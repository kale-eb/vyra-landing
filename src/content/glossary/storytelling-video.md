A storytelling video is a short built on a narrative arc with a setup, a turn, and a payoff, rather than a list or a demonstration.
**Example.** "I quit my job with $400" as the setup, the first failed launch as the turn, the first sale as the payoff.
**In Vyra.** quote the exact lines that mark each beat and the agent structures the cut around them.
