An insert shot is a close-up detail of something already in the scene, cut in to show texture, text, or a hand action clearly.
**Example.** a close-up of a knife slicing after the wide shot of the cutting board.
**In Vyra.** search your footage by what is in it ("the close-up of the palette") and ask the agent to insert it at the matching line.
