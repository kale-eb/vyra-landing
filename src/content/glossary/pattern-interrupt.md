A pattern interrupt is a sudden change in visuals, sound, or pacing meant to reset attention and keep viewers watching.
**Example.** a cut to black, a zoom, a sound effect, or a change of location.
**In Vyra.** "add a pattern interrupt around the 8-second mark: cut to the wide shot with a sound hit."
