A POV video is shot or framed so the viewer feels they are seeing from the creator's point of view, often with a text setup like "POV: you are the new intern."
**Example.** a phone camera at eye level walking into a room with the text on screen.
**In Vyra.** "add the POV text at the top for the first 3 seconds, then remove it."
