Frame rate is the number of still images shown per second, with 24 feeling cinematic, 30 standard for social, and 60 smooth for motion and slow-motion source.
**Example.** a 60 fps clip slowed to half speed still plays at a clean 30 fps.
**In Vyra.** the timeline keeps your source frame rate; ask for slow motion on a 60 fps clip and the agent applies time remapping.
