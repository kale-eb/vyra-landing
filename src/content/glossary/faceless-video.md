A faceless video is content built from voiceover, screen recordings, b-roll, or stock footage without the creator appearing on camera.
**Example.** a narrated explainer over stock clips and text callouts.
**In Vyra.** assemble from your footage plus Pexels stock and Freesound effects, then add captions; the voice over panel is experimental.
