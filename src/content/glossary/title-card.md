A title card is a full-frame or near-full-frame text screen that names a section, a chapter, or the video itself.
**Example.** a black card reading "Day 3" between two locations in a travel vlog.
**In Vyra.** ask for a title card at a timestamp and the agent builds it from the title face in your style, or pick one from the presets panel.
