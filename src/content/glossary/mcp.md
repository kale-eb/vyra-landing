MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to external tools and data; Vyra uses it so Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor can control the editor.
**Example.** Claude Desktop connected to Vyra can list your footage and cut a video.
**In Vyra.** connect your assistant once, then edit from its chat.
