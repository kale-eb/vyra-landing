Stock footage is licensed video or photo clips from a library, used to cover gaps when you have no footage of your own for a moment.
**Example.** a drone shot of a city to open a travel recap you filmed on the ground.
**In Vyra.** the Videos and Images panels search Pexels; click to add, or ask the agent to find "a wide city skyline at night."
