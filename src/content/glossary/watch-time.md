Watch time is the total minutes viewers spend on a video, and average view duration is the per-viewer version platforms use to rank it.
**Example.** a 60-second Reel with a 45-second average view duration is retaining 75 percent.
**In Vyra.** improve it with tighter pauses, an earlier hook, and a mid-video re-hook; see the retention guides for prompts.
