Time remapping is changing a clip's playback speed over time, covering slow motion, fast forward, freeze frames, and speed ramps.
**Example.** a two-minute pour becomes a five-second timelapse with a hold on the final frame.
**In Vyra.** time remapping handles timelapses, slow motion, and freezes; say "speed this section up 8x and hold the last frame for one second."
