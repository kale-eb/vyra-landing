Transcript editing is cutting video by editing its text. Delete the words and the matching footage is removed.
**Example.** removing a false start from the transcript trims the retake from the timeline.
**In Vyra.** every upload is transcribed with word timestamps; say "remove every sentence I restart" or quote the exact line to cut.
