Noise reduction lowers constant background sound such as hum, hiss, or room tone so speech sits cleaner in the mix.
**Example.** a fridge hum under a kitchen vlog drops away while the voice stays intact.
**In Vyra.** cleanup is part of the audio guides; describe the problem ("there is a hum under my voice") and keep expectations honest, since heavy noise is best fixed at recording.
