WebM is an open web video container that pairs with VP8 or VP9 video, useful for browser embeds and lightweight web delivery.
**Example.** a product page background loop is often served as WebM to keep the file small.
**In Vyra.** WebM export uses VP8 and is limited to 1080p and 20 Mbps; pick MP4 for social platforms.
