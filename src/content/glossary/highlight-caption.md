A highlight caption is a render mode where the full phrase stays on screen and the currently spoken word changes color as it is said.
**Example.** a five-word line in white with each word turning yellow in turn.
**In Vyra.** highlight is one of five caption render modes, alongside default, word-by-word, slide-up, and slide-left.
