Audio sync is aligning a separately recorded audio track, or multiple camera clips, so sound and picture match to the frame.
**Example.** a lav mic recording on a phone lined up with the camera's scratch audio.
**In Vyra.** the agent aligns clips by transcript matching; for music, beat sync uses BPM, beat positions, and onsets.
