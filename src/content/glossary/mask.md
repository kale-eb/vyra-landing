A mask is a shape that hides or reveals part of a layer, used for cropping, reframing, spotlighting a subject, and depth effects.
**Example.** a rectangle mask crops a 16:9 clip to the vertical center for a Reel.
**In Vyra.** masks are how cropping and reframing work; there is no separate crop tool. Rectangle, ellipse, polygon, feathered, and tracked smart masks are available.
