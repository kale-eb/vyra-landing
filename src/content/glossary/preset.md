A preset is a saved, reusable configuration for a text treatment, motion graphic, caption style, or export setting.
**Example.** a lower third with your handle saved once and reused across every video.
**In Vyra.** select a motion graphic on the timeline and click "Save as Preset"; the Presets panel also ships title cards, lower thirds, info cards, panels, overlays, and collages.
