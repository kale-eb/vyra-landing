Emphasis captions are caption phrases given a larger or accented treatment because they carry the hook, the turn, or the payoff.
**Example.** "never again" set bigger and yellow while the surrounding captions stay plain white.
**In Vyra.** AI caption templates classify each phrase for emphasis automatically, and you can steer them with guidance like "emphasize the hook in the first three seconds."
