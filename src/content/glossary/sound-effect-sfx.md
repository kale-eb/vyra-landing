A sound effect is a short non-musical audio cue, such as a whoosh, click, or impact, placed to underline a cut, a reveal, or a text pop.
**Example.** a soft whoosh on a fast pan; a single click when a caption lands.
**In Vyra.** the Audio panel searches Freesound with preview; the default direction is selective cues, not one per word.
