A layout is a multi-cell arrangement of clips on one canvas, such as a split screen, a grid, or a picture-in-picture inset.
**Example.** a reaction video shows the source clip on top and the creator's face below in a vertical split.
**In Vyra.** layout presets include split horizontal and vertical, three-up, 2x2 grid, PiP in any corner, and feature left or right.
