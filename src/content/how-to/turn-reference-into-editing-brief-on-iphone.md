**To turn a reference Reel into an editing brief in Vyra on iPhone, share the reference to the app, describe the choices you want to borrow and map them to footage you actually have before requesting the edit.**

You like the video, but “make mine like this” leaves too many decisions open. Write down what happens at the beginning, where the pace changes and how the words sit on the screen. Those observations give you something specific to approve.

Caleb’s mobile release post documents sharing references from Instagram or TikTok and starting or saving a project. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Share the reference from Instagram or TikTok to Vyra and add your clips.
2. Ask for an analysis before any timeline changes.
3. Separate the reference’s structure, caption treatment and audio behavior.
4. Match each useful part to an available shot and identify missing coverage.
5. Approve a short brief that states what to borrow and what to keep unchanged.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| You like only the captions | Limit the brief to captions |
| The opening needs a shot you never filmed | Choose an opening supported by your clips |
| The reference is much longer | Describe a shorter structure rather than copying every beat |

## A brief to try

```
Analyze this reference without editing. Give me a short brief covering its opening, changes in pace and caption placement. Map the useful parts to my footage, mark missing shots and wait for my approval.
```

## Check the result

Keep the brief short enough to compare with the draft. “Start with my finished piece and use small captions above my hands” is easier to check than a long list of adjectives. If the analysis describes something you cannot see in the reference, correct it before proceeding.

## FAQ

**Does analyzing a reference create the edit?**
Ask explicitly for analysis only and approve the brief before requesting timeline changes.

**Must I copy every effect?**
Choose the elements that serve your material. Exact reproduction of every effect is not guaranteed.

**Can a reference supply missing footage?**
The reference guides editing choices. Add your own required shots rather than treating the reference as replacement footage.
