**In Vyra on iPhone, ask chat for a small shadow behind the selected text. Review the words against the brightest and busiest frames the text crosses.**

White lettering may be clear over a dark shirt and disappear when the camera moves past a window. Check the whole interval before making the shadow darker. Moving the title to a quieter area may solve the problem with less styling.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Properties panel text controls”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Select or identify the title that is hard to read.
2. Find its least readable frame.
3. Ask for a small shadow and describe the intended color and direction.
4. Play the full interval and check that letters stay separate.
5. Review at normal phone size before exporting.

## A brief to try

> Add a small dark shadow to the opening white title. Keep its font and timing. Make the shadow subtle enough that the small letters stay distinct over the window.

## Check the result

A large soft shadow can merge neighboring letters into a dark patch. Reduce it if the title looks heavy. Use a clear font size and enough time to read the text as well.

## FAQ

**Should I use a shadow or a solid background?**
Try a shadow when a small contrast improvement is enough. A background can help when the picture behind the text changes dramatically.

**Can I change one title without restyling all captions?**
Identify the title by its exact words and ask to keep existing caption styling unchanged. Review both afterward.
