**To adapt a reference Reel in Vyra on iPhone when you have fewer clips, keep its main story beats, use longer holds where your footage needs them and leave out shots you cannot support.**

The reference changes shots every second. You filmed the setup, one useful process angle and the result. Stretching those three clips into a pretend ten-shot sequence usually makes the missing coverage more obvious.

Caleb’s mobile post supports importing a shared reference; the App Store listing supports trimming and rearranging supplied footage. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Add the reference and the footage you actually filmed.
2. Identify the opening, main action and ending that give the reference its structure.
3. Assign your available shots to those roles.
4. Ask for a shorter adaptation with deliberate holds instead of invented coverage.
5. Review repeated shots and remove repetitions that do not help the story.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Only one process angle | Keep the clearest part and give it room |
| No finished-result shot | Film it or choose an ending you can support |
| The reference repeats a visual motif | Repeat a shot only when it communicates something intentional |

## A brief to try

```
Use the reference’s setup, process and result structure with my three clips. Keep my best process shot longer. Do not invent missing footage or repeat clips just to match the reference’s number of cuts.
```

## Check the result

Count meaningful changes rather than cuts. A new stage in a painting deserves a new shot; another view of the same unchanged brush stroke may not. The adaptation should explain your material even to someone who has never seen the reference.

## FAQ

**Do I need the same number of shots?**
No. Match the useful structure to your own coverage rather than copying the shot count.

**Can Vyra generate the missing video?**
Vyra does not generate video footage. Add material you filmed or otherwise have permission to use.

**Should every short reference become a longer edit?**
Choose a length your footage supports. More repetition does not necessarily add useful information.
