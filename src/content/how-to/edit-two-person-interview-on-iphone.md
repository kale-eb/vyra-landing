**To edit a two-person interview on iPhone, keep enough of the question to make the answer understandable and preserve the speaker's meaning. Add the footage to Vyra, describe the topic to keep and review each speaker change with the audio.**

This guide starts with an interview recording whose sound already matches its picture. Separate-camera synchronization is a different task. A short edit needs a complete exchange, not simply the most dramatic sentence.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose one question and answer with a clear beginning and conclusion.
2. Add the recording and identify who is asking and who is answering.
3. Shorten introductions and repetition without removing qualifications from the answer.
4. Keep a reaction only where its original context supports the exchange.
5. Check speaker names, captions and the full meaning before exporting.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A question gives needed context | Keep a concise version |
| A qualification changes the claim | Keep it with the answer |
| A reaction came from another topic | Avoid presenting it as a response to this answer |

## A brief to try

```
Cut a short exchange about the first project. Keep the question that explains what the answer is about. Preserve the speaker's qualification about the difficult part. Do not move reactions from unrelated answers.
```

Listen without watching once. The exchange should still make sense. Then watch for cuts that accidentally put another person's reaction against the wrong statement. Check spelling with the people involved rather than guessing from transcription.

## FAQ

**Can I remove the interviewer entirely?**
Only if the remaining answer supplies enough context. Otherwise keep the question or add an accurate introduction.

**Does this guide synchronize separate camera files?**
No. It covers selecting and shortening an exchange from footage whose picture and sound already match.

**Should captions identify the speaker?**
Use speaker labels when they help understanding, and verify every name before publishing.
