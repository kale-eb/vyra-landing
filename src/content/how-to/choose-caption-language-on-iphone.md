**In Vyra on iPhone, tap Captions, choose the audio under Generate from and set Spoken language before generating the captions. Choose the language being spoken in the recording.**

You recorded a cooking explanation in Spanish, but the draft guesses English words. Fix the source language before correcting twenty separate caption blocks. Check a sentence with ordinary speech and one with a name.

The [official iPhone listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) document native editing. Vyra’s current in-app help section Captions supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Open the project and listen to the speech you want to caption.
2. Tap Captions in the editor’s tool row.
3. Choose the intended audio under Generate from.
4. Set Spoken language to the language in the recording, choose a style and generate captions.
5. Read the result against the recording and correct words that are still wrong.

## A brief to try

> Caption the Spanish voiceover. Keep the original spoken wording. Leave the ingredient names as I say them and flag words you cannot make out.

## Check the result

Selecting a spoken language is transcription guidance. It does not establish translation into another language. If several languages occur in the same recording, review each passage separately before relying on the result.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

## FAQ

**Does Spoken language translate my video?**
Use Spoken language for the language being transcribed. Do not assume that this setting translates the speech or creates a dubbed audio track.

**Should I replace existing captions after changing the language?**
Review the existing track first. The native caption panel offers Generate captions or Replace captions. Replacing generated text means checking any manual corrections again.
