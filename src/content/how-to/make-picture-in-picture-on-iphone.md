**Use Layout in Vyra on iPhone to put a small video over a larger one. Ask for a picture-in-picture arrangement and identify the main recording and the inset recording.**

For a screen demonstration, the screen usually needs most of the space. Your face can sit in a corner until the screen reaches a button or label in that area. Check that moment before leaving the inset there.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Layouts”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Add the screen recording and the camera recording.
2. Open Layout or ask chat for a picture-in-picture arrangement.
3. Assign the screen as the main picture and the camera as the inset.
4. Play the full screen demonstration and check what the inset covers.
5. Listen for doubled narration and keep the clearest voice track.

## A brief to try

> Use the screen recording as the main picture and my camera video as a small inset at the top right. Keep the bottom navigation visible. Use the screen recording audio only.

## Check the result

If a menu opens under the inset, move the inset for that section or choose a safer corner. A layout that works on the first frame can hide a later step.

## FAQ

**Can I choose the inset corner?**
Vyra documents picture-in-picture corner presets. Tell the agent which corner keeps the important content visible.

**How large should the inset be?**
Keep it large enough to read the expression while leaving the demonstrated controls visible. Review at phone size.
