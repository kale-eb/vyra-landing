**To give a caption track one consistent style in Vyra on iPhone, style one caption and apply the change to the whole track. Review longer lines after applying it.**

Pick a representative caption before changing the whole track. A short word can look fine at a size that makes a long sentence cover your face. Use a line with enough text to expose that problem early.

Version 0.1.7 documents applying the position or style of one caption to its whole track. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Choose a caption from the finished speech edit.
2. Set the style and position using a representative line.
3. Apply the change to the whole caption track.
4. Check the longest lines and captions over the brightest shots.
5. Watch the exported video at phone size with sound off.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| All lines need the same position | Apply the chosen position across the track |
| One line is much longer | Check wrapping before increasing every caption |
| A caption covers an important hand movement | Choose a position that works through that section |

## A brief to try

```
Use a consistent caption style throughout this video. Keep it readable over both the white wall and the dark studio shot. Place the captions away from my hands and check the longest sentence before export.
```

Consistency should make the speech easier to follow. It should not force every line into a position where it hides the point of the shot. Review moving subjects as well as static frames, especially if a hand or face crosses the caption area.

## FAQ

**Can I apply a style to the full caption track?**
Yes. Version 0.1.7 describes moving or styling one caption, then applying it to the whole track.

**Should every caption have the same number of words?**
Keep phrases readable and faithful to the speech. Do not remove needed words just to make every line equal.

**Does a matching style guarantee readable captions?**
No. Review contrast, wrapping and placement on the actual shots.
