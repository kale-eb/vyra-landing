**Pacing is how often the viewer gets something new. A cut, a move, a caption, a sound, an idea. Short-form that holds isn't fast the whole way. It goes fast, then slow where the real point lands, then fast to the end. A visual change every 2 to 4 seconds keeps the eye busy without rushing the content.**

## Why it matters

Retention graphs all look the same.

## The method

1. **Map the beats first.** The 4 to 8 ideas the video delivers, in order.
2. **Front-load.** Hook, a cut, and a text element in the first 3 seconds.
3. **Slow down for the payoff.** Hold the shot, drop the music, let a caption sit. Contrast is what makes the fast parts feel fast.
4. **Interrupt every 5 to 8 seconds.** A punch-in, a b-roll insert, a word on screen, a sound, an angle change. Rotate.
5. **Cut on action or on the word.** A hand moving, a head turning, a key word landing.
6. **Speed up into the ending.** Shorter shots over the last 20 percent, end on the last useful frame.

## Rules of thumb

| Format | Shot length | Interrupt | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talking head | 3 to 6 s | every 6 to 8 s | 30 to 60 s |
| UGC hook and demo | 2 to 3 s | every 4 to 5 s | 20 to 40 s |
| Montage | 0.5 to 2 s | on phrases | 15 to 30 s |
| Vlog | 2 to 5 s | every 5 to 8 s | 45 to 90 s |
| Cinematic | 3 to 8 s | on swells | 20 to 45 s |

The middle third should have the longest shots. If your middle is the fastest part, the value got buried.

- "Re-pace this 45 second talking head. Keep every sentence but add a visual change at least every 5 seconds: alternate punch-ins, my b-roll clips, and single emphasized words. Slow down on the part where I explain the pricing and drop the music under it."
- "Cut this footage to the beat of the track. Start with cuts every 2 beats, switch to every beat after the first chorus, and hold one long shot on the finished painting before the last section. 9:16, 25 seconds max."
- "The middle of this video drags from 12 to 25 seconds. Tighten the pauses, add two b-roll inserts from my library that match what I am describing, and put a caption emphasis on the word 'free'."

## Common mistakes

- A cut every second the whole way. It reads as noise.
- The same interrupt twenty times.
- Fading out at the end.
- Speeding through the payoff because you're worried it's boring. Slow it down.

## FAQ

**Is faster always better?**
Faster openings help. Faster middles usually hurt.

**Where do people drop?**
Check the retention graph. Put an interrupt 1 to 2 seconds before each dip.
