**To make an L-cut in Vyra on iPhone, keep the outgoing shot’s speech playing while the picture changes to the next shot. Ask chat to preserve the complete sentence and change only the visible footage at that point.**

You explain the glaze while looking at the camera, then show the cup before the sentence ends. The viewer can hear the explanation and inspect the detail at the same time. Choose a visual that actually supports the words.

The [official iPhone listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) document native editing. Vyra’s current in-app help section Audio features and Editing tools on iPhone supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Find a complete sentence and the detail shot that belongs with it.
2. Mark where the picture should change while the sentence continues.
3. Ask Vyra to carry the outgoing speech over the next shot without repeating it.
4. Listen at the picture change for a clipped word or competing source audio.
5. Watch the full sentence and check that the detail appears when it becomes relevant.

## A brief to try

> Keep my full sentence about the glaze. When I say the rim, switch the picture to the close-up of the cup and let my sentence finish over it. Keep that close-up’s audio quiet.

## FAQ

**Does an L-cut need a transition effect?**
The overlap is between picture and sound. A straight picture cut can work while the previous speech continues.

**How is this different from a J-cut?**
In an L-cut the outgoing audio continues over the next picture. In a J-cut the incoming audio begins before its picture appears.
