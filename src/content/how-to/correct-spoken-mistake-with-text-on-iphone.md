**To correct a spoken mistake with text in Vyra on iPhone, add a clearly labeled correction at the relevant moment and keep the captions faithful to what the recording actually says.**

You said Tuesday while holding a card that says Thursday. A quiet change to the subtitles can make the video more confusing. A separate correction lets someone understand both the recording and the information you want them to keep.

The official listing supports text overlays and generated captions. Separating a correction from a transcript is the editorial method taught here. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Identify the exact statement and verify the correct information.
2. Decide whether a clearer take or a new recording would work better.
3. If keeping the take, add a text overlay labeled as a correction.
4. Show the correction while the relevant statement is visible and check its reading time.
5. Review the audio, captions and correction together in the saved file.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A minor date slip | Use a clear, timely correction if the rest remains accurate |
| The explanation depends on the wrong fact | Consider recording the passage again |
| An uncertain claim | Verify it before adding replacement wording |

## A brief to try

```
At the moment I say Tuesday, add a separate text correction reading “Correction. Thursday”. Keep the transcript faithful to my speech. Show the correction long enough to read and leave the rest of the edit unchanged.
```

## Check the result

Read the correction without the sound, then listen without looking at it. If those versions suggest opposite instructions, make the correction more explicit or replace the take. For consequential instructions, a new clear recording can be easier to follow than a dense overlay.

## FAQ

**Should I change the subtitles to the corrected fact?**
Keep captions faithful to the recording and identify a factual correction separately.

**Can Vyra verify the fact for me?**
Supply information you have checked. This workflow explains displaying a correction, not verifying its truth.

**Can I revise a video after posting?**
Revise the editable project and export another file. Updating an existing social post is a separate step.
