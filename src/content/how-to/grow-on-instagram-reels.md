**Reels grow on shares and saves more than likes. Instagram weights sends-to-a-friend and saves heavily when deciding whether to push a Reel beyond your followers, so the videos that spread are the ones a viewer wants to send someone or keep for later. That favors relatable, useful, or beautiful content over pure entertainment, and it rewards a clear caption that tells people who to send it to.**

## Why it matters

Instagram is where most creators already have their friends and their existing audience, so it converts attention into followers and DMs better than TikTok. But Reels reach is gated by different signals: a Reel that gets watched but not shared stalls, while a Reel that gets sent 200 times gets pushed to a much larger audience.

## The method

1. **Make things people send.** Relatable moments, useful tips, and beautiful process shots all get shared. Ask "who would someone send this to" before posting.
2. **Make things people save.** Tutorials, supply lists, and reference-worthy visuals get saved. Saves count as much as shares.
3. **Open on the visual, not the intro.** Reels autoplay muted in many placements. The first frame has to work without sound.
4. **Write the caption for the share.** "Send this to the friend who..." or a question that invites a reply. Keep the first line short; it is cut off after two lines.
5. **Use trending audio when it fits, original audio when it does not.** Trending audio helps discovery on Reels more than on TikTok, but only if the video matches the mood.
6. **Post 4 to 5 Reels a week and one carousel.** Carousels get saves; Reels get shares. They feed each other.
7. **Reply to every DM and comment in the first hour.** Early engagement is a signal.

## Rules of thumb

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Reels per week | 4 to 5 |
| Length | 7 to 30 s for shares, 30 to 60 s for saves |
| Caption first line | under 60 characters |
| Shares per 1,000 views | typically above 5 on a Reel that will spread |
| Saves per 1,000 views | typically above 10 on tutorial-style Reels |
| Trending audio | when it matches, not by default |

## Examples

A 15-second Reel of a preserved-flower painting with the caption "attaching these felt like gambling" gets sent to friends who paint. Same footage with the caption "new piece" does not.

A supplies-review Reel that ends with "full list in the comments" gets saved at three times the rate of the same video without the line.

An "I'm taking a leave from school to build this" storytelling Reel gets shared because it is a decision people have opinions about.

## Common mistakes

- Opening with 2 seconds of you settling into frame. Muted autoplay means that is 2 seconds of nothing.
- Captions that describe the video instead of prompting a share or reply.
- Posting a TikTok export with the watermark. Reels down-ranks it.
- Chasing trending audio that does not match the content.
- Ignoring the saves number because likes feel better.

## FAQ

**Reels or carousels?**
Both. Reels for reach and shares, carousels for saves and depth. One carousel a week is enough.

**Does posting time matter?**
Less than consistency. Post when your existing followers are active so the first hour has engagement.

**How long before a Reel is done growing?**
Typically 2 to 5 days, but a Reel that keeps getting shared can run for weeks.
