**Reels grow on shares and saves more than likes. Instagram pushes a Reel past your followers when people send it to a friend or save it. So relatable, useful, or beautiful beats purely entertaining, and the caption should tell people who to send it to.**

## Why it matters

Instagram is where your friends and existing audience already are, so it turns attention into followers and DMs better than TikTok.

## The method

1. **Make things people send.** Relatable moments, useful tips, pretty process shots. Ask "who would someone send this to" before posting.
2. **Make things people save.** Tutorials, supply lists, reference shots.
3. **Open on the visual.** Reels autoplay muted in a lot of places. The first frame has to work silent.
4. **Write the caption for the share.** "Send this to the friend who..." Keep the first line short, it's cut after two lines.
5. **Trending audio only when it fits.** It helps more on Reels than TikTok, but only if the mood matches.
6. **4 to 5 Reels a week plus one carousel.** Carousels get saves, Reels get shares.
7. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.**

## Rules of thumb

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Reels per week | 4 to 5 |
| Length | 7 to 30 s for shares, 30 to 60 s for saves |
| Caption first line | under 60 characters |
| Shares per 1,000 views | typically above 5 on a Reel that spreads |
| Saves per 1,000 views | typically above 10 on tutorials |

## Examples

- A 15 second Reel of a preserved-flower painting captioned "attaching these felt like gambling" gets sent to friends who paint. The same clip captioned "new piece" doesn't.
- A supplies Reel ending with "full list in the comments" gets saved about three times as often.

## Common mistakes

- Two seconds of you settling into frame at the start.
- Captions that describe the video instead of asking for a share.
- Posting a TikTok export with the watermark.
- Ignoring saves because likes feel better.

## FAQ

**Reels or carousels?**
Both. Reels for reach, carousels for saves. One carousel a week is enough.

**How long does a Reel keep growing?**
Usually 2 to 5 days. One that keeps getting shared can run for weeks.
