**In Vyra on iPhone, tap the caption on the timeline and use Split to divide it. Review both parts against the recording so the words remain in the right order and appear when they are spoken.**

A sentence may contain a useful pause before the important detail. Dividing its caption there can make it easier to follow than keeping the full sentence on screen. Splitting the caption is a different job from cutting the speaker’s recording.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing supplied media. Vyra’s current in-app help, Captions, documents the controls described here.

## Work through the edit

1. Play the sentence and identify the natural phrase break.
2. Tap the caption on the timeline and choose Split at the intended point.
3. Use Edit to check the wording on both resulting pieces.
4. Play before and after the split and correct any repeated or missing words.
5. Check that each phrase stays readable in the exported video.

## A brief to try

> Divide the caption after the words first coat. Keep both parts in the order I said them, and leave the actual video and audio cuts unchanged.

## Review the result

The native Captions help lists Edit and Split for a selected caption. Inspect the resulting words rather than assuming the split chose the phrase break you meant. For words that are all late or early, use the separate timing troubleshooting guide.

## FAQ

**Does splitting a caption cut the recorded sentence?**
The selected item matters. Work on the caption and replay the audio to confirm the recording stays intact.

**What should I check immediately after splitting?**
Read both pieces in order while listening. Look for a repeated boundary word, a missing qualifier or a phrase that now disappears too quickly.
