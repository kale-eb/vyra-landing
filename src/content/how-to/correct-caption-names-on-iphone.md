**To correct a name in video captions on iPhone, supply the exact spelling, fix each occurrence and compare the text with the spoken line. Keep the timing if the words already appear at the right moment.**

Names are easy to hear and still easy to spell wrong. Make a short list of the people, places and product names in your clip before reviewing captions. Include capitalization when it matters.

The listing confirms generated captions with styling. Version 0.1.4 describes wrapping long caption words. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Generate captions for the version of the speech you intend to keep.
2. Write the correct spelling of each unusual name.
3. Find and correct each occurrence in the caption text.
4. Listen to the surrounding sentence and check the corrected words stay in time.
5. Watch the saved export muted and check the spelling once more.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Right sound, wrong spelling | Correct the text without changing the sentence |
| The speaker says a different name | Resolve the source wording before adding a correction |
| One long name wraps badly | Adjust the caption layout and check the full name remains visible |

## A brief to try

```
Correct every caption spelling of my name to Sulan and the app name to Vyra. Keep the spoken wording and existing timing. Show me the corrected lines so I can review them.
```

Check the first appearance and later repetitions. A correct title at the start does not prove the captions are right everywhere. If the spelling came from an automatic guess, compare it with your own source rather than another guess.

## FAQ

**Should I regenerate the entire caption track for one name?**
Start with a text correction when the timing and remaining words are right. Review the full track if there are broader errors.

**Can captions verify how someone spells their name?**
Supply a spelling you have checked. Speech alone may not establish it.

**Will correcting captions change what I said?**
It changes the displayed words. Listen to the recording and keep the text faithful to it.
