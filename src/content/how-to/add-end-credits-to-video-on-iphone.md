**To add end credits on iPhone, place your checked names and roles over the ending of the video, then leave enough time to read them. In Vyra, use text over your footage or a simple closing composition.**

Collect the spellings before opening the editor. An editor can place the names, but it cannot establish who deserves a credit. For a short video, a few readable lines are often easier to check than a long moving list.

The listing confirms text overlays, timeline editing and export. The guide uses supplied credits, without claiming a dedicated scrolling-credits feature. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Prepare the names, roles and any wording you have agreed to use.
2. Choose the closing shot and the section where credits will appear.
3. Add the supplied text and position it away from important action.
4. Set the display time by reading the credits at normal playback speed.
5. Check every spelling in the exported file before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A small list of contributors | Use a simple readable closing card |
| Too much text fits only at tiny size | Break the credits into separate displays |
| The ending shot is busy | Choose clearer placement or a quieter closing composition |

## A brief to try

```
Add these end credits exactly as supplied over the final studio shot. Keep the painting visible and give each line time to read. Do not invent contributor names or add a scrolling animation.
```

Ask someone unfamiliar with the list to read it during playback. If they need to pause, reduce the amount shown at once or extend the section. Check that the final frame does not cut a name off halfway through its display.

## FAQ

**Does this require scrolling credits?**
No. A static card or a few separate text displays can work for a short video.

**Can Vyra decide who should be credited?**
Provide the names and roles yourself and check them before publishing.

**Should credits cover the final reveal?**
Keep the reveal readable. Place the credits after it or use a composition that gives both enough space.
