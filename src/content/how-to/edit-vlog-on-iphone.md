**To edit a vlog on iPhone with Vyra, add the clips you filmed, describe the day or story you want to show and review the draft. Keep the moments where something changes, preserve the lines that explain the day and give the ending enough time. You can refine the draft in chat or on the timeline.**

A day can contain a lot of footage and still have one small story. You tried something, it went differently than expected, and you got home with something to show. Pick that thread before choosing the song.

## Make the first draft

1. Add the relevant clips from Photos to the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
2. Write one sentence about what happened and which moments matter.
3. Ask for a draft with a target length and say which spoken lines must stay.
4. Watch the opening and check whether someone who was not there can follow it.
5. Shorten repeated setup shots and keep the moment the day changes.
6. Review the ending, then export the video to Photos.

## Give the edit a specific brief

```
Make a short vertical vlog from my studio-day clips. Start with the finished piece, then show arriving, setting up and painting. Keep the line where I explain why I changed the color. Leave the quiet room sounds during the close-ups and hold the final painting shot for 3 seconds.
```

This is an example brief. Replace the moments with things your own clips actually show. If you want a chronological vlog, say so instead of asking for the result first.

## Cut repeated information

Three shots of opening a door might all look nice. Keep the one that explains where you are. Use the room you save for the conversation, mistake or small decision that makes the day yours.

Captions help with spoken sections. They do not need to describe every silent shot. If you want to follow a particular Reel's rhythm, use a [reference](/how-to/match-a-reference-video-on-iphone) and name the editing choices you like.

## FAQ

**Do I need to arrange every clip first?**
You can describe the story and ask Vyra for a draft. The timeline is available when you want to change the order yourself.

**Can I keep natural sound instead of adding music?**
Yes. Say which sounds and spoken lines to preserve in the edit.

**Can I edit the footage after I have already filmed the whole day?**
Yes. Add the existing clips from Photos. You do not need to record them inside Vyra.
