**To trim a video on iPhone in Vyra, add the clip from Photos and shorten its beginning or end on the timeline. You can also ask in chat to start or finish at a specific moment. Play the cut back before exporting so you keep the whole word, movement or reveal.**

The first second often contains you reaching for the phone. The last few seconds might be you looking for the stop button. Those are useful places to start trimming. Leave the part someone came to watch.

## Trim the start and end

1. Add your video to a project in the [iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
2. Play the beginning and find the first moment you want the viewer to see.
3. Select the clip on the timeline and adjust its starting edge, or ask chat to start at that moment.
4. Find where the useful action finishes and adjust the ending edge.
5. Listen across both edges. Keep the beginning of the first word and the end of the last one.
6. Export the shorter video and check it in Photos.

## Choose a useful boundary

For a talking clip, listen for the intake of breath before the sentence. For a painting video, watch when the brush starts moving. If the scene needs context, keep a little of the setup instead of cutting straight into an unexplained detail.

Trimming changes the outside edges of a clip. To remove a mistake in the middle, [split the clip](/how-to/split-video-on-iphone) around the mistake and remove that section.

## Prompt to try

```
Trim the beginning so the video starts just before I say "here is the finished piece". End after I finish showing the painting. Keep the whole sentence and the original audio.
```

## Check before posting

A cut can look tidy while sounding abrupt. Play it with headphones once. If a word starts halfway through, move the start earlier. If the final frame disappears before you can see the result, keep more of the ending.

## FAQ

**Can I name the moment instead of a timestamp?**
Yes. Describe what happens in the clip in Vyra's chat, then check that the draft starts at the moment you meant.

**How do I remove something from the middle?**
Split before and after that section, remove the unwanted piece and check the new join. The split guide walks through that separate job.

**Can I export a trimmed video for free?**
New mobile accounts currently receive 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. Paid mobile plans are available in the App Store.
