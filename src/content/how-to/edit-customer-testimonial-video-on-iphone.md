**To edit a customer testimonial in Vyra on iPhone, keep the speaker’s meaning, remove distractions and check captions against the original recording before sharing the approved version.**

A customer explains what worked for them and adds one important condition. Cutting the condition may make the clip shorter while making the statement inaccurate. Start with the complete answer and decide what it actually supports.

The native listing supports trimming, caption generation and text. The guidance about faithful excerpts is an editorial review method. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Confirm you can use the recording and the intended excerpt.
2. Identify the complete statement and any condition needed to understand it.
3. Trim setup or pauses without joining words into a different claim.
4. Generate captions and verify names, numbers and important qualifiers.
5. Review the exported excerpt against the source and get the appropriate approval before sharing.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A pause adds no meaning | Trim it while preserving natural speech |
| A qualifier changes the claim | Keep it with the statement |
| A result is not verified | Do not turn it into an unsupported title or statistic |

## A brief to try

```
Shorten this customer answer while keeping the full meaning and the condition about their weekly workflow. Preserve their exact words. Add faithful captions and show me the proposed excerpt before finalizing it.
```

## Check the result

Compare the first and last sentence with the full answer. A clip can quote individual words correctly and still leave a misleading impression. Use only real approved statements, and keep editorial claims separate from what the speaker actually says.

## FAQ

**Can I combine separate answers into one sentence?**
Avoid making the speaker appear to say something they did not say. Preserve context and review any shortened excerpt.

**Should I change their words to sound more enthusiastic?**
Use the actual statement. Correct transcription mistakes without rewriting the speaker’s opinion.

**Does this workflow guarantee more conversions?**
No. It explains producing an accurate, readable excerpt rather than predicting sales or engagement.
