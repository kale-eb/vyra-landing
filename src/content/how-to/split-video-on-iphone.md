**To split a video on iPhone in Vyra, add the clip to a project, find the point where you want two pieces and split it on the timeline. To remove a section, make a split on either side of it, remove the middle piece and check the join. You can ask chat to make that edit too.**

A retake usually has a useful sentence on each side. The job is to keep those sentences and remove the restart without making the person sound like they changed their mind halfway through.

## Remove a section from the middle

1. Add the full clip to the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
2. Play through the mistake and find where the part you want to remove starts.
3. Split the clip at that point, then make another split where the useful footage resumes.
4. Remove the unwanted middle piece.
5. Bring the remaining pieces together and listen across the new cut.
6. Adjust the join if it clips a word, then export and review the result.

## Decide what should stay together

Keep a sentence intact when its meaning depends on the start. If you are removing a pause between two sentences, the cut has more room. If you are removing a word halfway through a thought, listen carefully for a change in meaning.

For a silent process clip, cut where the action pauses. A hand that suddenly jumps from the left side of the frame to the right can make a small removal much more noticeable.

## Prompt to try

```
Remove the take where I stop and restart the sentence. Keep the complete take after it. Join the remaining footage without adding music or captions, and leave a short natural pause between sentences.
```

## If a gap remains

Look at the space between the pieces. The current app supports Ripple behavior and deleting gaps. If you change a timeline setting, play the whole affected section again so you catch anything else that moved.

Use [trimming](/how-to/trim-video-on-iphone) when you only need to shorten an outside edge. You do not need two splits for that.

## FAQ

**Is splitting the same as deleting footage?**
No. A split divides a clip into timeline pieces. Removing a piece is a separate edit.

**Can I remove a pause by asking Vyra?**
Yes. Ask for the pause or retake you want removed. Review the resulting sentence to make sure the meaning and timing still work.

**Can I keep both pieces?**
Yes. Split the clip and keep the pieces on the timeline. You can then arrange them with your other footage.
