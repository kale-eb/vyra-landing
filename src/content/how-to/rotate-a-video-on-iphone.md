**To turn a sideways clip upright in Vyra on iPhone, select the clip and use Transform, or ask chat to rotate that clip. Review the framing after rotation because the picture can leave empty space around it.**

A phone recording may arrive sideways while the rest of the project is upright. Fix the individual shot before changing the whole canvas. That keeps the other clips in their intended orientation.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Editing tools on iPhone and Properties panel”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Find the sideways clip on the timeline.
2. Select Transform or ask chat for the direction and amount of rotation.
3. Check which edge is now at the top and whether faces are upright.
4. Adjust the size and position while keeping the important action visible.
5. Play into the next shot to check the two clips feel consistent.

## A brief to try

> Rotate only the sideways studio clip a quarter turn clockwise. Keep the painting upright. Refit that clip to the current canvas without changing the other shots.

## Check the result

Use a small correction for a tilted horizon and a quarter turn for a sideways recording. A large rotation can require a crop to hide empty corners. Check the corners before you decide the correction is finished.

## FAQ

**Should I rotate the canvas or the clip?**
Rotate the clip when only one recording is sideways. Change the canvas when the entire finished video needs another shape.

**Can rotation cut off part of the image?**
The rotated picture may need scaling or repositioning to fit. Check every edge before exporting.
