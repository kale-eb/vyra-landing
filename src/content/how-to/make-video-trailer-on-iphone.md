**To make a trailer on iPhone, choose a short sequence that explains why someone would watch the longer video while leaving its main payoff intact. In Vyra, select moments from your own footage and write a separate brief for the trailer.**

A trailer has a different purpose from a full recap. It should establish the subject and a reason to keep watching. Copying the first few seconds of the longer edit may leave viewers without either.

## Work through the edit

1. Watch the longer video and identify its central question or outcome.
2. Choose a setup moment and a few excerpts that make the subject clear.
3. Decide which reveal to save for the full version.
4. Ask Vyra for the short sequence and provide accurate closing text.
5. Check that excerpts preserve meaning and the call to action points to the right destination.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| The main reveal is the reason to watch | Show only as much as the trailer needs |
| A quote sounds different without context | Choose another excerpt or retain its qualification |
| A long introduction uses most of the trailer | Start nearer the actual subject |

## A brief to try

```
Make a short trailer for the studio film. Show the unfinished painting, the problem I describe and a glimpse of the exhibition. Save the complete reveal for the full video. End with the release wording I provide.
```

The trailer should still be understandable as its own short video. Avoid making a promise that the full version never delivers. If the excerpts feel unrelated, reduce the number of moments and keep one clear thread.

## FAQ

**Is a trailer the same as a highlight recap?**
A recap summarizes what happened. A trailer invites someone into a longer piece and can deliberately hold back its payoff.

**Can I use an already exported video?**
You can edit footage you add, but working from original clips can give more room to adjust existing text or sound.

**Should the trailer repeat the full ending?**
Choose according to its purpose. Keep the ending out when it would remove the main reason to watch.
