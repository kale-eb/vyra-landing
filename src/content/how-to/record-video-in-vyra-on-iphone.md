**The Vyra iPhone app lets you record video straight into your editing timeline. Use the in-app camera when an edit needs one more shot, then review that recording alongside the footage already in the project. You can also add videos you recorded earlier from Photos.**

You might finish a rough cut and realize you never filmed the result. Record that shot before adding more text to explain what is missing. A clear view of the finished painting can do more than a paragraph about it.

## Record a missing shot

1. Open the relevant project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
2. Decide which part of the story needs another shot and how it should fit the existing footage.
3. Open the app's camera and review any camera or microphone access iOS requests.
4. Record the shot with a little stillness before and after the action.
5. Check the recording on the timeline and arrange it where it belongs in the edit.
6. Trim the camera handling, compare its sound and framing with nearby clips, then continue editing.

## Match the shot to the edit

If the other clips are vertical, record the extra shot with that composition in mind. If the scene uses quiet room sound, listen for a loud fan before recording. A new shot that looks or sounds very different may need a clear place in the story rather than being slipped into the middle of a sentence.

## Prompt to try after recording

```
Use the new recording as the final shot. Keep it long enough to see the whole finished painting. Trim the part where I reach for the phone and leave the rest of the edit in its current order.
```

## Keep one useful take

Review the actual recording before you move on. Check whether the subject is in frame and the spoken line is understandable. If the shot does not show what you needed, record it again while everything is still set up.

## FAQ

**Can I record inside Vyra on iPhone?**
Yes. The App Store release notes describe a camera that records clips straight onto your timeline.

**Can I use videos from Photos too?**
Yes. You can add footage from Photos and use it with recordings made in the app.

**Will recording replace the need to review the edit?**
No. Check the new shot in context, especially its beginning, ending and audio level.
