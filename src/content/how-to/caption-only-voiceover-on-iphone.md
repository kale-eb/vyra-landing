**In Vyra on iPhone, open Captions and choose Voiceover under Generate from. Set the spoken language and caption style, generate the captions and check them against the voiceover.**

A process clip may contain conversation in the room as well as the explanation you recorded later. Choose the voiceover source so the captions follow the explanation you want the viewer to hear.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Captions”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Open the project and check that the intended voiceover is present.
2. Tap Captions in the editor tool row.
3. Choose Voiceover under Generate from and select the spoken language.
4. Choose the style and words per caption, then generate the captions.
5. Replay with the voiceover and correct names, timing and line breaks.

## A brief to try

> Caption my recorded voiceover only. Keep the room conversation out of the captions. Use short phrases and leave the finished painting unobstructed.

## Check the result

Check the first and last caption carefully if the voiceover starts after the first shot or ends before the final reveal. If you replace the narration later, review the caption words and timing again.

Keep your source footage while checking the edit. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans are sold through the App Store. Review the saved file in Photos before posting it.

## FAQ

**Should I choose All audio instead?**
Use All audio when the words from multiple audible sources belong in the captions. Choose Voiceover for the separate voiceover source.

**Does choosing Voiceover mute the original clips?**
Caption source selection determines what gets captioned. Check the audio mix separately when you want to quiet other recordings.
