**In Vyra on iPhone, select the unwanted caption on the timeline and use Delete. Keep the underlying video and audio selected separately so the spoken recording stays in the edit.**

A title card may already explain the next section, while a duplicate caption sits over it. Remove only the text that gets in the way. If the words matter to someone watching muted, move or restyle them instead.

The [official iPhone listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) document native editing. Vyra’s current in-app help section Captions supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Play the section and identify the caption blocks you want to remove.
2. Tap the caption on the timeline and confirm that you selected text rather than footage.
3. Use Delete for the unwanted caption.
4. Replay both boundaries and check that nearby captions still appear.
5. Watch the section muted and with sound before exporting.

## A brief to try

> Remove the duplicate caption over my chapter card. Keep the spoken audio, footage and all captions after that card.

## Check the result

A silent-looking gap may contain a quiet word. Listen before deleting its caption. If regeneration replaces the caption track later, inspect this section again rather than assuming the deletion carries over.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

## FAQ

**Will deleting a caption mute the recording?**
A caption is a separate text item. Select the caption itself and check playback afterwards to confirm that the source audio remains.

**Should I remove captions when they cover my subject?**
Try moving or restyling the words first when the speech carries important information. Removing the caption may make the section harder to follow muted.
