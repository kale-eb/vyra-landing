**In Vyra on iPhone, select the masked layer and use Invert to switch which side of the mask is visible. Check the layers beneath it before keeping the result.**

A circular mask normally gives you a small window onto the selected picture. Inverting it can leave the outside visible and reveal the layer underneath through the middle. Decide which picture belongs on each layer before adjusting the edge.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing supplied media. Vyra’s current in-app help, Masks, documents the controls described here.

## Work through the edit

1. Place the picture to be revealed underneath the masked layer.
2. Select the layer with the mask and check its current visible area.
3. Use Invert in the mask tools.
4. Preview the inside and outside of the shape against the lower picture.
5. Check the full moving interval and any edge treatment in the saved export.

## Review the result

The Masks help explicitly lists Invert among native mask tools. Inverting the mask changes the visible area, not the colors of the picture. Keep those two uses of the word invert separate. Avoid placing a label across the boundary until you have checked both sides.

## FAQ

**Is mask inversion the same as a color negative?**
No. Mask inversion changes which area of the layer is visible. A color-inversion effect changes the picture’s colors.

**Why is the center empty after I invert the mask?**
Check what is on the tracks underneath during that interval. The opening reveals those layers, so it needs the intended lower picture.
