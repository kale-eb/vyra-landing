**To speed up a video on iPhone you need an editing app, because the Photos app can't change the speed of a normal video. Apple's free iMovie app speeds up a clip to 2x. In the Vyra app you open the clip and ask in chat, "speed this up 2x". Vyra goes up to 4x per clip and can speed up only one part of a video while the rest plays at normal speed. Then save the result to Photos.**

## Your options on iPhone

| App | How fast | Part of a clip | How you do it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photos | Can't speed up normal video | Only Slo-mo clips, where you choose which part plays slow | Edit, then drag the lines on the speed bar |
| iMovie | 1/8x to 2x | Split the clip first | Tap the clip, tap the speed icon, drag the slider |
| Vyra | 0.1x to 4x per clip | Yes, ask for a time range | Type what you want in chat |

## Speed up a video in Vyra

1. **Get the app.** Download [Vyra AI Video Editor](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) from the App Store and sign in. New accounts get 100 free credits.
2. **Add your clip.** Start a project and pick the video from Photos, or record it in the app.
3. **Ask for the speed.** Type "Speed up the whole video 2x."
4. **Speed up one part instead.** Type "Speed up 0:12 to 0:40 to 4x and keep the rest at normal speed." Vyra reads your footage, so you can also name the moment, like "the part where I'm mixing paint."
5. **Watch it back and adjust.** "A bit slower, 1.5x" or "start the fast part 2 seconds later."
6. **Export.** Save it to Photos or share it straight to TikTok or Instagram.

## How fast to go

| Footage | Speed |
| --- | --- |
| Talking to camera that feels slow | 1.1x to 1.25x |
| Walking, driving, setting up | 2x |
| Typing, loading, screen recordings | 2x to 3x |
| Painting, cooking, cleaning, any long process | 4x, then cut the middle |
| A reveal or the result | Normal speed |

Speech gets hard to follow above about 1.3x. If a talking clip feels slow, cutting the pauses usually works better than speeding it up. See [how to cut a talking head](/how-to/cut-a-talking-head).

## Do it in Vyra

- "Speed up this clip 2x."
- "Speed up the parts where I'm walking to 3x. Keep every part where I'm talking at normal speed."
- "Turn this 6 minute painting clip into 30 seconds. Keep only the moments where the painting visibly changes, speed those up to 4x, and play the last 3 seconds at normal speed."
- "Slow down the jump to 0.5x and speed up everything before it to 2x."

## Common mistakes

- Speeding up talking. Cut the pauses instead.
- One speed for the whole video. Fast for the boring parts, normal for the moment people came for.
- Speeding up a clip with the song already in it. The song speeds up too. Mute the clip and add the music as its own track.

## FAQ

**Can I speed up a video in the Photos app?**
No. Photos can only change which part of a Slo-mo clip plays slow. For a normal video you need an app like iMovie or Vyra.

**How do I speed up only part of a video on iPhone?**
In iMovie, split the clip and change the speed of one piece. In Vyra, ask for the time range or describe the moment, and only that part speeds up.

**How fast can Vyra speed up a video?**
Up to 4x per clip. It also slows clips down to 0.1x. For a long timelapse, keep the key moments and speed those up.

**Is it free?**
The Vyra app is free to download and new accounts get 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. See [Is Vyra free?](/answers/is-vyra-free)

**Can I do this on Android?**
Vyra has no Android app yet. Use [app.usevyra.com](https://app.usevyra.com) on a computer.
