**To speed up a video in Vyra on iPhone, add it to a project and ask in chat for the clip to play faster. Name the clip and the speed you want. Play the result before exporting, especially if there is speech or music nearby.**

If you filmed six minutes of a painting, you can speed up the brushwork and still leave the last shot long enough to see what you made. Start with one clip. Once you like its speed, decide whether the video also needs cuts.

## Choose the tool for the edit

| You want to | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Change a supported high frame rate recording or adjust a Slo-mo section | Check the playback controls in [Apple Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/iphone/iph1d1344a2d/ios). Available options depend on the recording. |
| Set the speed of a specific range by hand | Use the [iMovie Speed inspector](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/imovie-iphone/kna47ca84b07/ios). Apple documents how to adjust the range handles. |
| Describe the speed change and keep editing the same project | Ask in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app), then review the timeline. |

## Speed up a clip in Vyra

1. Add the video from Photos to a project.
2. Identify the clip by an action you can see, such as the shot where you mix the paint.
3. Ask for one speed change. Try “Play the paint mixing clip at twice its current speed. Keep the finished painting shot unchanged.”
4. Replay the cut into and out of the faster clip. Check whether you can still see the action and whether the next shot starts where you expect.
5. Listen to the result. If the faster section includes words people need to hear, keep those words at their original speed.
6. When the edit is ready, [export the finished video to Photos](/how-to/export-video-to-photos-on-iphone) and check the saved file.

## When you only want a short section to go faster

Use the source footage as your reference. Find the first frame after you stop speaking and the first frame you want back at normal speed. Give the start and end of that span in your brief. Check the new boundary against those frames afterward.

A faster clip changes how long it occupies the timeline. If a label was timed to the old cut, watch for it showing up over the wrong shot. You can also [shorten the edit without speeding up speech](/how-to/shorten-video-without-speeding-up-speech-on-iphone) by removing waits and repeat takes.

## FAQ

**Can Photos speed up any video on iPhone?**
Apple documents playback options for supported recordings and separate controls for Slo-mo. Check the controls available on your clip. Use an editor such as iMovie when you need to set the speed of a specific range.

**Is a faster clip the same as a shorter cut?**
Speeding up a clip plays its action faster. A cut removes part of the footage. For speech, first decide whether you can remove a pause or repeat while keeping the voice at its natural pace.

**What can I export for free in Vyra on iPhone?**
New iPhone accounts get 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro are available through the App Store.
