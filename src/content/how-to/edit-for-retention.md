**People leave in three places. The first 3 seconds, the slow middle, and the moment they sense the ending. So I win the opening with a hook and an early cut, put a re-hook every 8 to 12 seconds, deliver part of the payoff before the midpoint, and end on the last useful frame. No outro, no fade, no "thanks for watching."**

## Why it matters

Watch time and completion are what the platforms push on.

## The method

1. **Cut the first 3 seconds last.** Once the video exists, rebuild the opening with the strongest line, a cut inside 1.5 seconds, and a text hook.
2. **Pay something off early.** The viewer stayed for a promise. Give part of it before the midpoint.
3. **Re-hook every 8 to 12 seconds.** Preview the next point, ask a question, change location, drop a word on screen. Rotate.
4. **Cut every "so anyway."** Transitions like "moving on" are exit doors.
5. **Tighten the last 20 percent.** Shorter shots, no summary.
6. **Stop on the last word.** The frame after it lands.

## Rules of thumb

| Point | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 1.5 s | hook line, text hook, first cut |
| by 3 s | second visual change |
| by 40 percent | first real payoff |
| every 8 to 12 s | re-hook |
| last 20 percent | shots getting shorter |
## Examples

- Re-hooks in a 45 second tutorial. 9 s, "and the second one is the one everyone gets wrong." 19 s, new angle plus the word WRONG on screen. 30 s, "this last one saved me an hour a week." 42 s, cut on "done."
- Two endings. Version A, "so yeah, that's how I do it, let me know what you think." Version B, "and that is why I charge by the day." B keeps 2 to 3 more seconds of average watch time on a 40 second video.

## Do it in Vyra

- "Edit this for retention. Open on the strongest claim in the transcript with a cut inside 1.5 seconds. Add a visual change or an on-screen word every 8 to 10 seconds. Remove any summary at the end and cut on the last useful word. 9:16, under 50 seconds."
- "Remove every transitional phrase like 'so anyway' and 'moving on' and cut directly to the next point."
- "The last 10 seconds drag. Shorten each shot by a third, drop the outro entirely, and end on the frame where I set the brush down."

## Common mistakes

- Saving the best part for the end. People who left at 15 seconds never see it.
- A summary paragraph at the end.
- The same pacing all the way through.

## FAQ

**Should I loop it?**
If the last frame naturally matches the first, yes. Don't force it.

**How short is too short?**
Under 7 seconds finishes well but watch time is tiny. 20 to 45 seconds works for most formats.
