**In Vyra on iPhone, select the masked overlay and use Feather, or ask chat for a softer edge. Review the result against the footage underneath so important detail remains visible.**

A small close-up over a wider studio shot may have an edge that feels abrupt. Soften only enough to help the two pictures sit together. The frame still needs to make clear which view is the close-up.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. The current Vyra help sections Masks and Editing tools on iPhone describe the relevant capability.

## Work through the edit

1. Place the supplied overlay over the intended background footage.
2. Select the overlay and check the mask that defines its visible area.
3. Use the mask’s Feather tool or describe the edge change in chat.
4. Preview the edge against both bright and dark parts of the background.
5. Check that lettering and the subject inside the overlay stay clear in the exported video.

## A brief to try

> Soften the edge of the circular detail overlay slightly. Keep the brush tip clear and do not fade the full studio shot beneath it.

## Check the result

Vyra’s current Masks help lists Feather among native tools after a layer has a mask. Feather changes the mask boundary rather than blurring every part of the overlay. Review moving footage throughout its full interval.

## FAQ

**Should I lower opacity instead of feathering the edge?**
Opacity changes how much of the whole item is visible. Feathering addresses its boundary. Choose based on whether the problem is the entire overlay or just the edge.

**Can I use a very soft edge around small text?**
Check the text carefully. A wide feather can make nearby detail fade, so leave enough clear area for what the viewer must read.
