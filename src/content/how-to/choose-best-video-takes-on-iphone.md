**To choose between repeated takes on iPhone, compare the complete message, sound and visible action before building the edit. Add your takes to Vyra and describe the selection criteria, then check the chosen clip yourself.**

The last take is not always the strongest. One version may have clear speech while another shows the action properly. Decide what the video needs to demonstrate before asking the editor to choose.

## Work through the edit

1. Add the alternate takes to one project and identify them in your brief.
2. Write the sentence or action the selected take must include.
3. Compare the full explanation, focus and useful framing across takes.
4. Choose one main take. Use another angle only if it adds information without changing the meaning.
5. Check the draft contains the selected version before adding titles or music.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| One complete explanation | Prefer it over fragments from several takes |
| Better picture but missing a step | Keep the complete take or film the missing step |
| A natural stumble with clear meaning | Decide whether it matters before replacing the whole take |

## A brief to try

```
Compare the three introductions. Keep the take where I explain who this is for without restarting. Preserve the full last sentence. Tell me which take you chose before cutting the rest.
```

If you cannot tell which version was used, compare a distinctive word or gesture with the original. Do not let a fluent edit hide that it combined statements you did not intend to put together.

## FAQ

**Can I ask Vyra to choose a take?**
Yes. Give concrete criteria, then verify the choice in the draft. An automatic choice still needs your review.

**Should I keep two versions of the same sentence?**
Usually choose one for a concise explanation. Keep both only when the repetition has a deliberate purpose.

**Can an editor recover an action I never filmed?**
Vyra edits supplied footage. Film and add a missing action when it is needed to explain the result.
