**Music sets the energy before the first word. I pick tempo by format. 90 to 110 BPM for vlogs and calm process videos, 120 to 140 for montages, slow or ambient for cinematic pieces, and little or none under a talking head. Under speech the track sits 6 to 10 dB down. When the music leads, cuts land on the beat.**

## Why it matters

The wrong track makes good footage feel off and nobody can say why.

## The method

1. **Decide who leads.** Talking head, tutorial, story. The voice leads and music is a bed. Montage, cinematic, hype. Music leads and cuts follow it.
2. **Pick tempo from the table.** Tempo matters more than mood.
3. **Find the energy curve.** A build, a drop where your best shot lands, a comedown for the end.
4. **Set the level.** Music-led is full. Voice-led is 6 to 10 dB under speech, rising a few dB in the gaps.
5. **Cut on the beat when music leads.** Cuts on downbeats, shot size changes every 4 or 8 bars, hold the hero shot through the drop.
6. **Trim the track to the video.** A phrase should end when the video ends.

## Rules of thumb

| Format | BPM | Music role | Under speech |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talking head | 70 to 95 or none | bed | minus 12 to 15 dB |
| Vlog | 90 to 110 | bed, lifts in gaps | minus 6 to 10 dB |
| Storytelling | 60 to 90 | bed, drops at the turn | minus 8 to 12 dB |
| Montage | 120 to 140 | lead | speech rare |
| Cinematic | 60 to 90 or ambient | lead | speech rare |

## Examples

- Montage at 128 BPM, 24 seconds. Cuts every bar for the first 8 seconds, every beat through the build, a 3 second hold on the hero shot at the drop, every 2 beats to the end.

## Common mistakes

- A 140 BPM track under a slow process video.
- Music at the same level as the voice.
- Cutting to the beat when the voice should lead.

## FAQ

**Where do I get music I can use?**
In-app sounds for that app, a royalty-free library for cross-posting, a direct license for client work.

**How do I find the BPM?**
Tap along for 15 seconds and multiply by 4. Vyra detects beats on upload for syncing.
