**Music sets the energy before the first word. Match tempo to the format: 90 to 110 BPM for vlogs and relaxed process videos, 120 to 140 BPM for montages and hype edits, slower or ambient for cinematic pieces, and minimal or no music under talking heads where the voice is the content. Under speech, duck the track 6 to 10 dB. In music-led edits, cut on the beat and change shots on phrase boundaries. Use platform-licensed or royalty-free tracks unless you have rights.**

## Why it matters

The wrong track makes good footage feel off in a way viewers cannot name. Music also decides where cuts land in montage formats and whether a talking head sounds professional or like a podcast with a beat under it. And on the business side, unlicensed music gets videos muted or removed.

## The method

1. **Decide whether the voice or the music leads.** Talking head, tutorial, storytelling: voice leads, music is a bed. Montage, cinematic, hype: music leads, cuts follow it.
2. **Pick tempo by format.** Use the table below. Tempo more than mood decides whether the edit feels right.
3. **Match the energy curve to the video.** Intro build, a drop or chorus where your best shot lands, a comedown for the ending. Find that in the track before you cut.
4. **Set the level.** Music-led: track at full, sound effects and ambience on top. Voice-led: track 6 to 10 dB under speech, rising 3 to 4 dB in gaps.
5. **Cut on the beat in music-led edits.** Place cuts on downbeats, change shot size on phrase boundaries (every 4 or 8 bars), and hold the hero shot through the drop.
6. **Trim the track to the video, not the reverse.** Cut the track so a phrase ends when the video ends. A fade-out under the last shot is the exception to the no-fade rule, and only in music-led formats.
7. **Check the license.** Platform libraries (in-app sounds) are licensed only inside that platform. Royalty-free libraries cover cross-posting. Commercial or client work needs a commercial license.

## Rules of thumb

| Format | BPM range | Music role | Level under speech |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talking head | 70 to 95 or none | bed | minus 12 to 15 dB |
| Tutorial | 80 to 100 | bed | minus 10 to 12 dB |
| Vlog | 90 to 110 | bed, lifts between speech | minus 6 to 10 dB |
| Storytelling | 60 to 90 | bed, drops at the turn | minus 8 to 12 dB |
| Montage | 120 to 140 | lead | speech rare |
| Cinematic | 60 to 90 or ambient | lead | speech rare |
| UGC demo | 100 to 120 | bed | minus 8 to 10 dB |

Cut points at 120 BPM: one beat is 0.5 seconds, one bar is 2 seconds, one 4-bar phrase is 8 seconds.

## Examples

Beat-synced montage at 128 BPM, 24 seconds: cuts every bar (1.875 s) for the first 8 seconds, every beat (0.47 s) through the build, 3 second hold on the hero shot at the drop, every 2 beats to the end, track trimmed so the phrase ends on the last frame.

Vlog with a 100 BPM bed: music sits at minus 8 dB under talking clips, rises to minus 3 dB during the walking b-roll, drops out completely for the 2 second reaction moment, returns on the next cut.

Talking head with no music: room tone only, one sound effect on the on-screen word at 15 seconds. This is often the right call for opinion content.

## Common mistakes

- A 140 BPM track under a slow process video. Energy mismatch is immediate.
- Music at the same level as speech. The voice fights the track and loses.
- Cutting to the beat when the format is voice-led. Cut on words instead.
- Starting the video on a quiet intro of the track. Trim to the section with energy.
- In-app sounds cross-posted to another platform. Muted on arrival.

## FAQ

**Where do I find music I can use?**
Platform sound libraries for in-platform posts, royalty-free subscription libraries for cross-posting, and direct licenses for client work.

**Should trending audio override the tempo rules?**
Trending audio is a distribution choice. If the tempo fights the footage, cut a version with the trend for that platform and a version with the right track elsewhere.

**How do I find the BPM of a track?**
Tap along for 15 seconds and multiply by 4, or use any BPM counter app. Vyra detects beats on upload for syncing.
