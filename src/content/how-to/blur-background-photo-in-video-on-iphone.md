**In Vyra on iPhone, put the supplied background photo beneath your text and apply a blur to that photo. Leave the text layer clear and check the complete composition at phone size.**

A busy studio photo can make a short announcement difficult to read. Keep the photo as context while softening its detail. Check that the words are still useful without needing a very large outline or shadow.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing supplied media. Vyra’s current in-app help, Effects and Editing tools on iPhone, documents the controls described here.

## Work through the edit

1. Place the supplied photo behind the text for the intended interval.
2. Select that photo rather than the text or the whole composition.
3. Open Effects and try a modest Gaussian Blur, or describe it in chat.
4. Check the text contrast and make sure important photo context remains recognizable.
5. Review the exported title section at its intended viewing size.

## FAQ

**Why did the text become blurry too?**
Check which item received the effect. Keep the photo treatment on its own layer and inspect the text separately.

**Will a blurred background always make text readable?**
Check contrast as well as detail. Bright lettering on a pale area can still be hard to read after blur.
