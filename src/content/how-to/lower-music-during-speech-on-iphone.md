**In Vyra on iPhone, ask chat to lower the music during the spoken passages and bring it back gradually in the gaps. Keep the voice track unchanged and review every transition by listening.**

Music can sound fine during the opening montage and cover the first sentence that follows. Lowering the whole song also makes the quiet sections feel empty. Give the music a different level where the voice needs room.

The [official iPhone listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) document native editing. Vyra’s current in-app help section Audio features supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Identify where each spoken passage starts and ends.
2. Ask Vyra to lower the music under those passages while preserving the voice.
3. Ask for gradual level changes before and after the speech.
4. Listen on the phone speaker at a normal volume.
5. Check quiet words, sentence endings and the return to music before exporting.

## A brief to try

> Keep my voice at its current level. Lower the music while I explain the repair, then bring it back gently after I finish. Keep the music quieter through the final word.

## Check the result

Vyra’s audio help documents volume keyframes for fades and ducking. Use chat for this timed instruction instead of assuming a named automatic ducking switch exists on iPhone. A percentage alone does not prove that two different recordings will sound equally clear.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

## FAQ

**Should I raise the voice instead of lowering the music?**
Listen to the original voice first. If the voice is clear alone and masked by the song, reducing the music addresses that overlap without making every sound louder.

**Can I use one music level for the whole video?**
That can work for a consistently spoken video. A montage with speech in only a few places may benefit from different levels across those sections.
