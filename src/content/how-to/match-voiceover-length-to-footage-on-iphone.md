**To fit a voiceover to footage in Vyra on iPhone, compare the narration with the shots it needs, shorten unsupported lines and give important explanations enough picture time.**

Your narration describes glazing the bowl while the video has already moved on to the finished shelf. Start by deciding whether the voice or the picture needs to change. Rushing the voice can make a simple explanation difficult to follow.

The official listing confirms recording voiceover and adjusting audio alongside timeline edits. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Lay out the footage in the order the narration should explain.
2. Record or add the voiceover and listen against the relevant shots.
3. Identify lines that have no matching coverage or overrun the available action.
4. Revise the narration, keep a longer source section or add a shot you actually filmed.
5. Watch the complete saved file and check that each explanation meets the right image.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A line names an unseen detail | Add real coverage or revise the line |
| An important instruction needs more time | Keep a longer suitable shot |
| A sentence repeats information | Shorten the wording before speeding the voice |

## A brief to try

```
Fit my recorded voiceover to the pottery footage. Keep the glazing explanation over the glazing shots. Flag any line without matching coverage and leave the important instructions at a natural speaking speed.
```

## Check the result

If you rewrite a line, record it with a similar tone and distance from the microphone. Review the join by listening as well as watching. A supplied voiceover can guide the edit, but it should not force a shot to claim something it does not show.

## FAQ

**How do I handle an overlong voiceover?**
First check repeated wording and shot duration. Natural speech may be easier to follow.

**Can I record a voiceover in the app?**
The native listing describes recording a voiceover. Check the recording and its placement before exporting.

**How do I handle a line with no matching footage?**
Film it, use suitable material you are allowed to use or revise the explanation. Vyra does not generate missing video footage.
