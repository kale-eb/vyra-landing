**In Vyra on iPhone, open Captions and choose one word under Words per caption before generating. Review the spoken rhythm and the longest words before keeping that treatment.**

A short reaction can work with one word at a time. A fast explanation may become tiring when every word replaces the last. Try the setting on the spoken section you actually plan to keep, then judge it at normal speed.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing supplied media. Vyra’s current in-app help, Captions, documents the controls described here.

## Work through the edit

1. Finish the cuts in the spoken recording before generating captions.
2. Tap Captions and choose the intended audio under Generate from.
3. Set Spoken language and choose one word under Words per caption.
4. Choose a style and generate captions, then play the full sentence.
5. Check long words and the saved export without pausing to read.

## A brief to try

> Show one word at a time for the short reaction after the reveal. Keep the complete spoken wording. Leave the longer explanation in readable phrases.

## Review the result

One-word captions set the amount of text shown together. They do not establish which word a viewer will find important. Keep negations and qualifying words visible, and use the phrase-based treatment if the sentence becomes hard to follow.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro are sold through the App Store.

## FAQ

**Should I use one-word captions through a long interview?**
Try a representative sentence first. The viewing pace matters more than keeping one treatment across every type of speech.

**Can I shorten the transcript to make the words fit?**
Keep the meaning faithful. Review the font size, framing and grouping before removing a spoken word.
