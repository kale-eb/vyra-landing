**To rename a media item in Vyra on iPhone, open Library and Assets, press and hold the item and choose Rename. Give the recording a name that helps you distinguish it from similar clips.**

Five clips named after camera timestamps can be difficult to tell apart. A name such as “palette close-up, clear brush sound” tells you why you kept that recording when you return to the project.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Library on iPhone”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Open Library and locate the media item under Assets.
2. Preview the clip to confirm which recording it is.
3. Press and hold the item and choose Rename.
4. Use a short description of the action, take or useful sound.
5. Check the renamed item before moving on to another clip.

## A brief to try

> Use names that distinguish the wide studio shot, the finished painting reveal and the palette close-up. Keep the words consistent across the footage list you are working from.

## Check the result

Rename similar recordings one at a time while their content is fresh in your mind. A name helps retrieval, but keep any separate notes about which parts of the recording you want to use.

Keep your source footage while checking the edit. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans are sold through the App Store. Review the saved file in Photos before posting it.

## FAQ

**Should I put private customer details in a filename?**
Use a neutral description when a job name or action is enough. Check names before sharing screenshots or footage lists.

**Does renaming replace the need to preview a take?**
Preview the material before choosing it. A short name can leave out a detail that matters for the edit.
