**In Vyra on iPhone, identify the title by its words and ask chat to change its font. Use a font available in Vyra and review the new line breaks before keeping it.**

A narrow title can fit on one line while a wider face pushes one word onto a second line. Changing the font may also change how long the title takes to read. Give the words enough room before reducing their size.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Properties panel text controls”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Find the exact title you want to change.
2. Ask for the new font or describe the intended lettering.
3. Check whether the chosen face is available in the project.
4. Review line breaks and placement on the canvas.
5. Play the title with its entrance and exit so every word stays readable.

## A brief to try

> Change only the title “An afternoon in the studio” to a simple serif font from the available catalog. Keep the captions as they are. Use two balanced lines if the title becomes too wide.

## Check the result

Choose a font for the smallest display where the video will be seen. A decorative face can work for a short title and become difficult in a longer paragraph. Read the words at the size of a normal phone preview.

## FAQ

**Can I assume every font name will be available?**
Vyra uses its available font catalog. Check the returned title when requesting a specific face.

**Will a font change alter the title duration?**
Review the timing separately. Ask to preserve the duration if the text already enters and leaves at the right moments.
