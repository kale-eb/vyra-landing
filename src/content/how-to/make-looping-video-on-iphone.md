**To make a video feel like it loops on iPhone, choose an ending that leads naturally back to the opening and check the join by playing the exported clip twice. In Vyra, trim and arrange your own footage around that repeated action.**

A hand passing over the lens, an object returning to its starting place or a sentence leading back into itself can give you a useful join. Choose from what the footage contains. The exported file and the player's repeat behavior are separate things.

## Work through the edit

1. Find a start and end with similar composition or a continuing action.
2. Add the clip to Vyra and remove unrelated setup.
3. Trim the opening and ending so the repeated movement feels continuous.
4. Listen for an abrupt sound cut at the boundary.
5. Export and play the file twice in succession to judge the end-to-start join.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| The object returns to its starting position | Try joining those moments |
| Speech ends halfway through a thought | Choose a complete phrase or revise the opening |
| Music ends abruptly | Reconsider the sound at the boundary |

## A brief to try

```
Make a short loop from the hand movement in this clip. Start just before the hand enters and end where it can lead back into the same movement. Keep the useful original sound and show me the end-to-start join.
```

A visual match can still feel wrong if the rhythm or audio jumps. Compare the final second and first second together. Do not stretch the clip merely to force two unrelated movements to meet.

## FAQ

**Will the exported MP4 repeat forever?**
An exported clip has a finite duration. Whether it repeats during playback depends on the destination player.

**Do I need a special transition?**
A direct join can work when the recorded movement lines up. Check the footage before adding an effect.

**Can every clip become a convincing loop?**
Some endings do not connect naturally to their openings. Use a different boundary or choose footage with a repeatable action.
