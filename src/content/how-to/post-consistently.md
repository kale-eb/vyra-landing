**The people who post daily for a year aren't more disciplined. They removed the decisions. One format they can repeat. One filming block a week. One editing pattern that runs the same way every time. A schedule set once. When each post is a prompt and a publish instead of a project, daily gets easy.**

## Why it matters

Every platform rewards volume, and everyone who quit did it because each video cost too much.

## The method

1. **Pick the frequency your worst week allows.** If a bad week allows three, commit to three.
2. **One format.** Decide what a post is so you never decide again.
3. **One filming block.** Same day, same time, every week.
4. **One editing pattern.** Same length, captions, music behavior. Save it as a prompt.
5. **Schedule the week on filming day.**
6. **Keep three in the buffer.** Three finished videos you haven't posted. That's what absorbs a bad week.
7. **Track the streak, not the numbers.** For 60 days the only metric is whether you posted.

## Rules of thumb

| Element | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | what your worst week allows |
| Buffer | 3 finished, unposted |
| Filming blocks per week | 1 |
| Edit time per post | under 15 minutes |
| Days before judging | 60 |
| Formats | 1 until the streak hits 30 days |

## Examples

- A student with studio time on Sundays. Film Sunday, post Monday to Friday. Five a week, and the buffer covers exam weeks.
- A founder posting "day N" vlogs. The footage is the day itself, the check-in is 20 seconds, the edit is one saved prompt. Under 10 minutes a post.

## Do it in Vyra

```
Save this as my standard edit: 30-45 seconds, 9:16, open on the strongest line, cut all pauses over 0.4 seconds, 3-5 word white captions in the lower third, music at 20% under my voice. Apply it to today's footage.
```

```
Apply my standard edit to each of the five takes in this project and give me five separate exports named by their opening line.
```

```
Same standard edit, but this one is a process video with no talking, so no captions, and bring the music to 60%.
```

## Common mistakes

- Committing to daily on week one and quitting on week three.
- No buffer, so one bad week ends the streak.
- Editing each video like it's the first.
- Checking analytics every day in the first 60 days.

## FAQ

**Is daily necessary?**
No. Three a week for six months beats daily for six weeks.

**The video isn't good enough?**
Post it. In the first 60 days the streak matters more, and your sense of "good enough" isn't calibrated yet.
