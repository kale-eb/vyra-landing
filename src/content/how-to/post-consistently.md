**Consistency is a system, not willpower. The creators who post daily for a year are not more disciplined; they have removed the decisions. One format they can repeat. One filming block a week. One editing pattern that runs the same way every time. A posting schedule set once. When each post is a prompt and a publish instead of a project, daily becomes easy.**

## Why it matters

Every platform rewards recency and volume, and every creator who quit did so because posting cost too much per video. Consistency is not about posting more. It is about making each post cost less.

## The method

1. **Pick the frequency you can hold on your worst week.** Not your best. If your worst week allows three posts, commit to three.
2. **One format.** Decide what a post is, so you never decide again. See the niche guide.
3. **One filming block.** Same day, same time, every week. See the batch-filming guide.
4. **One editing pattern.** Same length, caption style, music behavior. Write it down as a prompt and reuse it.
5. **Schedule the week on filming day.** Every platform has a scheduler. Set seven posts and stop thinking about it.
6. **Keep a buffer of three.** Three finished videos you have not posted. That is what absorbs a bad week.
7. **Track the streak, not the numbers.** For the first 60 days, the only metric is whether you posted.

## Rules of thumb

| Element | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | what your worst week allows, then hold it |
| Buffer | 3 finished, unposted videos |
| Filming blocks per week | 1 |
| Editing time per post | under 15 minutes |
| Days before judging results | 60 |
| Formats | 1 until the streak is 30 days |

## Examples

A student with studio time on Sundays: film Sunday, post Monday through Friday, weekends off. Five posts a week, sustainable through exams because the buffer covers it.

A founder posting "day N" vlogs: the daily footage is the day itself, the talking check-in is 20 seconds, the edit is one saved prompt. The cost per post is under 10 minutes.

## Do it in Vyra

Copy and adapt:

```
Save this as my standard edit: 30-45 seconds, 9:16, open on the strongest line, cut all pauses over 0.4 seconds, 3-5 word white captions in the lower third, music at 20% under my voice. Apply it to today's footage.
```

```
Apply my standard edit to each of the five takes in this project and give me five separate exports named by their opening line.
```

```
Same standard edit, but this one is a process video with no talking, so no captions, and bring the music to 60%.
```

## Common mistakes

- Committing to daily on week one and quitting on week three.
- No buffer, so one bad week ends the streak.
- Editing each video as if it were the first.
- Checking analytics daily during the first 60 days.
- Adding a second format before the first is automatic.

## FAQ

**Is daily necessary?**
No. Three a week, held for six months, beats daily for six weeks.

**What if a video is not good enough?**
Post it. The streak matters more than the video in the first 60 days, and your sense of "good enough" is not calibrated yet.

**How do I restart after a gap?**
Post the next one. Do not announce the gap. Nobody was counting.
