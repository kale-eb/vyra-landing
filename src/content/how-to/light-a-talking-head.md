**You need one big soft light on your face and nothing fighting it. The easiest version is a window 45 degrees to one side, a white wall or a matching lamp on the other side for fill, every other light in the room off, and your phone's exposure locked on your face. This costs nothing and looks better than a ring light straight on.**

## Why it matters

Light decides skin tone and where the eye goes.

## The method

1. **Find the biggest soft source.** A window in daytime, not direct sun. If the sun hits it, hang a white sheet.
2. **45 degrees off your face.** Not straight on, not behind you. Slightly above eye level.
3. **Fill the other side.** A white wall, foam board, or a lamp with a daylight bulb. About half as bright.
4. **Turn everything else off.** Ceiling light, warm lamps, screen glow. Mixed color is the most common amateur tell.
5. **Add separation if you can.** A small lamp behind you, off to one side, on your hair or shoulder.
6. **Lock exposure and focus.** Tap and hold on your face until the lock shows. Otherwise the phone re-exposes every time you move.

## Rules of thumb

| Setting | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Key angle | 30 to 45 degrees |
| Key height | slightly above eye level |
| Fill | about half the key |
| Color temperature | one only, 5000 to 5600 K daylight or 3200 K all lamps |
| Window to face | 1 to 1.5 m |
## Examples

- Daytime desk. Window camera left at 45 degrees, white foam board camera right, ceiling off, phone locked on my face. This is most of my studio videos, the window doubles as the studio light.
- Ring light done right. Off to the side at 45 degrees, dimmed to 40 percent, phone not mounted in the middle of it. Center-mounting is what makes the flat look and the ring in your eyes.

## Do it in Vyra

Light on the shoot, fix small things in the edit.

- "Warm up the skin tones in this talking head slightly and lift the shadows on the right side of my face. Keep it natural, no heavy grade."
- "Match the color between the two takes at 0 to 20 seconds and 20 to 45 seconds. The second one is bluer."
- "Apply a subtle cinematic grade to the whole video but protect skin tones."

## Common mistakes

- Window behind you. You're a silhouette.
- Ring light straight on.
- Ceiling light plus warm lamp plus window. Three colors on one face.

## FAQ

**Do I need to buy a light?**
Not in daytime. For evenings, one LED panel or a lamp with a daylight bulb.

**Glasses glare?**
Raise the key higher and move it further to the side. Tilt the glasses down a bit.
