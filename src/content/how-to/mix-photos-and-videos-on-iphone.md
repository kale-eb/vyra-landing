**To combine photos and videos on iPhone, place each image where it adds information to the moving footage, give it enough time to read and keep the frame consistent. In Vyra, assemble the material in a project and review the transitions between still and moving images.**

A photo can show an early sketch, a close detail or a moment you did not film. It does not need to fill the same duration as a video clip. Decide what viewers should notice in each still before choosing its length.

## Work through the edit

1. Gather the photos and clips that belong to the same story.
2. Add them to Vyra and choose the order before adding decoration.
3. Place still images beside the footage they explain.
4. Check each photo's framing and time on screen at normal playback speed.
5. Preview the sound across image changes, then export the complete sequence.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A sketch explains the finished work | Show it before the corresponding result |
| A still contains small writing | Allow enough time to read it |
| A landscape photo loses important detail | Keep a wider composition rather than forcing a tight crop |

## A brief to try

```
Combine these process clips with the sketch photo. Show the sketch before the painting begins and return to the finished piece at the end. Hold the photo long enough to see its details. Keep my existing spoken explanation.
```

A photo shown too briefly can become visual noise. Watch the edit without pausing and name what each still contributed. Remove images that repeat information without helping the story.

## Review and export

Save the finished edit to Photos and open that saved copy to review it. New mobile accounts currently get 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro are sold through the App Store. AI editing needs an internet connection.

## FAQ

**Should all photos have the same duration?**
Choose duration according to what the viewer needs to see or read. Equal timing is a stylistic choice, not a requirement.

**Can I mix portrait and landscape material?**
Yes. Check each composition and preserve important detail instead of filling the frame at any cost.

**Can the music continue while a photo is on screen?**
Yes. Keep the music under the sequence and listen across the change from a moving clip to a still image.
