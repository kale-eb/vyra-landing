**To highlight a detail in an iPhone video, place a simple sticker, label or frame beside the detail and limit it to the time it helps. Check that the annotation does not hide what you want people to see.**

You want viewers to notice the tiny edge you are painting. A large label covering the brush does the opposite. Choose the detail first, then decide whether a frame or a few words make it easier to follow.

Version 0.1.4 documents Elements with recolorable stickers, speech bubbles, labels and frames. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Find the shot and moment where the detail is clearest.
2. Choose a sticker, label or frame from the available Elements.
3. Place it so the relevant action remains visible.
4. Limit its duration to the section it explains.
5. Watch at phone size and remove the annotation if the original shot is clearer without it.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A small stationary detail | A simple frame can draw attention to its location |
| A short explanation is necessary | Use a label with the fewest useful words |
| The subject moves away | End or reposition the annotation instead of leaving it pointing at empty space |

## A brief to try

```
Highlight the edge of the canvas while I explain it. Use a small readable label that says "keep this edge clean." Leave the brush visible and remove the label before the next shot.
```

Watch once without pausing. The viewer should see both the annotation and the detail in the available time. This guide does not assume automatic tracking. Check a moving subject throughout the section rather than judging one still frame.

## FAQ

**Which annotation types are documented?**
Version 0.1.4 lists recolorable stickers, speech bubbles, labels and frames.

**Does a sticker automatically follow a moving subject?**
This guide does not promise tracking. Review placement through the shot and change it where needed.

**Should I add a sticker to every shot?**
Use it where it explains something specific. Too many annotations can compete with the footage.
