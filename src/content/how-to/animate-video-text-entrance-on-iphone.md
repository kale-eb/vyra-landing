**In Vyra on iPhone, add the text and ask chat for the entrance you want, including when it should settle into a readable position. Keep the words still long enough to read after the movement.**

The label can arrive with the new shot and then stay out of the way. If it spends the whole interval moving, the viewer has to chase the words instead of looking at the work.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. The current Vyra help sections Properties panel and Editing tools on iPhone describe the relevant capability.

## Work through the edit

1. Write the shortest wording that explains the shot.
2. Add the text or ask Vyra to create the text item.
3. Describe the entrance, timing and final position in chat.
4. Preview the settled text for readability and overlap with the subject.
5. Check the next cut and any exit so the label does not remain over the wrong shot.

## A brief to try

> Bring the words “final layer” in from the side as this shot begins. Settle it quickly above the brush, then keep it still and readable until the next cut.

## Check the result

The current Properties help lists text Animation, and the native listing documents text and timeline editing. Use the preview to judge the specific movement. A caption track for spoken words may need a quieter style than an occasional scene label.

## FAQ

**How much movement should I use for a long label?**
Shorten the label first. Leave enough of its screen time for reading rather than spending the whole interval on animation.

**Can I use the same moving style for speech captions?**
Review the spoken pace and caption length separately. A style that works for one title may be difficult to follow across a full conversation.
