**In Vyra on iPhone, select the intended recording, open Effects and its Audio category, then use Reverb or ask chat for the sound you want. Keep speech understandable and compare the ending of each phrase.**

A short sound in a room-tour edit may suit a little space around it. A spoken explanation can quickly become harder to follow with the same treatment. Decide which recording needs the effect before applying it.

The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own footage. Vyra’s current in-app help, Audio features, supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose the audio item that should receive the effect.
2. Open the clip’s Effects and the Audio category, or identify the clip in chat.
3. Apply a restrained reverb treatment.
4. Listen to word endings and the gap before the next sound.
5. Check that the effect does not spill across an important cut in the exported sequence.

## A brief to try

> Add a little room-like reverb to the bell sound only. Leave my spoken explanation dry and easy to understand. Keep the bell’s tail from covering the next sentence.

## Check the result

The Audio features help lists Reverb in the iPhone Audio effects category. Reverb is a creative treatment rather than evidence of the room in which you recorded. Keep the untreated version available for comparison.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

## FAQ

**Should I add reverb to the whole video?**
Choose the item that needs it. Applying the same treatment to speech, music and a short effect can make the mix harder to understand.

**Can reverb fix an already echoey recording?**
Adding reverb adds more of that kind of effect. It does not demonstrate that existing room reflections have been removed.
