**To keep your original audio while matching a reference in Vyra on iPhone, limit the reference request to visual choices and explicitly preserve your speech, source sounds and music decisions.**

The reference has a loud song and quick cuts. Your clip is someone explaining what they made. Borrowing the caption placement should not turn that explanation into a silent montage.

Caleb’s mobile post documents reference sharing, and the native listing confirms audio adjustment alongside timeline editing. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Share the reference and add the recording whose sound you want to keep.
2. List the visual properties you want to borrow.
3. Tell Vyra which source audio must remain and whether any new music is allowed.
4. Review the first draft with headphones, especially around shortened shots.
5. Compare the saved file with the original speech before publishing.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| You want the caption treatment | Request the caption style without reference audio |
| You need the sound of a tool | Name that source sound as something to preserve |
| Music is already right | Exclude music changes from the brief |

## A brief to try

```
Match only the reference’s caption placement and shot pacing. Keep my complete spoken explanation and the sound of the pottery wheel. Do not replace the audio or add the reference’s song.
```

## Check the result

Listen for clipped words, unexpected silence and a change in music level. A visually correct draft can still change what the viewer hears. If the requested pacing conflicts with a complete sentence, keep the sentence or choose a different place for the visual cut.

## FAQ

**Does sharing a reference transfer its music rights?**
No. Use audio you have permission to include in your own video.

**Can I borrow the pace without shortening a sentence?**
Ask to preserve the speech and review where the visual changes occur. Do not assume every reference cut fits your recording.

**Should I check the preview or the export?**
Check both. The saved file is what you will share.
