**To edit repeated actions in Vyra on iPhone, show one complete example of the action, shorten later repetitions and keep the moments where the process changes.**

You filmed every stroke while painting the background. The viewer needs to understand the action, then see what changes. Showing all forty similar strokes can hide the moment where you switch tools or reveal the result.

The native listing supports trim, split and rearrangement. This guide teaches which repeated material to retain, rather than claiming a special detection tool. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Identify one clear complete repetition that explains the action.
2. Mark later moments that change the tool, material or visible result.
3. Keep the first useful demonstration and shorten redundant repetitions.
4. Preserve any count or sequence that the explanation depends on.
5. Review whether someone can still follow the process from the edited footage.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A repeated action teaches the technique | Show one understandable cycle |
| The material changes after several repetitions | Keep the visible change |
| The exact number matters | State or show the count rather than implying fewer actions |

## A brief to try

```
Show one full sanding pass, then shorten the repeated passes. Keep the moment I change paper and the final surface check. Preserve any spoken instructions about how many passes are needed.
```

## Check the result

Look for progress in the image rather than aiming for a cut every second. If the video teaches a technique, leave enough time to see the movement. If it only summarizes a finished project, fewer repeated cycles may be enough.

## FAQ

**Should I speed up all repeated actions?**
You can also remove redundant sections. Keep a normal-speed example when the movement itself needs to be understood.

**Can this change the apparent work involved?**
A shorter edit can omit time. Use accurate labels or narration where the amount of work matters.

**Is this a promise of automatic repetition detection?**
No. Identify the actions in your footage and review the proposed selections.
