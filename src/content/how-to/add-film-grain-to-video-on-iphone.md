**In Vyra on iPhone, open the selected clip’s Adjust and Style controls, or ask chat to add restrained film grain. Check fine lines, skin and text in the exported file before keeping it.**

A little texture may suit an archival-looking studio sequence. A pencil line or small product label still needs to stay clear. Judge the treatment on the detail the viewer came to see.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. Vyra’s current Color and the Adjust panel and Effects help sections describe these controls.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose the section that should use the textured look.
2. Open Adjust and Style, or ask for a small film-grain treatment in chat.
3. Compare the picture with and without the treatment.
4. Watch fine lines and captions at a normal phone viewing size.
5. Inspect the saved export before applying the treatment to the rest of the sequence.

## A brief to try

> Add a little film grain to the studio montage. Keep the drawing close-up clean enough to see the pencil lines and keep the text readable.

## Check the result

The current help documents Grain in Adjust and describes Film Grain as moving texture. Added grain is a creative treatment. It does not demonstrate that a video was captured on film or improve the original recording’s detail.

## FAQ

**Is film grain the same as noise in a dark recording?**
Added grain is a chosen effect. Noise already present in a recording may have a different appearance. Compare the untreated source first.

**Can I judge grain from a screenshot alone?**
Play the video too. The help describes moving grain, so a single frame does not show its full appearance.
