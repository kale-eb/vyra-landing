**To caption fast speech in Vyra on iPhone, settle the spoken cut first, generate the captions and review the fastest phrases against the audio before exporting.**

The exciting part is often the part where you talk fastest. That section needs closer checking than the calm introduction. A caption that appears briefly can be accurate and still disappear before someone finishes reading it.

The official listing confirms caption generation, timeline trimming and caption styling. The reading checks are editorial guidance, not a measured transcription-accuracy claim. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Finish removing any speech you do not intend to keep.
2. Generate captions for that version of the recording.
3. Listen to each fast phrase while checking that the words appear with it.
4. Review phrase grouping and layout without changing the meaning.
5. Watch the saved video once without sound and once while following the voice.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Words are missing | Correct the transcript against the recording |
| Correct words disappear too quickly | Review the phrase boundaries and consider a less rushed spoken cut |
| A caption spans a removed sentence | Review caption timing after the cut |

## A brief to try

```
Caption the section where I explain the materials quickly. Keep the exact wording, including the quantities. Flag uncertain words for review and keep captions aligned with the retained speech.
```

## Check the result

If viewers would need to pause every sentence, the spoken edit may need more room. Keep an explanatory pause or use a clearer take when you have one. Increasing caption density will not make an overcrowded explanation easier to understand.

## FAQ

**Can AI guarantee every word in fast speech?**
No transcript should be treated as guaranteed. Listen closely to names, quantities and overlapping sounds.

**Should I generate captions before trimming?**
Generate or review them against the spoken edit you will actually export. Later cuts can change timing.

**Can captions repair unclear recorded audio?**
They can display checked wording, but they do not restore speech that was never recorded clearly.
