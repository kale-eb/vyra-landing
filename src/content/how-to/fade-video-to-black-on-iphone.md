**In Vyra on iPhone, ask chat to fade the final picture to black over the interval you choose. Specify what should happen to the audio separately and check the last frame of the export.**

A final view of the drawing may need time to be seen before it disappears. Start the fade after that view has done its job. Ending the image and ending the music are two different decisions.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. The current Vyra help sections Properties panel and Editing tools on iPhone describe the relevant capability.

## Work through the edit

1. Find the last shot that needs to remain visible.
2. Choose where its fade should start and where the black ending should be complete.
3. Ask Vyra to fade the picture over that interval.
4. Specify whether music or speech should continue, fade or end independently.
5. Play through the final frame and sound in the saved export.

## A brief to try

> Hold the finished drawing, then fade the picture to black over the last second. Let the music finish gently. Keep my full final sentence before the fade starts.

## Check the result

Vyra’s current Properties help documents opacity and animatable settings. Describe the fade’s timing in chat. Review whether an underlying layer remains visible when the top picture fades.

## FAQ

**Will fading the picture also fade the music?**
Specify the audio ending separately. A change to the picture does not establish that the music or voice has been changed.

**Why can I still see another image during the fade?**
Check the layers underneath the fading picture. Reducing opacity can reveal material below it, so review the whole composition.
