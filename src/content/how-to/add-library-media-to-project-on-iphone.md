**In Vyra on iPhone, locate the item in Library, press and hold it and choose Add to project. Check the destination project before you continue editing.**

A close-up you uploaded for one edit may also explain a step in another. Find that recording in the library before uploading the same file again. Preview it to check whether it contains the moment you remember.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Library on iPhone”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Open Library and locate the existing recording.
2. Preview it to check the action, sound and duration.
3. Press and hold the item and choose Add to project.
4. Choose the intended project when the app asks for a destination.
5. Open that project and check the added material before placing or requesting the cut.

## Check the result

Adding media gives the project material to use. Check the timeline afterward to see where the shot belongs. An old exported edit may contain captions or music you cannot adjust as separate source tracks.

Keep your source footage while checking the edit. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans are sold through the App Store. Review the saved file in Photos before posting it.

## FAQ

**Can I add several library items together?**
Vyra help lists Add in the bottom bar when several library items are selected. Confirm the chosen material and destination.

**Should I use an old export or the original recording?**
Use the original when you need control over the shot and audio. An export may already contain the edits from an earlier project.
