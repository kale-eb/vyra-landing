**In Vyra on iPhone version 0.1.8 or later, open Variations and choose Export all. Review the versions selected for export and start the export for the set you want.**

If you made one opening for an existing audience and another for people seeing your work for the first time, keep the files distinguishable. Otherwise you may send the right edit with the wrong opening when you come back to post.

## Work through the edit

1. Preview every variation you intend to export.
2. Give each version a name that describes its difference.
3. Open Variations and choose Export all.
4. Review the selected versions and the available export options, then start the export.
5. Check each finished file, including its first shot and final audio, before sharing it.

## A brief to try

> List the alternate edits and the opening used in each before exporting. I want the result-first version and the mistake-first version. Keep the versions distinguishable in the review.

## Check the result

The in-app help says variation exports run on Vyra’s servers. That describes where rendering happens. Check completion and the actual files before you assume the whole set is ready to share.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the Plus and Pro options in the App Store for paid export limits.

## FAQ

**Does Export all mean every draft has to be exported?**
Review the export selection and keep the versions you need. Play each selected variation before starting.

**Can I assume every exported version has identical captions?**
Edits can change words and timing. Check captions in every selected variation, especially when the opening changes.
