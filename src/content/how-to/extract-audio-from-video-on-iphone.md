**To use the sound from a video separately in Vyra on iPhone, add the video to a project, tap its clip on the timeline and choose Extract audio. Check the resulting audio against the picture before you move or trim either one.**

Say you filmed an explanation while holding your phone over a workbench. The explanation is clear, but you have a better shot of what you were describing. Keep the explanation and put the clearer shot over it. Vyra's in-app help lists Extract audio among the tools available when you select a video clip on iPhone.

## Keep the voice and change the picture

1. Add the clip from Photos and listen for the complete sentence you want to keep.
2. Tap that clip in the timeline and choose **Extract audio**.
3. Play the same passage. If you hear the source and the extracted audio together, mute one copy.
4. Put the clearer picture over the explanation. Leave enough time for the viewer to see the detail the speaker names.
5. Play from the sentence before the cut to the sentence after it. Check that the audio starts cleanly and that any visible lips still match.

Keep the original recording until the edit is done. It's easier to recover the start of a word from the recording than to hide a clipped syllable with music.

## A brief to try

> Keep my original explanation about how I mixed the blue paint. Show the close-up of the palette while I name each color. Keep my voice at its original speed. Check that the audio plays only once.

## FAQ

**Can I keep the audio while showing a different clip?**
Yes. Use Extract audio to work with the sound separately, then place the new picture over the part of the explanation it illustrates.

**How do I fix doubled audio after extracting it?**
Check whether the original clip and the extracted audio are both audible. Mute one copy and replay the cut. Also check for another copy of the clip elsewhere in the project.

**Does this save an MP3 to my iPhone?**
This workflow gives you editable audio inside the video project. You can then export the finished video to Photos.
