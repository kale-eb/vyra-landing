**In Vyra on iPhone, select the recording and use the Audio category in Add effect, or ask chat for a gentle low-cut filter. Compare the voice before and after so the treatment does not make it sound thin.**

A desk vibration can sit under an otherwise clear sentence. Listen to a quiet stretch and a spoken stretch. Treat the low rumble while keeping the part of the voice that makes the words sound natural.

The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own footage. Vyra’s current in-app help, Audio features, supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Listen to the recording alone and identify the low rumble.
2. Select the relevant audio or video clip.
3. Open Effects and its Audio category, or ask for a gentle low-cut filter in chat.
4. Compare the voice and the quiet gaps with the treatment on and off.
5. Check the exported sound on headphones and the phone speaker.

## A brief to try

> Try a gentle low-cut filter on this recording to reduce the desk rumble. Keep my voice natural. Do not turn down the entire clip to hide the problem.

## Check the result

Vyra’s Audio features help documents Filter with low and high cut, including the native Audio effects category. This is a targeted tone adjustment. It does not establish that wind, clipped speech or every kind of background noise can be repaired.

## FAQ

**Is a low-cut filter the same as lowering the volume?**
Lowering volume turns down the whole recording. A low-cut filter reduces the low-frequency range, which can change the voice as well as the rumble.

**Should I use the strongest filter setting?**
Compare a gentle setting first. Stop if the voice loses too much body or the unwanted sound is mainly outside the low range.
