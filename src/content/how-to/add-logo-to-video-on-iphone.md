**In Vyra on iPhone, add your logo image to the project and ask chat to place it over the footage. Specify the corner, size and the part of the video where it should appear.**

A maker’s mark may look fine over a clear tabletop and disappear over a patterned apron. Check the whole section behind it. Keep the logo small enough that the work still gets most of the frame.

## Work through the edit

1. Add the image you want to use to your Vyra library.
2. Use Add to project for the existing image.
3. Ask chat to place the logo over the intended footage and give its appearance interval.
4. Review the mark over the brightest and busiest shots.
5. Check the exported file for readable lettering and an unobstructed subject.

## A brief to try

> Place my supplied logo in the upper-left corner during the last five seconds. Keep its proportions. Leave room around it and keep it away from the painting title.

## Check the result

Use an image with enough detail to stay clear at its final size. If the logo file includes a solid background, check whether you want that background visible. Review the source image before asking for a different treatment.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the Plus and Pro options in the App Store for paid export limits.

## FAQ

**Can I use my existing logo image?**
Yes. Vyra’s library can hold images and add them to a project. Check that the intended file is selected.

**Will adding my logo remove Vyra’s free-export watermark?**
The free iPhone export watermark follows the plan’s export limits. Your own logo is a separate graphic in the edit.
