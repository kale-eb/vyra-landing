**To make a podcast teaser on iPhone, choose one understandable exchange and preserve the words that qualify its main point. Add your recording to Vyra, explain the topic to keep and check the excerpt against the full conversation before exporting.**

A striking sentence can become misleading when separated from what came before it. Choose a passage with its own setup and conclusion. For a teaser, the goal is to show the conversation's value without turning it into a different claim.

## Work through the edit

1. Identify a passage with a clear topic and a complete thought.
2. Add the recording and tell Vyra the passage or words you want to keep.
3. Remove repetition while preserving relevant context and qualifications.
4. Check the framing keeps the active speaker and useful reactions visible.
5. Review the captions against the original recording and export the final excerpt.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| The opening uses an unexplained pronoun | Include enough setup to identify the subject |
| A qualification follows the strongest sentence | Keep it when it changes the meaning |
| The excerpt needs a title | Write an accurate description of what is actually discussed |

## A brief to try

```
Make a teaser from the section about starting the first project. Keep the setup and the complete answer, including the limitation at the end. Use readable captions. Do not combine words from different answers into a new statement.
```

Compare the finished teaser with the original passage. Verify that shortened pauses did not cut off words and that a title does not make a stronger claim than the speaker. Publishing the teaser is separate from exporting it.

## FAQ

**Should I choose the loudest moment?**
Choose a complete, useful thought. Intensity alone does not make an excerpt understandable.

**Can I remove a qualification to make the clip shorter?**
Keep it when removing it would change the claim. Choose another passage if the remaining clip cannot fit your purpose.

**Does this require several camera angles?**
No. A single clear recording can work. Check its framing and sound before adding decoration.
