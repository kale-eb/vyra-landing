**To keep natural sound in an iPhone edit, identify the recorded sounds that matter before adding music or shortening clips. In Vyra, name those moments in your brief and review the sound around every cut.**

Paper tearing, a brush touching the canvas or a door closing can explain a moment without extra narration. The useful sound may last longer than the visible gesture, so cutting the picture tightly can also cut away part of what you wanted to hear.

## Work through the edit

1. Listen to the original clips and note the sounds you want to keep.
2. Add the footage and name those moments in your request.
3. Build the sequence before deciding whether music helps.
4. Adjust audio balance so important recorded sounds remain audible.
5. Listen to the export on your phone and with headphones for abrupt joins.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A small sound explains the action | Give it room to be heard |
| Music masks the material texture | Lower it or leave it out |
| A cut jumps between different room noise | Check a different boundary or shot |

## A brief to try

```
Keep the paper sounds and the brush touching the surface. Remove the waiting between actions without cutting those sounds short. Leave music out of the first draft so I can review the original audio.
```

Check the actual recording before requesting cleanup. If the intended sound is missing or overwhelmed, another take may work better. Do not assume an editor can restore a sound the microphone never captured.

## FAQ

**Do I need music in a process video?**
No. Useful recorded sound can carry the sequence on its own.

**Can I lower music without changing the original footage?**
Ask for an audio-balance change and review the result. Keep the original sound you want preserved explicit in the request.

**Will removing a pause always sound natural?**
No. Listen across the cut because the room sound or action may change abruptly.
