**To edit an unboxing video on iPhone, keep the sequence from unopened package to the first useful view of the product, then remove repetitive handling. In Vyra, ask for those stages explicitly and check that the reveal remains visible.**

Before filming or importing, cover shipping labels and other details you do not want published. A clear package-opening shot and a steady product view usually explain more than several fast close-ups of packaging.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose footage of the closed package, opening and product reveal.
2. Add it to the project in that order.
3. Shorten repeated wrapper removal while preserving the moment the product appears.
4. Keep useful package or material sounds if they contribute to the experience.
5. Check labels, visible personal information and the final product framing before export.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Long repetitive wrapping removal | Keep enough to understand the opening |
| The hands hide the product | Choose a clearer reveal shot |
| A first impression includes a limitation | Keep the limitation with the reaction |

## A brief to try

```
Edit the package opening in order. Shorten the repeated wrapping, keep the first clear reveal and finish on the product detail. Preserve the opening sounds. Use only the product facts I provide.
```

Make sure the final sequence does not imply you used a product when the footage only shows opening it. Keep the wording of any impression tied to what you actually observed and intend to say.

## FAQ

**Should I include the entire opening?**
Keep the stages that make the reveal understandable. Repeated handling can often be shortened.

**Should I add music over the package sounds?**
Listen to the opening sounds first. Leave music out when it hides the tearing, handling or click that makes the reveal satisfying.

**Can I make it without speaking?**
Yes. Use clear footage, useful original sound and concise labels where context is missing.
