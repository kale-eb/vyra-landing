**To edit a recipe video on iPhone, arrange the preparation steps in the order someone needs to follow them, then add quantities as readable labels. Use Vyra to assemble your own cooking footage and review the instructions against what actually appears.**

A finished dish makes a useful opening, but the method still needs a clear order. Write down the quantities and any timing you intend to teach before editing. Keep the recipe facts separate from the choices about pace.

## Work through the edit

1. Add the ingredient, preparation and finished-dish clips.
2. Choose a short result shot for the opening, then return to the first preparation step.
3. Keep the action that shows each change in the food. Shorten repeated stirring or setup.
4. Add your supplied quantities and step labels, checking every number.
5. Review the sequence with sound off, then check any spoken instructions before export.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A quantity appears only in speech | Add a checked text label |
| The same action repeats for a long time | Keep a representative section |
| A change in texture matters | Show it clearly before moving on |

## A brief to try

```
Use my clips to explain this recipe. Show the finished dish first, then follow the preparation order. Add only the ingredient quantities I provide. Keep the shot showing the final texture and do not invent cooking times.
```

Watch for a cut that skips an ingredient or makes the order misleading. If a crucial stage is missing, add a new shot or explain the omission clearly. The editor should not supply unverified recipe instructions.

## FAQ

**How do I show ingredient quantities without a voiceover?**
Add the quantities you use as short labels beside the matching ingredient or step. Watch muted and check that each label stays long enough to read.

**Does every step need a transition effect?**
A direct cut often keeps the method clearer. Use an effect only when it helps show a change.

**Can I make the recipe understandable without speech?**
Use visible actions and short labels, then watch muted to find missing instructions.
