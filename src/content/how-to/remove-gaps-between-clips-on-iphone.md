**To remove an empty gap between clips in Vyra on iPhone, long-press the empty timeline space and delete the gap. Ripple can close gaps when you move or delete clips.**

A black moment can come from an empty space, a dark shot or a pause recorded inside a clip. Zoom in on the join before changing it. Only the empty space calls for deleting a timeline gap.

Version 0.1.5 describes deleting empty space with a long press and closing gaps with Ripple. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Find the black moment during playback and locate it on the timeline.
2. Check whether the playhead is over empty space or actual footage.
3. Long-press an empty space between clips to delete that gap.
4. When rearranging clips, use Ripple if you want the space behind a moved or deleted clip to close.
5. Play the join and check music, captions and deliberate pauses before export.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Empty space with no clip | Delete the timeline gap |
| Silence within a recording | Split and trim the recorded pause |
| An intentional pause between chapters | Keep enough space for the intended effect |

## FAQ

**Is a quiet section always a gap?**
No. A quiet section can be part of a recorded clip. Check what is underneath the playhead.

**Will Ripple remove pauses inside speech?**
Ripple closes space when clips move or are deleted. A recorded pause still needs an edit within the clip.

**Can I keep a deliberate black pause?**
Yes. Leave intentional space where the story needs it and review that choice in the exported file.
