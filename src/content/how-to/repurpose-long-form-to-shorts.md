**A short from a long video works when it's a complete thought. Find a 20 to 60 second piece that opens on a claim or question and closes on a payoff, reframe 16:9 to 9:16 with the face on the upper third, add captions, and fix any missing context with a short text hook. One 20 minute video usually gives 3 to 6 usable shorts.**

## Why it matters

Long-form takes hours and earns one upload.

## The method

1. **Read the transcript, not the timeline.** Mark 8 to 10 lines that make a claim, ask a question, or start a story.
2. **Check each one stands alone.** Setup, point, and end inside 60 seconds. If it needs the previous 5 minutes, drop it.
3. **Start on the claim.** Remove "so like I was saying" and any reference to earlier.
4. **Reframe to vertical.** Face on the upper third, shoulders in. Two speakers means cut between them or a split layout.
5. **Add phrase captions.** Podcast audio is mostly muted in the feed.
6. **Restore context.** A text hook for 2 seconds ("Why I stopped taking commissions") or one bridging sentence.

## Rules of thumb

| Element | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Short length | 20 to 60 s |
| Shorts per 20 min episode | 3 to 6 |
| First spoken word | under 0.5 s |
| Reframe | face upper third, 15 to 25 percent zoom max |
| Context fix | text hook or 1 sentence |
Two speakers, cut to whoever is talking. Split screen only when the reaction matters as much as the words.

## Examples

- A 22 minute podcast about pricing art. Keepers, "why I charge by the day" (44 s), "the client who ghosted" (58 s), "my first sale" (37 s). Rejected, "as I mentioned earlier about materials."
- Context fix. The clip starts "and that is when she said no." Text hook for 2 seconds, "The client said no to Friday." Now it stands alone.

## Do it in Vyra

- "Find 4 to 6 self-contained segments in this podcast, 20 to 60 seconds each, that open on a claim or question and end on a payoff. List them with timestamps and the first line."
- "Take the segment at 14:22 to 15:06, reframe it to 9:16 with my face on the upper third, remove pauses over 0.3 seconds, add phrase captions, and put 'Why I charge by the day' as a text hook for the first 2 seconds."
- "For the two-person segment at 8:10, cut to whoever is speaking and use a split layout only for the 3 seconds where the guest laughs."

## Common mistakes

- Clipping the funniest 15 seconds with no setup.
- Keeping long-form pauses.
- Center-cropping a wide shot and losing the speaker's head.

## FAQ

**Should I shoot long-form with shorts in mind?**
Yes. Leave room to crop vertical and say key points as complete sentences.

**Do shorts hurt the long video?**
No. They're discovery for it. Link the episode in the caption.
