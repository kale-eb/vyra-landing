**To cut on action in Vyra on iPhone, join two views of the same movement while the action is still happening, then check that the movement continues rather than restarting.**

You reach for the paintbrush in a wide shot and pick it up in a close-up. A cut during that reach can connect the views. A cut after both clips have finished the movement can make you pick up the same brush twice.

The native listing supports trimming, splitting and rearranging clips. Cut-on-action decisions are editorial guidance applied to that footage. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Choose two recordings that show the same action from useful angles.
2. Find the shared movement in both clips.
3. Trim the outgoing shot during the movement.
4. Start the incoming shot at the corresponding point and review the join.
5. Play the sequence at normal speed with its original sound before export.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The hand repeats the whole reach | Start the second view later |
| The object jumps to another position | Check whether these takes can join convincingly |
| Only one recording exists | Use a straightforward trim instead of inventing another angle |

## A brief to try

```
Join the wide shot to the close-up while I reach for the brush. Keep one continuous pickup, without showing the same reach twice. Use only the two supplied takes and preserve the relevant source sound.
```

## Check the result

Look at the object as well as the hand. Its position, direction and state should still make sense. If the brush has already changed color between takes, a cut on action will not repair the underlying continuity difference.

## FAQ

**Do I need a transition effect?**
A direct cut is often enough. The join comes from the movement in the supplied shots.

**Can one shot become a second camera angle?**
Use actual available coverage. This guide does not generate a new view of the action.

**Should I judge it frame by frame only?**
Inspect the cut closely, then watch at normal speed. A precise boundary can still feel awkward in playback.
