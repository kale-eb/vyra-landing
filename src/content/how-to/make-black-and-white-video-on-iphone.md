**In Vyra on iPhone, select the intended clip and ask chat to apply a grayscale look. Preview the result before applying the same treatment to the rest of the sequence.**

A pencil drawing can read clearly without color, while two similarly bright paints may become hard to distinguish. Check the parts of the image that explain your work before deciding to remove the color.

The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own footage. Vyra’s current in-app help, Effects and Color and the Adjust panel, supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose the clip and identify the detail that needs to remain clear.
2. Ask Vyra to apply a grayscale look to that clip.
3. Compare the result with the original picture.
4. Review contrast between the subject and its background across the full shot.
5. Check any colored labels or other clips before exporting the sequence.

## A brief to try

> Make this pencil-drawing clip black and white. Keep the paper and graphite distinct. Leave the finished color painting at the end unchanged.

## Check the result

The Effects help lists Grayscale, while the native editor exposes Effects. Use chat to specify the selected clip without assuming that a filter will preserve every useful color distinction. Keep an original-color version when the color itself is evidence in the video.

## FAQ

**Should I make every clip black and white?**
Choose that from the story. A process section can use grayscale while a final color result stays unchanged, provided the difference is clear to the viewer.

**Can black and white recover missing detail?**
Removing color does not restore detail that was never recorded. Review the source and the result before adding more contrast.
