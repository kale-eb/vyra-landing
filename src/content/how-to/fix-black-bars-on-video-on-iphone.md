**In Vyra on iPhone, compare the clip shape with the canvas shape. Fit the whole picture when every edge matters, or enlarge and reframe the clip to fill the canvas when you can crop its edges.**

A landscape recording inside a tall canvas can leave space above and below. Before enlarging it, check whether the hands at the sides are important. Filling the frame may hide the very detail the viewer needs.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Canvas resize, Layouts and Properties panel”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Check whether the empty space belongs to the project framing or is already part of the recording.
2. Decide whether the finished video should keep its current canvas shape.
3. Ask chat to fit or fill the selected clip.
4. Review the edges throughout the action after any enlargement.
5. Check the exported file in Photos to confirm the framing.

## A brief to try

> Fill the vertical canvas with the close-up, keeping the brush tip visible. For the wide group shot, keep everyone in frame even if some space remains above and below.

## Check the result

If a border was already recorded into the source, fitting the source keeps that border too. You may need a crop inside the recording. Enlarging a low-resolution clip can also make softness more visible.

## FAQ

**Can I fill the frame without losing any picture?**
When clip and canvas shapes differ, filling usually requires losing some picture at the edges. Keep the full picture when those edges matter.

**Should I change the canvas instead?**
Change it when the destination accepts that shape and preserving the full shot is more important than filling the existing frame.
