**In Vyra on iPhone version 0.1.8 or later, open Variations in the editor’s top bar. Make another version of the edit and try a different opening there. Preview the versions before choosing which one to export.**

One opening shows the finished painting. Another starts with the part where the color went wrong. Both can use the same footage, but they promise different stories. Pick the one the rest of the edit actually delivers.

## Work through the edit

1. Open the project and review the current opening in Main.
2. Tap Variations in the editor’s top bar and choose New, or use Ask Vyra to make 5 hooks.
3. Name the alternate version by the opening it uses.
4. Open that version and ask for the opening change you want.
5. Play past the new hook into the next scene. Check captions, sound and whether the main point still follows.

## A brief to try

> Try an opening that shows the ruined blue mixture first, then cut to the explanation. Keep the ending and the corrected painting in the alternate edit.

## Check the result

A strong first shot needs a clear next step. If the new opening reveals the result, remove any later line that treats the result as a surprise. Compare the openings at the same playback size and volume.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the Plus and Pro options in the App Store for paid export limits.

## FAQ

**Why is the Variations button missing?**
The in-app documentation lists native Variations for version 0.1.8 and later. Check your installed version. The web editor also supports variations.

**Will five versions tell me which hook gets more views?**
Preview helps you choose an edit. Actual response depends on the audience and distribution. Keep a record of which version you post before comparing results.
