**To make a square video in Vyra on iPhone, tap the canvas size chip in the editor bar and choose the square canvas. Reframe each clip afterward so the subject fits.**

A wide studio shot may include two people at opposite edges. Changing to a square can hide one of them. Choose whether to keep the whole shot with space around it or crop to the person who is speaking.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Canvas resize”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Open the project and tap the canvas size chip.
2. Choose the square canvas.
3. Ask chat to refit the clips or adjust their framing yourself.
4. Review every shot and reposition titles and captions that sit near an edge.
5. Export and check the saved square file.

## Check the result

Canvas shape and export resolution are separate decisions. Changing the canvas does not automatically refit all the material. Check wide photos and tall clips individually.

Keep your source footage while checking the edit. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans are sold through the App Store. Review the saved file in Photos before posting it.

## FAQ

**Will switching the canvas automatically crop every clip?**
Vyra help says canvas resizing does not refit items. Ask for a refit and review the result.

**Does a square canvas guarantee a higher-quality export?**
Export quality depends on source material and the available export settings. Choose the export size separately.
