**To caption two speakers on iPhone, check the words at each speaker change and add names only when you have confirmed who is speaking. Review overlap separately.**

A conversation can become confusing when the next caption appears before the other person starts talking. Work through the speaker changes first. Save decorative choices until the exchange reads correctly.

The listing confirms caption generation and styling. Speaker-label verification is an editorial review step, not a promised automatic identity feature. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Add the final conversation edit to a Vyra project.
2. Generate captions and listen through each change of speaker.
3. Correct words that have been joined across the two voices.
4. Use supplied names or neutral labels only where they help the viewer.
5. Review overlapping speech with sound, then watch the export muted.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| One person clearly finishes before the next | Align the caption change with that turn |
| Two voices overlap | Listen carefully and avoid combining them into an invented sentence |
| The identity is uncertain | Confirm it before displaying a name |

## A brief to try

```
Caption this interview faithfully. I am the person on the left and Sam is on the right. Check every change of speaker and flag lines where our voices overlap. Do not guess missing words or identities.
```

Compare the captions with the complete exchange, including short interruptions. A fluent sentence assembled from two people can change the meaning. Ask for a review of uncertain sections rather than treating speaker identification as automatic.

## FAQ

**Does this promise automatic speaker identification?**
No. This workflow uses your confirmed labels and a review of the recording.

**Should I give each speaker a different color?**
You can choose a visual distinction, but readable words and correct timing come first.

**What should I do when both people talk at once?**
Replay that section and retain the words you can verify. Avoid presenting a guessed combined sentence as a transcript.
