**To edit event highlights on iPhone, choose a few moments that show where you were, what happened and how it ended. In Vyra, assemble those clips into a short sequence and keep any important spoken line in its original context.**

An event folder can contain many nearly identical shots. Pick moments with different jobs. An arrival shot establishes the place, an interaction shows what happened and a final wide shot can give the ending room.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose an establishing shot, the key event moments and an ending.
2. Add the clips and tell Vyra which moment the edit is about.
3. Remove repeated angles that do not add a different view or detail.
4. Preserve a short relevant line or natural sound to keep the occasion recognizable.
5. Check names, event labels and the final sequence before export.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Several clips show the same moment | Choose the clearest view |
| A speech supplies the meaning | Keep enough context around the line |
| A reaction belongs to another moment | Avoid implying it happened in response to a different event |

## A brief to try

```
Make a short recap of this exhibition night. Start with the room, show people looking at the work, keep my one sentence about opening the show and finish on the whole space. Remove repeated crowd shots.
```

The final edit should make sense even to someone who was not there. If every shot is a close-up, restore a wider view. If music hides the one meaningful sentence, adjust the balance before adding more effects.

## Review and export

Save the finished edit to Photos and open that saved copy to review it. New mobile accounts currently get 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro are sold through the App Store. AI editing needs an internet connection.

## FAQ

**Should I keep the event in chronological order?**
Chronology is a useful starting point. If you open with a highlight, make the rest of the sequence clear.

**Can I include people's conversations?**
Choose material you are comfortable and authorized to publish. Avoid using unrelated private conversation as background sound.

**Does exporting publish the recap?**
No. Review the saved video, then publish it separately on your chosen platform.
