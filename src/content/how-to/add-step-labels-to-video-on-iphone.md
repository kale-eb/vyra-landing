**To add step labels on iPhone, attach each short instruction to the action it describes and leave enough time for someone to read it. In Vyra, ask for text overlays using your exact wording, then check their timing and position.**

A step label tells someone what to do. A caption records what was said. You may need both, but they should not sit on top of one another or give conflicting instructions.

## Work through the edit

1. Write the steps in plain language before editing.
2. Find the shot where each action starts and ends.
3. Ask Vyra to add the matching label using your exact words.
4. Move labels clear of hands, tools and any speech captions.
5. Play without pausing and shorten text that cannot be read in time.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A label explains a whole action | Keep it visible across that action |
| The wording repeats a caption | Remove unnecessary duplication |
| A number or unit matters | Verify it against your own instructions |

## A brief to try

```
Add these labels to the matching steps: "Prepare the surface", "Apply the first layer", "Show the finish". Keep labels away from my hands. Preserve the spoken captions and leave their wording unchanged.
```

Read labels in the exported file at normal phone size. If a long instruction needs several pauses to understand, split the instruction or give the action more time. Styling cannot compensate for unclear wording.

## FAQ

**Are step labels generated from speech?**
They can use wording you supply even when no one speaks. Tell the editor exactly what each label should say.

**Can I use labels and captions together?**
Yes. Place them so both remain readable and neither hides the important action.

**Should a label remain after the step ends?**
Usually remove it when its action is complete, unless it is a reminder needed for the next step.
