**To mirror a clip in Vyra on iPhone, ask chat to flip that clip horizontally. Check writing, logos and the direction of the action afterward. A horizontal flip changes which side of the frame everything appears on.**

A selfie shot can look unfamiliar next to another camera angle. Before flipping it, check a label or sign in the background. The version that feels familiar to you may make that writing backward.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Properties panel and Editing tools on iPhone”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose the single clip you want to mirror.
2. Ask for a horizontal flip and identify the shot clearly.
3. Check text and logos in the result.
4. Compare the direction of movement with the shots before and after it.
5. Keep the flip only if the whole sequence still reads clearly.

## A brief to try

> Flip the selfie clip horizontally. Leave the product close-up alone so its printed label stays readable. Keep all timing unchanged.

## Check the result

Watch a hand reach across the cut. Mirroring can make it appear to switch sides suddenly. If that switch is distracting, keep the original orientation or choose another neighboring shot.

## FAQ

**Does flipping fix a sideways recording?**
A flip mirrors the picture. Rotate a sideways recording to turn it upright.

**Should I flip product labels?**
Check that labels read correctly in the final video. Keep the original orientation when mirroring reverses important writing.
