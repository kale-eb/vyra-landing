**To edit a product demonstration on iPhone, show the problem, the actual action and the result in a clear sequence. Use Vyra to assemble footage you filmed, keeping any qualification needed to understand what the product did.**

A product looking good on a desk does not show how it works. Choose footage that demonstrates the claim you want to make. Write the exact claim yourself and remove anything the recording cannot support.

## Work through the edit

1. Identify the single action the demo should explain.
2. Add a starting-state shot, a clear action shot and the result.
3. Trim handling and repeated attempts while keeping the important sequence honest.
4. Add a short label that names what viewers are seeing.
5. Review the final result, any claim and the spoken explanation before export.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A close-up shows texture | Use it as supporting detail |
| A cut hides the main action | Restore the full action shot |
| The result needs a qualification | Keep the qualification beside the result |

## A brief to try

```
Make a demo from these clips showing how I use the print stand. Start with the setup, keep the complete adjustment and end on the finished display. Add only my supplied labels. Do not invent performance claims.
```

Watch for a result that seems to happen before the action that produced it. You can shorten setup, but preserve enough context for the viewer to understand what the product actually did.

## FAQ

**Can I make a demo without product footage?**
Film the relevant action first. Vyra edits supplied footage rather than generating missing product video.

**Should I include every feature?**
Start with one job and its result. Several features can make a short demo harder to follow.

**Can I add a spoken explanation afterward?**
Yes. Record a voiceover or add your explanation, then match it to the visible action.
