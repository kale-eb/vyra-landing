**In a Vyra iPhone project, change the duration of the photo item on the timeline or tell chat how long it should stay. Review the text and narration over the photo before shortening it.**

An old sketchbook photo can need more time than a quick process clip. If people have to read a handwritten note, play it once at normal speed and see whether the note disappears before you finish reading.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Timeline navigation and tools”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Find the photo on the timeline.
2. Choose the moment when it should appear and the point when it should clear.
3. Adjust its duration or give chat the requested length.
4. Check the following shot and any caption that continues over the photo.
5. Play the whole passage with its narration.

## A brief to try

> Hold the sketchbook photo for four seconds so the handwritten date is readable. Keep the next video shot intact and move it later if needed.

## Check the result

Longer photo holds can extend the edit or overlap another item depending on its placement. Check the surrounding timeline after the change. Keep any sound that explains the photograph audible for the intended passage.

## FAQ

**Does extending a photo make it slow motion?**
A still image has no recorded motion to slow down. You are changing how long it appears.

**Can the photo stay up through a whole sentence?**
Ask for the photo to begin and end with that sentence, then check the timing against the audio.
