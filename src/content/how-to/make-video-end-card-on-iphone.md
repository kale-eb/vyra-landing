**In Vyra on iPhone, use the final shot or a supplied image as the background and add a short text instruction. Ask chat to hold that ending long enough to read before exporting.**

The finished painting deserves a moment before a follow-me message arrives. Keep one useful next action on the end card. A small handle, a long sentence and an animated logo can compete for the same few seconds.

The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own footage. Vyra’s current in-app help, App Store native text and timeline editing, supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose a final shot or supplied background image with clear space for text.
2. Write the one action you want the viewer to understand.
3. Ask Vyra to add the text and hold the ending at a readable pace.
4. Watch the ending muted and check the handle or wording character by character.
5. Play through the last frame and final audio before sharing.

## A brief to try

> Hold the finished painting at the end. Add the text see the full process with my supplied handle below it. Keep the brushwork visible and let the music finish gently.

## Check the result

The official iPhone listing supports supplied photos, text, timeline editing and export. An end card is part of the video picture. It does not create a clickable platform button, so keep any website or handle accurate and readable.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

## FAQ

**Does a URL on an end card become clickable?**
The text is rendered into the video picture. Add a supported link separately in the publishing destination when that destination allows it.

**How long should I hold the card?**
Preview it on a phone and allow enough time to read the actual wording. A longer sentence needs more time or fewer words.
