**In Vyra on iPhone, ask chat to lift the shadows in the selected clip while keeping the brighter parts of the picture stable. Review dark textures and noise before increasing the adjustment.**

The underside of a shelf may be difficult to see even when the wall beside it looks fine. Brighten the darker range enough to explain the detail, then stop before the whole frame loses its shape.

The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own footage. Vyra’s current in-app help, Color and the Adjust panel, supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Find a dark area that matters to the explanation.
2. Select the clip and open Adjust or identify it in chat.
3. Ask for a small shadow lift with the highlights kept stable.
4. Replay the shot and inspect dark textures for noise or a gray haze.
5. Compare the saved result with the original at a normal viewing size.

## A brief to try

> Lift the shadows under the shelf enough to see the repaired corner. Keep the wall and window close to their current brightness. Leave some depth in the dark areas.

## Check the result

The Adjust help lists Shadows as a separate control. A lift can reveal existing dark information, but it can also make recorded noise easier to see. Use the smallest change that lets the viewer follow the detail.

## FAQ

**How should I judge noise after brightening a dark area?**
A brighter adjustment can make noise already present in the recording more visible. Try a gentler lift and judge the exported file.

**Should every dark area become equally bright?**
No. Keep enough difference between light and dark to understand the subject. Brighten the part that carries useful information.
