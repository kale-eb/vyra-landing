**In Vyra on iPhone, add the chosen music once and ask chat to keep it continuous beneath the sequence of clips. Review each picture cut for duplicate music or a jump in the recording.**

Five clips from the same afternoon can sound disconnected if the song starts again with every shot. A continuous music bed gives them a shared timeline. The original clip audio still needs its own decision.

The [official iPhone listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) document native editing. Vyra’s current in-app help section Audio features supports the controls or chat workflow below.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose the section of music that belongs under the sequence.
2. Add the music and tell Vyra to use one continuous track.
3. Decide which original sounds should remain audible.
4. Listen through every picture cut for a restart, gap or doubled song.
5. Check the beginning and final music ending in the exported file.

## A brief to try

> Use one continuous section of my uploaded song under these five clips. Keep the shop bell from the second clip. Do not restart the music at each cut, and fade it at the end.

## Check the result

The App Store listing documents music and audio editing. Use music you have permission to publish. If music is already baked into the source videos, adding the same song on another track can produce a doubled or out-of-time result.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

## FAQ

**Does continuous music mean every video clip must be muted?**
No. Keep the original sounds that matter, then review their level against the music. A short sound can help explain a visible action.

**Why do I hear the song twice?**
Check whether the source clips already contain the song and whether another music item overlaps it. Listen to the layers separately before changing the whole edit.
