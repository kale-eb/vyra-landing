**In Vyra on iPhone, add your photo and ask chat for a gradual zoom over its screen time. Name the detail to keep visible and review the crop at both the beginning and the end.**

A photograph of your print can move toward the signature while the voiceover explains it. Keep the motion restrained enough that the viewer can still inspect the work. The edge of the paper should not disappear by accident.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. The current Vyra help sections Properties panel and Editing tools on iPhone describe the relevant capability.

## Work through the edit

1. Add the supplied photo and decide how long it should remain on screen.
2. Identify the detail that the motion should approach.
3. Ask Vyra for a slow change in scale over that interval.
4. Preview the first and last views for cropped lettering or important edges.
5. Review the whole motion and exported result at phone size.

## A brief to try

> Give this photo a gentle zoom toward the signature during its five seconds on screen. Keep the whole signature readable at the end and leave the voiceover unchanged.

## Check the result

The Properties help documents scale, position and animatable settings. This is a framing change to a still image. It does not create new footage outside the source photograph. A larger zoom can expose limited source detail.

## FAQ

**Does a zoom create detail that is not in the photo?**
The edit changes the visible framing. Use a source image with enough detail for the close view you want.

**Should I add movement to every photo?**
Choose movement when it helps direct attention. A still comparison may be easier to inspect when the viewer needs to compare two images.
