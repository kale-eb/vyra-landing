**Motion graphics are animated text, shapes, and media over your footage. In short-form they work as a small set used on purpose. A big animated word for one or two key phrases, a lower third when someone needs a name, a callout when the viewer should look at one spot, and a build-and-clear layout when a few images should gather and then go. The footage stays the subject.**

## Why it matters

A graphic that shows what the footage can't, a number, a name, a comparison, lands the point faster.

## The method

1. **Name the job.** Emphasize a claim, identify a person, point at a detail, compare two things, show a collection. No job, no graphic.
2. **Use the simplest thing.** Native text, a shape, or a placed image before a coded animation. They're faster to change.
3. **Match the type.** Vyra's default is Montserrat Black for titles and Inter Bold for captions, one accent color per video.
4. **Time it to the words.** The word appears when you say it. The lower third appears when the person first speaks.
5. **Decide how it leaves.** Every graphic needs an exit, usually on the next cut or sentence.
6. **Build-and-clear as one scene.** Lay out the finished arrangement first, reveal each piece as it's named, hold the set 1 to 2 seconds, then clear everything together.

## Rules of thumb

| Graphic | On screen | Size on 9:16 | Entrance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Big key phrase | 1 to 2 s | 12 to 20 percent of height | instant or pop on the word |
| Lower third | 3 to 5 s | name 3 percent, role 2 percent | slide or fade, once per person |
| Build-and-clear | 4 to 10 s total | main item largest | staggered on each name |

Two or three per 60 seconds. Captions don't count.

## Examples

- Key phrase. I say "I just dropped out to build something." Captions stop, "I just / dropped / out." stacks in Montserrat Black with "dropped" in Sky, holds 1 second, clears.
- Build-and-clear. My face full frame, then a process clip, a sketchbook page, and a street shot appear as I name each. All three hold, then clear together on the cut.

## Do it in Vyra

- "When I say 'dropped out' at 7 seconds, hide the captions and put 'I just dropped out.' as a large accumulating title in Montserrat Black, with 'dropped' in the accent color. Hold it 1 second, then clear and bring captions back."
- "Build an accumulate-and-release composition from 8 to 16 seconds: keep my face as the main shot and add the sketchbook clip, the studio wide, and the street photo as smaller inserts, each appearing when I name it. Clear all three on the cut at 16 seconds."
- "Add a lower third the first time each person speaks: name in Inter Bold, role smaller underneath, bottom-left, on screen 4 seconds."

## Common mistakes

- A graphic on every sentence.
- Six tiny inserts instead of one you can read on a phone.
- Graphics that never leave.

## FAQ

**Do I need After Effects?**
No. Text, shapes, and placed media cover most of it. Coded animations are for things those can't do.

**Sound effects on graphics?**
Only when the sound means something. A whoosh on every entrance is the same problem as a bounce on every caption.
