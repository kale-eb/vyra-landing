**A story video has four parts in order. Setup, who and where and what's at stake, in one line. Tension, a specific problem. Turn, the moment something changed. Payoff, what happened and what it meant. An open loop planted early and closed late holds people through the middle, and a callback to the first line makes the ending feel done. The whole thing fits in 45 to 90 seconds.**

## Why it matters

Stories get shared and remembered, and they have the flattest retention curves because people are waiting for the payoff.

## The method

1. **Open on the tension.** The problem, or a line from the middle. Setup goes in sentence two.
2. **Say what's at stake in one line.** "If this doesn't dry by 5, the gallery pickup is off."
3. **Plant an open loop.** "And I had no idea what was about to go wrong." Don't resolve it yet.
4. **Show the turn as an event.** A text arrived, a plan failed, someone said something. Footage, not narration.
5. **Deliver the payoff plainly.** What happened, what it cost, what you learned. Two sentences.
6. **Close the loop and call back.** Answer the planted question and echo the opening line or image.

## Rules of thumb

| Part | Share of runtime | In a 60 s video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 5 percent | 3 s |
| Setup | 15 percent | 9 s |
| Rising tension | 35 percent | 21 s |
| Turn | 15 percent | 9 s |
| Payoff | 20 percent | 12 s |
One or two open loops. Plant before 20 percent, close after 70. One turn in 60 seconds.

## Do it in Vyra

- "This is a storytelling video. From the transcript, find the line with the most tension and open on it. Then order the footage as setup, problem, turn, payoff. Keep my talking head as the spine and use the b-roll to show each event. 60 seconds, vertical."
- "Find the moment I read the text message and make it the turn: hold on my reaction for 2 full seconds with no music, then bring the music back."
- "End on a callback: repeat the shot from the opening and put my first line on screen in Montserrat Black as the last frame."

## Common mistakes

- Starting with the setup. "So last week I got a commission" loses people before the problem shows up.
- No stakes.
- Narrating the turn instead of showing it.

## FAQ

**Does every video need a story?**
No. Tutorials, montages, and demos have their own shapes. Use this when something actually happened.

**No turn in the story?**
Then it's a vlog or a process video. Cut it as one of those.
