**To fix a continuity problem in Vyra on iPhone, identify what changes unexpectedly across a cut and choose compatible shots, a clearer order or an honest visible break.**

The cup is full in one shot and empty in the next, then full again. A viewer may notice that before noticing your captions. Review the state of the subject across the cut before covering it with an effect.

The native listing supports rearranging clips and adding text. Continuity assessment is editorial guidance, not an automatic detection claim. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Find the cut where the action or object seems to jump.
2. Compare the outgoing and incoming frames for position, direction and progress.
3. Look for a compatible take or a point in the clips that joins more clearly.
4. If time has passed, show that change deliberately through order or a short label.
5. Watch the surrounding sequence to ensure the fix creates no new contradiction.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| An action happens twice | Trim the repeated part |
| The process jumps ahead | Make the time change clear |
| No compatible take exists | Keep an honest cut or add context rather than implying continuity |

## A brief to try

```
Check the join where the cup becomes full again. Use a compatible take if one exists. Otherwise show that this is a later moment. Keep the real sequence of events and do not invent a replacement shot.
```

## Check the result

An edit can explain a time jump without pretending the camera filmed it continuously. A wider shot may help establish a new moment, but it should come from the same real sequence you intend to show. Review spoken claims alongside the new order.

## FAQ

**Can a transition fix every continuity problem?**
An effect changes the join’s appearance. It does not establish that two incompatible events occurred continuously.

**Should I rearrange a demonstration freely?**
Keep the actual order when it affects how someone follows the process.

**Can I use a label to show elapsed time?**
The native app supports text overlays. Use a label that accurately describes the time change.
