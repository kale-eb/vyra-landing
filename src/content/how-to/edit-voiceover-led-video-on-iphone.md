**To edit a voiceover-led video on iPhone, settle the narration first and place footage where it supports each spoken idea. In Vyra, use your own recorded explanation as the guide for the sequence rather than filling every gap with unrelated clips.**

This differs from adding a voiceover to a finished montage. The narration decides the order and the length of the important shots. A sentence about a mistake needs footage of that mistake or a clear explanation that it was not filmed.

## Work through the edit

1. Record a concise narration with the beginning, explanation and ending you want.
2. Add the narration and the relevant footage to the project.
3. Match each spoken point to a shot that actually supports it.
4. Remove visual repetition while preserving the full meaning of the narration.
5. Review both muted picture flow and sound-on timing before export.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A sentence names a visible detail | Show that detail during the sentence |
| A point has no matching footage | Use an honest wider shot or revise the narration |
| A cut interrupts the explanation | Let the spoken thought finish |

## A brief to try

```
Use my recorded voiceover to set the order. Show the first sketch when I mention the idea, the process during the explanation and the final painting at the end. Keep the narration intact and do not add unrelated stock video.
```

Read the narration while watching the export. Each reference such as "this edge" should have a clear visual target. If the picture changes before the sentence finishes, the viewer may attach the words to the wrong shot.

## FAQ

**Should I finish the picture before recording narration?**
For this workflow, make the narration clear first, then fit the picture to it. A picture-led edit uses a different order.

**Can I keep natural sound as well?**
Yes. Decide which original sounds help and check their balance against the voiceover.

**Can a caption replace a missing shot?**
A label can explain context, but it does not show an action you never filmed. Keep that distinction clear.
