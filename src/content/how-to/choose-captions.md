**Most people watch muted, so captions are the main text on the video. My default is 3 to 5 word phrases, bold sans-serif, about 5 percent of frame height, sitting lower-middle above the platform buttons. Word-by-word is for fast delivery. A couple of big emphasis words per video do more than animating everything.**

## Why it matters

Muted viewing is the norm on all three platforms.

## The method

1. **Pick the mode per video.** Phrases for talking head, tutorials, stories, vlogs. Word-by-word for fast opinions and hype.
2. **One font, one weight.** Vyra's default is Inter Bold for captions and Montserrat Black for titles. Don't mix three fonts.
3. **Size for a phone.** Cap height around 4 to 6 percent of frame height on 9:16. Check it on an actual phone.
4. **Stay in the safe zone.** Above the bottom 20 percent, inside the middle 80 percent.
5. **Pick 1 to 3 emphasis moments.** The claim, the turn, the payoff. Make those words big and hide normal captions for that beat.
6. **Fix the transcript.** Remove fillers, fix names, break lines where you'd pause.

## Rules of thumb

| Setting | Phrase captions | Word-by-word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Words per caption | 3 to 5 | 1 |
| Time on screen | 0.8 to 2 s | 0.15 to 0.4 s |
| Best for | explanation, story, vlog | hype, opinion, hooks |
| Font weight | 700 | 800 to 900 |
| Animation | none | pop or instant |
White with a soft dark outline works on almost anything. One accent color, only on emphasis words.

## Examples

- Phrase captions on a pricing talking head. "I charge by the" / "day, not the hour" / "and here is why."
- Emphasis moment. I say "I just dropped out to build something." Normal captions stop. "I just / dropped / out." stacks up big in Montserrat Black with "dropped" in the accent color, holds a second, clears, captions come back.

## Common mistakes

- Captions in the bottom 15 percent where the app covers them.
- A bounce on every word. It's noise after 10 seconds.
- Posting the auto transcript without fixing it.

## FAQ

**Burned-in or platform captions?**
Burn them in. Platform captions move around and are off by default for some people. Add the platform's on top if you want.

**Do captions need to match exactly?**
Match the meaning. Cut "um" and repeats, but never change what was said.
