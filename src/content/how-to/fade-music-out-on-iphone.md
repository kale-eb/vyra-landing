**In Vyra on iPhone, ask chat to lower the music gradually before the ending. Identify the music track and the point where it should become silent, then listen to the final export.**

If a video ends on the sound of a door closing, music that cuts off at that exact frame can make the ending abrupt. Let the song fall away before the door closes so the recorded sound has room.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Audio features”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Find the end of the music item and the intended final sound.
2. Tell chat when the fade should start and when the music should become silent.
3. Keep any narration or natural sound at its intended level.
4. Play the last several seconds and listen for a smooth change.
5. Export and check that the saved file includes the full ending.

## A brief to try

> Fade the background music to silence over the last three seconds before the door closes. Keep the door sound and the last word of my narration unchanged.

## Check the result

Start with a short fade and listen. A long fade can drain the energy before the final image arrives. If the song includes a strong musical ending, consider placing the cut around it and checking whether a fade is still needed.

## FAQ

**Will fading music also fade my voice?**
Identify the music item in the request. Replay the ending to confirm narration and recorded sound keep their intended level.

**Can I fade only the last use of a song?**
Name the timeline section you mean and check earlier uses of the track after the change.
