**To edit an art process video on iPhone in Vyra, add the clips that show the work changing, explain the sequence you want and keep a clear shot of the finished piece. Ask for a draft, then adjust the pacing so someone can follow the process without watching every repeated movement.**

You filmed the whole process, but the finished painting only gets half a second at the end. Hold that shot longer. Cut the part where nothing changes, and give people time to see what you made.

## Build the process edit

1. Add the setup, process details and finished-piece shots to the [iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
2. Identify the moments where the piece visibly changes, such as the first wash, a new color or the final detail.
3. Ask Vyra to build the edit around those moments and give it a target length.
4. Keep a spoken explanation or record a voiceover for the decision the picture does not explain.
5. Check that your hands, tools and artwork stay visible inside the frame.
6. Hold on the finished work, then export and watch the result on your phone.

## Prompt to try

```
Make a vertical process video from these painting clips. Keep the first marks, the color change and the final details. Shorten repeated brush movements. Keep the line where I explain the background color, and show the whole finished piece at the end for 4 seconds.
```

## Choose what the viewer should notice

A close-up can explain texture. A wider shot can explain scale. Use both when they show different information. If every cut is a tight detail, someone may finish the video without knowing what the whole piece looks like.

For a before-and-after edit, use the [two-stage reveal guide](/how-to/make-before-after-video-on-iphone). For a narrated process, the [voiceover guide](/how-to/add-voiceover-to-video-on-iphone) covers aligning a line with the right shot.

## FAQ

**Can I edit art footage with no talking?**
Yes. Tell Vyra which visible changes to keep and whether to preserve natural sound or use music.

**Do I need a reference video?**
No. A clear brief about your own footage is enough to start. A reference is optional when you want to explain a particular editing style.

**Can Vyra make the missing finished-piece shot?**
Vyra does not generate video footage. Film the finished piece and add that recording to your project.
