**To caption speech over music on iPhone, check the spoken words against the recording, then review caption timing with the music playing. Recheck any line where the music masks a word.**

A clear voice over a quiet track is easier to review than a voice buried in the original recording. Separate music you added during editing from music already recorded in the same clip. Lowering an added track does not repair unclear source speech.

The listing confirms captions, music and audio adjustment. Accuracy review is guidance, with no measured transcription claim. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Choose the speech edit before generating the captions.
2. Identify whether the music is a separate track or part of the original recording.
3. Generate captions and replay difficult words with headphones if useful.
4. Correct only the words you can confirm and adjust added music if it overwhelms speech.
5. Watch the export with sound and then muted to check timing and meaning.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Separate added music covers the voice | Lower that music while reviewing speech |
| Music is already mixed into the recording | Check the source and avoid guessing obscured words |
| A lyric is mistaken for speech | Correct the caption using the actual spoken line |

## A brief to try

```
Caption my explanation, not the song in the background. Keep my wording. Flag any word you cannot confirm and leave the music arrangement alone until I review the transcript.
```

Listen at the volume someone might use on their phone. Loud playback can hide that the balance is poor at a normal level. Check that the words on screen tell the same story as the audible voice, including negatives and numbers.

## FAQ

**Will captions always be accurate when music is loud?**
No. Review obscured speech and correct it from the source. Do not treat a plausible guess as a verified word.

**Can I lower music that I added separately?**
Vyra supports audio adjustments. Lower the added music and listen again.

**Is this a background-noise removal guide?**
This workflow reviews captions and audio balance. It does not promise recovery of speech that the recording does not clearly contain.
