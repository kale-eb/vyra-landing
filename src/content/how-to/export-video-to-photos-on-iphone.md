**To save a Vyra edit to Photos on iPhone, finish the timeline, export the video and choose to save it to your photo library. You can also share the finished video from the app. Wait for the export to finish, then open the saved file and check it before posting.**

The preview is where you make decisions. The export is the file you will actually share. Check that file once so you catch a missing ending, a caption over the wrong shot or a music track that is too loud.

## Save the finished edit

1. Open the finished project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
2. Play the beginning and ending, then check any sections you recently changed.
3. Start the export and review the quality options available for your plan.
4. Let the export complete and save the finished video to your photo library.
5. If iOS asks for photo-library permission, review the request and allow the access needed to save your video.
6. Open Photos, find the exported file and play it with sound before sharing it.

## Check the actual file

Watch the last few seconds. They are easy to miss while you are fixing the opening. Make sure the reveal lasts long enough and the audio ends where you meant it to.

If you are posting a vertical video, check that the exported framing matches the project. Canvas shape and export resolution are separate settings. A tall frame does not automatically mean a high-resolution export.

## If saving does not finish

Keep the project available and note whether the failure happened during export or while saving to Photos. Check the app's photo-library permission and available phone storage. If you need help, send support the error message and the stage that failed. Do not delete your source clips as a first troubleshooting step.

## FAQ

**Are free exports watermarked?**
Yes. Free mobile exports are 720p with a watermark. New accounts currently receive 100 free credits.

**Can I export without a watermark?**
The App Store listing describes watermark-free exports on paid mobile subscriptions. Check the plan and export options shown in your app.

**Does saving to Photos post the video publicly?**
Saving creates a file in your photo library. Posting it to a social platform is a separate action.

**Can I share from Vyra instead?**
Yes. The iPhone app supports sharing the finished video. Check the destination before sending it.
