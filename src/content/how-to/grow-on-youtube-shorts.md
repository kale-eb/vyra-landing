**Shorts grow on one number YouTube shows you directly: viewed versus swiped away. The Shorts feed shows your video and measures whether the viewer stays or flicks past. So the job is a first frame that stops the swipe and a video that pays off before 30 seconds. The difference from TikTok is what happens after: a Short can send people to a long video and a subscription, which is where YouTube growth compounds.**

## Why it matters

Shorts are the cheapest way onto YouTube, and YouTube is the platform where subscribers actually come back. A Short that gets 100k views and converts 1% is 1,000 subscribers who will see your next upload in their feed. TikTok and Reels followers rarely behave that way.

## The method

1. **Design the first frame.** Shorts show a still before playback in some placements. Make the first frame legible: a face, a piece, or a bold text hook.
2. **Pay off by 30 seconds.** Longer Shorts can work, but the viewed-versus-swiped number drops sharply past 30 seconds for new channels.
3. **Loop when it fits.** A Short that ends where it began gets rewatched, and rewatches count as views.
4. **Title like a search result.** Shorts titles are searchable. "Speed paint" is weak; "Oil painting a magnolia in 20 minutes" is a query.
5. **Point to a long video.** Mention or link the related long-form upload. That is where the subscription happens.
6. **Post 3 to 5 a week.** Shorts need volume less than TikTok but more than long-form.
7. **Watch the swipe-away number.** In YouTube Studio, "viewed vs swiped away" above 70% typically means the hook works.

## Rules of thumb

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Shorts per week | 3 to 5 |
| Length | 15 to 35 s |
| Viewed vs swiped away | typically above 70% |
| Average view duration | typically above 80% of length |
| Title length | under 60 characters, with a searchable phrase |
| Long-form uploads alongside | 1 every 1 to 2 weeks |

## Examples

A 25-second process Short titled "Painting with real magnolia petals" with a first frame of the petal being pressed into paint will typically hold above 70% viewed-versus-swiped. The same clip titled "new piece" with a first frame of a blank canvas will not.

A loop Short: the finished piece dissolves back to the blank canvas at the end, and a rewatch counts.

## Common mistakes

- A first frame that is a blank canvas, a black frame, or you reaching for the phone.
- Running past 45 seconds before you have data on what holds.
- Titles that are not searchable.
- No long-form channel for the Short to feed.
- Reposting a TikTok with the watermark. YouTube can suppress it.

## FAQ

**Do Shorts help my long-form videos?**
Indirectly. Shorts bring subscribers; long-form retains them. The channel needs both.

**Should Shorts and long videos be on the same channel?**
Yes, for most creators. Separate channels only make sense once each has its own audience.

**Do hashtags matter on Shorts?**
#shorts is no longer needed. The title matters more.
