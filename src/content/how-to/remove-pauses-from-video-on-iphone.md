**To remove a pause inside a video on iPhone, split around the quiet section, remove that section and check the new join. In Vyra, you can also ask for a first cut in chat, then review each cut against the spoken sentence.**

A pause before a new thought may help the story. A long wait while you find the next line usually does not. Listen once before deciding which gaps to remove. Keep a note of any breath or reaction you want left alone.

## Work through the edit

1. Add the talking clip from Photos to a Vyra project.
2. Find a pause between two complete phrases. Note the words on both sides.
3. Ask to shorten that gap, or split around it and remove only the unwanted section.
4. Play across the join with sound. Restore a little room if the sentence feels rushed.
5. Review captions after the cut, then export and check the saved video.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for the next line | Shorten the empty gap |
| A breath inside a sentence | Keep enough room for natural speech |
| A reaction after a joke | Judge its storytelling purpose before cutting |

## A brief to try

```
Shorten the long waits between my sentences. Keep normal breaths and the pause after "I actually liked it." Do not change my words or speed up the audio. Show me the first cut before adding music.
```

Listen for clipped consonants and abrupt changes in room sound. If the join clicks or feels harsh, move the cut away from the start of the next word. A shorter runtime is useful only if the explanation remains understandable.

## FAQ

**Does trimming the ends remove an internal pause?**
Trimming the start and end leaves the middle intact. Removing a gap inside the clip requires selecting that middle section.

**Should I remove every breath?**
Keep breaths that let the delivery sound natural. Decide by listening to the sentence rather than cutting every quiet moment.

**Can I review the first cut before captions?**
Yes. Ask for the speech edit first, check it, then add captions to the version you intend to keep.
