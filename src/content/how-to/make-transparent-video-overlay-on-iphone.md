**In Vyra on iPhone, place the overlay above the background and change the selected clip’s Opacity. Review the resulting blend while both pictures move.**

A sketch laid over a painting can help viewers see the original composition. Reduce the sketch’s opacity until the paint remains visible. Check the darkest lines so they still explain the comparison.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Editing tools on iPhone and Masks”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Place the overlay above the picture you want to keep visible.
2. Select the overlay and choose Opacity.
3. Lower its opacity gradually.
4. Play both items together and check any important edges and text.
5. Keep the effect only for the interval where the comparison is useful.

## A brief to try

> Place the sketch over the finished painting and make the sketch partly transparent. Align the main outline as closely as possible. Keep the comparison for three seconds and leave the narration alone.

## Check the result

Two moving pictures can become difficult to follow when neither has enough contrast. Use a still reference or a shorter comparison if the combined motion hides the detail.

## FAQ

**Is opacity the same as removing a background?**
Opacity affects the whole selected image. A mask controls which area stays visible.

**How can I keep captions clear of the overlay?**
Check the layer order and move the overlay or captions so the text stays readable. Replay the entire overlap.
