**To make a portfolio video on iPhone, choose a small set of work, decide what connects it and give each piece enough time to inspect. In Vyra, combine your photos and clips into that sequence and check every crop before exporting.**

A portfolio edit is an introduction to the work, not a catalogue of every file. You might organize by medium, scale or the development of an idea. Pick an order that someone can understand without your explanation beside them.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose pieces that serve the portfolio's purpose and intended audience.
2. Gather a clear whole-work view and any useful detail shot for each piece.
3. Arrange the work according to one consistent organizing idea.
4. Add accurate titles or materials only where they help.
5. Review whether each piece can be seen clearly at normal speed and phone size.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A piece needs its whole shape understood | Show the full work before a detail |
| Several pieces repeat the same idea | Keep the selection that best serves the portfolio |
| A title is too long for the shot | Shorten the label or allow more reading time |

## A brief to try

```
Make a portfolio sequence from these six pieces, ordered from smallest to largest. Show each whole work before its detail. Keep labels limited to the titles and materials I supply. Leave the final piece on screen long enough to inspect.
```

A crop that looks dramatic can hide the very thing the viewer should evaluate. Compare the export with the source images and restore the edges where they matter. Do not add awards, clients or performance claims that are not yours.

## Review and export

Save the finished edit to Photos and open that saved copy to review it. New mobile accounts currently get 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro are sold through the App Store. AI editing needs an internet connection.

For phone controls and setup, start with the [iPhone app guide](/ai-video-editor-app). The related reel, "Everything I painted this semester, smallest to largest", is a creator example rather than an iPhone walkthrough.

## FAQ

**Should I include every piece I have made?**
Select according to the purpose of this portfolio. A shorter coherent set can be easier to inspect.

**Can I use both photos and moving detail shots?**
Yes. Give each a clear role, and check that viewers can connect the detail with the whole piece.

**Will a higher export resolution fix a blurry source image?**
No. Start with a clear source and check the available export settings and plan.
