**To add lyrics to a video on iPhone, place the lines you supply as text and time each line to the part of the song it accompanies. Review the words and timing yourself.**

Write out the exact lines you want to show before editing. Singing can stretch a word across a much longer beat than normal speech. Work line by line rather than assuming speech captions will produce the lyric timing you want.

The listing confirms adding text and music. Supplied lyric text avoids an unsupported claim about singing transcription or karaoke features. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Add your footage and the song audio you are using to the project.
2. Supply the lyric lines with the spelling and line breaks you want.
3. Add each line as text over the matching section.
4. Adjust the start and end of each display while listening to the song.
5. Play the exported video through the lyric section without pausing.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A word is held over several beats | Keep the relevant line visible long enough |
| Two lines make the screen crowded | Split the display into shorter sections |
| Instrumental space follows a line | Decide deliberately whether the text should remain |

## A brief to try

```
Use only the lyric lines I provide as text over this sequence. Show one line at a time, aligned with the singing. Keep the painting visible and do not invent missing lyrics or change the clip order.
```

Check the first consonant and the last held note, not just the middle of the line. A lyric that appears too late can feel disconnected even when the spelling is correct. Choose material you are authorized to use and publish.

## FAQ

**Will automatic speech captions reliably transcribe singing?**
Do not assume that. Supply and check the lyrics for this text-overlay workflow.

**Is this word-by-word karaoke highlighting?**
This guide covers timed text lines. It does not promise a dedicated karaoke highlighting mode.

**Can I leave the text through an instrumental gap?**
Yes, if that is the intended design. Check that it does not imply the line is still being sung.
