**In Vyra on iPhone, split the clip at the beginning and end of the unwanted section, select the middle piece and delete it. Review the join and any remaining gap before exporting.**

You may need the explanation before a long wait and the result after it. Keep both. Mark where the wait starts and where something changes again so the viewer can follow the missing time.

## Work through the edit

1. Find the first frame of the section you want to remove.
2. Select the clip and split at that point.
3. Move to the end of the unwanted section and split again.
4. Select the middle piece and delete it. Check whether the remaining pieces need moving together.
5. Play across the join with sound and repair any clipped word, awkward silence or missing action.

## Check the result

A picture cut can also remove part of the clip’s recorded sound. If the voice needs to continue across a new shot, work with its extracted audio separately. Check existing captions against the revised speech.

## FAQ

**Will deleting the middle piece close the gap automatically?**
Check the timeline after deleting. Move the remaining pieces together when a gap remains.

**How do I avoid cutting a word in half?**
Listen around both boundaries and leave the complete spoken phrase. Adjust the split if a consonant or breath is clipped.
