**Yes. Give Vyra your own clips, a reference video and the audio you want to use. In the iPhone app, ask it to put your clips together, follow the reference’s pacing and transitions, and sync the edit to your supplied audio. Review the draft and ask for changes in the same chat.**

You already have the videos and the song. You can picture the result because you have an example. The job is to turn those inputs into an edit you can post without placing every clip and cut yourself.

Vyra edits the footage you supply. The reference explains how you want it edited. Your separate audio file supplies what the viewer should hear.

## Give each input a clear job

| Input | What to give Vyra | What it should do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your clips | The footage you want in the finished video | Select useful moments and assemble your story |
| Reference or demo video | An example of the edit you have in mind | Guide the opening, pacing, transitions or caption treatment you name |
| Your audio | The song, voiceover or recording you want to hear | Set the soundtrack and guide the timing of the picture |

Say explicitly that the reference is a reference. Vyra’s current help distinguishes importing a link as footage from using it to guide the edit. If you want only your recordings in the result, say that too.

## Add your clips, reference and audio on iPhone

1. **Add your footage.** Start a project with the clips you filmed. In Library, the **+** button offers **Gallery** and **Files** for adding assets. Check that the intended clips are available before asking for the edit.
2. **Add the reference.** Paste its Instagram, TikTok or YouTube link in chat and identify it as a reference. You can also save references through **Templates**, **References** and **Add references**. Sharing a video from another app offers **Save as a reference instead**.
3. **Add your audio file.** Add the supplied song or recording through Files and make it available to the project. Identify it in the brief, especially when the reference already has a different soundtrack.
4. **Ask for the whole edit.** Name the intended length, opening and ending. Tell Vyra what to follow from the reference and which audio to use. Ask it to assemble the footage, rather than only analyze the example.
5. **Review the first draft.** Watch for the right moments, cuts that fit your audio, readable captions and a complete ending. Ask for a focused change where something is wrong.
6. **Export and check the file.** Review the finished video in Photos before posting it. Check the sound as well as the picture.

## A request you can copy

```
I want to make a video for my socials. I’m giving you my own clips, a reference video and the audio I want to use. Put my clips together for me, following the reference’s pacing and transitions, and sync the edit to my audio.

Use the reference only as an editing guide. Use my supplied clips for the picture and my audio file for the soundtrack. Do not use the reference’s footage or song.

Make a 30-second vertical video. Open with my finished result, then show the process, and give the final result time to be seen. Keep any spoken explanation complete and lower the music while I speak. Show me a draft I can review.
```

Replace the length, opening and ending with what your own video needs. If your clips contain no speech, leave out the speech instruction. If the supplied audio is a voiceover, tell Vyra to build the picture around those sentences.

## Follow the example while keeping your audio

A different song may build or pause in different places. Ask Vyra to follow the reference’s changes in energy while placing the actual cuts against your track. The current Audio features help documents rhythm analysis and cutting to music beats.

For a song with a clear beat, try this.

```
Follow the reference’s slow opening and quicker middle. Place the faster cuts on beats in my supplied song. Keep the reveal visible through the last phrase. Use my song throughout, with none of the reference audio.
```

For a spoken recording, try this.

```
Use my voiceover as the full explanation. Place my process clips under the matching sentences. Borrow the reference’s caption placement and transitions, but keep every sentence intact. Do not speed up the voice to match the reference’s length.
```

For audio without a steady beat, name a phrase, sound or timestamp to cut around. A quiet recording does not need a cut on every sound. The [music guide](/how-to/add-music-to-video-on-iphone) explains balancing a track with the sound already in your clips.

## Fix the part that missed your idea

| What you notice | A useful follow-up |
| --- | --- |
| It used the reference’s song | Replace that soundtrack with my supplied audio and keep the picture order |
| It included a shot from the reference | Remove that shot and use only my uploaded footage |
| The cuts follow the example but miss your music | Retain the overall pace and retime the cuts to my track |
| The important moment disappeared | Restore that moment and shorten the setup instead |
| The music hides your explanation | Keep the full sentence audible and lower the music underneath it |
| The ending is rushed | Hold the finished result longer and finish the audio cleanly |

Listen to the joins where speech crosses a visual cut. Check that the saved video still contains the words and sounds you meant to keep. If the brief needs the original sounds from your clips as well as the supplied track, name both. See [keeping original audio while matching a reference](/how-to/keep-original-audio-when-matching-reference-on-iphone).

## What the reference cannot supply

The example may have a reveal or camera angle you never filmed. Add that shot or choose an opening your clips can support. Vyra does not generate missing video footage from the reference. Exact reproduction of every effect is not guaranteed.

## FAQ

**Can I give AI my videos, a demo video and my own audio and have it put everything together?**
Yes. In Vyra, supply your clips, identify the demo as the editing reference and add the audio you want to use. Ask for the assembled edit, including the desired length and ending. Review the draft before exporting.

**Can it sync my clips to the music I supply?**
Vyra’s current help documents cutting to music beats through rhythm analysis. Ask it to use your supplied track, then review the cuts and the important moments. For audio without a steady beat, name phrases or timestamps instead.

**Will the reference’s footage or song appear in my video?**
Identify it as a reference and ask Vyra to use only your supplied clips and soundtrack in the result. Review the draft to confirm that separation before posting.

**How should I use a song with a different beat from the reference?**
Follow the reference’s overall pacing and changes in energy while asking for cuts timed to your own song. Its exact cut timings may not fit the new track.

**Can my audio be a voiceover instead of music?**
Yes. Supply the spoken recording and ask Vyra to place the footage around its sentences. Keep the explanation complete and check captions against that voice.

**Do I have to place every clip on the timeline myself?**
You can ask Vyra’s built-in chat to assemble the draft from your clips. Use follow-up requests or the manual timeline to refine the result.

**Do I need Claude or ChatGPT for this?**
No. The iPhone app has built-in chat. External assistant integrations are a separate option.
