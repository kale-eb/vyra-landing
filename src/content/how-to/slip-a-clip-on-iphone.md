**In Vyra on iPhone, tap a clip and choose Slip to change which part of the original recording it shows. The clip keeps its place and length in the edit. Use it when the cut is already timed well and you want a different moment inside it.**

Say you have a two-second cutaway of your desk. It lines up with the voiceover, but your hand only picks up the brush near the end. Slip the clip to show the pickup earlier in those two seconds. The shot that follows can still start at the same point.

## Work through the cut

1. Play through the cutaway with the surrounding shots. Decide which action needs to be visible.
2. Tap the clip in the Vyra timeline and choose **Slip**.
3. Shift the source moment within the clip. Keep enough time to see the action finish.
4. Play across both cuts. Check that the new first frame makes sense after the previous shot and the new last frame leads into the next shot.
5. Listen again. If you changed a clip with useful sound or speech, check which words or sounds now play inside the same interval.

## Decide between slipping, trimming and replacing

| What needs to change | What to use |
| --- | --- |
| The moment shown inside a cutaway of the right length | Slip within the same recording |
| How long the clip stays on screen | [Trim the clip](/how-to/trim-video-on-iphone) |
| Which recording appears in the project | [Replace the clip](/how-to/replace-clip-in-video-on-iphone) |

If you are on the last usable seconds of the recording, there may be nowhere further to go. Check the original clip before trying again. A different take may give you a better start and ending.

## A brief to try

```
In the two-second desk cutaway under my voiceover, show the moment where I pick up the brush. Keep the cutaway in the same position and at the same length. Leave the voiceover and neighboring cuts alone.
```

## FAQ

**Does slipping change the length of my edit?**
Slipping keeps the clip’s position and length. It changes which source frames play in that time.

**Can I slip into footage that was never recorded?**
No. Slip chooses another part of the same recording. If you need a different angle or another take, replace the clip.

**When should I trim instead?**
Trim when the edit needs more or less time on the shot. Slip when the duration works and you want to change the moment shown inside it.
