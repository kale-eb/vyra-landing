**Clean audio is mostly distance and room. A phone mic 30 cm from your mouth in a bedroom with curtains and a rug beats an expensive mic across a bare kitchen. When you can't stay close, clip on a lav or use a wireless mic. Outside, a furry windscreen is mandatory. People tolerate soft video and leave over bad audio.**

## Why it matters

Audio is the half people judge first and forgive last.

## The method

1. **Get close.** Built-in mic at 20 to 30 cm. That means arm's length, not a tripod across the room.
2. **Pick the room.** Soft surfaces absorb echo. A closet full of clothes is a free vocal booth.
3. **Pick the mic.** Built-in for close talking. Wired lav for a tripod talking head. Wireless lav for walking, cooking, demos.
4. **Kill the noise first.** Fridge, fan, AC, open window. Turn them back on after.
5. **Block the wind.** Furry windscreen on any external mic. For the built-in, turn your back to the wind and cup a hand loosely under the phone.
6. **Record a test.** Say your first line, play it back on headphones. Peaks around minus 12 dB, never at zero.

## Rules of thumb

| Situation | Mic | Distance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talking head, phone in hand | built-in | 20 to 30 cm |
| Talking head on tripod | wired lav | clipped at the sternum |
| Walking vlog | wireless lav | same |
| Cooking or demo | wireless lav | same |
| Two people | two wireless lavs | each |
Peaks between minus 12 and minus 6 dB. Record 10 seconds of room tone for noise reduction later.

## Examples

- Studio talking head. Wired lav on the collar, phone on a tripod 80 cm away, fan off, curtains closed, 10 seconds of room tone first.
- Walking vlog through a market. Wireless lav under a jacket lapel with a furry windscreen, phone at chest height, talking in 8 second bursts.

## Do it in Vyra

- "Sync the separate lav recording to the camera audio using the clap at the start, then mute the camera audio."
- "Reduce the room echo and background hum on my voice track, then normalize speech to around minus 14 LUFS. Do not touch the music."
- "Duck the music 8 dB whenever I speak and bring it back between sentences."

## Common mistakes

- Phone on a tripod across the room using the built-in mic.
- Recording in a kitchen or bathroom.
- No windscreen outside. Wind isn't fixable.

## FAQ

**Are AirPods good enough?**
For a quick talking head in a quiet room, sure. They compress the voice and pick up rustle. A wired lav costs less and sounds better.

**Can AI fix bad audio?**
Mild echo and hum, yes. Wind, clipping, or a mic two meters away, no.
