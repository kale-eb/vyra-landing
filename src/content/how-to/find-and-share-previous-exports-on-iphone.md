**In Vyra on iPhone, open Library and Exports to find finished videos. Press and hold the export for playback, naming and sharing options, then check that you selected the intended version.**

You finished two openings yesterday and now need to send the approved one. Play the file before sharing it. A familiar thumbnail may belong to a different version with the same ending.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. Vyra’s current Exporting on iPhone help describes the saved-export controls.

## Work through the edit

1. Open Library, then Exports.
2. Find the candidate video and play it to check the opening and ending.
3. Press and hold the finished export for its available actions.
4. Rename it clearly if you need to distinguish it from other completed versions.
5. Use Share or Save to Photos and check the chosen file before sending it.

## A brief to try

> Before making another edit, help me distinguish the result-first and explanation-first versions of this project. I want to share the result-first ending I already reviewed.

## Check the result

The current Exporting on iPhone help says finished exports are saved under Library and Exports, with press-and-hold actions including Play, Rename, Share and Save to Photos. Finding a finished export is a separate job from changing the project that created it.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro options are sold through the App Store.

## FAQ

**Do I have to render another video just to share an existing export?**
Look for the finished file in Library and Exports first. Review and share that saved result when it is the version you need.

**Will changing the project rename an older export for me?**
Inspect the existing export separately. Keep its filename or label clear so you do not confuse the saved file with a later project state.
