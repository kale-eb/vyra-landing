**Before editing an iCloud video on iPhone, confirm the full clip loads in Photos and check available device storage. Then add the material to your Vyra project and wait for it to become ready.**

A thumbnail in Photos does not tell you whether the original file is already on the device. If you use optimized storage, leave time for the source clip to load before deciding that an editor cannot read it.

The Vyra listing confirms adding footage from Photos. Apple explains optimized iCloud storage and checking device storage. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012). [Apple’s storage guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105061) explains the source-file distinction.

## Work through the edit

1. Open the source video in Photos and play through the section you need.
2. If it is still loading, keep a working connection while Photos retrieves the original.
3. Check iPhone storage if the clip cannot finish loading or the device is nearly full.
4. Add the prepared clip to Vyra and check that it becomes available in the project.
5. Make a small test cut and review it before adding the rest of a large shoot.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Only a thumbnail appears quickly | Wait for the full clip to play |
| The source will not load in Photos | Resolve that source-file issue before editing |
| The clip loads but the project upload stalls | Use the upload troubleshooting guide rather than repeatedly replacing the source |

## A brief to try

```
I have added the clip after checking it plays in Photos. Before editing, confirm which footage is ready in this project. Do not start cutting from a missing clip.
```

Keep the source until you have checked the project and exported result. If loading fails, note whether the failure happens in Photos or after adding the clip to Vyra. That distinction gives support a useful starting point without requiring technical logs.

## FAQ

**Why can I see a video that is not fully on my phone?**
With optimized iCloud storage, Apple keeps original files in iCloud and smaller copies on the device. Opening the full video may require a download.

**Does this guarantee an upload will succeed?**
No. It checks the source first. A separate connection or upload problem can still need troubleshooting.

**Should I delete the original to free space first?**
Keep the original until you have confirmed a usable copy and a checked export. Review your storage options before deleting footage.
