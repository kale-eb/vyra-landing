**In Vyra for iPhone version 0.1.9, open the Ideas tab to browse short videos picked for your niche, or tap the Vyra button on a main screen to ask for ideas. Choose something you can make with your own footage.**

An interesting reference is useful when you can explain which part belongs in your video. Maybe it is the order of the shots, or the way the result appears before the explanation. Write that down before starting an edit.

The [official iPhone listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) document native editing. The version 0.1.9 notes specifically describe the Ideas feature.

## Work through the edit

1. Check that the iPhone app includes the version 0.1.9 Ideas feature.
2. Open Ideas or ask the assistant for ideas related to your subject.
3. Choose one example and identify the editing choice you want to learn from.
4. Collect your own clips that can support the idea and describe the intended edit.
5. Review the draft against your footage and revise any missing context.

## A brief to try

> Suggest a video idea for the clips of my sketchbook. I have the rough sketch, a page of mistakes and the finished drawing. I want to explain one decision I changed.

## Check the result

The official version 0.1.9 release notes describe the Ideas tab, niche suggestions and starting a project with supplied clips through the assistant. A displayed view count describes a reference video. It does not predict your result or grant permission to reuse its footage.

New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

## FAQ

**Does the Ideas tab make new footage for me?**
Vyra edits the footage you supply. Use the idea to plan or shape your own recording, and keep any third-party media permissions separate.

**What should I do if I cannot see Ideas?**
Check your installed app version against the official App Store listing. The feature is documented in version 0.1.9. The public ideas library can also help you plan your own footage.
