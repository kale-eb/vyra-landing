**In Vyra on iPhone, use the selected clip’s Adjust and Style controls, or ask chat for a gentle vignette. Inspect the edges for useful details before keeping the treatment.**

Darkening the edges can draw attention toward a centered subject. It can also hide a hand, a tool or the corner of a painting. Check the entire action instead of judging one attractive paused frame.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. Vyra’s current Color and the Adjust panel help describes these controls.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose the clip and identify the detail you want to emphasize.
2. Open Adjust and Style, or describe a gentle vignette in chat.
3. Apply a restrained treatment to the selected clip.
4. Watch the subject and hands move through the darker edges.
5. Compare the original and exported result before applying the look more widely.

## A brief to try

> Add a gentle vignette around this centered vase. Keep my hands readable when they enter from the sides and leave the bright glaze detail visible.

## Check the result

The current Color and the Adjust panel help lists Vignette under Style and documents the native Adjust tabs. A vignette changes the image’s edge treatment. It does not replace a better camera position when the subject sits near the edge.

## FAQ

**Should the vignette be identical on every shot?**
Judge the framing of each shot. A centered close-up and a wide shot with important edge detail may need different treatment.

**Can a vignette make my captions harder to read?**
Check the caption contrast against the changed background. Review the whole composition with text visible before exporting.
