**To change music while keeping your video cuts, replace the music track and review the existing sequence before asking for any timing changes. Tell Vyra which clip positions must stay fixed.**

A new song can change the feeling of an edit even when every shot stays in place. Decide whether you need a different soundtrack or a new cut to a different rhythm. Those are separate requests.

The listing confirms music and audio adjustments alongside timeline editing. Preserving the picture cut is an explicit editing constraint to verify. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Note the sequence and cut positions you want to preserve.
2. Add the replacement music to the Vyra project.
3. Remove or mute the earlier added music so the tracks do not compete.
4. Listen through the unchanged cuts and adjust the music level or chosen section.
5. Check the ending and export only after reviewing the new soundtrack.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| The new song starts with a long quiet intro | Choose a more useful section of that track |
| A cut falls between the new beats | Keep it if the sequence matters more than beat alignment |
| The old and new tracks both play | Remove the unwanted overlap before reviewing balance |

## A brief to try

```
Use the new music instead of the old added track. Keep the video clip order, cut positions and spoken audio unchanged. Check the music under my explanation and let me review it before any beat-sync changes.
```

Check the whole ending instead of listening only to the first few seconds. A different song may stop in the middle of a phrase or leave the last shot without sound. Make the audio choice deliberately before requesting a new picture edit.

## FAQ

**Will new music automatically match the old cuts?**
Not necessarily. Listen to the result and decide whether to keep the cut positions or recut to the new rhythm.

**Can I preserve the spoken audio?**
State that the voice must stay and check that only the added music changed.

**Can I use this when the old music is part of the recording?**
This workflow assumes a separate music track. Music already mixed into a source clip is a different problem.
