**To make a match cut in Vyra on iPhone, choose two shots with a similar shape or movement, line up their useful frames and review a direct cut between them.**

A round plate can lead into a round paint palette. The connection comes from where those shapes sit in the frame. Adding an effect before choosing the frames may hide the very connection you wanted people to notice.

The native listing confirms trim and rearrangement controls. The shot-selection method explains how to use those controls for a match cut. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Find two supplied shots that share a clear visual feature.
2. Choose the moment where that feature is easiest to recognize in each shot.
3. Arrange the clips next to each other and trim to those moments.
4. Review the subject position and direction across the join.
5. Play the cut in the full sequence and keep it only if the connection reads clearly.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Similar shape, different frame position | Choose a better source moment or adjust framing within the available material |
| Same motion, opposite direction | Decide whether the reversal is intentional |
| No useful similarity | Use a normal cut or select another pair |

## Check the result

A match cut should work at ordinary playback speed. If someone needs an explanation to see the connection, simplify it or choose a clearer pair. Keep the surrounding shot long enough for the first shape to register.

## FAQ

**Is a match cut the same as a transition preset?**
A match cut connects the visual content of two shots. A preset adds a separate effect between them.

**Can the app create a missing matching shot?**
Vyra edits supplied footage. Film or provide the second shot if you need a particular visual match.

**Must the subjects be identical?**
A recognizable shared shape, action or composition can connect different subjects.
