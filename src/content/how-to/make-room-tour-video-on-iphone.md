**To edit a room tour on iPhone, show the layout before moving into close details and keep a route someone can follow. Add your footage to Vyra and describe the order through the space rather than asking for unrelated attractive shots.**

A close-up of every object can leave viewers unsure how the room fits together. Start with a wide view, choose a direction and return to that context when moving into a new area.

## Work through the edit

1. Choose a wide establishing view and detail shots from each area.
2. Arrange the tour in a route through the room.
3. Remove repeated sweeps that show the same area without adding information.
4. Keep labels tied to the area currently visible.
5. Check that a person unfamiliar with the room can follow the transitions before export.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| A detail has no visible location | Introduce its area with a wider shot |
| A fast pan makes objects hard to see | Choose a steadier part of the recording |
| The tour jumps across the room | Restore a connecting view or clearer order |

## A brief to try

```
Edit this studio tour from the entrance to the desk and then the painting wall. Begin with the wide shot. Keep one useful detail in each area and finish with the full room. Do not add location details I have not provided.
```

Check reflections, personal documents and visible addresses before posting. A room can look different after a heavy crop, so confirm the framing still gives an honest sense of where things are.

## FAQ

**Does every object need a label?**
Label the areas or details that need explanation. Too much text can make the space harder to see.

**Can I cut a long walking shot?**
Yes. Shorten unhelpful movement while keeping the route understandable.

**Should I use a vertical or wide canvas?**
Choose for your destination and footage, then check that the layout remains understandable in that shape.
