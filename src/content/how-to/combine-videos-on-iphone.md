**To combine videos on iPhone with Vyra, add the clips from Photos to one project, arrange them on the timeline and export the finished sequence. Tell chat which order to keep if the order matters. You can use plain cuts or add a transition between selected clips.**

Before you add everything, pick the shot that explains the video and the shot that finishes it. A few clips in a deliberate order are easier to follow than a whole camera roll played from start to finish.

## Make one video from several clips

1. Start a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app) and add the clips you want to use.
2. Decide whether the sequence should follow time, steps in a process or the story you are telling.
3. Arrange the clips in that order on the timeline, or tell chat the order explicitly.
4. Trim the camera handling and dead time at each join.
5. Check the sound between clips. A quiet room followed by loud outdoor audio can be a bigger jump than the picture.
6. Export the sequence as one video and watch it in Photos.

## A simple order for a day out

Start with a shot that establishes where you are. Keep the moments where something changes, then finish on the result or the way home. If there is a spoken line that explains the day, give it space rather than putting it under a loud song.

For a tutorial, preserve the actual order of the steps. Rearranging a process to make a stronger opening should not make the instructions impossible to follow.

## Prompt to try

```
Combine these clips into one vertical video. Keep them in the order I added them. Trim the starts and ends where I am moving the phone. Use straight cuts, keep the spoken audio and hold the final shot for 3 seconds.
```

## Keep joins simple

You do not need an effect after every clip. Use a plain cut inside one scene. If the place or time changes, try one [transition](/how-to/add-transitions-to-videos-on-iphone) and check whether it makes that change clearer.

## FAQ

**Can I choose the clip order myself?**
Yes. Arrange the clips on Vyra's timeline or tell chat to keep the order you supplied.

**Will I get one exported video?**
Yes. Exporting the timeline produces the finished sequence as one video.

**Can I mix talking clips with silent footage?**
Yes. Check the audio at each join and say which spoken lines you want to preserve.
