**To reuse a shot in Vyra on iPhone, select its timeline clip and choose Duplicate. Check where the new copy appears and decide which part of the original recording each copy should show.**

You may want the finished painting at the beginning and again at the end. Reusing that shot can work, but a long repeated reveal can make the ending feel familiar before the process is over.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Editing tools on iPhone”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Find the shot you want to reuse.
2. Select the timeline clip and choose Duplicate.
3. Place the copy where it belongs in the sequence.
4. Check each copy for the intended crop, sound and duration.
5. Watch the opening and ending together to judge the repetition.

## A brief to try

> Use this finished-painting shot briefly at the start and hold the second copy longer at the end. Keep the process clips between them. Mute the opening copy.

## Check the result

Check whether the duplicate also carries audio or styling you need to adjust. Keep the original source available if you want each copy to use a different moment from the recording.

Keep your source footage while checking the edit. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans are sold through the App Store. Review the saved file in Photos before posting it.

## FAQ

**Is duplicating a clip the same as uploading it again?**
Duplicate reuses a timeline item in the edit. Check the library separately when managing the source file.

**Can the copies have different lengths?**
Trim each timeline copy to the part you need and replay the cuts around it.
