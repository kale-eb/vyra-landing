**In Vyra on iPhone, open Layout and ask for two clips side by side. Choose which recording goes in each cell, then check framing, timing and audio for both.**

If you are comparing two brush techniques, showing both at once lets viewers watch the difference. Start each recording at the useful action. A hand waiting off screen can make one technique look slower for the wrong reason.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Layouts”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Add both recordings to the project.
2. Tap Layout and choose a two-cell arrangement, or ask chat to arrange the clips.
3. Identify the left and right recordings.
4. Align the useful action and review both crops.
5. Choose which audio stays audible and check the entire comparison.

## A brief to try

> Put the dry-brush example on the left and the wet-brush example on the right. Start each at the first stroke. Keep the labels readable and use only my narration for sound.

## Check the result

Keep enough of the material visible to make a fair comparison. Match the apparent size of the area being demonstrated. Review what happens when the shorter recording finishes.

Keep your source footage while checking the edit. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans are sold through the App Store. Review the saved file in Photos before posting it.

## FAQ

**Can each side use a different clip?**
Yes. Layouts can contain separate videos or images. Name which material belongs in each cell.

**Do both clips need their own audio?**
Choose the sound that explains the comparison. Two recordings of speech playing together can be hard to follow.
