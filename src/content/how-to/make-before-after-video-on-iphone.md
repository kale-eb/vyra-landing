**To make a before-and-after video on iPhone with Vyra, add a shot of the starting point and a shot of the finished result. Put them in the order you want, trim the join and hold the result long enough to see the change. Similar framing makes the comparison easier to follow.**

The change is the reason for the video. Before adding an effect, check whether the viewer can actually see it. A finished painting filmed too close can hide the difference just as easily as a cut that arrives too early.

## Make the comparison clear

1. Add the before and after clips to the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
2. Choose moments with a similar angle, distance and amount of light where possible.
3. Put the starting shot first and the finished shot after it, or explain another order in chat.
4. Trim the join so the change happens without camera handling between the two views.
5. Hold the finished shot long enough to see the detail. Try a plain cut before adding a transition.
6. Export and watch the result without pausing to check whether the comparison reads clearly.

## Prompt to try

```
Use the blank canvas clip first and the finished painting clip second. Trim out the camera movement. Cut to the finished piece on the beat of the music I added, and hold that shot for 4 seconds. Keep the whole painting visible.
```

## When to include the middle

A before-and-after video shows the change. A process video explains how it happened. If the important part is one decision in the middle, keep a brief shot of that decision between the two states.

Do not imply a result happened instantly or through a method the footage does not show. Labels such as "before" and "after" can clarify which shot is which, especially when the scene looks similar.

## FAQ

**Do I need a transition effect?**
No. A clear cut between similar frames may show the change better. Add an effect only if it helps the join.

**Can I use more than two clips?**
Yes. Include a brief process moment if it explains the result, then keep the starting and finished states easy to identify.

**Can I add labels?**
Yes. Ask for exact wording and timing, or add text on the timeline. Keep the labels away from the detail being compared.
