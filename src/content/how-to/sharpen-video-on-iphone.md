**In Vyra on iPhone, open the clip’s Adjust and Style controls, or ask chat for a modest sharpening change. Compare edges and small text against the original and inspect the saved result.**

The paint texture may look clearer while a bright outline appears around the frame. Watch for that trade-off before adding more. A recording that was badly out of focus may still need a better source shot.

The [official iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents editing your own media. Vyra’s current Color and the Adjust panel and Effects help sections describe these controls.

## Work through the edit

1. Find a section with the fine detail you want to show.
2. Select the clip and open Adjust and Style, or identify it in chat.
3. Apply a modest sharpening change.
4. Compare fine texture and high-contrast edges with the untreated recording.
5. Check the exported file at a normal viewing size and reduce the treatment if it looks brittle.

## A brief to try

> Try a modest sharpening change on this detail shot. Keep the canvas texture natural and avoid bright outlines around the frame. Leave the other clips unchanged.

## Check the result

The current help documents Sharpen in the Adjust controls. Sharpening changes the appearance of recorded edges. Do not treat the result as recovery of detail the camera never captured. The export-blur guide covers a separate problem with the saved output.

## FAQ

**Will more sharpening always make a video clearer?**
Compare the result with the source. Excess treatment can make edges look harsh without making the useful detail easier to see.

**Is this the same problem as a blurry export?**
No. Inspect the source and preview first. If only the saved output looks worse, review export settings and compare the files separately.
