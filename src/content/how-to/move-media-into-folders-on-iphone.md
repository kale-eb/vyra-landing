**In Vyra on iPhone, press and hold an item in Library, choose Move and select its folder. When selecting several items, the library’s bottom bar also offers Move.**

Keep the footage for one studio visit together before you start choosing the opening. If the folder also contains old exports, label those clearly so you can tell them apart from source recordings.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Library on iPhone”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Open Library and preview the items you intend to organize.
2. Press and hold an item and choose Move.
3. Choose the destination folder shown by the app.
4. For several items, select the intended files and use Move in the bottom bar.
5. Open the destination and check that the expected recordings are there.

## FAQ

**Can I move more than one recording together?**
Vyra help documents a Move action in the library selection bar. Review the selected items before applying it.

**Will a folder tell me which take is best?**
Folders group material. Preview similar takes and mark your choice through a clear name or separate editing brief.
