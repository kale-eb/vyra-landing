**To edit a travel recap on iPhone, build a sequence around the journey rather than every clip you recorded. Add your footage to Vyra, name the places or moments that matter and check the story before choosing music.**

A trip often has many landscape shots and only a few clips that show what it felt like to be there. Keep the details that distinguish one stop from the next, including a short conversation, a sound or an action.

## Work through the edit

1. Group the footage by day or location before choosing the final clips.
2. Choose a beginning that establishes the trip and an ending that closes it.
3. Keep one or two distinct moments from each stop instead of every repeated view.
4. Use brief location labels only where they help someone follow the sequence.
5. Check chronology, sound and framing in the exported recap.

The [official Vyra App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms chat editing, timeline changes, text, audio and export to Photos. Use the brief below as a starting point and check the result before posting.

## Make the important choices

| What you find | Editing choice |
| --- | --- |
| Several views of the same landmark | Keep the one with a different action or detail |
| A spoken moment identifies the place | Preserve its useful sound |
| A wide shot becomes unreadable in portrait | Keep the important view rather than forcing a crop |

## A brief to try

```
Make a recap of the trip in the order we visited the places. Keep the arrival, the market conversation and the final evening view. Remove repeated scenery. Use only the place names I supply.
```

If the recap feels like unrelated postcards, bring back a clip that connects two places. A door opening, a walk or a line about the next stop can supply more continuity than a transition effect.

## FAQ

**Does every location need equal time?**
Give time according to the story and footage. A stop with one useful moment may need less space.

**Can I use photos from part of the trip?**
Yes. Place them where they add information and hold them long enough to see the detail.

**Can the editor infer exact locations reliably?**
Supply and verify the names you want displayed. Do not rely on an automatic guess.
