**To crop a clip in Vyra on iPhone, select it and choose Crop. Vyra adds a rectangle mask that controls the visible area. Check the subject at the start and end of the shot before you keep the crop.**

You filmed a painting on your desk, but a bag is sitting beside it. Crop around the painting while keeping the hand that enters the frame. A crop that looks right on the opening frame can cut off that hand later.

Vyra’s in-app help describes this workflow under “Masks and Editing tools on iPhone”. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents native editing and export.

## Work through the edit

1. Add the clip and find a frame where the subject occupies the most space.
2. Select the clip and choose Crop.
3. Set the visible rectangle around the material you want to show.
4. Scrub through the full shot and check moving hands, faces and objects against each edge.
5. Review the crop in the final canvas and export a short preview.

## A brief to try

> Crop the desk shot to leave out the bag on the right. Keep the entire painting and my hand visible throughout. Leave the clip length and audio alone.

## Check the result

Decide whether you need to hide part of a shot or fill a differently shaped canvas. A mask hides part of the image. You may also need to change the clip size or position to fill the frame.

Keep your source footage while checking the edit. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans are sold through the App Store. Review the saved file in Photos before posting it.

## FAQ

**Will cropping change the video length?**
Cropping changes the visible area. Check the timeline separately if you also need a shorter clip.

**Can a crop follow a moving subject automatically?**
This guide covers a fixed crop. Review the entire shot and adjust the framing when the subject leaves the visible area.
