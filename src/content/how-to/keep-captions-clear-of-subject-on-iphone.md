**To keep captions clear of your subject in Vyra on iPhone, find where the important action happens, place the words outside that area and review every shot after applying a shared caption style.**

Your hands are showing how to tie the knot, and the captions cover the knot. Moving every line to the bottom may fix that shot and hide the finished result in the next one. Choose the position from the footage rather than from a caption preset.

Native release notes document moving or styling a caption and applying it to the whole track. See the [official Vyra App Store listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012).

## Work through the edit

1. Play the whole sequence and note where faces, hands and small details appear.
2. Generate captions after the spoken edit is settled.
3. Move one caption into a clear part of the frame and choose a readable size.
4. Apply the position or style to the track, then inspect shots with a different composition.
5. Check the exported file and the destination app preview for covered details.

## Make the important choices

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Hands work near the bottom | Try captions above the action |
| A face fills the upper frame | Use clear space below the face |
| The composition changes | Review that section instead of assuming one position fits |

## A brief to try

```
Keep the captions away from my hands while I demonstrate the knot. Use the clear space above the table. Keep the full knot visible and leave my spoken words unchanged.
```

## Check the result

Watch once with the sound off and follow the demonstration using the words. Then watch the action without reading. Both should remain understandable. Leave room for the destination app’s buttons and description, whose positions can vary between views.

## FAQ

**Does applying a style check every shot for me?**
A shared style keeps the treatment consistent. Review the placement against each shot yourself.

**Should captions always go at the bottom?**
Use the part of the frame that leaves the important subject visible. Bottom placement can cover a demonstration.

**Can I fix placement after generating captions?**
The native release notes describe moving a caption and applying its position or style to the track.
