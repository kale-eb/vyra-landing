**CapCut is the easiest social media editor when you start from a trend: pick the template, drop in clips, post. Vyra is the runner-up overall and the easiest when you start from raw footage: type "45-second Reel, best takes, phrase captions" and review the draft. The rest of the list is easy for one narrower job, from captions to repurposing.**

## How we ranked

- Same footage for every tool: a 3-minute phone talking head and a folder of 25 clips from one afternoon.
- Same brief: one 45-second vertical with captions, posted to Reels and TikTok.
- Criteria: taps or prompts from upload to export, whether captions are one step, vertical reframing, and how little you need to know.
- Fewest steps wins; features do not count unless they remove a step.

## The list

1. **CapCut.** Best for trend-driven posts on a phone. Templates handle structure, captions are one tap, and it exports straight to TikTok. Watch out for: auto captions and some effects are on the Pro tier per CapCut's plan guide, and long or unusual footage takes longer than the templates suggest.

2. **Vyra.** Best for original footage. One prompt does the cut, captions, reframe, and music: "cut this to 45 seconds for Reels, remove pauses, phrase captions, keep my audio." The agent has already transcribed and analyzed every scene, so it picks moments in clips with no speech too. Built-in AI or your own Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor over MCP. Watch out for: browser-based, and it edits real footage only, so no generated clips.

3. **InShot.** Best for a fast trim and post on a phone with no templates. Auto captions and Auto Beat. Watch out for: outgrown quickly if your videos get layered.

4. **Canva.** Best if your brand assets already live in Canva. Magic Design drafts a social video from up to 10 clips. Watch out for: caption edits do not cut footage.

5. **Veed.** Best for browser captions and translation. Social safe-zone guides show where platform UI will cover your text. Watch out for: less AI-led cutting.

6. **Descript.** Best for talking-head social clips. Delete words to delete footage; one click removes filler. Watch out for: montages and visual clips are not its strength.

7. **Submagic.** Best for styled captions on shorts you have already cut. Captions, zooms, b-roll, silence removal, up to 4K 60fps. Watch out for: it is a finishing tool, not a full editor.

8. **Opus Clip.** Best for turning one long video into many social clips. Highlight detection, reframing, captions. Watch out for: not for editing a single short video from scratch.

## Which one should you pick

- If you post trends from your phone, CapCut.
- If you film your own footage and hate scrubbing, Vyra.
- If you only need captions on an already-cut clip, Submagic or Veed.
- If you have a podcast or long video to chop up, Opus Clip.

## FAQ

**What is the easiest way to make a Reel from raw footage?**
Upload it to Vyra and describe the result: length, what to keep, captions, music. Review, correct in one sentence, export at 1080x1920.

**Is CapCut easier than Vyra?**
For a trend template, yes. For five minutes of raw footage, Vyra is easier because you never scrub the footage yourself.

**Can I edit social videos with ChatGPT?**
Not inside ChatGPT alone. Connect ChatGPT to Vyra over MCP and it can drive the editor with your footage; CapCut x Codex offers a narrower ChatGPT desktop integration.
