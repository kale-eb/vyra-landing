**CapCut is the top pick for TikTok and Instagram Reels because it is built around the formats those platforms reward: templates, trending audio, styled captions, and posting from the app. Vyra is the runner-up, and the better pick when your Reel starts as real footage you need cut, captioned, and reframed from a plain description.**

## How we ranked

- Same footage: a 6-minute studio vlog and a 15-clip product b-roll set.
- Same brief: two 20-second Reels, one with word-level captions and one silent with music, both 9:16.
- Criteria: template and trend support, caption styling, reframing quality, and how fast the first draft appears.
- Direct posting to TikTok and Instagram counted as a plus.

## The list

1. **CapCut.** Best for trend-driven Reels. The template library maps to what is trending, auto captions are one tap, and beat templates handle timing. Watch out for: original, off-template edits take more manual work, and long files are slow.

2. **Vyra.** Best for original edits from real footage. Describe it: "cut a 20-second Reel from the studio vlog, open on the canvas reveal, bold white captions, lo-fi under it." Vyra sees every scene, so it finds visual moments without speech. Reframing to 9:16 uses masks so you keep the subject centered. Watch out for: no phone app; upload from your phone to the browser.

3. **InShot.** Best lightweight mobile editor. Trims, text, stickers, music, and export presets. Watch out for: almost no AI assistance.

4. **VN Video Editor.** Best free mobile editor with a real timeline. Keyframes, curves, and no watermark. Watch out for: mostly manual editing; AI is limited to auto captions and templates.

5. **Submagic.** Best for TikTok-style animated captions on a finished clip. Watch out for: it is not a cutting tool.

6. **Opus Clip.** Best for making Reels from long videos in batches. Watch out for: no control of the source cut.

7. **Descript.** Best for talking-head Reels selected by transcript. Watch out for: visual-first Reels get less help.

8. **Canva.** Best for text-and-graphics Reels, announcements, and carousels turned into video. Watch out for: transcript edits do not cut footage.

## Which one should you pick

- If you ride trends and post from your phone, CapCut.
- If your Reels are your own footage and you want to describe the cut, Vyra.
- If you want a free mobile timeline with no watermark, VN.
- If you have long videos to slice up, Opus Clip.

## FAQ

**Does TikTok penalize CapCut watermarks?**
Instagram has said it deprioritizes visibly reposted, watermarked content. Export clean from any tool and post natively to each platform.

**Can I make a Reel without showing my face?**
Yes. Product b-roll, screen recordings, and text-led edits work. Vyra's scene descriptions help select b-roll moments; Canva helps with text-led designs.

**What size should a Reel be?**
1080 by 1920, 9:16. Keep captions above the bottom 20% so the platform UI does not cover them.
