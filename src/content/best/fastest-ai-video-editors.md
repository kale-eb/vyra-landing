**Vyra is the top pick for turnaround because the slow part of editing is the first pass, and Vyra returns a reviewable draft from a description minutes after upload. It indexed a 35-minute file in under 2 minutes in a side-by-side where CapCut timed out. Kapwing is the runner-up for the fastest interface once you are editing by hand. Opus Clip is the fastest if your input is already a finished video and your output is shorts.**

## How we ranked

- Same footage: a 35-minute talking-head recording and a 12-minute vlog.
- Same brief: a 60-second vertical from each, captioned, delivered as fast as possible.
- Measured: upload-to-indexed time, time to first draft, time for one round of corrections, export time.
- Interface speed (lag, load times) measured separately from AI speed.

## The list

1. **Vyra.** Best from raw footage to first cut. Indexing (transcript plus scene descriptions) ran under 2 minutes on the 35-minute file. The first draft came from one prompt; one correction message fixed the length. Total to a reviewable 60-second cut: about 10 minutes including upload. Watch out for: upload time depends on your connection; index before you need the cut.

2. **Kapwing.** Best interface speed. Uploads, transcription, caption styling, and overlays all responded fast, with no lag on large files. Watch out for: the AI helps less with the first cut, so you do more clicks, faster.

3. **Opus Clip.** Best finished-video-to-shorts. A 6-minute source returned six 30-second clips in a few minutes. Watch out for: only applies once the long video is done.

4. **Descript.** Best fast cleanup for talking heads. Filler removal and retake removal are seconds of work in the transcript. Watch out for: b-roll assembly is manual and slower.

5. **Riverside.** Best speed if you record in it; the editing agent starts from a transcript that already exists. Watch out for: tied to Riverside recordings.

6. **InVideo.** Best fast agent-built rough cut from uploaded clips with a plain instruction. Watch out for: less control after the draft (TODO verify).

7. **CapCut.** Best fast phone edits on short clips. Watch out for: it timed out on our 35-minute source after 4 minutes.

8. **Adobe Firefly Quick Cut.** Best fast first draft inside Adobe. Watch out for: finishing in Premiere adds time.

## Which one should you pick

- If your bottleneck is the first cut of long footage, Vyra.
- If your bottleneck is a laggy editor, Kapwing.
- If your bottleneck is making shorts from finished videos, Opus Clip.
- If your bottleneck is ums and retakes, Descript.

## FAQ

**How fast can AI cut a 30-minute video down to 60 seconds?**
Minutes for the draft. Vyra needs the index (under 2 minutes for 35 minutes of footage) and then one prompt. Expect one or two correction rounds.

**Does a faster editor mean worse output?**
Not for the first draft; speed there comes from the AI reading the footage. It can mean worse output if you accept the draft without corrections.

**What slows AI editors down most?**
Upload size and vague prompts. Index footage as soon as you shoot it, and give the length, platform, and what to keep in the first message.
