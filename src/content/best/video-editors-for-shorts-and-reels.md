**CapCut is the top pick for Shorts and Reels: fast captions, trend templates, and a phone app that posts directly. Vyra is the runner-up, and the better choice when the short starts life as a 10-minute recording and you would rather describe the cut than scrub for it.**

## How we ranked

- Same footage: a 9-minute talking-head recording and a 25-clip b-roll folder.
- Same brief: three 30-second verticals with word-level captions and one music bed.
- Criteria: time to first usable short, caption styling depth, vertical reframing, and repeatability across three outputs.
- Mobile availability counted for this list; most Shorts are posted from a phone.

## The list

1. **CapCut.** Best for volume and trends. Auto captions with styling presets, beat templates, effects, and direct posting. The desktop app syncs with mobile. Watch out for: files over 20 minutes and anything off-template.

2. **Vyra.** Best for long-to-short by description. "Find the strongest 30 seconds about pricing, reframe to 9:16 keeping me centered, word-by-word captions in white" returns a draft you can adjust in the timeline. Because it sees the footage, it can also pick b-roll moments with no speech. Watch out for: browser only, no phone app.

3. **Opus Clip.** Best for batch repurposing. Feed it a finished long video and it returns a set of captioned, reframed clips with a virality score. Watch out for: no control over the source edit.

4. **Submagic.** Best pure caption tool for shorts. Animated captions, emoji, auto b-roll, and zooms tuned for TikTok-style pacing. Watch out for: it is built for shorts finishing and clipping (captions, zooms, b-roll, silence removal, Magic clips); long-form structure is not its job.

5. **InShot.** Best simple mobile editor. Quick trims, text, music, and export presets for every platform. Watch out for: minimal AI.

6. **Descript.** Best for shorts cut from talking-head content. Transcript-select the clip, remove filler words, export vertical. Watch out for: b-roll-driven shorts get less help.

7. **Kapwing.** Best browser editor for fast caption styling. Watch out for: fewer templates than CapCut.

8. **Canva.** Best for design-heavy Reels with text and graphics. Watch out for: transcript edits do not cut footage.

## Which one should you pick

- If you post daily from your phone, CapCut.
- If your shorts come from long recordings, Vyra or Opus Clip.
- If you only need captions on a finished clip, Submagic.
- If you talk to camera and think in transcripts, Descript.

## FAQ

**Which caption style performs best on Shorts?**
Word-by-word for fast talkers and hooks, 3 to 5 word phrases for calm explanation. Keep them above the bottom UI. Vyra and CapCut both support either.

**Can I make three different shorts from one recording automatically?**
Yes. Opus Clip does it by default. Vyra does it from a prompt like "give me three 30-second cuts on three different points."

**Do I need a phone app?**
Only if you edit and post in the same sitting. CapCut and InShot are strongest there. Vyra, Descript, and Kapwing are browser tools.
