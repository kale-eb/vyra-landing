**Vyra is the top pick for reference-based style matching because it treats a reference video as an editing brief: attach one, say what to copy (cut rhythm, caption style, text placement, music behavior), and it applies that to your footage. Adobe Firefly is the runner-up when what you want to match is the look (color, texture, lighting) rather than the edit.**

## How we ranked

- Same reference: a 40-second creator Reel with fast intro cuts, blocky all-caps captions, a music drop at the turn, and a warm grade.
- Same footage: a 6-minute studio vlog.
- Same brief: a 45-second Reel that matches the reference's pacing and text treatment but keeps our content.
- Criteria: how many reference properties the tool could copy, whether it needed manual measurement, and how editable the result was.

## The list

1. **Vyra.** Best for matching the edit itself. "Match the attached reference: same cut rhythm, roughly one cut per 1 to 2 seconds in the intro, same caption font weight and placement, music drops out when I make the point, warm grade. Do not match its length or content." Vyra analyzes the reference and applies those properties to your clips. Say which wins if your instructions and the reference disagree. Watch out for: attaching a reference is not an instruction. Name what to take from it.

2. **Adobe Firefly.** Best for matching a visual look. Style reference and generative tools transfer color, texture, and lighting characteristics. Watch out for: it matches look, not pacing or caption behavior.

3. **Runway.** Best for video-to-video stylization when you want the reference's aesthetic applied frame by frame. Watch out for: generative output, not an edit of your cut.

4. **CapCut.** Best for matching trends via templates, which are effectively references someone else already built. Watch out for: only works if the reference is a template.

5. **Adobe Premiere Pro.** Best for manual matching by a skilled editor: Color Match for grade, then rebuild pacing by hand. Watch out for: everything except color is manual.

6. **DaVinci Resolve.** Best free color match; the Color Match tool and shot match are strong. Watch out for: pacing and text are manual.

7. **Descript.** Best for matching a caption template you saved. Watch out for: cut rhythm and music behavior are not reference-driven.

8. **Kapwing.** Best for reusing a caption style preset across videos. Watch out for: no reference analysis.

## Which one should you pick

- If you want your footage to feel like a video you admire, Vyra.
- If you want it to look like that video (color, texture), Firefly or Resolve.
- If the reference is a trending template, CapCut.
- If you are an editor who can measure pacing by eye, Premiere.

## FAQ

**How do I apply a reference video's style to mine?**
Attach the reference, then list what to copy: pacing, caption style and placement, music behavior, grade. Say what not to copy (length, content). In Vyra that is one prompt; elsewhere it is a manual rebuild.

**Will the AI copy the reference's content?**
Not if you tell it not to. Style matching copies treatment, not footage. Always say "use my footage only."

**What if my instructions conflict with the reference?**
Say which wins. "Where my instructions and the reference disagree, follow my instructions."
