**On a zero budget, Clipchamp (no watermark on the free tier) and CapCut (phone and desktop) are the best starts, and DaVinci Resolve is the best free professional editor if you are willing to learn it. When you are ready to pay, Vyra is the best value AI editor for creators: free to start, and from $24 a month when you bring the Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor subscription you already have. Every tool on this list is free to start, so test before paying.**

## How we ranked

- Same footage for every tool: a 5-minute phone talking head and 30 clips from a weekend.
- Same brief: a 45-second vertical with captions, exported at 1080p or better.
- Criteria: what the free tier lets you actually publish (watermark, resolution, captions), the cost of the first paid tier, and how much editing time the tool saves for that cost.
- Free tiers ranked first; paid value ranked by hours saved per dollar, not by feature count.

## The list

1. **Clipchamp.** Best free tier for a laptop. All the basic AI tools (silence removal, captions, text to speech) and 1080p exports with no watermark. Included with many Microsoft plans. Watch out for: 4K needs Premium; no beat sync.

2. **CapCut.** Best free start on a phone. Templates, trims, effects, and a huge tutorial ecosystem. Watch out for: auto captions and some effects moved to the Pro tier per CapCut's plan guide, so check what is still free.

3. **DaVinci Resolve.** Best free professional editor. Editing, color, audio, and in 21.1 an MCP assistant that can organize media and cut highlights from long-form. Watch out for: the learning curve. Budget in time, not money.

4. **Vyra.** Best paid value for creators who edit real footage. Free to start. MCP Starter is $24 a month if you already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor; Vyra AI Starter is $54 a month with the built-in AI. Both include 6,000 processing credits, 100 GB storage, and 1440p export. The saving is time: a rough cut, captions, reframing, and music from one prompt. Watch out for: browser-based; real footage only.

5. **Descript.** Best value for speech content. Transcript editing and filler removal on the free tier; 720p free exports, 1080p and up on paid. Watch out for: visual-first edits are weaker.

6. **Kapwing.** Best value for speed in a browser. Fast uploads, captions, and MCP for uploads, subtitles, dubbing, and exports. Watch out for: 720p free exports, 4K paid.

7. **Veed.** Best value for captions and translation. Watch out for: 720p free, 1080p and up on paid, no beat sync.

8. **iMovie.** Best free start on a Mac. Watch out for: outgrown fast.

## Which one should you pick

- If you cannot spend anything and use Windows, Clipchamp.
- If you cannot spend anything and edit on a phone, CapCut.
- If you have time but no money and want pro skills, DaVinci Resolve.
- If you already pay for Claude or ChatGPT and want your editing hours back, Vyra MCP Starter at $24 a month.

## FAQ

**Is there a completely free AI video editor?**
Free tiers with AI features exist (Clipchamp, CapCut, Descript). The AI that saves the most time, a rough cut from a description on your footage, is generally paid. Vyra is free to start so you can test it on real footage.

**What is the cheapest way to get AI editing?**
If you already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor, Vyra's MCP Starter reuses that subscription for $24 a month. That is usually the lowest total cost for description-based editing.

**Is DaVinci Resolve really free?**
Yes, the standard version is free and very capable. Studio is a one-time purchase. The cost is the weeks it takes to learn.
