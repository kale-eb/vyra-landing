**Riverside alternatives depend on which half you need. For recording remote guests, look at other studio tools. For editing and clipping what you recorded, Vyra edits real footage from a description, Descript edits by transcript, and Opus Clip or Vizard clip talks at volume.**

## Why people look for a Riverside alternative

- You do not record remotely, so you are paying for a studio you never use.
- The editing agent works from the transcript and does not see visual moments.
- The editor is fine for trims and clips, thin for anything with b-roll, graphics, or a visual story.
- Plan pricing for occasional use (see site).

## The alternatives

1. **Vyra.** For editing anything you recorded, anywhere. Strengths: transcribed and scene-indexed footage, full edit from a description or from Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor over MCP, captions, b-roll placement, motion graphics, reference matching, full timeline. Limitation: no remote recording.

2. **Descript.** For transcript-first editing of talk. Strengths: text editing, filler removal, Studio Sound, multicam. Limitation: no visual understanding, basic recording.

3. **Opus Clip.** For clipping long talks into shorts at volume. Strengths: scored clips, reframing, MCP server. Limitation: clips only.

4. **Vizard.** For the same clipping job, often cheaper (see site). Strengths: scored clips, simple flow. Limitation: clips only, no MCP.

5. **Zoom or your camera plus an editor.** For recording. Strengths: you already have it. Limitation: audio quality depends on the connection, and you still need an editor.

6. **Kapwing.** For a browser editor with many tools. Strengths: fast, team features. Limitation: manual, no agent that plans the edit.

## How to switch

1. Keep recording wherever your guests are comfortable. Export the separate tracks.
2. Upload the tracks to Vyra and wait for transcription and scene indexing.
3. Ask for the full episode edit and the clips in one conversation.

```
Edit this two-person interview to a 15-minute YouTube cut. Switch to whoever is speaking, remove tangents that do not serve the main question, and add a lower third for each guest the first time they speak. Then pull three 45-second 9:16 clips with phrase captions and tell me why you picked each.
```

## FAQ

**Which alternative records remote guests as well as Riverside?**
None of the editors here. Keep a recording tool and pair it with the editor you prefer.

**Which alternative can see the video, not only the transcript?**
Vyra describes every scene on upload.

**Which is cheapest for occasional podcasts?**
Descript's free tier for light use, Vizard for clipping. Vyra is free to start.
