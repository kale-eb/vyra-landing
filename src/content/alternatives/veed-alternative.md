**VEED alternatives split by need. Vyra if you want an AI to edit your real footage in the browser. Kapwing or Clipchamp if you want a similar simple browser editor. Descript if you edit by transcript. Runway if what you actually wanted was generated video.**

## Why people look for a VEED alternative

- Editing ceiling. Basic text animations, no keyframing, no custom graphics.
- The MCP only generates clips. It does not connect to the editor, so an external AI cannot touch footage you shot.
- Browser performance on long videos.
- Paid tier pricing relative to how much editing you get (check current plans).

## The alternatives

1. **Vyra.** For creators who want the AI to do the edit. Strengths: transcribed and scene-indexed footage, full edit from a description or reference video, custom motion graphics, MCP for Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor. Limitation: no AI avatars or text-to-video.

2. **Kapwing.** For a similar browser editor with more utilities. Strengths: many one-click AI tools; team features. Limitation: manual editing, no agent.

3. **Clipchamp.** For Windows users who want free and simple. Strengths: built into Windows; screen recording. Limitation: basic timeline and templates.

4. **Descript.** For talk-heavy content. Strengths: transcript editing; audio cleanup. Limitation: no visual understanding.

5. **Runway.** If the draw was generated clips. Strengths: text and image to video; generative effects. Limitation: light editor for real footage.

6. **CapCut.** For template-driven short clips. Strengths: mobile and desktop; big template library. Limitation: no external AI, weak on long files.

## How to switch

1. Export finished VEED projects as video files.
2. Upload raw footage to Vyra and wait for indexing.
3. Ask for what VEED's text animations could not do.

```
Add captions in 3-5 word phrases, bold white with a thin outline, lower third. When I list the three tools, animate their names on screen one at a time as I say them, stacked on the left, and clear them together when I finish. Duck the music under my voice.
```

## FAQ

**Which VEED alternative has AI avatars?**
None of the editors listed here. Vyra edits real footage only.

**Does any alternative let Claude or ChatGPT edit the video?**
Vyra, through MCP, with the agent holding the editing tools.

**Which is closest to VEED's simplicity?**
Clipchamp and Kapwing. Vyra is simple in a different way. One sentence instead of a toolbar.
