**Vlog is for footage shot across a day. The agent uses location and time changes as the structure, cuts b-roll to the music, keeps the talking clips that move the story, and turns the track down whenever you speak.**

## What the template tells the agent

- Structure. Chronological, using recording times. Location changes become sections. Optional title card or spoken hook at the start.
- Pacing. Fast through movement and b-roll, slower when you're talking. Around two to four seconds a shot on short vlogs (TODO confirm defaults).
- Captions. Phrases during speech only. None over b-roll.
- B-roll. Anything that isn't a talking clip. Cut to the beat and ordered to match what you say.
- Ending. Last location, last line, short music tail.

## Prompts that work with it

```
Cut a first draft of this day from all the clips, about 60 seconds, 9:16, to the attached track. Keep me talking where I explain what we're doing. Everything else is b-roll on the beat.
```

```
Add a title card at the start that says "day four in SF" in the title font, and a small city label when the location changes.
```

```
Make it calmer. Longer shots, fewer cuts in the middle, and drop the music to 20% during the conversation at the table.
```

## When to pick a different template

- Mostly one person talking to camera. Use [/templates/short-talking-head](/templates/short-talking-head).
- Two people in conversation most of the time. Use [/templates/long-podcast](/templates/long-podcast).
- A montage with no speech. Vlog works. Say "no captions, cut everything to the beat."

## FAQ

**Does it need music?**
No. Say "original audio only."

**Will it reorder my day?**
Not unless you ask.

**Can I use it for a multi-day trip?**
Yes. Upload all the days and ask for a title card per day.
