**Submagic adds captions and polish to short clips you have already cut. Vyra cuts the clip from your raw footage and then captions it, so it wins when the work starts before the clip exists.**

Submagic got popular for one thing. Drop in a finished short, get animated captions, emojis, b-roll suggestions, and zooms back in a minute. That is a finishing step. Vyra covers the finishing step and the hours before it, when you still have twelve minutes of raw footage and no clip.

## At a glance

| | Submagic | Vyra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edits your real footage | Polishes clips you upload | Yes, agent-driven from raw footage |
| Edit by chat / natural language | No, presets and toggles | Yes, full edit by chat |
| Bring your own AI (MCP: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) | No | Yes, agent gets the full editing toolset |
| Understands footage (transcript + scene analysis) | Transcript for captions | Transcript plus every scene described |
| Captions | Yes, its core strength, animated templates | Yes, word-by-word or phrases, styled by prompt |
| Beat sync | No | Yes |
| Motion graphics | Emojis, stickers, zooms | Custom, built from a prompt |
| Reference style matching | No | Yes |
| Manual timeline | Light trimming | Yes, full editor |
| Export cap | Up to 4K at 60fps | 1440p (AI Starter), 4K (AI Pro) |
| Best for | Captioning finished shorts fast | Cutting and finishing from raw footage |

## Where Submagic is stronger

- Caption templates. Dozens of animated styles that match what is trending.
- Speed on the last step. Upload a clip, pick a template, export.
- Auto b-roll and emoji suggestions for talking-head shorts.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- It makes the clip. Rough cut, take selection, silence removal, and reframing from a description.
- Captions styled by description or by copying a reference video, not only from a template list.
- A full timeline when you want to adjust anything after.

## Choose Vyra if

- You start from raw footage, not finished clips.
- You want captions plus the cut, the b-roll, and the music in one conversation.
- You want to drive it from Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor.

## Choose Submagic if

- Your clips are already cut and you only need captions and polish.
- You want a trending caption template in two clicks.
- You batch-caption many finished shorts a week.

## Try the same edit in both

Same task for both. A 30-second captioned short from a 6-minute recording.

In Submagic you cut the 30 seconds somewhere else first, upload, pick a caption template, export.

In Vyra:
```
Cut this 6-minute recording to the best 30 seconds for TikTok. Open on the strongest line. Remove pauses and restarts. Word-by-word captions, bold, white with a yellow highlight on the current word, positioned above the bottom UI. Punch in 15% on sentence breaks.
```

## FAQ

**Are Vyra's captions as animated as Submagic's?**
Vyra does word-by-word and phrase captions with emphasis words and entrance animation on request. Submagic has a bigger library of preset caption effects.

**Can I use Vyra only for captions?**
Yes. Upload a finished clip and ask for captions. It is more than you need if that is all you do.

**Which is cheaper?**
Vyra starts at $24/mo and is free to start. Submagic pricing is on its site.
