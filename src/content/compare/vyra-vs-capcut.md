**CapCut is faster for 15-second template clips on your phone. Vyra is better when you have a pile of real footage and want it cut from a description.**

CapCut is a normal editor with AI features added on. Auto captions, background removal, a big template library. You still do the editing yourself. In Vyra you describe the edit and the AI does the cutting. It has already transcribed your footage and looked at every scene, so it knows what it is working with.

## At a glance

| | CapCut | Vyra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edits your real footage | Yes, manual timeline | Yes, agent-driven |
| Edit by chat / natural language | No | Yes, built-in chat |
| Bring your own AI (MCP: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) | Partial, CapCut x Codex (ChatGPT desktop only) | Yes, Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor |
| Understands footage (transcript + scene analysis) | No, transcript for captions only | Yes, every scene described and searchable |
| Captions | Yes, built-in auto-captions | Yes, word-by-word or phrases |
| Beat sync | Yes, auto beat templates | Yes, cut to any uploaded track |
| Motion graphics | Templates only | Custom, built from a prompt |
| Reference style matching | No | Yes |
| Manual timeline | Yes | Yes, full editor |
| Export cap | High-res on free and Pro (CapCut lists up to 8K) | 1440p (AI Starter), 4K (AI Pro) |
| Platform | Mobile and desktop apps | Browser |
| Best for | Quick trend edits from templates | AI-edited real footage, long-to-short |

## Where CapCut is stronger

- Templates. If a trending sound already has a cut pattern, tapping a template is faster than describing it.
- Phone workflow. Shoot, edit, and post without a computer.
- The free tier covers basic cuts, captions, and transitions.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- Long footage. On the same 35-minute file, CapCut timed out after 4 minutes. Vyra indexed the whole thing in under 2 minutes.
- Custom motion graphics. Describe a lower third or an animated list and it gets built.
- Reference matching. Attach a video you like and say "match this pacing and caption style." CapCut has nothing like it.

## Choose Vyra if

- You film long and need short.
- You want to use the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already pay for.
- You need graphics that are not a preset.

## Choose CapCut if

- You edit 15-second trend clips on your phone.
- A template already exists for what you want.
- You would rather tap than type.

## Try the same edit in both

Same task for both. Turn a 6-minute talking head into a 45-second Reel.

In CapCut you scrub the timeline, split at every pause, delete the gaps, run auto captions, pick a caption style, add a sound.

In Vyra:
```
Cut this 6-minute talking head into a 45-second vertical Reel. Keep the strongest take of each point, remove pauses, retakes, and filler words. Add 3-5 word captions, bold white, lower third. Put a calm lo-fi track under it at 25% while I'm speaking.
```

## FAQ

**Can I use CapCut with Claude or ChatGPT?**
Only through CapCut x Codex on the ChatGPT desktop app. There is no Claude path.

**Does Vyra have templates like CapCut?**
No. Vyra builds each edit from your footage and your description, or from a reference video you attach.

**Is Vyra overkill for a 15-second clip?**
Often, yes. If a CapCut template already matches what you want, use it. Vyra is worth it when the footage is long or the look is custom.
