**Vyra and CapCut both edit footage you supply. Choose Vyra when you want to describe an edit, use an Instagram or TikTok reference on iPhone, or connect an external AI through MCP. Choose CapCut when its templates or desktop editing tools fit the job. Both now offer ways to edit from written instructions.**

The useful comparison starts with your footage and the device you want to use. A short phone edit and a folder of clips you want Claude to work through are different jobs. Try a small piece of your actual material before deciding which workflow fits.

CapCut's [official EditPilot page](https://www.capcut.com/tools/edit-pilot) describes instruction-driven editing inside CapCut for PC. Its [CapCut × Codex guide](https://www.capcut.com/tools/capcut-x-codex) documents a connected workflow in ChatGPT's desktop app on PC. Availability can depend on version, account and region.

## Editing on iPhone

Vyra has a native [iPhone app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012). Add clips from Photos, ask for the edit, then refine the timeline yourself when needed. You can generate captions, adjust audio and save the exported video to Photos.

[Caleb's mobile release post](https://www.usevyra.com/blog/vyra-mobile) documents sharing an Instagram Reel or TikTok into Vyra as a reference. Tell the app which part to follow, such as pacing or caption placement, and keep your own footage as the subject.

CapCut's desktop EditPilot documentation describes a PC workflow. Check the app you intend to use rather than assuming every desktop feature is present on the phone.

## Choose Vyra if

- You want to describe a draft made from your own clips and keep refining it in chat.
- You want a documented iPhone workflow for sharing a reference Reel or TikTok.
- You want Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client to inspect and edit a Vyra project.

## Choose CapCut if

- A CapCut template already gives you the structure you want.
- You prefer its desktop timeline or want to try EditPilot for supported cleanup tasks.
- You already have a CapCut workflow that gets your videos finished reliably.

## Try the same small edit

Use the same supplied talking clip, target duration and caption wording. Compare the retained meaning, time you spend correcting the draft and the final file. A useful trial includes the review work after the first result.

```
Make a 45-second vertical edit from this talking clip. Keep the complete explanation and the important qualification at the end. Remove unnecessary pauses, add readable phrase captions and keep my original voice. Show me the draft before changing the music.
```

## FAQ

**Does Vyra have an iPhone app?**
Yes. Vyra is available for iPhone as well as web. New mobile accounts get 100 free credits, and free mobile exports are 720p with a watermark. There is no Android app.

**Can CapCut edit from a written instruction?**
Yes. CapCut documents EditPilot for supported tasks in its PC editor and a separate CapCut × Codex workflow. Check the current availability for your device and account.

**Does Vyra have templates?**
Yes. The current iPhone listing describes starting from a template or using saved reference videos. You can also describe an edit using your own footage.

**Is the free iPhone plan the same as the web trial?**
No. iPhone accounts receive free credits with watermarked 720p exports. Web has a three-day trial and then requires a paid plan.
