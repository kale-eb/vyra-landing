**Captions is a phone app that polishes talking-head shorts with AI captions, zooms, b-roll, and eye contact. Vyra runs on web and iPhone, where an AI cuts the whole video from raw footage and gives you a full timeline.**

Captions (captions.ai) is the phone-in-hand tool. Record or import a short clip, tap AI Edit, and it adds captions, zooms, b-roll, sound effects, and can fix your eye line. Vyra has a native iPhone app and a web editor. Upload everything you shot, describe the video, and the agent builds it. Then you finish in a timeline if you want.

## Where Captions is stronger

- Phone workflow. Shoot, edit, and post without a computer.
- Eye contact correction and other face-aware fixes.
- Preset styles that match current short-form trends.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- Long, messy footage. It picks takes and builds structure instead of decorating one clip.
- Visual search across everything you uploaded.
- Custom graphics, reference matching, and a real timeline.
- Works from Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor.

## Choose Vyra if

- Your video starts as a pile of clips.
- You want to describe the structure, captions, and music in one message.
- You edit at a desk and want a timeline to fall back on.

## Choose Captions if

- You shoot and post from your phone and want polish in a minute.
- Your content is single-take talking heads.
- You want eye contact correction and preset styles.

## Try the same edit in both

Same task for both. A 30-second talking-head short with captions and a b-roll insert.

In Captions you import the take, tap AI Edit, choose a style, adjust, export.

In Vyra:
```
Cut this take to the best 30 seconds for TikTok. Remove pauses and restarts. Word-by-word captions, bold white with a yellow current-word highlight, above the bottom UI zone. When I mention the sketchbook, cut to the sketchbook clip for 2 seconds and back. Punch in 15% at sentence breaks.
```

## FAQ

**Can Vyra fix eye contact?**
No. Captions has that. Vyra edits what the camera saw.

**Is there a Vyra mobile app?**
Yes. Vyra has a native iPhone app as well as web. New iPhone accounts get 100 free credits, with free 720p watermarked exports. There is no Android app.

**Which is cheaper?**
Check Captions pricing on its site. Vyra’s web plans have a three-day trial. Vyra’s iPhone app has separate free credits and Plus/Pro plans through the App Store.

## Use Vyra on iPhone or web

Vyra has a [native iPhone app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) as well as the web editor. [Caleb’s mobile release post](https://www.usevyra.com/blog/vyra-mobile) documents the app and reference-sharing workflow. New mobile accounts get 100 free credits, with free 720p watermarked exports. Web has a three-day trial followed by a paid plan. There is no Android app. Platform information checked November 15, 2026.
