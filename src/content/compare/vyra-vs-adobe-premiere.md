**Premiere Pro is the professional NLE with the deepest manual toolset; Vyra is the faster route from raw footage to a finished social edit, because an AI does the first 80% from a description and you polish the rest on a timeline.**

These are different categories. Premiere is what broadcast editors, agencies, and film students use: multicam, nested sequences, Lumetri color, After Effects round-tripping. Its AI features (text-based editing, auto-reframe, generative extend) assist a human editor. Vyra flips that: the AI is the editor, you are the director, and a full timeline exists for when you want hands on.

## At a glance

| | Premiere Pro | Vyra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edits your real footage | Yes, professional NLE | Yes, agent-driven |
| Edit by chat / natural language | Partial, text-based editing on transcript | Yes, full edit by chat |
| Bring your own AI (MCP: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) | No | Yes |
| Understands footage (transcript + scene analysis) | Transcript, scene edit detection | Transcript plus every scene described |
| Captions | Yes, speech to text | Yes, word-by-word or phrases |
| Beat sync | Manual, or via plugins | Yes |
| Motion graphics | Yes, via Essential Graphics and After Effects | Custom, built from a prompt |
| Reference style matching | No | Yes |
| Manual timeline | Yes, industry standard | Yes, full editor |
| Export cap | 8K and beyond | 1440p (AI Starter), 4K (AI Pro) |
| Platform | Desktop app | Browser |
| Best for | Professional and long-form production | Fast social edits from real footage |

## Where Premiere Pro is stronger

- Depth. Multicam, advanced color, audio mixing, effects, and plugins have no equal in a browser tool.
- After Effects integration for any motion graphic you can imagine.
- Output formats, codecs, and delivery specs for broadcast and film.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- Speed to first cut. "Cut this to 60 seconds, best takes, captions" is done while Premiere is still importing.
- No learning curve. Users go from upload to export in minutes on day one.
- Your AI subscription drives it. Claude or ChatGPT can search footage, build the timeline, and check its own frames.

## Choose Vyra if

- You make social content weekly and editing time is the bottleneck.
- You do not want to learn an NLE to get a clean talking head with captions.
- You want to describe a graphic instead of building it.

## Choose Premiere Pro if

- You deliver for broadcast, film, or agency clients.
- You need multicam, advanced color, or plugin ecosystems.
- You already know it and edit long-form for a living.

## Try the same edit in both

Task: turn a 6-minute talking head into a 45-second Reel.

In Premiere: import, create a sequence, text-based edit to delete pauses, ripple-delete, auto-reframe to 9:16, add captions, style them, export.

In Vyra:
```
Make a 45-second 9:16 Reel from this talking head. Keep the clearest take of each point, cut pauses over half a second and all filler. Keep me centered. Phrase captions, bold white, lower third. Straight cuts only.
```

## FAQ

**Can Vyra replace Premiere for professional work?**
For short social edits, yes. For broadcast delivery, multicam, or heavy color work, Premiere remains the tool.

**Can I use Vyra's output in Premiere?**
Yes. Export from Vyra and import the file like any other clip.

**Does Premiere have anything like MCP?**
No. Its AI features are built in and not driven by an external agent.

**Is Vyra's timeline as capable as Premiere's?**
No, and it is not trying to be. It covers trims, transitions, effects, masks, keyframes, and captions, which is enough to polish an AI-built edit.
