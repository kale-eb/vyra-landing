**Firefly Quick Cut is Adobe's one-shot AI first draft inside the Adobe ecosystem; Vyra is a conversational editor you keep talking to after the first draft, with footage search, reference matching, custom motion graphics, and your own AI over MCP.**

Adobe added Quick Cut to the Firefly video editor in February 2026. You upload raw footage, give a short brief (type of video, pacing, duration), and it generates an edited first draft. It is Adobe's answer to prompt-first editing and it comes with Adobe's generative tools and brand trust. Vyra starts from the same idea, a first draft from a description, and then makes the conversation the editor: search the footage, correct the cut, restyle captions, build a graphic, match a reference, all by describing what you want.

## At a glance

| | Adobe Firefly (Quick Cut) | Vyra |

## Where Firefly is stronger

- Adobe ecosystem. Quick Cut output moves into Premiere Pro for finishing, and Creative Cloud assets, fonts, and brand kits are already there.
- Generative video. Extending a shot, filling a gap, or generating b-roll is native. Vyra does not generate footage.
- Trust and support. Adobe's commercial-safety positioning matters to brands and agencies.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- Iteration. The first draft is the start of the conversation, not the end. "Tighter middle, swap the intro, make that line a big caption" are three messages, not a manual re-edit.
- Footage search. "Find every moment I mention the deadline" works because every word and scene is indexed.
- Reference matching and custom graphics. Attach a video you like and copy its pacing and captions; describe a lower third and get it built.
- Your own AI. Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor can drive the editor over MCP and check frames of the result.

## Choose Vyra if

- You want to keep directing after the first draft without opening an NLE.
- You need to find moments inside long footage by what was said or shown.
- You already pay for Claude or ChatGPT and want it to edit video.

## Choose Firefly if

- Your team lives in Creative Cloud and finishes in Premiere.
- You need generative extends or fills on real footage.
- Brand policy requires Adobe's content credentials and indemnification.

## Try the same edit in both

Task: turn a 6-minute talking head into a 45-second Reel.

In Firefly: upload, write a brief (social clip, fast pacing, 45 seconds), generate the Quick Cut, then adjust in the Firefly editor or send to Premiere for caption styling and trims.

In Vyra:
```
Make a 45-second 9:16 Reel from this talking head. Open on the line "this is the part everyone skips", keep the clearest take of each point, cut pauses over half a second and all filler. Phrase captions, bold white, lower third. Then show me frames at 0:03 and 0:30.
```

## FAQ

**Is Firefly Quick Cut a full editor?**
It produces a first draft inside the Firefly video editor. Adobe describes it as a starting point you shape and refine afterward.

**Does Vyra generate video?**
No. Vyra edits the footage you upload. If you need generated clips, make them elsewhere and upload them.

**Can I use both?**
Yes. Generate b-roll in Firefly, upload it to Vyra with your real footage, and cut by conversation.

**Which is faster to a first draft?**
Both produce a first draft in minutes. The difference is what happens next: Vyra keeps the conversation going; Firefly hands you a timeline.
