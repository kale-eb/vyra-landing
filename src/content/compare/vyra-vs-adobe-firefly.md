**Firefly Quick Cut makes one AI first draft inside Adobe's editor. Vyra keeps taking instructions after the first draft, with footage search, reference matching, custom graphics, and your own AI over MCP.**

Adobe added Quick Cut to the Firefly video editor in February 2026. You upload footage, give a short brief (type of video, pacing, length), and it makes an edited first draft. Vyra starts the same way and then keeps going. Search the footage, fix the cut, restyle captions, build a graphic, match a reference, all by describing what you want.

## At a glance

| | Adobe Firefly (Quick Cut) | Vyra |

## Where Firefly is stronger

- Adobe ecosystem. The draft moves into Premiere Pro, and your Creative Cloud fonts and brand kits are already there.
- Generative video. Extending a shot or generating b-roll is built in. Vyra does not generate footage.
- Adobe's commercial-safety terms, which matter to brands and agencies.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- Iteration. "Tighter middle, swap the intro, make that line a big caption" is three messages, not a manual re-edit.
- Footage search. "Find every moment I mention the deadline" works because every word and scene is indexed.
- Reference matching and custom graphics from a description.
- Your own AI. Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor can drive the editor and check frames of the result.

## Choose Vyra if

- You want to keep directing after the first draft without opening Premiere.
- You need to find moments in long footage by what was said or shown.
- You already pay for Claude or ChatGPT and want it to edit video.

## Choose Firefly if

- Your team lives in Creative Cloud and finishes in Premiere.
- You need generative extends or fills on real footage.
- Brand policy requires Adobe's content credentials.

## Try the same edit in both

Same task for both. Turn a 6-minute talking head into a 45-second Reel.

In Firefly you upload, write a brief (social clip, fast pacing, 45 seconds), generate the Quick Cut, then adjust in the Firefly editor or in Premiere.

In Vyra:
```
Make a 45-second 9:16 Reel from this talking head. Open on the line "this is the part everyone skips", keep the clearest take of each point, cut pauses over half a second and all filler. Phrase captions, bold white, lower third. Then show me frames at 0:03 and 0:30.
```

## FAQ

**Is Firefly Quick Cut a full editor?**
It makes a first draft inside the Firefly video editor. Adobe calls it a starting point you refine afterward.

**Does Vyra generate video?**
No. It edits the footage you upload. If you need generated clips, make them elsewhere and upload them.

**Can I use both?**
Yes. Generate b-roll in Firefly, upload it to Vyra with your real footage, and cut by conversation.
