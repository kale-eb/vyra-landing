**VEED is a simple browser editor for subtitles and trims, with AI avatars and generative clips on the side; Vyra is a browser editor where an AI agent edits your real footage end to end, including custom graphics and reference matching.**

Both run in a browser tab. The difference is what the AI is allowed to do. VEED's AI helps with subtitles, background removal, avatars, and generating short synthetic clips. It does not understand your footage or make cuts. Vyra's AI reads the transcript and every scene, then builds the edit. VEED put AI in a browser. Vyra put AI in the driver's seat.

## Where VEED is stronger

- AI avatars. If you need a synthetic presenter, VEED has one and Vyra does not.
- Text-to-video generation via its Fabric model.
- Very low learning curve for a first-time editor doing a trim and subtitles.

## Where Vyra is stronger

- The agent edits. VEED's MCP only generates new clips; it cannot touch footage you shot. Vyra's MCP gives Claude or ChatGPT the timeline.
- Motion graphics beyond fade, slide, and bounce. Describe a branded lower third and it gets built.
- Reference matching: show a video, get its pacing and text treatment applied to yours.

## Choose Vyra if

- You have real footage and want it edited, not decorated.
- You want more than preset text animations.
- You want to bring the AI subscription you already have.

## Choose VEED if

- You need an AI avatar or a generated clip.
- The job is subtitles plus a trim, nothing more.
- You are on a machine where a very simple UI matters most.

## Try the same edit in both

Task: subtitle a 3-minute clip and cut it down to 60 seconds.

In VEED: run auto-subtitles, then manually split and delete sections on the timeline.

In Vyra:
```
Cut this to the best 60 seconds. Keep the intro line and the demo, drop the tangent about pricing. Add phrase captions, white with a thin black outline, lower third. Straight cuts, no transitions.
```

## FAQ

**Does VEED's MCP let AI agents edit video?**
No. It connects to VEED's generative model to create short synthetic clips. It does not expose the editor.

**Can VEED understand what is visually in my video?**
It detects faces for background removal and eye-contact correction, and transcribes audio. It does not describe scenes.

**Is Vyra browser-only like VEED?**
Yes. Both run in the browser. Vyra also accepts connections from desktop AI clients over MCP.

**Which has better captions?**
Both auto-caption well. Vyra lets you specify grouping, emphasis words, and placement in plain language, and can copy a reference video's caption style.
