**Vyra makes the rough cut for you. Upload the raw footage, say the target length and platform, what to keep and what to cut, and it assembles a first draft from the best takes with pauses and retakes removed. A 35-minute file indexes in under 2 minutes. The first cut arrives minutes after that, and every correction is one sentence.**

The rough cut is where editing time goes: watching everything, choosing takes, hunting the moment. Vyra has already watched it.

## How it works

1. Upload the footage. Every clip is transcribed with timestamps and every scene is visually described.
2. Ask for a plan if the footage is long: "propose a 60-second structure with timestamps."
3. Describe the cut: length, platform, what opens, what to keep, what to remove, how tight.
4. Vyra selects takes, trims pauses, orders the clips, and places them on the timeline.
5. Correct one thing per message, then move on to captions, music, and graphics.

## What you can ask for

| You say | Vyra does |
| --- | --- |
| "Cut this 8 minutes to 60 seconds for Reels" | Hits the length from the strongest material |
| "Best take of each point, in the order I filmed" | Compares takes and preserves structure |
| "Remove pauses over half a second and every retake" | Threshold-based cleanup |
| "Open on the line 'nobody tells you this'" | Pins the hook |
| "Do not touch 1:20 to 1:35, that pause is intentional" | Protects a range |
| "List every cut you made with timestamps" | Reports the edit for verification |

## Prompts to paste

```
Take this 8-minute raw video down to 60 seconds for Instagram. Use the best takes of each point, cut pauses, stumbles, retakes, and any moment I look at my notes. Keep it in the order I filmed it. No captions or on-screen text yet.
```

```
Make a 60-second vertical rough cut for Reels. Open on the line "nobody tells you this about commissions", then the three main points in order, then end on the shot where I hold up the finished piece. Prefer takes where I sound confident and do not restart a sentence. Remove pauses longer than half a second. Straight cuts only.
```

```
This is a 35-minute recording. First, summarize it scene by scene. Then propose three possible 60-second cuts with different hooks. Wait for me to pick one before you build it.
```

## Limits

- The agent's judgment on "best take" is good, not perfect. Quote the line you want if it matters.
- Vague prompts ("edit this") give vague cuts. Give length, platform, keep, cut.
- Verify before trusting a summary. Ask for the timestamped list of cuts.

## FAQ

**How long from upload to first cut?**
Indexing takes minutes, then the cut arrives minutes after your prompt. Most users go from upload to export in under 5 minutes on short footage.

**Will it reorder my points?**
Only if you ask. Say "keep my order" and it does.

**Can I protect a section?**
Yes. "Do not touch 1:20 to 1:35" keeps that range intact.

**What if it says it removed something and did not?**
Ask for the list of cuts with timestamps and compare. Then say exactly what to remove.
