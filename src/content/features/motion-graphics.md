**Vyra builds motion graphics from a description. Say "animate the three tool names on screen one at a time as I say them, stacked on the left, white on a dark bar" and it creates that graphic, timed to the transcript. Lower thirds, kinetic type, callouts, accumulate-and-release compositions. No template library to scroll, no After Effects.**

Templates give you someone else's graphic. A prompt gives you yours, in your fonts and colors, timed to what you actually said.

## How it works

1. Upload footage. The transcript gives the agent word-level timing to sync graphics to.
2. Describe the graphic: what it shows, where it sits, when it appears, how it moves, what it looks like.
3. Vyra builds it as a native composition on the timeline, editable afterward.
4. Check a frame. "Show me 0:12" captures the composed canvas.
5. Adjust in one line: "bigger", "under my chin not over my face", "clear them all together at the end".

## Prompts to paste

```
When I list the three tools, animate the three names on screen one at a time as I say them, stacked on the left side, bold Montserrat, white on a dark bar. Keep them all on screen until I finish the list, then clear them together.
```

```
Add a lower third with "Sulan Zhang" and "@sulansart" for the first 4 seconds. Simple slide in from the left, small, bottom left corner, white text, no box.
```

```
Make the line "this is the part everyone skips" a large composed caption: words appear as I say them, stay together, then clear. Use the title font, white on the footage, positioned in the upper third so my face stays clear.
```

## Limits

- The agent works from the fonts in Vyra's catalog. Default title face is Montserrat Black; name another if you want it.
- Complex character animation or 3D is out of scope. This is typography, shapes, callouts, and layout.
- Ask for a frame capture after each graphic. Text placement is the thing most worth checking.

## FAQ

**Do I need to know After Effects terms?**
No. "Slide in from the left, small, bottom corner" is enough. Use technical terms if you have them.

**Can it match a graphic from a reference video?**
Yes. Attach the reference and name what to copy: "same blocky title style with the glow and drop shadow."

**Are the graphics editable after?**
Yes. They are native items on the timeline with editable text, position, and timing.

**Can I get the same graphic on every video?**
Describe it once, then reuse the prompt. Over MCP, an agent can apply it across a batch.
