**Say "animate the three tool names on screen one at a time as I say them, stacked on the left, white on a dark bar" and Vyra builds that graphic, timed to your transcript. Lower thirds, big type, callouts, lists that build up and clear. No template library, no After Effects.**

A template gives you someone else's graphic. A prompt gives you yours, in your fonts, timed to what you actually said.

## How it works

1. Upload footage. The transcript gives the agent word-level timing.
2. Describe the graphic. What it shows, where it sits, when it appears, how it moves, what it looks like.
3. Vyra builds it on the timeline. You can edit it after.
4. Check a frame. "Show me 0:12."
5. Adjust in one line. "Bigger." "Under my chin, not over my face."

## Prompts to paste

```
When I list the three tools, animate the three names on screen one at a time as I say them, stacked on the left side, bold Montserrat, white on a dark bar. Keep them all on screen until I finish the list, then clear them together.
```

```
Add a lower third with "Sulan Zhang" and "@sulansart" for the first 4 seconds. Slide in from the left, small, bottom left corner, white text, no box.
```

```
Make the line "this is the part everyone skips" a large caption. Words appear as I say them, stay together, then clear. Title font, white, upper third so my face stays clear.
```

## Limits

- The agent uses the fonts in Vyra's catalog. Default title face is Montserrat Black.
- No character animation or 3D. This is type, shapes, callouts, and layout.
- Check a frame after each graphic. Text placement is the thing that most often needs a nudge.

## FAQ

**Do I need to know After Effects terms?**
No. "Slide in from the left, small, bottom corner" is enough.

**Can it match a graphic from a reference video?**
Yes. Attach it and name what to copy. "Same blocky title with the glow and drop shadow."

**Are the graphics editable after?**
Yes. Text, position, and timing.
