**Give it the four beats (setup, tension, turn, payoff) and the line that starts each one. Editors default to chronological. A story often needs the ending first. You have to say so.**

Minimal:
```
Cut this into a 60-second story. Start with the setup, build the tension, then the turn, then the payoff.
```

Standard:
```
Make a 60-second vertical storytelling video from this talking head. Open cold on the line "I almost didn't post it." Then the setup, the two sentences about starting the account. Then the tension, the part where I explain the first month of no views. Then the turn, the line "and then one video hit." Then the payoff, what changed. Cut everything that is not one of those beats. Straight cuts, no music yet.
```

Full:
```
Build a 75-second 9:16 storytelling video from this footage. Cold open on "I almost didn't post it," 3 seconds max. Setup starts at "so in January I made an account," keep it under 12 seconds. Tension runs from "for a month, nothing" through the sentence about wanting to quit, up to 25 seconds, and let the pauses breathe here, do not tighten below 0.6 seconds. Turn is the line "and then one video hit," punch in 15% on that line only. Payoff runs from "now I" to the end, under 20 seconds. Cover the tension section with 2 to 3 b-roll clips of me at the desk at night. Everything else stays on my face. End on the last word, no fade, no CTA.
```

## What each part does

Four beats with a quoted opening line each is the structure. Per-beat length caps keep the middle from swallowing the video. "Let the pauses breathe" in the tension beat is the opposite of the usual rule, and it matters. One punch-in marks the turn without decorating the rest. "No fade, no CTA" protects the ending.

## Fixes

- Chronological instead of cold open. "Move 'I almost didn't post it' to the very start."
- Middle drags. "Cut the tension section to 18 seconds. Keep the sentence about wanting to quit."
- Payoff buried. "End on 'now I edit in ten minutes' and cut the two sentences after it."
- Feels rushed. "Allow pauses up to 0.8 seconds in the tension section."

## FAQ

**Do I need a cold open?**
Not always. It's the fastest way to earn the first three seconds. Take a line from the turn or payoff and put it first.

**Can it find the beats for me?**
Ask. "List the setup, tension, turn, and payoff lines from this transcript with timestamps." Fix the list, then use it as the prompt.

## Rules that apply to every prompt

1. One target per prompt. Length, platform, purpose.
2. Quote the exact line when you mean a specific moment.
3. Give numbers, not adjectives. "Pauses over half a second," not "snappy."
4. Say what not to add. Zooms, transitions, music, effects.
5. One fix per follow-up message. Ask for a list of changes with timestamps if you want to check.
