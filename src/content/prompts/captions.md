**Say how the words group (one at a time or 3 to 5 word phrases), how they look, where they sit, and which words get bigger.**

Minimal:
```
Add captions to the whole video.
```

Standard:
```
Add captions for all speech. Group them into 3-5 word phrases, bold white text with a thin black outline, centered in the lower third. No pop or bounce animation.
```

Full:
```
Caption the whole video word by word, TikTok style. Each word appears as it is spoken, bold sans-serif, white, slightly above center so it clears the bottom UI. When I say "eighty percent" and "never again", make those words bigger and yellow. Turn captions off during the 5 seconds of b-roll at the end.
```

Copying a style from another video:
```
Copy the caption style from the reference video exactly. Blocky all-caps font, soft glow, hard drop shadow, two lines max. Apply that to my whole video.
```

One user asked for "nice and blocky with glow and drop shadow" and got something generic the first time. Naming the three visible things fixed it.

## Fixes

- Captions over your face. "Move captions to the lower third."
- Wrong word. "At 0:12 the caption should say gesso."
- Too many words on screen. "Max 3 words per caption."

## FAQ

**Word-by-word or phrases?**
Word-by-word for fast talking and hooks. Phrases for anything calmer. If you're not sure, phrases.

**Can it fix spelling of names?**
Yes. List them in the prompt. "Spell these as Vyra, sulansart, gesso."

## Rules that apply to every prompt

1. One target per prompt. Length, platform, purpose.
2. Quote the exact line when you mean a specific moment.
3. Give numbers, not adjectives. "Pauses over half a second," not "snappy."
4. Say what not to add. Zooms, transitions, music, effects.
5. One fix per follow-up message. Ask for a list of changes with timestamps if you want to check.
