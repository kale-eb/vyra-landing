**Switching video editors typically takes one afternoon if you do it in the right order: export every finished video as an MP4, keep your raw footage in dated folders (the footage is the asset, not the project file), write down your recurring style choices (fonts, caption position, music behavior), then rebuild those choices in the new tool as saved prompts, presets, or a reference video. Project files rarely transfer between editors, so plan to lose those and keep everything else.**

## Why

- **Project files are proprietary.** A CapCut, Premiere, or Descript project does not open in another editor. Finished exports and raw footage do.
- **Your style lives in a few repeatable decisions.** Caption font and position, music level under speech, cut rhythm. Capture those and the new tool can reproduce them.
- **Reference videos travel.** Your own best video is the best spec for the new editor. Tools that match a reference (Vyra does) can rebuild your look from it.
- **Muscle memory is the real switching cost.** Shortcuts and menu locations take weeks to relearn. Description-based editors reduce that cost because there is less interface to learn.

## What to do about it

1. **Export everything finished.** MP4 at the highest quality. Store them with the raw footage.
2. **Organize raw footage by video.** One folder per video with the date. This is your real archive.
3. **Write a one-page style sheet.** Font, caption style and position, music rules, intro and outro habits, aspect ratios, typical length.
4. **Pick your three best videos as references.** Upload them to the new tool as style references.
5. **Rebuild one video end to end.** Re-edit a recent video in the new tool from raw footage. Compare to the original. Adjust the style sheet.
6. **Keep the old editor installed for a month.** For the one project you forgot to export.

## Migrating a style into Vyra

| Old habit | In Vyra |
| --- | --- |
| Caption preset | A saved prompt: "3-5 word phrases, bold white, lower third" |
| Music ducking rule | "Music at 25% under my voice, 70% during b-roll" |
| Intro title card | "Title card in Montserrat Black, 2 seconds, then cut to me" |
| Signature pacing | Attach your best video as a reference and say "match the cut rhythm" |
| Export preset | "Export 1080x1920 at high quality" |

## Do it in Vyra

```
Match the attached reference video (my best edit from my old editor): same cut rhythm, caption style and placement, and music behavior. Apply it to this new footage and keep it at 45 seconds.
```

```
Save the caption and music settings from this project as my defaults for future projects.
```

## FAQ

**Can I import a Premiere or CapCut project into Vyra?**
No. Import the raw footage and finished exports. Rebuild the look with a prompt or a reference video.

**Will I lose quality re-editing from exports?**
Edit from raw footage where you have it. Re-cutting a finished export is fine for social video but loses some quality; a 1080p export re-exported at 1080p is typically acceptable.

**How long does it take to feel fast in a new editor?**
Timeline editors: two to four weeks. Description-based editors: the first video, because the interface is a chat box and a preview.
