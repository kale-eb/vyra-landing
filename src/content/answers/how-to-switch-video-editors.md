**Switching video editors typically takes one afternoon if you do it in order. Export every finished video as an MP4. Keep raw footage in dated folders, because the footage is the asset, not the project file. Write down your recurring style choices (fonts, caption position, music behavior). Then rebuild those in the new tool as saved prompts, presets, or a reference video. Project files rarely transfer between editors, so plan to lose those and keep everything else.**

## Why

- Project files are proprietary. A CapCut, Premiere, or Descript project doesn't open elsewhere. Exports and raw footage do.
- Your style is a few repeatable decisions. Caption font and position, music level under speech, cut rhythm.
- Your own best video is the best spec for the new editor. Tools that match a reference (Vyra does) can rebuild your look from it.
- Muscle memory is the real switching cost. Shortcuts take weeks to relearn. Description-based editors have less interface to learn.

## What to do about it

1. **Export everything finished.** MP4 at the highest quality.
2. **Organize raw footage by video.** One folder per video with the date.
3. **Write a one-page style sheet.** Font, caption style and position, music rules, intro and outro habits, aspect ratios, typical length.
4. **Pick your three best videos as references.**
5. **Rebuild one video end to end** in the new tool. Compare to the original. Adjust the style sheet.
6. **Keep the old editor installed for a month.** For the one project you forgot to export.

## Migrating a style into Vyra

| Old habit | In Vyra |
| --- | --- |
| Caption preset | A saved prompt, "3-5 word phrases, bold white, lower third" |
| Music ducking rule | "Music at 25% under my voice, 70% during b-roll" |
| Intro title card | "Title card in Montserrat Black, 2 seconds, then cut to me" |
| Signature pacing | Attach your best video and say "match the cut rhythm" |
| Export preset | "Export 1080x1920 at high quality" |

## Do it in Vyra

```
Match the attached reference video, my best edit from my old editor. Same cut rhythm, caption style and placement, and music behavior. Apply it to this new footage and keep it at 45 seconds.
```

```
Save the caption and music settings from this project as my defaults for future projects.
```

## FAQ

**Can I import a Premiere or CapCut project into Vyra?**
No. Import the raw footage and finished exports. Rebuild the look with a prompt or a reference video.

**Will I lose quality re-editing from exports?**
Edit from raw footage where you have it. Re-cutting a finished 1080p export is fine for social video.

**How long until I feel fast in a new editor?**
Timeline editors, two to four weeks. Description-based editors, the first video.
