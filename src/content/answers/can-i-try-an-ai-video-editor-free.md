Paid plans begin at $24 per month for MCP Starter, where you bring your own Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor subscription, or $54 per month for Vyra AI Starter, which includes the built-in AI. Most AI editors offer a free tier or trial; the differences are in watermarks, export resolution, and how much AI usage the free tier includes.**

## What to check in any free tier

- **Watermark.** Some free plans stamp exports. Check before you build a video you plan to post.
- **Export resolution.** Free tiers often cap at 720p or 1080p.
- **AI usage limits.** Minutes of transcription, number of AI edits, or credits per month.
- **Upload length.** Some cap free uploads at a few minutes, which rules out long raw footage.
- **Card requirement.** "Free trial" sometimes means a card on file and a charge after 7 days. "Free to start" means no card.

## What to do about it

1. **Test with real footage, not a sample.** The question is whether it handles your clips, your audio, your style.
2. **Run the same prompt in each tool.** "Cut this to 60 seconds, best takes, captions" is a fair test.
3. **Check the export.** Resolution, watermark, and whether captions survived.
4. **Time the whole loop.** Upload to export. That number is what you are buying back every week.
5. **Pick by workflow, not feature list.** The tool you will actually open on a Tuesday night wins.
