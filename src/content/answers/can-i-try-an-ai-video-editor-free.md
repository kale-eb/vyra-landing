## What to check in any free tier

- **Watermark.** Some free plans stamp exports.
- **Export resolution.** Free tiers often cap at 720p or 1080p.
- **AI usage limits.** Minutes of transcription, number of AI edits, or credits.
- **Upload length.** Some cap free uploads at a few minutes, which rules out raw footage.
- **Trial length.** A trial gives you full features for a few days. A free tier lasts forever but holds features back.
- **Card requirement.** Some trials need a card on file and charge when the trial ends.

## What to do about it

1. **Test with real footage.** Your clips, your audio, your style.
2. **Run the same prompt in each tool.** "Cut this to 60 seconds, best takes, captions."
3. **Check the export.** Resolution, watermark, whether captions survived.
4. **Time the whole loop.** Upload to export. That's what you're buying back every week.
5. **Pick by workflow.** The tool you'll actually open on a Tuesday night wins.
