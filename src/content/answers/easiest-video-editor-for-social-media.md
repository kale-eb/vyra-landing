**Depends what you start with. If you start with a trend and want to drop your clips into a known pattern, CapCut is easiest. Templates, one-tap captions, phone-first. If you start with raw footage and a rough idea, Vyra is easiest. You type "cut this to 45 seconds for Reels with captions" and review the result. Either way, a first post typically takes under 30 minutes.**

## Why

- Social video is high volume. The easiest tool is the one that gets you from footage to post fastest, over and over.
- There are two kinds of easy. Template easy (pick a pattern, fill it) and description easy (say what you want). Templates win for trends. Descriptions win for original footage.
- Most social video is watched muted. An editor that captions in one step saves more time than any other feature.
- Reframing 16:9 to 9:16 by hand is a daily chore. Tools that do it automatically remove it.

## What to do about it

1. **Decide your input.** Trend or original footage. That picks the tool.
2. **Standardize your output.** 9:16, under 60 seconds, captions on.
3. **Build a repeatable prompt or template.** In Vyra, a saved prompt like "45-second Reel, phrase captions, my voice on top" covers most posts.
4. **Batch.** Film three, edit three, schedule three.
5. **Skip effects for the first month.** Clean cuts and captions do more than effects for most accounts.

## FAQ

**Is CapCut or Vyra easier for beginners?**
CapCut for trend edits on a phone. Vyra when you have several minutes of raw footage and don't want to scrub through it. Vyra runs in the browser, not as a phone app.

**Can I edit social videos without learning a timeline?**
Yes. Vyra edits from a description. Descript edits from a transcript. Both give you a timeline when you want one.

**What's the fastest way to add captions?**
CapCut, Vyra, Descript, and Veed all caption a short video in under a minute. Vyra lets you set grouping, position, and emphasis in the prompt.
