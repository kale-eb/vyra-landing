**Free video editors cover cutting, trimming, and basic captions well enough to publish. Paying becomes worth it when you hit a hard limit (export resolution, watermark, processing minutes) or a soft one (your time). For most creators the soft limit arrives at two or three videos a week: if a subscription saves an hour per video, it pays for itself in the first week. Nearly every serious editor, including Vyra, is free to start, so test before paying.**

## Why

- **Free tiers are designed to convert.** They hold back the thing you will want once you are consistent: 1080p or 4K export, no watermark, more storage or processing.
- **Time is the real cost.** A free tool that costs you two extra hours a week is more expensive than a paid tool at almost any price.
- **AI features are usually paid.** Automatic rough cuts, footage understanding, and agent editing sit behind paid tiers across the market. Manual features stay free longer.
- **Switching has a cost too.** Learning a second editor takes time. Pick the one you will grow into, then pay when you need to.

## What free typically includes and withholds

| Feature | Usually free | Usually paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cut, trim, split | Yes | |
| Basic captions | Often | Styled or translated captions |
| 1080p export | Sometimes | 4K, no watermark |
| AI rough cut from a description | Rarely | Yes |
| Storage and processing | Small | Larger caps |
| Reference style matching, motion graphics from a prompt | | Yes |

Check each tool's current limits; free tiers change often.

## What to do about it

1. **Start free everywhere.** Vyra, CapCut, Clipchamp, Descript, and Veed all have free starts. Use them on the same footage.
2. **Track the hours.** Note how long each video takes for two weeks.
3. **Pay when the math flips.** If a plan saves more time than it costs at your hourly value, upgrade.
4. **Pay for the AI, not the effects.** The paid features that change output are the ones that remove review and trimming, not the sticker packs.
5. **Match the plan to your AI setup.** If you already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor, Vyra's MCP plans ($24 and $65 per month) reuse that subscription; the built-in AI plans ($54 and $129) need no external AI.

## FAQ

**Is there a fully free AI video editor?**
Free tiers exist, but the AI-heavy features (rough cuts from a description, footage understanding) are typically limited or paid. Free is enough to test whether the workflow fits.

**What does Vyra cost after the free start?**
MCP Starter $24/mo and MCP Pro $65/mo with your own AI subscription; Vyra AI Starter $54/mo and Vyra AI Pro $129/mo with the built-in AI. Starter tiers export up to 1440p, Pro up to 4K.

**Is a one-time purchase editor better value than a subscription?**
For manual editing, a one-time purchase like DaVinci Resolve Studio or Final Cut Pro can be cheaper over years. AI editing features are almost always subscriptions because the processing runs on servers.
