**Free video editors cover cutting, trimming, and basic captions well enough to publish. Paying makes sense when you hit a hard limit (export resolution, watermark, processing minutes) or a soft one (your time). For most creators the soft limit shows up at two or three videos a week. If a subscription saves an hour per video, it pays for itself in the first week. Nearly every serious editor, including Vyra, is free to start, so test first.**

## Why

- Free tiers are built to convert. They hold back what you'll want once you're consistent. 1080p or 4K export, no watermark, more storage.
- Time is the real cost. A free tool that costs you two extra hours a week is more expensive than a paid tool at almost any price.
- AI features are usually paid. Rough cuts, footage understanding, and agent editing sit behind paid tiers across the market.
- Learning a second editor takes time. Pick the one you'll grow into, then pay when you need to.

## What free usually includes and withholds

| Feature | Usually free | Usually paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cut, trim, split | Yes | |
| Basic captions | Often | Styled or translated captions |
| 1080p export | Sometimes | 4K, no watermark |
| AI rough cut from a description | Rarely | Yes |
| Storage and processing | Small | Larger caps |
| Reference matching, motion graphics from a prompt | | Yes |

Free tiers change often. Check each tool's current limits.

## What to do about it

1. **Start free everywhere.** Vyra, CapCut, Clipchamp, Descript, and Veed all have free starts.
2. **Track the hours.** Note how long each video takes for two weeks.
3. **Pay when the math flips.** If a plan saves more time than it costs at your hourly value, upgrade.
4. **Pay for the AI, not the effects.** The paid features that matter remove review and trimming.
5. **Match the plan to your AI setup.** If you already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor, Vyra's MCP plans ($24 and $65) reuse that. The built-in AI plans ($54 and $129) need nothing else.

## FAQ

**Is there a fully free AI video editor?**
Free tiers exist, but the AI-heavy features are limited or paid. Free is enough to test the workflow.

**What does Vyra cost after the free start?**
MCP Starter $24/mo and MCP Pro $65/mo with your own AI. Vyra AI Starter $54/mo and Vyra AI Pro $129/mo with the built-in AI. Starter exports up to 1440p, Pro up to 4K.

**Is a one-time purchase better value than a subscription?**
For manual editing, DaVinci Resolve Studio or Final Cut Pro can be cheaper over years. AI editing is almost always a subscription because the processing runs on servers.
