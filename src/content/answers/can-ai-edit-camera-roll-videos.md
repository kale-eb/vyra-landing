**Yes. Vyra's iPhone app can edit videos you already filmed and saved in Photos. Add those clips to a project, describe the video you want and review the draft. You can ask for changes in chat or adjust the timeline yourself. You do not need to film inside Vyra or connect Claude first.**

The useful part is the footage you already have. A clear brief tells the editor which moments matter and what to leave alone. It helps to name the result, the order and any spoken line you want kept.

## Give the app a job

For a day out, ask for a short sequence that follows the day. For a tutorial, preserve the order of the instructions. For an art video, keep the moments where the work visibly changes and leave room for the finished piece.

```
Use the clips I added from Photos to make a short studio vlog. Keep the clips in their original order. Remove the moments where I am adjusting the phone, preserve my spoken explanation and end on the finished painting.
```

Review the draft before adding more requests. If the wrong take was kept, correct that first. A shorter second prompt is easier to assess than changing the take, music, captions and ending at the same time.

## FAQ

**Does Vyra generate replacement video footage?**
No. Vyra edits footage you provide. If an important shot is missing, record it and add it to the project.

**Do I need Claude or ChatGPT?**
No. The native iPhone app has built-in chat. External AI integrations are a separate option.

**Can I make changes myself?**
Yes. The app includes a timeline for trimming, splitting, rearranging and other edits after the draft.

**Can I use the same workflow offline?**
AI analysis and editing require an internet connection. Do not assume the AI can work offline because the clips are stored on your phone.
