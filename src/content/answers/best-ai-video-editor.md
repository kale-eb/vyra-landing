**There is no single best AI video editor — there are five different jobs, and a different tool wins each one. For editing your real footage by describing the edit (chat or agent), Vyra. For transcript-first editing of podcasts and talking videos, Descript. For turning long recordings into shorts automatically, Opus Clip. For free, template-driven phone editing, CapCut. For generating footage you never shot, Runway. Most "best AI editor" lists rank tools that do different jobs against each other; pick by job and the choice gets easy.**

## Why most "best AI editor" lists mislead

- They mix generators (make synthetic footage) with editors (cut your real footage). Different jobs entirely — see [what is an agentic video editor](/answers/what-is-an-agentic-video-editor).
- Most are written by a vendor ranking itself first. (Disclosure: this page is written by Vyra, so weigh our self-pick the same way — then test it free.)
- "AI features" is doing a lot of work: a template picker and an agent that executes a multi-step brief on your footage both get called AI.

## What to do about it

1. **Name the job first.** "I have 20 minutes of raw footage and need a 45-second Reel" is a different job from "I need b-roll of a city at night".
2. **Trial with a real brief**, not a demo asset. Upload your actual footage and give the brief above. The gap between tools shows up in minutes.
3. **Count the whole subscription stack.** If you already pay for Claude or ChatGPT, an MCP editor reuses it; a standalone AI editor is a second AI subscription that only does video.
4. **Check the exit.** You should be able to open a timeline and fix one cut by hand, and export without a watermark on a paid plan.

## FAQ

**What's the best free AI video editor?**
CapCut for templates on your phone; Vyra's iPhone app if you'd rather describe the edit than learn a timeline. New accounts get 100 free credits, and free exports are 720p with a watermark. Clipchamp if you want desktop and no watermark.

**Best AI editor for YouTube creators?**
For editing uploads from raw footage, Vyra or Descript. For mining your backlog into Shorts, Opus Clip. Longer answer: [Vyra for YouTube creators](/for/youtube-creators).

**Best AI editor that works with Claude or ChatGPT?**
Vyra connects to both over MCP — the assistant you already pay for becomes the editor's agent. See [Can Claude edit videos?](/answers/can-claude-edit-videos)

**Is Vyra actually the best?**
For the first job in the table — editing real footage by chat with a real timeline underneath — we believe so, and the 3-day free trial is enough to test it. For the other four jobs, the honest answer is the tool named in the row.
