**Vyra’s official iPhone listing says its AI features require an internet connection. Plan AI edits while you are connected, and check any saved video separately before relying on it during a trip or other offline period.**

A clip sitting in Photos and an AI edit being ready are different states. If you need a finished video before leaving a connection, check the actual saved file rather than only the project preview.

## Separate the jobs before you leave

| Job | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Ask Vyra to make an AI edit | An internet connection is required |
| Check a completed export | Confirm the intended file has finished and is saved where you need it |
| Depend on manual editing offline | Verify the exact workflow before relying on it |

The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) establishes the AI connection requirement. Verify any manual editing you need before relying on it offline.

## Make the brief useful before reconnecting

You can plan the edit in your own notes. Identify the clips, the spoken line to keep and the intended ending so the instruction is ready when you connect.

> Use my recorded setup, the main action and the finished piece. Keep my full explanation and make the captions readable above the subject.

When connected again, submit the brief and review the draft. For a completed result, inspect Library and Exports and save the intended copy before treating it as ready to share. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro options are sold through the App Store.

## FAQ

**Does having the video in Photos remove the AI connection requirement?**
No. Local source footage and an available connection for AI features are separate requirements.

**Can I assume a project preview is the saved video I need?**
Check the finished export and its saved location. The editable project and a completed video file are separate items.
