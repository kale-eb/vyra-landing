**Yes. Describe the graphic in plain words, like "animate the three tool names on screen as I say them, stacked left, bold, on a dark bar," and an AI editor builds it timed to your speech. The result is editable. Text, colors, timing, and position can all be changed after. It won't invent a brand identity for you, so the more specific you are, the closer the first result.**

## Why

- Motion graphics are mostly rules. A lower third is a name, a title, a position, an entrance, and a duration. Rules are easy to turn into code.
- Timing comes from the transcript. The editor knows when each word is spoken, so "show the price when I say it" lands on the right frame.
- Compositions are code, not templates. An unusual layout is as easy as a common one.
- Text, shapes, and media stay as timeline items you can move and retime by hand.

## What works best

- Lower thirds, title cards, and end cards.
- Animated lists that build as you name items.
- Callouts, arrows, and highlight boxes on screen recordings.
- Kinetic captions that emphasize one line.
- Simple charts and comparison bars.

## What to describe

1. **What it says.** The exact text.
2. **Where it sits.** Top left, under your chin, centered.
3. **When it appears and leaves.** On a spoken word, for a duration, or until the list ends.
4. **How it moves.** Slide in, pop, or nothing.
5. **What it looks like.** Font weight, color, background bar, size.

## Do it in Vyra

```
When I list the three tools, animate each name on screen as I say it, stacked on the left, bold Montserrat, white text on a dark bar. Keep all three up until I finish the list, then clear them together.
```

```
Add a lower third for the first 4 seconds. "Sulan Zhang" on line one, "@sulansart" on line two, bottom left, small, slide in from the left, no drop shadow.
```

## FAQ

**Can it match my brand fonts and colors?**
Yes, if you name them. "Use Montserrat Black and #F4DD45 for the accent."

**Can I edit the graphic after?**
Yes. Ask for changes in chat or drag items on the timeline.

**Does it make 3D or particle effects?**
Not really. It's built for type, shapes, callouts, and layout, which is what social video uses.
