**Manual editing typically takes 30 to 60 minutes per finished minute for a short social video and 1 to 2 hours per finished minute for a polished YouTube video. A 60-second Reel is 30 to 60 minutes of work. A 10-minute YouTube video is 5 to 10 hours. An AI rough cut takes minutes, so the human time moves to reviewing and polishing.**

## Why

- Format sets the floor. A talking head with captions is fast. A multi-location vlog with b-roll, music, and graphics is slow.
- Editing time tracks raw minutes more than finished minutes. Two hours of clips for a 5-minute video takes longer than 20 minutes of clips for a 10-minute video.
- An editor who knows shortcuts and works in passes is two to three times faster than someone learning on the project.
- Revision rounds add 20 to 50 percent on top of the first cut.

## Typical ranges

| Video | Raw footage | Manual edit | AI rough cut + polish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30-second talking head Reel | 3 to 6 minutes | 30 to 60 minutes | 5 to 15 minutes |
| 60-second UGC hook and demo | 10 to 20 minutes | 1 to 2 hours | 15 to 30 minutes |
| 5-minute travel montage | 1 to 2 hours | 4 to 8 hours | 30 to 60 minutes |
| 10-minute YouTube talking head with b-roll | 30 to 60 minutes | 5 to 10 hours | 1 to 2 hours |
| 20-minute vlog | 2 to 4 hours | 10 to 20 hours | 2 to 4 hours |

These are typical ranges for solo creators, not studio work.

## What to do about it

1. **Estimate from raw minutes.** Multiply raw footage by 3 to 5 for a manual estimate in minutes.
2. **Cut the review pass.** The first hour of any edit is watching footage. An editor that indexes on upload removes it.
3. **Work in passes.** Story, then sound, then text, then music, then look.
4. **Batch similar videos.** Five talking heads in one session go faster than five sessions.
5. **Stop at good.** Set a deadline per video and export when it hits.

## FAQ

**How long does a 1-minute video take?**
Manually, 30 to 60 minutes for a simple cut with captions, up to 2 hours with b-roll and graphics. With an AI rough cut, 5 to 20 minutes including review.

**How long does a 10-minute YouTube video take?**
Typically 5 to 10 hours manually. Heavily produced channels report 20 hours or more.

**Does AI editing save time or just move it?**
It removes the review and the trimming, which is 60 to 80 percent of a typical edit. The creative decisions stay with you, but they take minutes.
