**Manual editing takes about 30 to 60 minutes per finished minute for a simple video, like a talking head with captions. It takes 1 to 2 hours per finished minute for a produced video with b-roll, music, and graphics. So a 1-minute Reel is 30 minutes to 2 hours, a 5-minute video is 2.5 to 10 hours, and a 10-minute YouTube video is 5 to 20 hours. An AI rough cut takes minutes, so the human time moves to reviewing and polishing.**

## Editing time by video length

| Finished length | Simple edit | Produced edit | AI rough cut + polish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 minute (a Reel or TikTok) | 30 to 60 minutes | 1 to 2 hours | 5 to 20 minutes |
| 2 minutes | 1 to 2 hours | 2 to 4 hours | 10 to 30 minutes |
| 3 minutes | 1.5 to 3 hours | 3 to 6 hours | 15 to 40 minutes |
| 5 minutes | 2.5 to 5 hours | 5 to 10 hours | 30 to 60 minutes |
| 10 minutes | 5 to 10 hours | 10 to 20 hours | 1 to 2 hours |
| 20 minutes | 10 to 20 hours | 20 to 40 hours | 2 to 4 hours |
| 30 minutes | 15 to 30 hours | 30 hours or more | 3 to 6 hours |

Simple means one camera, cuts, captions, and music. Produced means b-roll, several locations, sound design, titles, or motion graphics. These are typical ranges for solo creators, not studio work.

## By type of video

| Video | Raw footage | Manual edit | AI rough cut + polish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30-second talking head Reel | 3 to 6 minutes | 30 to 60 minutes | 5 to 15 minutes |
| 60-second UGC hook and demo | 10 to 20 minutes | 1 to 2 hours | 15 to 30 minutes |
| 5-minute travel montage | 1 to 2 hours | 4 to 8 hours | 30 to 60 minutes |
| 10-minute YouTube talking head with b-roll | 30 to 60 minutes | 5 to 10 hours | 1 to 2 hours |
| 20-minute vlog | 2 to 4 hours | 10 to 20 hours | 2 to 4 hours |

## Why it takes that long

- Raw footage sets the time more than finished length. Two hours of clips for a 5-minute video takes longer than 20 minutes of clips for a 10-minute video, because someone has to watch all of it.
- Format sets the floor. A talking head with captions is fast. A multi-location vlog with b-roll, music, and graphics is slow.
- An editor who knows the shortcuts and works in passes is two to three times faster than someone learning on the project.
- Revision rounds add 20 to 50 percent on top of the first cut.

## How to estimate your own

1. **Count your raw minutes.** Multiply by 3 to 5 for a manual estimate in minutes.
2. **Add time for each extra.** Plan on 15 to 30 minutes each for captions, music, and a title card, and more for b-roll and graphics.
3. **Add a revision round.** Another 20 to 50 percent.

## How to cut the time

1. **Cut the review pass.** The first hour of any edit is watching footage. An editor that indexes footage on upload removes it.
2. **Work in passes.** Story, then sound, then text, then music, then look.
3. **Batch similar videos.** Five talking heads in one session go faster than five sessions.
4. **Stop at good.** Set a deadline per video and export when it hits.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to edit a 1-minute video?**
30 to 60 minutes for a simple cut with captions, up to 2 hours with b-roll and graphics. With an AI rough cut, 5 to 20 minutes including review.

**How long does it take to edit a reel?**
Most Reels are 15 to 60 seconds, so 20 to 60 minutes for a simple edit and up to 2 hours with b-roll and effects.

**How long does it take to edit a 2-minute video?**
1 to 2 hours for a simple edit, 2 to 4 hours for a produced one.

**How long does it take to edit a 5-minute video?**
2.5 to 5 hours for a simple edit, 5 to 10 hours for a produced one with b-roll and graphics.

**How long does it take to edit a 10-minute YouTube video?**
5 to 10 hours for a talking head with some b-roll. Heavily produced channels report 20 hours or more.

**How long does it take to edit a 30-minute video?**
15 to 30 hours for a simple edit. Long talk formats like podcasts and lectures go faster per minute because there are fewer cuts.

**Does AI editing save time or just move it?**
It removes the review and the trimming, which is most of a typical edit. The creative decisions stay with you, and they take minutes.
