**Turning on Live Captions on iPhone does not add permanent captions to your original video file. Live Captions displays a transcription while you listen. If you want the words to stay in the picture when someone else opens your video, add captions in an editor such as Vyra and export that captioned version.**

You might see the words while watching a clip on your phone and assume they will travel with the file. Check the saved video itself before posting. The text you see during playback can come from your phone, the video player or the edit.

## Choose the result you need

| What you need | What to use |
| --- | --- |
| Follow speech playing on your own phone | Live Captions, where available |
| Keep styled words visible in a Reel you share | Captions added to the picture before export |
| Let viewers switch captions on or off | A caption track supported by the destination player |
| Keep a written record of speech | A transcript, checked against the audio |

[Apple's Live Captions guide](https://support.apple.com/en-ae/guide/iphone/iphe0990f7bb/27/ios/27) describes a listening aid with a floating transcription window. It also describes copying text and capturing that window. Those are separate actions from exporting an edited video with captions in the picture. Availability depends on the device, language and region.

## Make a captioned copy from your own footage

In Vyra on iPhone, add your clip from Photos and ask for captions from its spoken audio. Review every phrase, especially names and numbers. Choose a readable style, then export the finished video. The [full iPhone caption guide](/how-to/add-captions-to-videos-on-iphone) walks through that workflow.

For a talking video, try a brief like this. Replace the example name with the name spoken in your recording.

```
Add captions to the speech in this clip. Use short phrases in white text with a dark outline. Keep the words clear of my face. The name is Maya. Keep the original audio and clip order.
```

Check the preview with sound to catch wrong words and timing. Then check readability with the video muted. If the captions arrive late, describe the phrase and its time using the [caption timing guide](/answers/why-are-my-captions-out-of-sync).

Vyra's [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) confirms generated captions, styling and saving an exported video to Photos. New mobile accounts currently get 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro are available through the App Store.

## Check the file you are about to post

Open the export made after your latest changes. Look for a phrase you corrected and check that its spelling is right. If you have several copies in Photos, confirm you picked the captioned version.

For a second check, play the file on another device. Look for the same styled text in the same position. A transcript pasted into a post description is also separate from captions inside the video.

The examples on this page are finished Vyra edits. They illustrate captioned video, rather than the iPhone Live Captions window or a recording of the mobile controls.

## FAQ

**Will Live Captions change my original video in Photos?**
Turning on Live Captions displays a transcription during listening. To save captions in the picture, make and export a captioned edit.

**Can I copy a transcript instead of adding captions?**
A transcript can help you review the words. It still needs timing and placement to become captions that follow speech in a video.

**Can viewers turn off captions that are part of the picture?**
Text rendered into the picture remains visible in that exported copy. A player-controlled caption track works differently. Check the destination's caption support if viewers need that choice.

**Can Vyra caption a video I already filmed on iPhone?**
Yes. Add the clip from Photos and ask for captions in the iPhone app. Review the words and export the edited version.

**Is the free iPhone export watermarked?**
Yes. Free mobile exports are 720p with a watermark. The web app has a separate three-day trial and no ongoing free tier.
