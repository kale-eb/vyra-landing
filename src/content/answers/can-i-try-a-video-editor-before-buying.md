**Yes. In 2026 nearly every video editor lets you start free, either as an ongoing free tier or a time-limited trial. The useful test is not clicking around the interface; it is uploading one real video you actually need to post and taking it to export. Vyra is free to start, and you can use its built-in AI without connecting any external subscription.**

## Why

- **Free starts are the industry norm.** Editors compete on conversion, so almost all of them let you in without a card. What differs is what the free level withholds.
- **Feature lists do not predict fit.** Whether an editor suits you depends on your footage, your format, and how you like to work. Only a real video reveals that.
- **Trials have expiry, free tiers have caps.** A 7-day trial pushes you to test fast. A free tier lets you test slowly but may cap resolution or processing.
- **AI editors need your footage to show their value.** The point of a tool like Vyra is what it does with your raw clips. A demo video proves nothing.

## What to do about it

1. **Pick the video you need to post this week.** Not a test clip. A real one.
2. **Write the brief before you open the tool.** Length, platform, keep, cut, captions, music.
3. **Take it all the way to export.** Half the surprises live in the export step: watermark, resolution cap, file size.
4. **Time it.** From upload to export. Compare against your current process.
5. **Check the upgrade path.** What does the next tier add, and does it address the limit you hit?

## What to look for in the free start

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Watermark on export | Some free tiers stamp the video |
| Max export resolution | 720p is common on free tiers; 1080p is the social minimum |
| Processing or upload limits | Long footage may not fit |
| Whether AI features are included | Some show the AI but gate it |
| Card required | Trials sometimes need one; free tiers usually do not |
