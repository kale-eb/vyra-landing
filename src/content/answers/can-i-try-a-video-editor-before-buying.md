**Yes. In 2026 nearly every video editor lets you start free, either as an ongoing free tier or a timed trial. The useful test isn't clicking around the interface. It's uploading one real video you need to post and taking it to export. Vyra is free to start, and the built-in AI works without connecting any other subscription.**

## Why

- Free starts are the norm. What differs is what the free level holds back.
- Feature lists don't predict fit. Whether an editor suits you depends on your footage and how you like to work.
- A 7-day trial pushes you to test fast. A free tier lets you test slowly but may cap resolution or processing.
- An AI editor needs your footage to show what it does. A demo video proves nothing.

## What to do about it

1. **Pick the video you need to post this week.** A real one.
2. **Write the brief before you open the tool.** Length, platform, keep, cut, captions, music.
3. **Take it all the way to export.** Half the surprises live there. Watermark, resolution cap, file size.
4. **Time it.** Upload to export. Compare to your current process.
5. **Check the upgrade path.** Does the next tier fix the limit you hit?

## What to look for in the free start

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Watermark on export | Some free tiers stamp the video |
| Max export resolution | 720p is common on free tiers |
| Processing or upload limits | Long footage may not fit |
| Whether AI features are included | Some show the AI but gate it |
| Card required | Trials sometimes need one, free tiers usually don't |
