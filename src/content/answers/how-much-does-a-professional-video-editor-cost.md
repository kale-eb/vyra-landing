**A video editor costs whatever their hourly rate is times the hours the edit takes. In the US, staff film and video editors earned a median of $75,420 a year in May 2025, about $36 an hour, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Freelancers charge more per hour than staff pay because they cover their own software, gear, and gaps between jobs. A 2026 rate guide from the goLance marketplace puts junior freelancers at about $20 to $40 an hour, mid-level at $40 to $75, and senior editors at $75 to $130. Global marketplaces go much lower. goLance's own median is $12 an hour.**

## Rates by experience

| Level | Hourly rate | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Junior, 0 to 2 years | About $20 to $40 | Clean cuts, captions, music from a brief. Needs clear notes. |
| Mid-level, 2 to 5 years | About $40 to $75 | Pacing, b-roll choices, simple motion graphics, fewer revisions |
| Senior, 5 to 10 years | About $75 to $130 | Story structure, sound design, color, brand look |
| Expert, 10+ years or a niche | About $130 to $220 | Ads, documentary, broadcast |

Freelance tiers are from [goLance's 2026 rate guide](https://golance.com/hiring/best-freelance-video-editors-hourly-rate). Staff pay is from the [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/media-and-communication/film-and-video-editors-and-camera-operators.htm). In the BLS May 2023 survey, the middle half of US staff editors earned between $23.52 and $48.99 an hour, and the top 10 percent earned more than $74.27 ([BLS OEWS](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes274032.htm)).

## What a video costs, by type

Hours are the real price. Multiply the editing time by the rate.

| Video | Typical editing time | At $40/hr | At $75/hr |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30 to 60 second Reel or TikTok | 30 to 60 minutes | $20 to $40 | $40 to $75 |
| 60-second UGC ad with b-roll | 1 to 2 hours | $40 to $80 | $75 to $150 |
| 5-minute YouTube video | 2.5 to 5 hours | $100 to $200 | $190 to $375 |
| 10-minute YouTube video with b-roll | 5 to 10 hours | $200 to $400 | $375 to $750 |
| 20-minute vlog | 10 to 20 hours | $400 to $800 | $750 to $1,500 |

Editing times are from our [editing time estimates](/answers/how-long-does-it-take-to-edit-a-video). Most editors round up to a flat per-video price once they've done one for you.

## The four ways editors price

| Model | How it works | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly | You pay for time spent | Unclear scope, ongoing changes |
| Per finished minute | A fixed rate for each minute delivered | YouTube, courses |
| Per video | A flat fee for a defined deliverable | Social clips, ads |
| Monthly retainer | A set number of videos a month | Creators posting weekly or more |

A day rate is usually eight hours at a small discount to the hourly rate.

## What pushes the price up

- **Raw footage.** Two hours of clips for a 5-minute video takes longer than 20 minutes of clips for a 10-minute video. Editors price the review time.
- **Motion graphics and color.** Custom titles, animated lists, and grading add hours.
- **Revisions.** Most quotes include one or two rounds. More rounds cost extra.
- **Turnaround.** Next-day delivery often costs more.
- **Vague briefs.** If the editor has to guess, the quote gets padded.

## How to pay less

1. **Write the brief.** Length, platform, captions, music, graphics, revision rounds.
2. **Send a reference video.** It saves the editor hours of guessing and you a revision round.
3. **Send less footage.** Pick your best takes first. Less raw footage means fewer billed hours.
4. **Hand over a rough cut.** An AI editor like Vyra can cut the takes, pauses, and captions first. The editor bills only for the finishing pass.
5. **Start with one paid test.** One video, clear brief, fixed price. Move to a retainer after three good videos.

## FAQ

**How much does a video editor charge per hour?**
Most freelancers charge between about $20 and $130 an hour depending on experience. US staff editors earn a median of about $36 an hour.

**How much does it cost to edit a YouTube video?**
A 10-minute YouTube video with b-roll is typically 5 to 10 hours of work, so roughly $200 to $750 at common freelance rates.

**How much does it cost to outsource video editing for YouTube?**
Most channels pay per video or on a monthly retainer. At four videos a month, that's typically four times the per-video price with a small discount.

**Is it cheaper to hire an editor or use AI?**
For short social video at volume, an AI subscription costs less per video. For a flagship YouTube video with heavy graphics, a human editor still adds a lot, often on top of an AI rough cut.

**How do I know if a quote is fair?**
Get two or three quotes for the same brief. A big spread usually means the brief is unclear.
