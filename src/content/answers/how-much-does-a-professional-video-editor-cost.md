**Professional video editors typically price one of three ways: hourly, per finished minute, or per video. Rates vary widely by experience, country, and format. A short social clip is usually the cheapest job, a polished 10-minute YouTube video with b-roll and graphics the most expensive, and ongoing retainers sit in between. Before hiring, price the alternative: an AI editor that builds the rough cut for a monthly subscription, with a human or you doing the finishing pass.**

## Why

- **Editing time drives cost.** A 60-second Reel is 30 to 60 minutes of work for a pro; a 10-minute YouTube video is typically 5 to 10 hours. Whatever the rate, the hours multiply.
- **Experience changes the rate more than the format.** A beginner freelancer and a broadcast editor can differ several times over for the same job.
- **Revisions are often extra.** Many editors include one or two rounds; more rounds are billed.
- **Retainers lower the per-video price.** Committing to a monthly volume usually gets a better rate than one-off jobs.

## Typical pricing models

| Model | How it works | Typical fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly | You pay for time spent | Unclear scope, ongoing work |
| Per finished minute | A fixed rate per minute of delivered video | YouTube, courses |
| Per video | A flat fee for a defined deliverable | Social clips, ads |
| Monthly retainer | A set number of videos per month | Creators posting weekly or more |

Exact figures move with the market and the editor. Ask for a quote on one representative video before committing to volume.

## What to do about it

1. **Define the deliverable.** Length, platform, captions, music, graphics, revision rounds. Vague briefs get padded quotes.
2. **Send a sample edit.** A reference video for pacing and style saves the editor hours of guessing and you a revision round.
3. **Price the AI option honestly.** For social volume, an AI editor like Vyra (from $24/mo, free to start) can produce the rough cut and captions; you or a freelancer finish. Many creators pair the two.
4. **Start with one paid test.** One video, clear brief, fixed price. Judge speed and communication, not just the output.
5. **Move to a retainer only after three good videos.**

## FAQ

**Is it cheaper to hire an editor or use AI?**
For social video at volume, an AI subscription is typically far cheaper per video. For a flagship YouTube video with heavy graphics, a human editor still adds value, often on top of an AI rough cut.

**How do I know if an editor's quote is fair?**
Get two or three quotes for the same brief. Large spreads usually mean the brief is unclear, not that one editor is overcharging.

**Can I edit myself instead?**
Yes. With an AI rough cut, the finishing pass for a short video is 10 to 20 minutes and does not require timeline expertise.
