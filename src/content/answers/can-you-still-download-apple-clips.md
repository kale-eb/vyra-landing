**Apple says Clips stopped being updated and became unavailable to new users on October 10, 2025. People who downloaded Clips previously can still redownload it through their Apple account, according to Apple’s support guidance.**

An older tutorial may start by telling you to install Clips for Live Titles. Check whether the app is actually available to your account before building a new caption workflow around that instruction.

## Keep the video you already made

[Apple’s Clips support page](https://support.apple.com/en-am/123359) explains saving a finished video with its effects or saving individual original clips without effects. Those are different starting points for a new edit. Keep both when you need the finished version and the clean source material.

Apple’s instructions say to save a video rather than a Clips project. Use the source clips for a fresh edit when you want to change text that has already been rendered into the finished picture. Do not assume a project file becomes an editable project in another app.

## Choose a current caption workflow

If you need captions on an existing recording, choose an editor that can import that recording and generate captions. The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents that workflow. In Vyra, add the source clip, generate captions and check the words before export. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

A starting brief could be this.

> Caption my supplied recording. Keep its original audio, correct the spoken name from my note and leave the demonstration visible.

## FAQ

**Does Apple say existing Clips users must stop using it immediately?**
Apple’s support guidance says Clips can continue to be used on iOS 26 and iPadOS 26 or earlier, and explains redownloading for previous users. Check the current guidance for your device.

**Can I bring a Clips project straight into Vyra?**
Use a saved video or your original source clips for a new edit. No direct Clips project import is established here.
