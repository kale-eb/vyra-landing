**Captions burned into a video are part of its image. To change the wording cleanly, return to the editable project or a source recording without those words, correct the caption track and export again.**

A misspelled product name may be easy to fix in the project and difficult to fix in the only exported copy. First find out whether you still have editable text or just a picture containing the old text.

## Identify the copy you have

[HandBrake’s subtitle documentation](https://handbrake.fr/docs/en/latest/advanced/subtitles.html) distinguishes permanent text in the image from separate selectable subtitle tracks. A player’s subtitle switch cannot turn off words already in the picture.

| What you have | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| The editable project with a caption track | Correct the wording and export again |
| The original recording without text | Make a new captioned edit from that source |
| Only a video with words in the picture | Look for the original or project before attempting a visual workaround |

## Correct the project rather than covering the subject

The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents caption and timeline editing. If the captions are still separate items in your Vyra project, correct them there and inspect the new export. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

A useful instruction is specific about what should stay unchanged.

> Correct the product name in the editable caption track. Leave the spoken audio and video cuts unchanged. Show the corrected sentence before exporting.

Cropping or covering a word can also cover the object behind it. Those approaches do not restore the hidden image. No automatic removal of burned text is promised here.

## FAQ

**Will generating a new caption track remove the old visible words?**
No. If the old words are already part of the imported picture, a new track can sit on top of them. Start from clean footage when you can.

**Can selectable subtitles be different from burned captions?**
Yes. A separate subtitle track may be switched by compatible playback software. Words rendered into the image stay visible regardless of that switch.
