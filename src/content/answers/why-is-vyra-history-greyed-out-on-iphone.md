**Vyra’s current help says History is greyed out while Vyra is editing. Let the edit finish, then open History again. Decide what you want to recover before restoring an earlier version.**

You may be trying to check the earlier opening while a new edit is still running. Wait for that operation to finish before treating an unavailable History control as a missing project.

## Check the editing state first

The Saving and History help documents the native clock button and opening History by pressing and holding Undo. It also explicitly identifies active editing as the reason History is greyed out.

After the edit finishes, open History and compare the version you want. Restoring a version replaces edits made after that point. Write down any later change you still want so you can review it deliberately.

## Recover the right section

An illustrative instruction for the next review could be this.

> Help me identify the earlier opening with my complete explanation. I want to review it before changing the current timeline.

If the control stays unavailable after the edit has finished, preserve the project and record what you were doing for [Vyra support](/app-support). Do not delete the project as the first troubleshooting step.

The [official iPhone release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) document History and earlier saves. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro options are sold through the App Store.

## FAQ

**Does a grey History button mean my earlier saves were deleted?**
The documented active-editing state does not establish that saves were deleted. Wait until the operation finishes and inspect History again.

**Does restoring History change a video I already shared?**
History changes the editable project. Review and export a revised file separately if you need a different shared copy.
