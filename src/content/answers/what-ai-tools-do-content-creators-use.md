**A working creator stack in 2026 is small: typically two to four AI tools, not ten. The core is an editor that works on real footage (Vyra, Descript, or CapCut depending on format), a writing assistant for hooks, scripts, and captions (Claude or ChatGPT), and a scheduler. Creators who post long-form add a repurposing tool for clips. Generative video tools (Runway, Sora, Veo) show up for b-roll and ads, rarely for the main content.**

## Why

- **Editing is where the hours were.** The AI tool that changes a creator's week is the one that removes footage review and trimming. Everything else is marginal.
- **Writing help is cheap and universal.** Almost every creator drafts hooks or scripts with Claude or ChatGPT. With MCP, the same assistant can now also drive the editor.
- **Captions became a solved problem.** Every serious editor captions automatically. Standalone caption tools survive on styling and speed for shorts.
- **Generators are for supplements.** Audiences follow real people; generated footage fills gaps, not feeds.

## The typical stack by creator type

| Creator | Editor | Writing | Repurposing | Other |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short-form vlogger | Vyra or CapCut | Claude or ChatGPT | none | scheduler |
| Podcaster | Descript or Riverside | ChatGPT for show notes | Opus Clip or Vyra | audio cleanup |
| YouTube educator | Vyra or Premiere | Claude for scripts | Vyra or Vizard | thumbnail tool |
| UGC creator | Vyra or CapCut | ChatGPT for hooks | none | none |
| Artist or process creator | Vyra | none or light | none | none |

## What to do about it

1. **Start with the editor.** It is the only tool that changes your hours. Pick by format: real footage across formats (Vyra), speech-heavy (Descript), trend templates on a phone (CapCut).
2. **Use the assistant you already pay for.** If you have Claude or ChatGPT, use it for hooks and, over MCP, to drive Vyra. Do not add a second chatbot.
3. **Add repurposing only if you make long-form.** Otherwise it is a subscription you will not use.
4. **Skip generators until you have a specific gap.** A missing establishing shot, an ad concept. Not the main video.
5. **Cap the stack at four.** More tools means more switching, exporting, and re-uploading.

## FAQ

**Do professional creators use AI editors or hire editors?**
Both, increasingly together. The AI builds the rough cut and captions; a human editor or the creator does the finishing pass. Solo creators often skip the human.

**Which AI tool is best for captions?**
For most creators, the captions built into their editor. Vyra, Descript, CapCut, and Veed all caption automatically; Submagic and Captions specialize in styled captions for shorts.

**Is one all-in-one tool better than a stack?**
For social video, an editor that handles cutting, captions, reframing, music, and graphics from one prompt removes most of the stack. Vyra is built that way; you still want a writing assistant and a scheduler.
