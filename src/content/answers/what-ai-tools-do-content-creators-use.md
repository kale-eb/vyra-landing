**A working creator stack in 2026 is small. Two to four AI tools, not ten. The core is an editor that works on real footage (Vyra, Descript, or CapCut depending on format), a writing assistant for hooks and scripts (Claude or ChatGPT), and a scheduler. Long-form creators add a repurposing tool for clips. Generative video tools (Runway, Sora, Veo) show up for b-roll and ads, rarely for the main content.**

## Why

- Editing is where the hours were. The AI tool that changes a creator's week is the one that removes footage review and trimming.
- Almost every creator drafts hooks or scripts with Claude or ChatGPT. With MCP, that same assistant can now drive the editor.
- Captions are solved. Every serious editor captions automatically. Standalone caption tools survive on styling and speed.
- Audiences follow real people. Generated footage fills gaps, not feeds.

## The typical stack by creator type

| Creator | Editor | Writing | Repurposing | Other |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short-form vlogger | Vyra or CapCut | Claude or ChatGPT | none | scheduler |
| Podcaster | Descript or Riverside | ChatGPT for show notes | Opus Clip or Vyra | audio cleanup |
| YouTube educator | Vyra or Premiere | Claude for scripts | Vyra or Vizard | thumbnail tool |
| UGC creator | Vyra or CapCut | ChatGPT for hooks | none | none |
| Artist or process creator | Vyra | little or none | none | none |

## What to do about it

1. **Start with the editor.** It's the only tool that changes your hours. Real footage across formats, Vyra. Speech-heavy, Descript. Trend templates on a phone, CapCut.
2. **Use the assistant you already pay for.** For hooks, and over MCP, to drive Vyra. Don't add a second chatbot.
3. **Add repurposing only if you make long-form.**
4. **Skip generators until you have a specific gap.** A missing establishing shot, an ad concept.
5. **Cap the stack at four.** More tools means more exporting and re-uploading.

## FAQ

**Do professional creators use AI editors or hire editors?**
Both, increasingly together. The AI builds the rough cut and captions, a person does the finishing pass. Solo creators often skip the person.

**Which AI tool is best for captions?**
For most people, the captions built into their editor. Submagic and Captions specialize in styled captions for shorts.

**Is one all-in-one tool better than a stack?**
For social video, an editor that does cutting, captions, reframing, music, and graphics from one prompt removes most of the stack. You still want a writing assistant and a scheduler.
