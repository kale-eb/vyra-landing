**The caption field in iPhone Photos is descriptive metadata for finding a photo or video. It does not place timed words into the video picture. To share visible subtitles, add them in a video editor and export a captioned copy.**

You may type a description under a video and expect it to show up when someone plays the file. The note helps organize the library. Timed speech captions serve a different job.

## Decide where the words need to appear

| Need | Suitable text |
| --- | --- |
| Find a clip later in Photos | A descriptive metadata caption |
| Show spoken words throughout a shared video | Timed video captions |
| Explain one object in the picture | An on-screen text label |

[Apple’s photo-information guide](https://support.apple.com/en-asia/guide/iphone/iph0edb9c18f/27/ios/27) identifies the caption field as metadata used to describe an item and make it easier to find. The guide explains the purpose of the field.

## Make a separate captioned copy

The [official Vyra iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012) documents generated captions from supplied footage. Add your recording to a project, generate its speech captions, review spelling and placement, then export. Keep the original recording while checking the saved copy. New iPhone accounts receive 100 free credits. Free iPhone exports are 720p with a watermark. Check the App Store’s Plus and Pro options for paid limits.

Try a brief tied to the words in your recording.

> Caption the speech in this clip. Keep the product in view and use short phrases. Preserve the spoken audio.

Play the exported file muted. That checks whether the words are in the picture rather than relying on a note or a player’s caption display.

## FAQ

**Should I delete my Photos description before editing?**
You can keep a descriptive note for your own organization. The note and the visible video captions have separate purposes.

**Will the exported caption wording always match the recording?**
Review the generated text against the audio. Names, quantities and unclear speech still need a spelling and timing check.
