**Yes, to start. On the web, Vyra has a 3-day free trial, so you can upload your own footage, ask the agent for an edit, and see the result before you pay. In the iPhone app, new accounts get 100 free credits, and free exports are 720p with a watermark. When you want to keep going, plans start at $24 a month if you bring your own Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor, or $54 a month with Vyra's built-in AI. Plans are monthly and you can cancel anytime.**

## What the plans cost

| Plan | Monthly | AI | Export |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3-day trial (web) | $0 | Try the editor and the agent | |
| iPhone free tier | $0 | 100 credits | 720p with a watermark |
| MCP Starter | $24 | Your own Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor over MCP | Up to 1440p |
| MCP Pro | $65 | Your own AI, more credits and storage | Up to 4K |
| Vyra AI Starter | $54 | Built-in AI, no other subscription | Up to 1440p |
| Vyra AI Pro | $129 | Built-in AI, more credits and storage | Up to 4K |

Every paid plan includes 1,000 exports a month. Starter plans have 6,000 processing credits and 100 GB of storage. Pro plans have 20,000 credits and 500 GB. Full details are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Which plan to pick

- **You already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor.** Get an MCP plan. It's the cheapest way in and uses the assistant you have.
- **You want everything in one app.** Get a Vyra AI plan. The chat is built in, so you don't need another subscription.
- **You post in 4K or edit long footage.** Get a Pro plan for 4K export and more credits.

## What credits pay for

Credits cover the work Vyra does on your footage. Transcribing it, describing every scene, and each editing turn. Longer footage uses more. A few short clips use very little, a two-hour podcast uses more.

## Try it

Start the trial at [app.usevyra.com](https://app.usevyra.com), or open the iPhone app. Upload one real clip and paste this.

```
Cut this down to a 60-second vertical video. Keep the best takes, cut the pauses and filler words, and add short captions in the lower third.
```

Do it inside the 3-day trial. If the cut is close to what you'd make yourself, the paid plan is worth it.

## FAQ

**Do I need a Claude or ChatGPT subscription to use Vyra?**
No. Vyra AI plans include the agent. MCP plans cost less if you already pay for an assistant.

**Is there a Vyra free trial?**
Yes. The web app has a 3-day free trial.

**Is there a free version of Vyra?**
In the iPhone app. New accounts currently get 100 free credits. Free exports are 720p and carry a Vyra watermark. The Plus and Pro plans in the App Store add more credits and export options.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Plans are monthly.

**Is there a free alternative to Vyra?**
CapCut and Clipchamp both have free plans with a manual timeline. Neither edits from a description of the cut you want. See the [CapCut comparison](/compare/vyra-vs-capcut) and [Clipchamp comparison](/compare/vyra-vs-clipchamp).

**Does the iPhone app cost extra?**
The app is free to download. On iPhone you upgrade to the Plus or Pro plan in the App Store, and the App Store bills it. The web plans on this page are bought at app.usevyra.com.
