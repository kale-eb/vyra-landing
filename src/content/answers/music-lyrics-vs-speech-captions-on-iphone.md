**In Vyra on iPhone, choose the intended source under Captions and Generate from. Music lyrics, Voiceover, Video only and All audio serve different recordings. Pick the sound the viewer needs to follow before generating captions.**

A studio video can contain a song under a spoken explanation. If the explanation is the point, check that the captions follow it rather than the chorus. If you made a lyric-led edit, review the song wording and timing instead.

## Choose the audio you mean

The current Captions help documents the native Generate from choices. Match them to the audio actually present in the project.

| Situation | Starting check |
| --- | --- |
| A recorded voiceover explains the process | Review the Voiceover source |
| Speech belongs to the supplied video | Review Video only |
| The words of a song are the point | Review Music lyrics |
| Several sound sources matter | Review All audio and inspect what was transcribed |

## Check the competing sound

Choose the spoken language where applicable, generate, and compare the caption text with the intended voice. A source selection does not establish perfect recognition when voices and music overlap.

> Caption my voiceover explaining the paint mixture. Keep the background song audible at a lower level, but do not put its lyrics on screen.

For a lyric-led edit, describe the lyric source instead. Keep track of which changes affect the soundtrack and which affect the words shown. If you choose Replace captions, review the new track before relying on earlier text corrections.

## FAQ

**Does choosing Music lyrics translate the song?**
That source choice identifies what to caption. It does not establish translation or dubbing.

**Can the source choice fix unclear words automatically?**
Review the transcript against the recording. The right source is a starting point, not proof that every word was recognized correctly.
