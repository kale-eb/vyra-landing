**Yes, with one caveat about what "understand" means. A modern AI editor transcribes every spoken word with timestamps and writes a description of every scene. Who's in frame, what they're doing, what's visible, when the shot changes. That's enough to find "the part where I hold up the canvas" without scrubbing. It isn't taste. The AI knows what's in the clip. You still decide what the video is about.**

## Why

- Two layers. Speech is transcribed word by word. Picture is analyzed for scene changes, people, objects, motion, and setting.
- A 40-minute upload becomes a list of moments you can search in plain language.
- With three takes of the same line, the agent can see which one is clean and which has a stumble.
- The agent knows you have four wide shots of the street and one close-up of the sign, so it can pick b-roll to cover a cut.
- Pauses, ums, and restarts show up in the transcript timing and can be removed by rule.

## What it doesn't do

- Judge whether your story is good.
- Know your audience or brand unless you tell it.
- Read a reference video's style unless you point it there and say what to copy.

## What to do about it

1. **Upload everything.** Don't pre-trim. It's better at finding the good take than you are at remembering which file it's in.
2. **Ask before you edit.** "Summarize what's in my footage" gets you a map in seconds.
3. **Search by content.** "Find every moment I mention the deadline."
4. **Give selection rules.** "Prefer takes where I don't restart a sentence."
5. **Check the frames.** The agent can capture stills at any timestamp.

## Do it in Vyra

```
Before editing anything, summarize what's in my footage. How many clips, where they were shot, who's in them, which ones have me talking to camera. List the three strongest moments with timestamps.
```

```
Find every clip where the painting is visible on the easel and list them with a one-line description. Then build a 30-second montage from the five best, slow to fast.
```

## FAQ

**Does it work on footage with no speech?**
Yes. Scene analysis runs on the picture. Silent b-roll gets described and is searchable.

**How long does the analysis take?**
It runs automatically after upload. A 35-minute file indexed in under 2 minutes in a side-by-side test.

**Can I see what the AI thinks is in a clip?**
Yes. Ask for the description of any clip, or open it in the footage library.
