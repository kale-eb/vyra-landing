**No. Generative AI can make video from text, but it can't make the thing people actually follow, which is a real person with a real life and footage that happened. What AI is replacing is the hours between filming and posting. A creator who used to spend six hours editing a vlog now spends one. The creator stays. The timeline shrinks.**

## Why

- Audiences follow people. Engagement on social video tracks the person on screen and how consistent their voice is. A synthetic host has no history and no stakes.
- Real footage carries proof. A painting reveal, a first day at a new job, a bad take that stays in. These work because they happened.
- Generators (Runway, Sora, Veo) make new clips. Editors that understand footage (Vyra, Descript) work on what you shot. The second kind makes creators faster. The first mostly makes ads and b-roll.
- The bottleneck was never ideas. It was the edit.

## What's actually changing

| Task | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Filming | Creator | Creator |
| Reviewing footage | Creator, hours | AI indexes it on upload |
| Rough cut | Creator or hired editor | AI from a description |
| Captions, reframing, music | Creator, tedious | AI, one prompt |
| Hook, story, ending | Creator | Creator, with drafts to react to |
| Being the reason anyone watches | Creator | Creator |

## What to do about it

1. **Keep your face and voice in the work.** Don't hand it to an avatar.
2. **Automate the mechanical steps.** Rough cut, captions, reframe, music.
3. **Spend the time on volume and range.** More formats, more posts, more experiments.
4. **Use generation where it belongs.** A generated b-roll shot over a real voiceover can work. A generated person can't replace you.
5. **Say your footage is real.** People are learning to spot the other kind.

## FAQ

**Will AI-generated creators take over feeds?**
Some feeds will fill with generated content and some of it will get views. Following and repeat viewing still cluster around real people.

**Is using AI to edit cheating?**
No more than autofocus. The creative decisions are still yours. Vyra only edits footage you shot.

**What should a creator learn now?**
Hooks, structure, and how to brief an editor, human or AI.
