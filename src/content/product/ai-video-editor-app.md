**Vyra is an AI video editor app for iPhone. You add the clips you filmed, describe the video you want in chat, and Vyra builds a draft from your footage. Then you fine-tune it on a timeline. Trim, split, rearrange, captions, music, and voiceover, all on your phone. It's free to download, and new accounts get 100 free credits.**

[Download Vyra on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vyra-ai-video-editor/id6808889012)

## What you can do in the app

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Chat edits | Describe the video and get a draft from your clips. Change pacing or swap shots by asking. |
| Timeline | Trim, split, and rearrange clips, adjust audio, add text, pick a canvas size |
| Captions | Generated from what you say, with styles to pick from |
| Music and voiceover | Add a track, record a voiceover, or type a script and pick an AI voice |
| Effects | An effects browser with keyframes |
| Transitions | Tap between two clips for Zoom in, Zoom out, Swipe, Light leak or Film burn |
| Camera | Record straight onto the timeline |
| Templates and references | Start from a template, or attach a reference video to guide the edit |
| Export | Save to Photos or share straight to an app |

## How it works

1. **Add your clips.** Pick them from Photos or record in the app. Vyra transcribes what you say and describes each scene.
2. **Describe the edit.** "45 seconds for TikTok, open on the funniest moment, short captions."
3. **Check the draft.** Ask for changes in plain words. "Tighter in the middle." "Make the captions smaller."
4. **Fine-tune by hand.** Open the timeline to trim a clip by a few frames or move a caption.
5. **Export.** Save to Photos and post.

## Limits

- iPhone only for now. There's no Android app, and Vyra doesn't publish APK files. See [Is Vyra on Android?](/answers/is-vyra-on-android)
- AI features need an internet connection. Analysis runs on Vyra's servers.
- Free exports are 720p and carry a watermark.
- Vyra edits footage you filmed. It isn't a generator that makes clips from a prompt.

## FAQ

**Is the Vyra app free?**
It's free to download. New accounts currently get 100 free credits, and free exports are 720p with a watermark. Plus and Pro plans in the App Store add more.

**What iPhone do I need?**
Any iPhone that runs iOS 17 or later.

**Is there an Android version?**
Not yet. On Android, use [app.usevyra.com](https://app.usevyra.com) from a computer.

**Do I need to know how to edit?**
No. Describe what you want in your own words. The timeline is there when you want to adjust something by hand.

**Can I edit videos on iPhone without Claude or ChatGPT?**
Yes. The app has built-in AI chat. Add footage from Photos and describe the edit there. Connecting an external AI assistant is optional.

**Can I use a Reel or TikTok as a style reference?**
Yes. Share it to Vyra, add your own footage and say what to follow, such as pacing or caption placement. Vyra uses your clips for the edit.
