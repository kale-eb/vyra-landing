**Vyra is a video editor. It has the timeline, tracks, captions, music, motion graphics, and export settings you'd expect, and an AI that has already watched your footage. Use it like a normal editor, describe the edit and let the AI build it, or switch between the two on the same project. It runs on the web at app.usevyra.com and on iPhone.**

## What's in the editor

| Part | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Timeline | Trim, split, rearrange, layer clips, adjust audio levels |
| Footage library | Every clip transcribed with timestamps and every scene described, so you can search what was said or shown |
| Captions | Generated from speech, styled by prompt or by hand, with word-level emphasis |
| Text and titles | Title cards, lower thirds, and text overlays |
| Music | Add a track, duck it under speech, cut to the beat |
| Motion graphics | Animated lists, callouts, and titles from a description |
| Masks | Put text behind a person, or reframe with a tracked mask |
| Presets | Save caption styles and graphics and reuse them across projects |
| Export | MP4 (H.264) up to 4K on Pro plans, or WebM up to 1080p |

## Two ways to edit, one project

**By hand.** Drag clips on the timeline, trim to the frame, type a caption, set a volume. The editor works like any other.

**By description.** Type what you want and the AI does the clicking. "Cut this to 60 seconds, best takes only." "Make 'eighty percent' bigger and yellow." It edits the same timeline you see, so you can take over at any point.

Most people do both. Let the AI make the rough cut and the captions, then fix the last few things by hand.

## FAQ

**Is Vyra a real video editor or just an AI tool?**
A real editor. There's a full timeline, and the AI works on that same timeline.

**Can I edit without the AI?**
Yes. Everything the AI does, you can do by hand.

**What does it export?**
MP4 (H.264) at 480p, 720p, 1080p, or original size, up to 4K on Pro plans, and WebM up to 1080p.

**Is there a free version?**
The iPhone app gives new accounts 100 free credits. The web app has a 3-day free trial. See [Is Vyra free?](/answers/is-vyra-free)
