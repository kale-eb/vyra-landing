**Vyra is an AI video editor that runs in your browser. Go to app.usevyra.com, upload your clips, and describe the video you want. Vyra transcribes and describes every clip, builds the timeline, and you export an MP4 up to 4K. There's nothing to download, and the heavy work happens on Vyra's servers, so it runs on an ordinary laptop.**

[Start the 3-day free trial](https://app.usevyra.com)

## Why edit in the browser

- **No install, no updates.** Open the tab and the editor is there, on any Mac or Windows computer.
- **Your laptop doesn't do the heavy work.** Transcription, scene analysis, and rendering run on Vyra's servers. You edit smooth 720p proxies, and the export uses your originals.
- **Your projects follow you.** Sign in on another computer or on the iPhone app and your work is there.
- **Your AI can drive it.** Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor connect over MCP and edit the same project while you watch the tab.

## How it works

1. **Open app.usevyra.com and upload.** MP4, MOV, and common phone and camera formats, plus images and audio.
2. **Wait for indexing.** Every word gets a timestamp and every scene gets a description.
3. **Describe the edit.** Length, platform, what to keep, captions, music.
4. **Adjust.** Ask for changes in chat or open the timeline and drag.
5. **Export.** MP4 (H.264) at 480p, 720p, 1080p, original size, or 4K on Pro plans.

## Tips for browser editing

- Keep the Vyra tab in front while it exports. Browsers slow down background tabs.
- Upload the original files, not screen recordings or re-exports. Re-encoding loses quality.
- Use Chrome, Edge, or another current browser.

## FAQ

**Do I need to download anything?**
No. Vyra runs at app.usevyra.com. There's also an optional iPhone app.

**Does it work on Windows?**
Yes. Any current browser on Windows or Mac.

**Will it slow down my computer?**
Not much. Analysis and rendering run on Vyra's servers, and you edit lightweight proxies.

**Can I edit on my phone too?**
On iPhone, yes, with the [Vyra app](/ai-video-editor-app). Android isn't supported yet.
