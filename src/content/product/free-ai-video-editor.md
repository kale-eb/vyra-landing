## What credits pay for

Credits cover the work Vyra does on your footage. Transcribing it, describing every scene, and each editing turn. Short phone clips use few credits. A long recording uses more, so try free credits on a short video first.

## Getting the most out of free

1. **Start with short clips.** Three or four phone clips for a 30-second video, not an hour of raw footage.
2. **Write the whole edit in one prompt.** Length, platform, captions, music. Fewer rounds means fewer credits.
3. **Fix small things on the timeline.** Moving a caption by hand costs nothing.
4. **Use the web trial for the big project.** Long footage and 4K belong on a web plan.

## FAQ

**Is there a completely free AI video editor?**
Most have a free tier with limits on resolution, watermarks, or AI usage. Vyra's free tier is in the iPhone app, with 100 credits and 720p exports with a watermark.

**Does the free version have a watermark?**
Yes. Free exports from the iPhone app carry a Vyra watermark. Paid plans remove it.

**What happens after the web trial?**
You pick a plan from $24 a month, or stop. See /pricing.
