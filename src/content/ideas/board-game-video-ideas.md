**Explain one decision at the table. Keep the relevant pieces visible and distinguish your personal strategy from the game’s official rules.**

These ideas are for players filming their own tabletop games. Choose the one you can support with your own recordings. Keep the explanation tied to what the camera actually shows.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. One turn with two choices

Show the board before the turn, describe two available choices and show the one you took. Keep labels near the relevant pieces.

### 2. The rule we checked

Film the situation that raised a question and explain the rule you verified in the official rulebook. Avoid reproducing more material than needed.

### 3. A final board with one earlier moment

Start with the final state and show the earlier turn that helps explain it. Make the time jump clear so viewers do not mistake the order.

## FAQ

**Should I explain the whole game first?**
Include enough context for this decision. Link or refer to the official rules for a full introduction instead of rushing every rule into a short clip.

**How do I keep the board readable on a phone?**
Use a close view of the pieces that matter and test the exported frame on a phone. Avoid placing captions directly over those pieces.
