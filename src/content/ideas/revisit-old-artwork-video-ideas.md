**Put the older work on screen before describing how you see it now. Keep the recorded dates and the reasons for a new decision accurate.**

These ideas are for artists reflecting on work they made earlier. Choose the one your own recordings can support. Keep the explanation and the visible outcome connected.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The choice I would keep

Show the older piece and explain one decision that still works for you. Add a current example only when it helps the viewer see the connection.

### 2. The detail I now notice

Move from a full view to the relevant area. Explain what you observe now without pretending the old recording shows an intention you never documented.

### 3. The new attempt at the same idea

Show the old work and your current attempt in a clear order. Keep them labelled so the viewer can compare the actual difference.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source clips it needs.
2. Add your footage to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the wording or sounds that must remain.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.
5. Play the saved export before posting it.

> Show the earlier painting before my current attempt. Keep the dates I supplied accurate and hold the detail where I changed the composition.

## FAQ

**Should I make the older work look worse for the comparison?**
Use a fair view of each piece. Different lighting or cropping can change the apparent comparison.

**Can I add a year if I do not know when I made it?**
Use the date you can support or describe it more generally. Do not give an uncertain date the appearance of a documented one.
