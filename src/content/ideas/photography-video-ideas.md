**Show the decision behind one photograph. A change in framing or light gives the viewer something specific to compare.**

These ideas are for photographers. Choose the one you can support with your own recordings. Keep the explanation tied to what the camera actually shows.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The frame you left out

Film the wider scene, show your chosen crop and finish with the photograph. Explain what you removed and why. Keep the final image large enough to inspect.

### 2. One subject, two light positions

Record the same subject under two positions of light and show the resulting photographs in the same order. Keep labels attached to the right example.

### 3. The contact sheet decision

Show a few of your own candidate photographs and explain why one works for this use. Hold each long enough to see the difference instead of flashing the whole sheet.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source footage that explains it.
2. Add your clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the words or sounds that must stay intact.
4. Review the draft for missing context and text covering important details.
5. Export and play the saved video before sharing it.

## FAQ

**Should I show every photograph from the session?**
Choose the few that explain the decision. A large collection can make it harder to see why the final image was selected.

**Can I mix still images with the recorded explanation?**
Vyra supports supplied photos and video. Review how long each image stays visible so the viewer can inspect what you describe.
