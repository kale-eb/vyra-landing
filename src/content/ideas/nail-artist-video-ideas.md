**Give a finished nail set a clear close-up and enough time to see the details. Film any process shots around the feature you want the viewer to notice.**

These ideas are for nail artists filming their own material. Pick the one that fits the clips you can make or already have. Keep the useful explanation and give the finished result enough screen time to see it.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The detail under a moving hand

Record a close, slow change in hand angle so the design catches the light. Keep a steady view as well. Choose the part where the detail is visible and avoid hiding it under the title.

### 2. One design across both hands

Film the hands together, then a closer view of one repeated element. End with the whole set again. Keep orientation consistent so viewers can understand how the design repeats.

### 3. The color choice beside the result

Show the color sample you chose and the finished set under similar light. Keep the sample label readable. Use a short voiceover about the choice if it adds something the images do not explain.

## FAQ

**How long should I hold the finished set?**
Hold it long enough to see the detail at phone size. Replay the draft once without pausing to check.

**How do I avoid text covering the nails?**
Place labels in the clear part of the shot and review every movement while the text is visible.
