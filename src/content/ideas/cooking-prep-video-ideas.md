**Give a cooking-prep video one practical outcome. Keep the step or texture change the viewer needs to see, even when the rest of the preparation is shortened.**

These ideas are for home cooks filming their own preparation. Choose the one you can support with your own recordings. Keep the explanation tied to what the camera actually shows.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The texture before the next step

Show the mixture before and after the visible change you are explaining. Keep the close-up steady and preserve your own checked recipe wording.

### 2. One component used two ways

Film the prepared component and the two finished uses. Make clear which ingredient belongs to which dish.

### 3. What I prepared ahead

Show the parts you actually prepared and what you finished later. State storage and food-safety guidance only when supported by a reliable source.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source footage that explains it.
2. Add your clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the words or sounds that must stay intact.
4. Review the draft for missing context and text covering important details.
5. Export and play the saved video before sharing it.

## A brief to adapt

> Keep the texture check at normal speed. Shorten the repeated stirring and label the two finished uses separately. Preserve my recipe quantities exactly as recorded.

## FAQ

**Should I cut out all repeated preparation?**
Shorten repetition that adds little, while keeping the action needed to understand the result. A texture check may deserve more time than the stirring before it.

**Can the edit fill in a missing cooking step?**
Record the missing step or explain that it is absent. Do not make the finished sequence imply a complete demonstrated recipe when important footage is missing.
