**Use consistent views to show the shape, length or finish you want people to notice. Keep enough of the final result on screen for the style to read.**

These ideas are for hair stylists filming their own material. Pick the one that fits the clips you can make or already have. Keep the useful explanation and give the finished result enough screen time to see it.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The same turn before and after

Film the starting and finished views with a similar head turn and camera position. Keep the cut between matching angles. An unrelated angle change can hide the feature you intended to show.

### 2. The finished shape in motion

Record a steady side view and a slow movement that shows how the finished hair sits. Use the clear portion of each take. Keep the final silhouette visible before the end card.

### 3. One detail and the whole style

Start on a visible braid, part or texture detail, then show the whole finished style. Put any explanation in a short label beside the detail. End with the wider view so the close-up has context.

## FAQ

**How do I make the comparison easy to follow?**
Keep camera position and the subject’s pose similar. Review the same area on both sides of the cut.

**Should the final view be faster than the process?**
Give the finished style enough time to inspect. Shorten the waiting in the process before shortening the result.
