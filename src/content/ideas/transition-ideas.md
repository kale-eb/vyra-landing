**A transition is the cut between two shots that makes a change feel planned. The best ones are filmed, then trimmed. You end the first shot and start the second with the same motion, the same shape, or the same thing covering the lens, and the cut hides inside it. Here are 28 transition ideas in five types. Each one says how to film it, which format it fits, and gives a prompt that cuts it in Vyra.**

The transition that did best for me was a plain hard cut. A blank canvas, then the finished painting on the beat, and that reel got 641K views. Where you put the cut matters more than how clever it is.

## How to use this list

1. Pick the transition before you film. Most of them need both shots planned.
2. End shot one and start shot two in the same part of the frame, moving at the same speed.
3. Film each side two or three times so you can pick the pair that lines up.
4. Paste the prompt into Vyra with both clips, then watch the cut at full speed and at half speed.

## Cover the lens

### 1. The hand cover
Move your palm toward the lens until the frame goes dark, then pull it away somewhere new. The dark frame hides the cut completely.
> "Okay, let's go."

Fits [vlog](/formats/vlog) and [day in the life](/formats/day-in-the-life-story).
```
Find the two clips where my hand covers the lens. Cut clip one on the frame where my palm fills the screen and start clip two on its darkest frame. Put the cut on the next beat of the track and add a 2 frame white flash at the join.
```

### 2. Walk into the lens
Walk straight at the camera until your shirt blocks the view. In the next shot, walk away from the camera in the new place, starting from the same blocked frame.

Fits [travel recap](/formats/travel-recap).
```
Cut the walk-in clip on the frame where my body blocks the whole lens. Start the next clip on its first fully blocked frame so I walk away from camera in the new location. Keep the city ambience running under the cut and duck the music by 30 percent for that second.
```

### 3. Throw something at the camera
Toss a pillow, jacket, or towel at the lens. Start the next shot with the same object being pulled away from the lens.
> "Get ready with me."

Fits [skit](/formats/skit) and [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video).
```
Join the two clips at the moment the jacket hits the lens. Speed up the last 6 frames of the throw to 150 percent so it lands hard, then let the second clip play at normal speed. Captions stay centered, white with a black outline.
```

### 4. Close the book
Film your sketchbook or notebook from above and close it toward the lens. Open the next shot on the cover, then flip it open to a new page or a new day.

Fits [sketchbook flip-through](/formats/sketchbook-flip-through).
```
Cut on the frame where the closing sketchbook cover fills the screen. Open the next clip on the cover and play the page turn. Add a soft paper rustle sound at the cut and keep the shot overhead and square to the frame, 9:16.
```

## Move the camera

### 5. The whip pan
Swing the phone fast to one side at the end of shot one. Start shot two mid-swing in the same direction, then settle on the new subject. The motion blur carries the cut.

Fits [travel recap](/formats/travel-recap) and [beat-synced edit](/formats/beat-synced-edit).
```
Line up the two whip pans so the blur from clip one flows into the blur at the start of clip two, both moving left to right. Cut in the middle of the blur, on the snare. Add a directional blur of 4 frames on each side of the cut.
```

### 6. The spin
Turn in place with the phone held at arm's length, so the background smears. Start the next shot mid-turn in the new place.
> "Day two."

Fits [vlog](/formats/vlog).
```
Cut both spin clips at the fastest point of the turn. Add a 0.2 second zoom-in on clip one before the cut and a matching zoom-out on clip two after it. Put "day two" as text in the top third on the first steady frame of the new clip.
```

### 7. Tilt to the sky
Tilt the camera up to the sky or the ceiling at the end of a scene. Start the next scene tilting down from the sky somewhere else. Use it to jump hours or cities.

Fits [day in the life](/formats/day-in-the-life-story) and [cinematic b-roll](/formats/cinematic-b-roll).
```
Cut on the frame where clip one shows only sky, and start clip two from its sky frame as it tilts down. Cross-dissolve over 8 frames. Add a small time stamp in the lower left, "6:40 pm", that fades in when the new scene settles.
```

### 8. Zoom through
Push the camera into something dark, like a mug, a pocket, or a doorway. Start the next shot pulling out of something dark.

Fits [cinematic b-roll](/formats/cinematic-b-roll) and [montage](/formats/montage).
```
Keyframe a scale-up on the end of the mug clip from 100 to 180 percent over the last 10 frames. Start the next clip at 180 percent on its darkest frame and scale back to 100 percent over 10 frames. Add a whoosh sound across the join.
```

### 9. Walk past a wall
Track sideways past a pillar, tree, or doorframe so it fills the frame for a moment. Start the next shot coming out from behind a different object, moving the same way.

Fits [silent vlog](/formats/silent-vlog).
```
Cut on the frame where the pillar fills the screen in clip one, and start clip two on the frame where the tree fills the screen. Both move right to left. No music for this section, just the room sound from each clip, crossfaded over 6 frames.
```

## Match the frame

### 10. Shape match
End on a round thing and start on a round thing in the same spot. A clock to a plate, a lamp to the moon, a paint lid to a coffee cup.

Fits [montage](/formats/montage) and [cinematic b-roll](/formats/cinematic-b-roll).
```
Line up the paint lid in clip one and the coffee cup in clip two so the circles sit in the same place on screen. Scale and position clip two to match, then hard cut with no effect. Hold each shot for 1.5 seconds.
```

### 11. Action match
Start an action in one place and finish it in another. Open a door at home, step out of a door at the studio.

Fits [day in the life](/formats/day-in-the-life-story) and [storytelling video](/formats/storytelling-video).
```
Cut in the middle of the door opening. Use the first half of the motion from the home clip and the second half from the studio clip, so the door swing looks continuous. Keep my voiceover running across the cut with captions in the bottom third.
```

### 12. Gesture match across days
Reach for the same tool every day from the same angle. Cut on the reach, and the days stack up without a single title card.

Fits [process video](/formats/process-video) and [all-nighter painting vlog](/formats/all-nighter-painting-vlog).
```
Find every clip where my hand reaches for the brush from the right side. Cut each one at the moment my fingers touch the handle and play them in date order, 0.8 seconds each. Add a small day counter in the top right that changes at each cut.
```

### 13. Color match
End on a big block of one color and start on the same color. A red door to a red dress, a blue wall to a blue sky.
> "Everything blue this week."

Fits [montage](/formats/montage).
```
Order the clips so each one ends and the next one starts on the same dominant color. Hard cut on every second beat. Put "everything blue this week" in lowercase at the bottom, holding for the first 3 seconds.
```

### 14. Same frame, different time
Lock the phone on a tripod and leave it. Film the same frame in the morning, at night, before and after. The room stays still while time jumps.

Fits [before and after](/formats/before-after-transformation) and [art timelapse](/formats/art-timelapse).
```
All of these clips are from the same locked tripod spot. Play them in time order and cut each one on the beat, 1 second each. Add a 4 frame cross-dissolve so the light shift reads as one continuous change. Time of day as small text in the lower right.
```

### 15. The hand, then the result
Frame your hand making a mark, then cut to the finished work in the exact same framing. The viewer connects the gesture to the outcome. My video about the hand versus what it created got 160K views.

Fits [faceless video](/formats/faceless-video) and [speed paint](/formats/speed-paint).
```
Alternate close-ups of my hand working with the matching area of the finished painting, framed the same way. Cut on each beat for 8 pairs. No captions and no voice, music only. End on a 3 second wide shot of the full piece.
```

## Change on the beat

### 16. Outfit change on the beat
Stand in the same spot, same pose, and film one take per outfit. Cut on every beat so the clothes change while you stay still.

Fits [beat-synced edit](/formats/beat-synced-edit).
```
Line up all the outfit clips so I'm in the same position in each. Cut on every beat for the first 8 beats, then every half beat for the next 8. Lock the framing so my head stays in the same spot across every cut.
```

### 17. Jump on the beat
Jump in one place and land in another. Film the takeoff in the first place and the landing in the second, at the same height in frame.

Fits [travel recap](/formats/travel-recap) and [vlog](/formats/vlog).
```
Cut each jump at its highest point and start the next clip at the highest point of the next jump. Place every cut on the kick drum. Slow the top 6 frames of each jump to 50 percent for a short float, then back to normal speed for the landing.
```

### 18. Head drop
Look down at the end of a shot. Look up at the start of the next one, with a different look, hair, or setting. Simple, and it works with any face-to-camera video.

Fits [talking head](/formats/talking-head) and [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video).
```
Cut on the frame where my face points down in clip one, and start clip two on the frame where my face is still down. Put the cut on the beat. Zoom in 8 percent on clip two as I look up.
```

### 19. Object pass
Hand an object toward the lens from one side. In the next shot, someone else, or you in a different place, takes it from the other side.

Fits [skit](/formats/skit) and [day in the life](/formats/day-in-the-life-story).
```
Cut on the frame where the coffee cup is closest to the lens and fills the most of the frame. Start the next clip on its closest frame, then let it move out of the other side. Add a soft whoosh under the cut and keep the music at full level.
```

### 20. Throw and catch
Throw something out of the top of the frame. Catch it in the next shot, somewhere else, or as someone else.
> "Your turn."

Fits [skit](/formats/skit).
```
Cut on the frame where the ball leaves the top of clip one, and start clip two on the frame where it enters from the top. Put "your turn" as text in the middle on the catch, then remove it after 1 second.
```

### 21. The finger snap
Snap your fingers toward the camera and freeze. In the next shot, hold the same pose with the room cleaned, the hair done, or the canvas finished.
> "Watch this."

Fits [before and after](/formats/before-after-transformation) and [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video).
```
Cut on the exact frame of my snap and line up the second clip so my hand is in the same pose. Add a short snap sound effect at the cut and a 3 frame scale bump from 100 to 106 percent on the new clip.
```

### 22. The beat photo dump
Cut a whole season of clips to the music, one beat each. The rhythm is the transition. My recap of everything I painted last semester at Brown was cut this way and got 29.2K views.

Fits [semester recap montage](/formats/semester-recap-montage) and [beat-synced edit](/formats/beat-synced-edit).
```
Use every clip of a finished painting, in date order. Cut each one on the beat, 1 beat each, and switch to half beats for the last 8 clips. End on the newest painting held for 2 seconds as the track resolves.
```

## Build it in the edit

### 23. Mask wipe
Walk across the frame in shot one. In the edit, a mask follows your body and reveals shot two behind you as you pass.

Fits [vlog](/formats/vlog) and [cinematic b-roll](/formats/cinematic-b-roll).
```
Track a mask on me as I walk left to right across clip one. Use it as a wipe so clip two appears behind me as I pass. The wipe should take the full length of my walk, about 1 second, with a soft 10 pixel edge.
```

### 24. Text behind you
Put a big word on screen, then mask yourself so the text sits behind you. Change the background shot while you stay in front.
> "Next stop."

Fits [vlog](/formats/vlog) and [travel recap](/formats/travel-recap).
```
Add "NEXT STOP" in large bold white text, centered, behind me using a person mask. After 1.5 seconds, swap the background to the next location clip while the text and I stay in place. Cut the swap on the beat.
```

### 25. Speed ramp
Speed the end of one clip up to a blur, then slow the start of the next one back to normal. Works on any shot with motion.

Fits [beat-synced edit](/formats/beat-synced-edit) and [travel recap](/formats/travel-recap).
```
Ramp the last 0.5 seconds of each clip from 100 to 400 percent and the first 0.5 seconds of the next clip from 400 back to 100. Put the fastest frame of each ramp on the beat. Keep the music at constant speed.
```

### 26. Zoom punch on the beat
Punch the frame in by 10 to 20 percent on a beat, then cut while zoomed. It turns a static talking shot into something with rhythm.

Fits [talking head](/formats/talking-head) and [listicle talking head](/formats/listicle-talking-head).
```
On each new point in my list, punch in 15 percent on the first word, then cut to the next shot at normal scale. Add the point number as large text in the top left for 1 second at each punch.
```

### 27. Sound first
Let the audio of the next scene start a second before its picture. The viewer hears the change, then sees it.

Fits [storytelling video](/formats/storytelling-video) and [silent vlog](/formats/silent-vlog).
```
For each scene change, start the next clip's audio 1 second before its picture. Fade the incoming sound up over that second and fade the outgoing sound down. No music under these transitions.
```

### 28. Frame in a frame
Play the next clip inside something in the current shot, like a phone screen, a picture frame, or a canvas. Then scale it up to fill the screen.

Fits [process video](/formats/process-video) and [storytelling video](/formats/storytelling-video).
```
Place the next clip inside the phone screen in my hand, matched to its corners. After 1 second, scale it up to full screen over 12 frames so it becomes the main shot. Add a soft click sound when it reaches full size.
```

## FAQ

**Do I need a gimbal for whip pans and spins?**
No. A phone held in both hands works if both shots move at the same speed and in the same direction. Shoot a few takes and keep the pair that matches.

**How long should a transition last?**
Most hide inside 2 to 6 frames of blur or cover. If you can see the trick at full speed, trim a frame or two from each side.

**How many transitions should one video have?**
One to three in a short video, placed where the place, outfit, or time changes. Plain cuts carry the rest.

**Can I add a transition to footage I already filmed?**
Edit transitions like mask wipes, zoom punches, and speed ramps work on any footage. In-camera ones like the hand cover need both shots planned before you film.
