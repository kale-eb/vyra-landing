**Give the session a small visible task and an honest stopping point. Use recorded times if you show a time limit, and keep unfinished work labelled accurately.**

These ideas are for makers documenting a limited session they actually recorded. Choose the one your own recordings can support. Keep the explanation and the visible outcome connected.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. One decision before stopping

Show the starting work, the decision you focused on and where you stopped. The video can end with progress rather than a completed piece.

### 2. The constraint I chose

Explain the material, time or subject constraint you actually used. Keep the resulting choices visible instead of adding an invented timer.

### 3. What I would do next

Finish on the current state and explain one next step. Keep future plans separate from the process already recorded.

## FAQ

**Do I need a countdown to make the video interesting?**
A clear task can be enough. Use a countdown only when it reflects the session you actually recorded.

**Should I hide unfinished areas at the end?**
Show the state that explains the session honestly. An unfinished area can help the viewer understand the next step.
