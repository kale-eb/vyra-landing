**Build a commission video around one decision you can show. Use your own work and describe the brief without exposing private client information.**

These ideas are for artists documenting an early commission. Choose the one you can support with footage or records you already have. Keep the explanation close to what the viewer can actually see.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The brief in my own words

State the part of the brief you can share, show your first sketch and finish with the delivered work. Keep identifying details out of the footage unless you have permission.

### 2. The revision that helped

Show two stages and explain one change you made. Keep the reason attached to the visual comparison rather than inventing a customer reaction.

### 3. The detail before delivery

Film the completed detail, then the full piece and your own packing footage. Give the work a clear ending before any next-action text.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source clips that explain it.
2. Add your footage to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and any wording or original sound that must remain.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.
5. Export and inspect the saved file before posting it.

## A brief to adapt

> Use my sketch, revised detail and finished piece. Keep the client’s name and messages out of view. Hold the final work long enough to see the detail.

## FAQ

**Do I need to show the client’s message?**
You can explain the permitted brief in your own words. Get permission before publishing private messages or identifying details.

**Should I add a review if I have not received one?**
Use the outcome you can document. Do not invent a quotation, reaction or delivery result.
