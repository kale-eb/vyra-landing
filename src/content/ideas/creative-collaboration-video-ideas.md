**Show what each collaborator contributed and how the parts came together. Agree on the material you can publish before assembling the story.**

These ideas are for creators explaining a shared project. Choose the one your own recordings can support. Keep the explanation and the visible outcome connected.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. One handoff

Show the state of the work before a handoff and the next collaborator’s contribution. Label roles clearly when the footage would otherwise be ambiguous.

### 2. The decision we changed together

Use the actual versions or process clips to explain the change. Keep attributed comments accurate and approved.

### 3. The finished work and its separate parts

Start with the result, then show the contributions that explain it. Return to the complete work so the viewer can place the parts.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

## FAQ

**Should I use one credit at the end?**
Choose credits that make the actual contributions clear. Review names and roles with the collaborators before sharing.

**Can I join separate process clips into one apparent session?**
Make time or location changes clear when they matter. Do not imply a continuous shared session if the clips came from separate work.
