**Show the care and sequence of packing while keeping addresses, order numbers and private notes outside the published frame.**

These ideas are for makers filming their own packing process. Choose the one you can support with footage or records you already have. Keep the explanation close to what the viewer can actually see.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The detail before the wrapping

Show the finished work clearly, then one wrapping action and the closed package. Let the viewer see the piece before it disappears.

### 2. The packing sound sequence

Choose a short section of the real paper, tape and box sounds. Keep those sounds aligned with the visible actions instead of adding an invented handling claim.

### 3. The final check

Film the check you actually performed before closing the package. Avoid showing an address label or implying that the edit proves shipping protection.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source clips that explain it.

3. Describe the sequence and any wording or original sound that must remain.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.

## FAQ

**Should I blur the address after filming it?**
Keep sensitive details out of the source footage when possible. Review every frame and reflection before sharing a packing video.

**Can I say the packaging guarantees safe delivery?**
Show the packing choices you actually made. A short recording does not establish a delivery guarantee or a shipping test result.
