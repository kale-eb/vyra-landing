**Art content works when the viewer gets to watch something change. A blank surface becomes a painting, a sketch becomes a final, a messy night becomes a finished piece by morning. You already make the thing, so the content is mostly about where you point the phone. Here are 30 art video ideas in six types, almost all taken from my own posts as a painter, with the real view counts and a prompt that edits each one in Vyra.**

I'm Sulan, I paint, and I post as @sulansart. My most viewed art reels are a two-clip reveal at 641K, the "longing" reveal at 464K, and my middle school drawings at 395K. None of them needed a new skill. They needed the phone propped up before I started.

## How to use this list

1. Prop the phone up before every session, even when you don't plan to post. Most of these ideas come from footage you'd otherwise never film.
2. Pick an idea that fits the piece you're working on this week.
3. Film the start, a few middle moments, and the finished piece in the same framing.
4. Upload everything to Vyra, paste the prompt, and change the title, size, or medium to match your piece.

## Reveals

### 1. Blank canvas to finished piece
The blank canvas, then the finished painting. Two clips and a song. Mine got 641K views.
> "About time I actually painted something."

Fits [before and after](/formats/before-after-transformation).
```
Use the blank canvas clip and the finished painting clip only. Play the blank canvas for 2 seconds with my caption in lowercase at the top, then cut to the finished piece on the beat. Hold the finished piece for 5 seconds with a slow push-in.
```

### 2. The titled reveal
Give the piece a name and its size, then show it. A title makes it feel like a finished work. My reveal of "longing", 28 x 37 acrylic and oil, got 464K views.
> "'longing', 28 x 37, acrylic and oil."

Fits [commission reveal](/formats/commission-reveal) and [process video](/formats/process-video).
```
Open on a close detail of the painting, then pull out to the full piece over 3 seconds. Put the title in lowercase italics in the lower third, with the size and medium in smaller text underneath. Music only, no voice.
```

### 3. The pattern in your work
Line up several pieces that share something you didn't plan. A color, a figure, a shape. My "hmm, I'm noticing a pattern here" reel got 292K views.

Fits [montage](/formats/montage).
```
Play 8 of my paintings that all use the same deep blue, 1 second each, cut on the beat. Put "hmm, I'm noticing a pattern here" at the top for the whole video. End on the newest one held for 2 seconds.
```

### 4. The POV reveal
Set up a tiny story in one line of text, then reveal the painting as the answer. My POV about a crush whose favorite color is blue got 67.4K views.
> "POV: your crush's favorite color is blue."

Fits [POV video](/formats/pov-video).
```
Show the POV line in white text in the top third over a clip of my hands mixing blue paint. Cut to the finished painting on the beat drop and remove the text. Hold on the painting for 4 seconds.
```

### 5. Reply to a comment with a reveal
Put a real question from your comments on screen, then answer it with the work. "How many months have you been painting?" got 48.1K views for me.

Fits [talking head](/formats/talking-head).
```
Put the comment as a white bubble in the top third for the first 3 seconds. Then cut through my paintings in date order, 0.8 seconds each, with the month as small text in the corner of each one.
```

### 6. The giant canvas story
Tell what happened with the biggest or hardest piece you've made, good or bad. My reel about what happened with the giant canvas for "skating" got 23.3K views.

Fits [story time](/formats/story-time) and [storytelling video](/formats/storytelling-video).
```
Use my voiceover about the giant canvas as the spine. Cover it with process clips in order and captions in the bottom third, 4 words at a time. Save the shot of the finished canvas on the wall for the last 4 seconds.
```

## Process

### 7. The unusual material
Paint with something people don't expect, and show the material up close. My piece "forest", made with preserved flowers on canvas, got 216K views.

Fits [process video](/formats/process-video).
```
Open on a macro shot of the preserved flowers for 1.5 seconds. Then play the process clips at 4x, cutting on every second beat. End with the full piece and its title in small text at the bottom.
```

### 8. The hand and the work
Keep your face out of it. Film only your hand working, then show what it made. "The hand vs what it created" got 160K views for me.

Fits [faceless video](/formats/faceless-video) and [speed paint](/formats/speed-paint).
```
Alternate a 1 second close-up of my hand with a 1 second shot of the matching part of the finished painting, for 6 pairs. Put "the hand" and "what it created" as small labels on each side. Music only.
```

### 9. The layer breakdown
Show each layer of the painting as its own step. Underpainting, block-in, details. My reel on acrylic underpainting with oil on top got 15.6K views.
> "Acrylic first, oil on top."

Fits [process video](/formats/process-video) and [tutorial](/formats/tutorial).
```
Split the process into three sections, the underpainting, the block-in, and the details. Put a small label for each section in the top left when it starts. Speed each section to fit 6 seconds and cut on the beat.
```

### 10. The part you left out
Admit the hard part the timelapse hides. A caption about struggling makes a smooth timelapse feel honest. My reel about the hours I spent struggling with the ripples got 10K views.
> "Not pictured: hours struggling with the ripples."

Fits [art timelapse](/formats/art-timelapse) and [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video).
```
Play the timelapse of the water section at 8x. Put my caption about the ripples at the top in lowercase for the full video. Slow to normal speed for the last 3 seconds as the final ripple goes on.
```

### 11. Building the canvas
Film the stretcher bars, the fabric, and the staple gun before any paint goes on. My "how to build a giant canvas" reel got 18.2K views.

Fits [tutorial](/formats/tutorial) and [process video](/formats/process-video).
```
Cut the canvas build into 6 numbered steps with the step name as text in the top third. Each step 3 seconds at 2x speed. End on me standing next to the finished canvas for scale.
```

### 12. The sound of the work
Mute the music and let the material make the sound. Brush on canvas, ink on crunchy paper. My crunchy paper ASMR reel of 10 ink paintings got 10.5K views.

Fits [silent vlog](/formats/silent-vlog) and [art timelapse](/formats/art-timelapse).
```
No music. Turn up the paper and brush sounds on every clip. Cut between the 10 ink paintings in order, 2 seconds each, with no captions and no text.
```

## All-nighters and deadlines

### 13. The all-nighter version
Film one piece from evening to morning with the time on screen. My all-nighter version of "decay" got 172K views.

Fits [all-nighter painting vlog](/formats/all-nighter-painting-vlog).
```
Use the clip timestamps to put the time in the top right of every clip, from the first one to sunrise. Cut the process to 30 seconds total. End on the finished painting with daylight in the room.
```

### 14. The two-hour nap confession
Make the joke about your sleep the whole caption. "Does it count as an all-nighter if I slept two hours" got 139K views.

Fits [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video) and [all-nighter painting vlog](/formats/all-nighter-painting-vlog).
```
Put my caption at the top in lowercase. Play the night clips at 6x and insert a 1 second clip of me asleep in the middle, with "2 hours" as small text. Then finish with the morning shot of the painting.
```

### 15. The impossible count
Name how many pieces are due and how few hours you have, then race the clock. "10 ink paintings due in 10 hours" got 12.4K views.

Fits [all-nighter painting vlog](/formats/all-nighter-painting-vlog) and [day in the life](/formats/day-in-the-life-story).
```
Add a counter in the top left that goes from 0/10 to 10/10 each time a new ink painting is finished. Cut the night to 40 seconds. End on all 10 laid out on the floor, shot from above.
```

### 16. The sleep schedule check-in
Post again from the same late-night setup with a line about your schedule. Regular viewers come back for the series. "Back at it again with my cooked sleep schedule" got 65K views.

Fits [all-nighter painting vlog](/formats/all-nighter-painting-vlog).
```
Open on the same overhead desk framing as my last all-nighter, with my caption in the center. Cut the session to 25 seconds on the beat and put a small moon icon in the corner that fades as the sky gets lighter.
```

### 17. The night before the show
Film the last hours before a show, then the show night itself. My "first solo show is in 10 hours" reel got 27.9K views, and the show night video got 12.4K.

Fits [storytelling video](/formats/storytelling-video) and [day in the life](/formats/day-in-the-life-story).
```
Start with the clips from the night before, cut tight with a countdown in hours in the top right. Then a black card with the show name. Then the show night clips at normal speed with the room sound up and the music lower.
```

## Old work and sketchbooks

### 18. Your drawings from middle school
Dig up your oldest work and show it with no apology. My middle school drawings got 395K views.

Fits [semester recap montage](/formats/semester-recap-montage) and [montage](/formats/montage).
```
Show each old drawing for 1.2 seconds, flat and centered on a white background. Add the grade I was in as small text below each one. End with one recent painting held for 3 seconds.
```

### 19. Old work from high school
Same idea, a few years later. Viewers like watching the skill change across a series of posts. My old works from high school got 114K views.

Fits [montage](/formats/montage).
```
Film each high school piece being pulled out of a portfolio, then cut between them on every second beat. Put the year in the top left. Keep the paper sounds at 30 percent under the music.
```

### 20. Sketch vs final
Put the sketch and the finished piece side by side, then wipe from one to the other. My sketch vs final reel got 12.1K views.

Fits [sketchbook flip-through](/formats/sketchbook-flip-through) and [before and after](/formats/before-after-transformation).
```
Show the sketch full screen, then wipe left to right to reveal the finished painting in the same framing over 1 second. Repeat for 4 pairs, each wipe on the beat.
```

### 21. The old sketchbook
Flip through a sketchbook from a specific time in your life. My sketchbook from covid got 12.9K views.

Fits [sketchbook flip-through](/formats/sketchbook-flip-through).
```
Cut the flip-through so each page holds for 1 second, cutting on the page turn. Keep the page turn sounds. Put "my sketchbook from 2020" in handwritten-style text at the top for the first 3 seconds.
```

## Recaps and the studio

### 22. The year in paintings
Every piece from one year in order, with a line about what changed. Mine, "2025, the year of learning to oil paint and finally hitting post", got 287K views.

Fits [semester recap montage](/formats/semester-recap-montage) and [storytelling video](/formats/storytelling-video).
```
Play every painting from the year in date order, 1 beat each, with the month in small text in the corner. Slow down to 2 seconds each for the last 3 pieces. End on my caption about the year in the center.
```

### 23. A season of work
Shorter than a year, and easier to make often. "A summer of paintings" got 220K views for me.

Fits [montage](/formats/montage) and [beat-synced edit](/formats/beat-synced-edit).
```
Use one clip of each summer painting, alternating a close detail and a full view. Cut on every beat. Add a warm color grade across the whole edit and put "a summer of paintings" at the top for 2 seconds.
```

### 24. Smallest to largest
Sort a semester of work by size instead of date. The order gives the video a build. "Everything I painted this semester, smallest to largest" got 32.3K views.

Fits [semester recap montage](/formats/semester-recap-montage).
```
Order the paintings from smallest to largest. Show each one held by me for scale, 1 second each, and add its size as small text below. Speed up the cuts to half beats for the last 5.
```

### 25. Your career so far
Tell your art story in under a minute, with the work as proof. My "painting career so far as an 18 year old at Brown" got 105K views.

Fits [storytelling video](/formats/storytelling-video) and [talking head](/formats/talking-head).
```
Use my voiceover as the spine with captions in the bottom third. Cover each year I mention with paintings from that year, 1.5 seconds each. End on a clip of me in the studio now.
```

### 26. The studio tour
Walk through where you work. Show the light, the walls, the half-finished pieces. My Brown University painting studios tour got 262K views.

Fits [studio tour](/formats/studio-tour) and [cinematic b-roll](/formats/cinematic-b-roll).
```
Cut the walkthrough into 2 second shots, following the order I walked. Label each area in small text in the lower left as it appears. Use soft music at 50 percent and keep the footsteps audible.
```

## Selling, trends, and small things

### 27. The prints launch
Announce prints with the work, the printed copy, and the date. My "I'm finally releasing prints" reel got 13.9K views, and "come design my print" got 11.4K.
> "I'm finally releasing prints."

Fits [product demo](/formats/product-demo) and [unboxing](/formats/unboxing).
```
Open on the original painting, then cut to the print being pulled from its sleeve. Show three close-ups of the print paper and color, 1 second each. End on a card with the release date in large text for 3 seconds.
```

### 28. Commissions going out
Film the packing and the shipping of a commission. "Commissions off to Texas" got 8.5K views.

Fits [commission reveal](/formats/commission-reveal).
```
Play the packing clips in order, wrap, box, tape, label, cut on the beat. Blur the address on the label. End on the box by the door with "off to Texas" as text in the center.
```

### 29. The color wheel trend
Fill a color wheel template with crops of your own paintings. "Trying the color wheel trend with my art" got 131K views.

Fits [beat-synced edit](/formats/beat-synced-edit) and [montage](/formats/montage).
```
Build a color wheel with 12 segments. Fill each segment with a crop from one of my paintings that matches that color, one segment per beat, going clockwise from red. Hold the full wheel for 3 seconds at the end.
```

### 30. Paint on something small and silly
Paint on a rock, a shoe, a phone case, anything that isn't a canvas. Small pieces are fast and fun to watch. "Silly little painting on a silly little rock" got 89.7K views.

Fits [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video) and [speed paint](/formats/speed-paint).
```
Open on the plain rock in my palm for 1 second. Play the painting process at 6x, cut on every second beat. End on the finished rock in my palm in the same framing as the first shot.
```

## FAQ

**What should an artist post if they don't want to show their face?**
Process clips, hand shots, reveals, and sketchbook flips all work without a face. My reel of the hand vs what it created shows only my hand and the painting.

**How often should artists post?**
As often as you finish something worth showing, plus the process clips in between. Filming every session gives you more to post than finishing pieces alone.

**Do art videos need a voiceover?**
No. Music, a short caption, and the work carry most art videos. Save a voiceover for stories and career recaps.

**What makes an art video get views?**
A visible change from start to finish, shown fast. Reveals, recaps, and throwbacks all show change.
