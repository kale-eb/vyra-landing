**Build a language-learning video around one real practice moment. Let the recorded phrase remain audible and keep any explanation clearly separate from the transcription.**

These ideas are for language learners. Choose the one you can support with your own recordings. Keep the explanation tied to what the camera actually shows.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The phrase I kept forgetting

Say the phrase, explain where you wanted to use it and show your later attempt. Keep the words in context rather than making an unsupported fluency claim.

### 2. A small correction from my notes

Show your original attempt and the correction you have checked. Put the two in order and explain the difference you learned.

### 3. One situation, one exchange

Film your own practice dialogue for a specific situation. Keep pauses that help the viewer follow the turn-taking and label who is speaking if needed.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source footage that explains it.
2. Add your clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the words or sounds that must stay intact.
4. Review the draft for missing context and text covering important details.
5. Export and play the saved video before sharing it.

## A brief to adapt

> Keep my full practice phrase audible. Show the checked correction after the first attempt. Caption the spoken language and keep my English explanation in a separate section.

## FAQ

**Can I assume the editor’s captions are a language lesson?**
Check the wording against a reliable learning source or fluent reviewer. A transcription result does not establish that a grammatical explanation is correct.

**Should I speed up the practice audio?**
Keep the phrase at a pace where it can be heard clearly. Shorten the setup footage first if the video needs to be shorter.
