**Give the market video a small story you can actually film. A stall being built, one product display or packing at the end can each make a complete sequence.**

These ideas are for makers selling at markets filming their own material. Pick the one that fits the clips you can make or already have. Keep the useful explanation and give the finished result enough screen time to see it.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The table becoming a stall

Record the empty table, a few placement decisions and the finished stall from the same position. Keep the completed wide view. It shows what the smaller setup actions were building toward.

### 2. One product finding its place

Follow one item from its box to its spot on the display. Show the display around it at the end. Keep the route and placement in order so the sequence feels like one action.

### 3. What came home at closing

Film the finished stall before packing and the packed boxes afterward. Use a brief spoken reflection if you have one. Keep any sales figures tied to information you actually know.

## FAQ

**Do I need footage of the whole day?**
Use one sequence with a clear start and finish if that is what you filmed. Setup alone can be a complete short video.

**How do I stop setup footage feeling repetitive?**
Keep the actions that visibly change the display. Shorten repeated trips and hold the finished stall longer.
