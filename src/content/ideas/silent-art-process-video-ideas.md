**Choose an action whose beginning and result are visible. Use a few labels when the images alone leave a necessary step unclear.**

These ideas are for artists making a process video without spoken narration. Choose the one your own recordings can support. Keep the explanation and the visible outcome connected.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. One material change

Show the surface before the action, the useful movement and its result. Keep the final texture visible long enough to inspect.

### 2. The order that matters

Use short labels for the stages that would be easy to confuse. Keep the sequence faithful to the process you recorded.

### 3. The detail and the whole piece

Move from the relevant close-up to the completed work. Keep enough context that the viewer understands where the detail belongs.

## FAQ

**Do I need text on every shot?**
Use text where the images need explanation. Leave clear actions visible without covering them with unnecessary labels.

**Can I keep natural sound in a silent-style video?**
Decide whether you mean no narration or no audio at all. State that distinction in the brief so the useful original sounds are handled as you intend.
