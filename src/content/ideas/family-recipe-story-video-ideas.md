**Pair the cooking step with the explanation the person actually gave. Preserve the small practical detail that makes the recipe understandable.**

These ideas are for people recording a permitted family food story. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The step they do by feel

Record the action and the words explaining what to look for. Do not turn an approximate quantity into an invented measurement.

### 2. The utensil with a story

Show the utensil in use, then include the approved explanation of its place in the family. Keep the cooking sequence clear.

### 3. The version we make now

Show a documented substitution and explain who made the change. Keep it separate from a claim about the original recipe.

## FAQ

**Should I add exact quantities when the recording is approximate?**
Use only the quantities you can verify. Label an estimate honestly or ask the cook to clarify it.

**Can I combine comments from different cooking sessions?**
Keep the speaker’s meaning and the relevant version of the recipe clear. Do not imply a continuous instruction if the steps changed.
