**A flower video can follow a shape taking form or show how an arrangement belongs in its setting. Choose the story before cutting the stems and room shots together.**

These ideas are for florists filming their own material. Pick the one that fits the clips you can make or already have. Keep the useful explanation and give the finished result enough screen time to see it.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The shape from the side

Record the empty vase, the arrangement taking shape and the finished side profile. Keep a final view from the same position. Let the silhouette read before adding a name or occasion label.

### 2. One flower that changes the balance

Show the arrangement before the last flower goes in, the placement and the result. Keep that placement continuous if the source allows it. The viewer should be able to see what changed without reading a paragraph.

### 3. From table to room

Start with a close-up of the finished arrangement, then show its place in the room. Use the room shot to establish scale. Keep the flower color consistent as the light changes between locations.

> Show the arrangement before the final stem, keep the placement of that stem visible and finish on the same side profile. Hold the finished shape without covering it in text.

## FAQ

**How do I keep colors consistent?**
Compare the source clips and the final export. Clips filmed under different light may need different corrections.

**Should I include the whole arranging process?**
Choose representative moments that explain the shape. Keep the full sequence when the point of the video is the complete method.
