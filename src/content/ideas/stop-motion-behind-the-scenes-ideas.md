**Pair a finished stop-motion moment with the physical change that made it happen. Use your rendered animation as supplied footage and show the setup separately.**

These ideas are for stop-motion makers. Choose the one you can support with your own recordings. Keep the explanation tied to what the camera actually shows.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. One movement between frames

Show the finished motion, then a close shot of how little the object moves between photographs. Keep both examples about the same action.

### 2. The support you hid

Film the support or rig before showing the finished shot. Explain its role using your own setup footage. Keep the completed scene visible at the end.

### 3. The test that changed the scene

Compare a short test render with the later version and identify one change. Keep the explanation about the observed difference rather than implying the editor generated the animation.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source footage that explains it.
2. Add your clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the words or sounds that must stay intact.
4. Review the draft for missing context and text covering important details.
5. Export and play the saved video before sharing it.

## FAQ

**Does this require Vyra to build the stop-motion animation?**
No. These ideas start with an animation you have already made and exported. Vyra edits that footage together with your process recordings.

**How much setup footage should I include?**
Include the part that explains the finished action. A long view of an unchanged setup adds little to that explanation.
