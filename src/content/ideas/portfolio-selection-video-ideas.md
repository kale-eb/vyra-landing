**Explain why a piece belongs in a particular portfolio. Give each example enough time to show the skill or decision you are discussing.**

These ideas are for creators choosing work for a portfolio. Choose the one you can support with footage or records you already have. Keep the explanation close to what the viewer can actually see.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The piece that fits the brief

State the kind of work you want to show, then explain how one project supports it. Avoid inventing a hiring or sales outcome.

### 2. What I left out

Compare two of your own candidates and describe why one suits this selection better. Keep the comparison about fit rather than declaring the other piece worthless.

### 3. The sequence I changed

Show a short selection in its original and revised order. Explain how the first example changes the context for the next one.

## FAQ

**Should I include every project I am proud of?**
Choose work that supports the purpose of this selection. Explain that purpose so the viewer can follow the choice.

**Can I claim the new order will win clients?**
You can explain the intended effect and later measure responses. Do not present an untested portfolio change as a guaranteed business result.
