**Build the video around a real recorded change or occasion. Use dates you can support and keep the animal’s behavior in context.**

These ideas are for people making a dated record of their pet. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The same place months apart

Choose comparable views and label their actual dates. Hold both long enough to see the difference instead of relying on a fast transformation effect.

### 2. A familiar routine

Show the beginning, the small action and how the routine ends. Use ordinary footage rather than repeating an activity just for a reaction.

### 3. One small first

Use the real first occurrence you recorded, or call it an early attempt when you cannot establish that it was the first.

## Assemble it on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather its recorded beginning, useful action and ending.
2. Add your own clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the exact words, dates or sounds that must stay.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.
5. Play the saved video before sharing it.

## FAQ

**Can I call an event a first when I am unsure?**
Use the description you can support, such as an early attempt. The edit should not create certainty the recording does not establish.

**Should I cut out the setup around a behavior?**
Keep enough context to avoid changing what the behavior appears to mean. A short clip can still include the relevant beginning.
