**Start with the last state a returning viewer may remember, then show the change made since that point. Give a new viewer a short explanation of the project too.**

These ideas are for creators continuing a project across several videos. Choose the one your own recordings can support. Keep the explanation and the visible outcome connected.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The change since the last update

Show the earlier state and the new section with matching labels. Keep the comparison focused on the actual change.

### 2. The decision I reversed

Explain the earlier choice, the evidence that changed your mind and the revised work. Use the relevant recordings rather than implying every intermediate step was filmed.

### 3. The remaining problem

Show the current difficulty and the small test you made. End with an accurate description of what still needs work.

## FAQ

**How much of the previous video should I repeat?**
Repeat enough to establish the earlier state. A short matching view can do that without replaying the whole process.

**Should each update promise a finished result?**
Describe the progress the footage supports. An honest remaining question can give the next update a useful purpose.
