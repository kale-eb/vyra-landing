**Pick a small moment that gives the cafe a recognizable story. A drink being finished, a counter opening or one regular order can work with a few phone clips.**

These ideas are for cafe owners and staff filming their own material. Pick the one that fits the clips you can make or already have. Keep the useful explanation and give the finished result enough screen time to see it.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. A drink in the place it belongs

Film the finished drink at the counter, the detail that makes it recognizable and the table where it will be served. Let the final room shot establish the mood and scale of the place.

### 2. The counter before the first order

Record the empty counter, the setup and the first finished order. Keep the sequence in order. Use a short title to distinguish setup footage from the moment the cafe opens.

### 3. One sound from the room

Choose a clearly recorded sound such as a cup meeting its saucer. Build a short edit that gives that sound room. Keep any music low while the natural sound is meant to be heard.

## FAQ

**Does the whole video need music?**
Try the first draft with the recorded sounds audible. Add music where it supports the footage and check the balance.

**How do I keep a room sequence clear?**
Give one view enough time to establish the space before cutting to small details. A series of close-ups may leave the location unclear.
