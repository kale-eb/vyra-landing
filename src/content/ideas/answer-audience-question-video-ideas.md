**Choose one question you can answer with footage. Show the part of the process that supports the answer and keep any limits of the explanation visible.**

These ideas are for creators answering a public or permitted audience question. Choose the one your own recordings can support. Keep the explanation and the visible outcome connected.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The step the question refers to

State the question in your own words, show the relevant action and give the short answer. Use a real public or permitted question when attributing it to someone.

### 2. The answer that needs a comparison

Show two examples from your own work and explain their difference. Keep the labels attached to the right example.

### 3. The question I still cannot answer

Show what you observed and the next test you actually recorded. Make clear what remains unresolved instead of editing the sequence into a certainty.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source clips it needs.
2. Add your footage to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the wording or sounds that must remain.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.
5. Play the saved export before posting it.

## FAQ

**Do I have to display a viewer’s message?**
You can summarize a public or permitted question in your own words. Do not invent a message or imply a private question was approved for publication.

**How do I avoid answering too many things at once?**
Choose the one question your footage can explain. Link a follow-up topic separately instead of rushing several answers into one sequence.
