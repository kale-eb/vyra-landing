**Give a workshop recap one observable takeaway. Use footage you have permission to share and keep participant work attached to the right person or anonymous.**

These ideas are for people documenting a class or workshop. Choose the one you can support with footage or records you already have. Keep the explanation close to what the viewer can actually see.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The demonstration and my attempt

Show the permitted demonstration detail, then your own attempt and one thing you noticed. Make clear which work is yours.

### 2. One idea from my notes

Show your own note and the recorded moment it refers to. Explain the point in your own words without making up an instructor quotation.

### 3. The table at the end

Film the materials or work you are allowed to show and use a short explanation of the session. Check names, faces and screens before publishing.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source clips that explain it.
2. Add your footage to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and any wording or original sound that must remain.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.
5. Export and inspect the saved file before posting it.

## A brief to adapt

> Use my own notes and practice piece. Keep other participants out of identifiable close-ups. Explain the one technique I tried without adding a quote from the instructor.

## FAQ

**Can I use every clip I recorded at the workshop?**
Check the organizer’s rules and participant permissions. Having a recording does not settle whether you may publish someone else’s work or identity.

**How do I keep a recap short?**
Choose one takeaway and the clips that explain it. An exhaustive event timeline can make that takeaway harder to see.
