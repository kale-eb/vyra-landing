**Choose the pages that explain one thread in your sketchbook. Hold the useful details still between page turns so a viewer can inspect them.**

These ideas are for artists showing a sequence of pages. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The idea that changes across pages

Show the first attempt, a later change and the page where the idea became clearer. Name the actual change while keeping the page order accurate.

### 2. One spread in detail

Begin with the whole spread and move to the small mark you want to explain. Return to the whole page so the viewer knows where it belongs.

### 3. The page I nearly skipped

Show the unfinished or ordinary page and explain the choice it helped you make. Keep private notes outside the frame.

## Assemble it on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather its recorded beginning, useful action and ending.

3. Describe the sequence and the exact words, dates or sounds that must stay.

## FAQ

**Should every page get the same time?**
Give more time to the detail you discuss. A blank turn and an intricate spread do different jobs.

**Can I rely on captions to explain a page moving past quickly?**
Keep the page visible while its explanation is readable. Text cannot make a detail inspectable after it has left the frame.
