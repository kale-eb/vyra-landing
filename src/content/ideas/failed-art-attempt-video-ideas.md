**Show the result that failed and the part of the process that helps explain it. Keep what you know separate from what you are still testing.**

These ideas are for makers explaining an unsuccessful attempt. Choose the one you can support with footage or records you already have. Keep the explanation close to what the viewer can actually see.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The moment I noticed it

Start with the visible problem, show the earlier step you recorded and explain your observation. Avoid editing the sequence to imply a cause you did not establish.

### 2. What I kept from the attempt

Show one useful part that survived and how you used it later. Keep the failed result visible enough that the later change has context.

### 3. The next small test

End with a limited test you actually recorded. Explain what that test checked rather than announcing that the whole problem is solved.

## FAQ

**Should I hide the failed result until the end?**
Showing it early can make the explanation easier to follow. Choose the order that makes the process understandable.

**Can the edit prove what caused the failure?**
A sequence alone cannot establish a cause. Describe observations and verified tests accurately, including what remains uncertain.
