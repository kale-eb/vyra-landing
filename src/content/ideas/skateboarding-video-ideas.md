**Keep the full recorded attempt visible when the landing matters. A sequence can show effort and context without using cuts that make an incomplete attempt look complete.**

These ideas are for skaters documenting their own sessions. Choose the one you can support with your own recordings. Keep the explanation tied to what the camera actually shows.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The attempt and the reset

Show a recorded attempt, a short reset and the later attempt. Keep their order clear and avoid hiding the landing behind text.

### 2. One spot from a wide and close view

Use the wide shot to establish the space, then show a detail filmed separately. Label or structure the edit so it does not imply two angles came from one take.

### 3. The sound of the session

Keep a short stretch of the actual board and landing sound, then use surrounding clips to show the setting. Let the visible action and sound stay aligned.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source footage that explains it.
2. Add your clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the words or sounds that must stay intact.
4. Review the draft for missing context and text covering important details.
5. Export and play the saved video before sharing it.

## A brief to adapt

> Keep the complete final attempt, including the roll-away. Shorten the waiting between tries. Do not join two attempts to make one look landed.

## FAQ

**Can I join the takeoff from one attempt to another landing?**
If you do, make the construction clear. Do not present separate attempts as proof of one completed take.

**How much of the landing should I keep?**
Keep enough to see the actual outcome, including the roll-away when relevant. A cut immediately at contact can hide what happened next.
