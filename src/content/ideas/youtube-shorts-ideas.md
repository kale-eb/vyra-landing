**YouTube Shorts are vertical videos under 60 seconds, and the best ones do one thing. They show one step, tell one story, or answer one question. Here are 27 YouTube Shorts ideas in six groups, from clips you can pull out of your long videos to loops and daily series. Each one has a line to say, the format it fits, and a prompt that cuts a 9:16 short in Vyra.**

Shorts live on the same channel as your long videos, so a short can send people to the full version. Several ideas below start from footage you already have. They also work as short video ideas for any vertical platform, with titles written the way people search on YouTube.

## How to use this list

1. Pick one idea and cut it down to a single point. If you need to say "and also", make two shorts.
2. Write a title that reads like a search, such as "how to stretch a canvas".
3. Make the last second lead back into the first, so a replay feels natural.
4. Paste the prompt into Vyra with your footage, then watch it once on mute before you post.

## Pull from your long videos

### 1. One step from a tutorial
Take the single most useful step from a long tutorial and post it alone. End by pointing to the full video.
> "This is the step everyone gets wrong."

Fits [tutorial](/formats/tutorial).
```
Find the part of my long tutorial where I explain the hardest step and cut it to 45 seconds. Reframe to 9:16 and keep my hands centered. Add captions in the middle third, 2 to 3 words at a time, white with a black outline. In the last 3 seconds, add "full tutorial on my channel" as text above the captions.
```

### 2. The blooper
Post the funniest mistake from a long video as its own short. A blooper needs no context to work.
> "Take 14."

Fits [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video) and [skit](/formats/skit).
```
Find the clip where I mess up and laugh, and cut a 15 second short around it. Reframe to 9:16 with my face in the upper half. Add a record scratch sound on the mistake and a half-second freeze frame. Put "take 14" as small text in the top left.
```

### 3. The before and after from a long build
Compress the start and the end of a long project into one short. The full video on your channel holds the process.
> "Three weeks of work in fifteen seconds."

Fits [before and after](/formats/before-after-transformation).
```
Make a 15 second 9:16 short from my long build video. Show the empty starting shot for 2 seconds, then ten one-second clips from the build in order, cut to the beat, then the finished result for 3 seconds. Add "3 weeks" as large text in the center on the final shot.
```

### 4. The strongest opinion from a conversation
Take the most direct sentence from a podcast or interview and give it big captions. One clear opinion is enough for a short.
> "I think most beginners buy the wrong camera."

Fits [podcast clip](/formats/podcast-clip).
```
Find the 30 to 40 seconds where the guest gives the most direct opinion and cut it out. Reframe to 9:16 with the speaker's face in the top half and large captions in the lower half, with the key word in each line highlighted in yellow. Remove every pause. Add a moving audio waveform along the bottom edge.
```

## One idea, one short

### 5. One tip in 20 seconds
Teach one thing fast, with the result shown first. The title should be the tip itself.
> "Tape the edges before you paint. Watch."

Fits [tutorial](/formats/tutorial).
```
Make a 20 second 9:16 short. Open on the finished result for 1 second, then show the tip in three quick steps with a large step number in the top third. Speed up any clip longer than 4 seconds. Keep the tip as one line of text at the top for the whole video.
```

### 6. One mistake and the fix
Show the wrong way and the right way at the same time. Viewers check whether they make the same mistake.
> "If your lines look shaky, this is why."

Fits [tutorial](/formats/tutorial) and [lessons learned](/formats/lessons-learned).
```
Make a 25 second 9:16 short as a stacked split screen. The wrong way plays in the top half with "wrong" in a red box, and the right way plays in the bottom half with "right" in a green box. Start both clips at the same moment so the difference shows side by side. Caption my voiceover in the thin band between the halves.
```

### 7. A fact people in your niche don't know
Share one surprising fact with the proof on screen. Facts get shared because people want to tell someone.
> "Most of the paint in this tube isn't pigment."

Fits [explainer](/formats/explainer) and [faceless video](/formats/faceless-video).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short with my voiceover over close-up b-roll. Add a simple labeled diagram that pops in at the center of the frame when I name the fact. Keep the music quiet, cut it completely on the reveal line, then bring it back.
```

### 8. Up close, then pull back
Start on an extreme close-up that's hard to read, then pull back to show the whole thing. The reveal is the payoff.
> "You'd never guess what this is."

Fits [cinematic b-roll](/formats/cinematic-b-roll) and [art timelapse](/formats/art-timelapse).
```
Make a 12 second 9:16 short. Start on the macro shot, then do a smooth digital zoom out over 4 seconds to the full frame. Land the final wide shot on the downbeat of the track. Keep the screen clear of text until the last 2 seconds, then add the name of the piece in small letters at the bottom.
```

## Loops and satisfying shorts

### 9. The perfect loop
Make the last frame match the first so the short plays again with no visible cut. People often watch twice before they notice.

Fits [art timelapse](/formats/art-timelapse) and [speed paint](/formats/speed-paint).
```
Make a 20 second 9:16 short that loops. Start on the blank surface, play the timelapse, and end with a quick wipe back to the blank surface so the last frame matches the first. Cut the music so its end flows into its start. No text or captions.
```

### 10. The satisfying sound
Record one sound up close, like peeling tape, pouring paint, or a lid snapping shut, and build the short around it. The sound carries the whole short.
> "the sound of peeling tape."

Fits [silent vlog](/formats/silent-vlog) and [process video](/formats/process-video).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short with no music. Raise the volume of the tape and the pouring sounds and remove the room noise between them. Cut so every clip starts on a sound. Add one line of small text at the bottom for the first 2 seconds, then leave the frame clean.
```

### 11. Start to finish in 30 seconds
Speed up an entire project so it fits in half a minute. Keep one moment at normal speed so viewers feel the real pace.
> "Start to finish."

Fits [speed paint](/formats/speed-paint). My start to finish of "free and flourishing" got 7.9K views.
```
Speed up three hours of footage into a 30 second 9:16 short. Ramp the speed so the start and end play slower and the middle plays fastest. Drop to real speed for 2 seconds at the moment the piece comes together. Sync the cuts to the beat of the track.
```

### 12. The sped-up cleanup
Film the mess, then the cleanup, sped up. Cleanups are satisfying to watch even for people outside your niche.
> "Cleaning up after a long night."

Fits [before and after](/formats/before-after-transformation) and [silent vlog](/formats/silent-vlog).
```
Make a 25 second 9:16 short. Hold the messy desk for 2 seconds, then speed the cleanup to 10x with a soft, steady track. End on the clean desk with a slow push in. Add "before" and "after" labels in the top corners.
```

## Series shorts

### 13. Day number something
Post a short every day on the same project or habit. The day number in the title brings people back for the next one.
> "Day 12 of painting every day until I get into a gallery."

Fits [montage](/formats/montage) and [process video](/formats/process-video).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short for today. Show "Day 12" as large text in the top third for the whole video. Open on today's result, then show today's clips at 4x. End with a 1 second flash of the day 1 result for comparison.
```

### 14. The weekly recap
Every Sunday, post what you made, did, or learned that week in under 40 seconds. A fixed day makes the series easy to keep up.
> "Everything I painted this week."

Fits [montage](/formats/montage). My recap of everything I painted in the week before school got 13.9K views.
```
Make a 35 second 9:16 short with one clip per thing I made this week, in the order I made them. Cut each clip to 2 beats of the track. Show the day of the week in the top left as each clip plays. End on all of them in a grid for 2 seconds.
```

### 15. A numbered tip series
Run a set of tips as numbered shorts, one tip each, such as "beginner tip 7 of 20". Viewers go to your channel to find the rest.
> "Beginner tip number 7."

Fits [listicle talking head](/formats/listicle-talking-head).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short with "7 / 20" in a small badge in the top right. Put my face in the top half and the demonstration clip in the bottom half, with captions in the band between them. Use the same intro sound and the same font as the earlier parts of the series.
```

### 16. A story told in parts
Split one longer story into three or four shorts, each ending right before something happens. Viewers go to your channel to find the next part.
> "Part 2. This is where it got weird."

Fits [story time](/formats/story-time).
```
Split my 3 minute story into four 9:16 shorts of about 45 seconds each. End each part on the sentence right before the next turn, and add "part 2 on my channel" at the top for the last 2 seconds. Start each new part with a one-line recap on screen for 3 seconds. Captions in the lower third.
```

## Talk to the viewer

### 17. Reply to a comment
Answer a comment with a short, and show the comment on screen. The person who asked often comes back to watch.
> "You asked how I keep my brushes clean."

Fits [talking head](/formats/talking-head) and [tutorial](/formats/tutorial).
```
Make a 40 second 9:16 short. Show the comment as a card in the top third for the first 3 seconds, then cut to me answering. Cut every pause and add captions in the center, 2 words at a time. Show the demonstration clip full screen whenever I say "like this".
```

### 18. React to a headline
Put a news story or post from your niche behind you and give your take. Your opinion is the reason to watch.
> "This headline leaves out the most important part."

Fits [green screen commentary](/formats/green-screen-commentary).
```
Make a 45 second 9:16 short with the screenshot of the headline as my background, green screen style, with me in the bottom half. Zoom in on the part of the headline I'm talking about. Add captions in the top third over the screenshot, and cut every breath.
```

### 19. Then and now
Put an old piece next to a new one and talk about what changed. Progress is easy to understand at a glance.
> "Same subject, three years apart."

Fits [reaction video](/formats/reaction-video) and [before and after](/formats/before-after-transformation).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short with the old piece in the top half and the new one in the bottom half, each labeled with its year. Zoom both halves in on the same detail while I describe it. Cut to the new piece full screen for the last 3 seconds.
```

### 20. Test one viral hack
Try a popular hack from your niche and show whether it works. Save the verdict for the final second.
> "Does this actually work?"

Fits [product demo](/formats/product-demo) and [UGC hook and demo](/formats/ugc-hook-and-demo).
```
Make a 35 second 9:16 short. Open on the hack's promise as text in the top third, then show my attempt at 2x. Freeze the frame on the result for 1 second, then add a large stamp in the center that says "works" or "nope", based on what I say.
```

### 21. Guess the number
Ask viewers to guess something, such as how long it took or how many pieces are in it. Hold the answer to the end.
> "Guess how many hours this took."

Fits [process video](/formats/process-video) and [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short. Put "guess how many hours" at the top for the first 3 seconds, then show the process clips cut to the beat. Add a ticking clock sound under the last 5 seconds and reveal the answer as large text in the center of the final frame.
```

### 22. This or that
Show two options and ask viewers to pick one in the comments. An easy choice is easy to answer, so people reply.
> "Top or bottom. You can only keep one."

Fits [relatable video](/formats/relatable-video).
```
Make a 15 second 9:16 short with the two options stacked, one on top and one on the bottom, labeled "A" and "B". Hold for 8 seconds with a ticking sound, then flash my pick with a check mark. Put "comment A or B" at the very top.
```

## Quick stories, lists, and demos

### 23. Point at the text
Stand on one side of the frame and point at lines of text that appear on the other side, one at a time. It turns a list into something to watch, with no b-roll needed.
> "Three signs you're ready to go full time."

Fits [listicle talking head](/formats/listicle-talking-head).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short. Keep me on the left third of the frame and add each line of text on the right side at the moment I point at it, popping in with a small bounce. Stack the lines so all of them stay on screen. Cut every pause, and end on the full list for 2 seconds.
```

### 24. A 45 second story with one twist
Tell a short story that turns at the end. Keep the setup to three sentences.
> "My first commission was for my dentist."

Fits [story time](/formats/story-time).
```
Make a 45 second 9:16 short from my story. Cut every pause and filler word. Keep the camera tight on my face and punch in 20 percent on the twist line. Add captions in the center, and make the twist line larger and yellow.
```

### 25. Unbox it in 30 seconds
Open something new and show the one detail that matters. Skip the packaging.
> "I've been waiting six weeks for this."

Fits [unboxing](/formats/unboxing).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short. Start at the moment the item comes out of the box and cut all the tape and cardboard. Show three close-ups of the details I point at, each for 2 seconds with a label. End on the item in use.
```

### 26. A tool demo with one result
Show an app or tool doing one job, from input to result. One result is clearer than a list of features.
> "I typed one sentence and it did this."

Fits [app walkthrough](/formats/app-walkthrough) and [UGC hook and demo](/formats/ugc-hook-and-demo).
```
Make a 30 second 9:16 short from my screen recording. Crop to the part of the screen where the action happens and zoom in on the cursor when I click. Show the result full screen for the last 5 seconds. Add captions for my voiceover at the bottom, 3 words at a time.
```

### 27. The top three countdown
Count down the top three of something in your niche, from three to one. Number one keeps people to the end.
> "Number one surprised me."

Fits [listicle talking head](/formats/listicle-talking-head).
```
Make a 40 second 9:16 short with three sections. Flash a big "3", "2", and "1" in the center for half a second before each section, with a drum hit. Put the item name in the top third during each section. Hold number one 3 seconds longer than the others.
```

## FAQ

**How long should a YouTube Short be?**
Under 60 seconds, and as short as the idea allows. A tip can be 20 seconds, and a story might need 45.

**What makes a good short video idea?**
One point, shown early, that someone could explain to a friend in a sentence. If it needs two points, make two shorts.

**How do I turn a long video into Shorts?**
Find the moments that make sense without context, such as one step, one opinion, or one reveal. Then reframe them to 9:16 and add captions.

**Can I post the same short on TikTok and Reels?**
Yes. Export a clean file with no watermark from another app, and rewrite the caption for each platform.
