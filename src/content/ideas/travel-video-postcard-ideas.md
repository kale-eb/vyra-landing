**Make the postcard about one place or moment instead of the whole trip. Connect a view, a small action and an ending that tells the viewer why you kept it.**

These ideas are for travelers making a small record of one place. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. One morning in one place

Show the arrival, a recorded everyday action and the place as you leave. Keep the sequence local instead of implying several locations are one.

### 2. A detail I would have missed

Start with the wider view, then show the small detail and your actual observation. Return to enough context to locate it.

### 3. The sound I remember

Use a permitted recording of the place’s sound with matching views. Keep music low or absent while that sound matters.

## Assemble it on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather its recorded beginning, useful action and ending.
2. Add your own clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the exact words, dates or sounds that must stay.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.
5. Play the saved video before sharing it.

> Make a short postcard from the harbor clips. Start with the wide view, keep the sound of the ropes and finish as we leave. Do not add footage from other places.

## FAQ

**Do I need footage from every stop?**
Choose the clips that explain this postcard. A focused record can work without summarizing the whole journey.

**Can I use a shot from elsewhere to fill a missing view?**
Keep location changes clear when they matter. Do not label a substitute as the place you did not record.
