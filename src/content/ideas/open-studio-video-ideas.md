**Help the viewer understand where to look in the studio. Use a wider view for context and a detail that explains one piece of work.**

These ideas are for artists introducing a studio visit. Choose the one you can support with footage or records you already have. Keep the explanation close to what the viewer can actually see.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The route to one piece

Show the room, move to the selected piece and finish with a detail. Keep the path understandable instead of joining unrelated rooms into one space.

### 2. One tool with its current job

Show the tool in the work area and the mark it made in your own project. Explain its role without turning the clip into an unsupported product endorsement.

### 3. The work still in progress

Show an unfinished section and explain the decision you are considering. Keep any finished example clearly separate from the current piece.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

## FAQ

**Should I make the room look larger through the edit?**
Keep the layout understandable. Use clear transitions when footage comes from different rooms or dates.

**Do I need to show a finished result?**
A specific unresolved decision can be the point of the video. Explain what is unfinished instead of implying the work is complete.
