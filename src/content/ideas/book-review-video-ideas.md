**Choose one observation you can explain from your reading. A clear reason to recommend or hesitate helps more than a fast stack of covers.**

These ideas are for readers making personal book reviews. Choose the one you can support with your own recordings. Keep the explanation tied to what the camera actually shows.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. Who I would give this book to

Show the cover and describe a particular kind of reader who may enjoy it. Explain your reason in your own words and avoid promising that everyone will feel the same.

### 2. My expectation and what changed

Record what you expected before reading and your later reaction. Keep a clear boundary before any plot detail that could spoil the experience.

### 3. Two books for different moods

Show two books you have read and explain the different experience you wanted from each. Hold the covers long enough for the titles to be read.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather the source footage that explains it.
2. Add your clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the words or sounds that must stay intact.
4. Review the draft for missing context and text covering important details.
5. Export and play the saved video before sharing it.

## A brief to adapt

> Keep my spoiler-free recommendation intact. Hold the cover while I say the title, then show the two reading clips. End before my spoiler section begins.

## FAQ

**Do I need to show pages from the book?**
Your own spoken observations and cover footage may be enough. Get permission where needed before reproducing substantial text or artwork.

**How should I handle spoilers in the edit?**
Mark the point where plot discussion begins and decide whether to remove it or clearly warn the viewer. Check that a preview or caption does not reveal it early.
