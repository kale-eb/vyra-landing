These ideas are for makers recording a gift they are permitted to show. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. Why I chose this detail

Show the color, material or motif and explain its real connection. Use only details the recipient is comfortable having shared.

### 2. The part hidden inside

Record the lining, engraving or small addition before assembly covers it. Show where that detail belongs in the finished gift.

### 3. Ready to give

Follow the final check and wrapping. Finish on the completed object rather than inventing a reaction you did not record.

## FAQ

**Do I need a reaction shot?**
The making can carry the story. Use a reaction only when it was recorded and the person agrees to its publication.

**Should I show the personalization in full?**
Show only what is appropriate to publish. Crop identifying details before assembling the final edit.
