**Choose a few sounds you actually recorded and pair them with the right views. Keep the route understandable without covering the audio with constant music.**

These ideas are for people recording a route through its sounds. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The change between places

Let the viewer hear the shift from the recorded street to the quieter section. Use a clear visual change rather than moving sound from an unrelated place.

### 2. One sound and its source

Begin on the wider scene, reveal the source and leave a little room to listen. Keep the description faithful to what you know.

### 3. The return along the route

Compare a recorded outward view with the return. Label a time change when it explains a different sound or setting.

## FAQ

**Can I move an interesting sound to a different location?**
Keep that choice clear if it would change the apparent place or event. For a documentary diary, use the sound with its recorded context.

**How long should I leave a sound audible?**
Give the viewer enough time to recognize it. Listen to the sequence without watching to find cuts that end the useful sound too soon.
