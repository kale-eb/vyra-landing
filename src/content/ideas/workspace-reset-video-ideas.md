**Show the problem in the workspace, the change you actually made and how you use the result. Keep the before and after views comparable.**

These ideas are for makers recording how they reorganize their work area. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The tool I could not reach

Record the original layout, the move and a real use afterward. Explain the practical difference instead of just showing a clean surface.

### 2. One drawer that needed a system

Show the categories you chose and how an item is found in the new arrangement. Keep labels readable long enough to understand them.

### 3. What still does not fit

Show the improvement and the remaining problem. A useful reset does not need to pretend that every storage issue was solved.

## FAQ

**Can I change the lighting for the after shot?**
Use comparable lighting when the video asks the viewer to judge the change. A brighter view can make the arrangement seem more different than it is.

**Should I end only on an empty desk?**
Show the space doing its job. A short use of the new arrangement explains more than a clean surface alone.
