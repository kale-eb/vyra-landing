**Show the plant’s overall shape as well as the leaf or growth detail that interests you. A clear full view helps viewers understand the close-up.**

These ideas are for plant shop owners filming their own material. Pick the one that fits the clips you can make or already have. Keep the useful explanation and give the finished result enough screen time to see it.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. New growth in context

Record the new leaf, then the whole plant from the same side. Use a short label naming the visible detail. Keep the full plant in view long enough to show where the new growth sits.

### 2. One shelf, different heights

Film the shelf before and after arranging plants by height. Include a view from normal standing distance. The finished arrangement should read as a whole rather than only as a set of close-ups.

### 3. The pot and plant together

Show the plant, the pot and the finished pairing. Keep the plant’s size recognizable across cuts. End on the actual placement so the viewer can see how much room the pairing occupies.

> Start on the new leaf, pull back to the whole plant and finish on its place on the shelf. Keep the leaf color close to the source footage. Use one short label beside it.

## FAQ

**Should I turn this into a care tutorial?**
Use a care tutorial when you have the necessary information and steps to show. A visual feature or shelf idea can stay focused on what the viewer can see.

**How do I keep a tiny leaf visible?**
Choose a close source view and avoid covering the leaf with a label. Give the shot enough time to inspect.
