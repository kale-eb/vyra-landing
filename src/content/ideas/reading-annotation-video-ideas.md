**Choose one observation from your own reading notes and explain it in your own words. Show only the material needed for that explanation.**

These ideas are for readers explaining their own notes without reproducing a book. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. A note I changed

Show your earlier note and explain what changed your interpretation. Keep uncertainty visible instead of cutting it into a definitive claim.

### 2. How I organize my notes

Record your own index, symbols or notebook layout. Use an example you wrote rather than showing long passages from the book.

### 3. A question I kept

State the question in your own words and show how you recorded it. End with the actual state of your thinking, even when it remains unresolved.

## FAQ

**Does this need to be a full book review?**
A single observation can be the whole job. Tell the viewer which part you are discussing without implying a complete review.

**Should I remove a qualification to keep the video short?**
Keep the qualification if it changes your meaning. Shorten repetition or the setup before making the observation sound more certain.
