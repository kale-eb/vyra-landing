**Choose a clear reason for the selection, then show each object well enough to inspect. Keep uncertain dates or histories separate from verified facts.**

These ideas are for collectors showing objects they are comfortable publishing. Pick the story your existing clips can explain, then film a missing view only when it adds needed context.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The three I use most

Show the actual use or handling detail that explains each choice. Give the viewer more than a row of similar close-ups.

### 2. One object from several views

Start with the whole piece, show the useful marking or construction detail and return to its place in the collection.

### 3. The gap in the collection

Explain the category you are still looking for without pretending you own it. Finish on the objects actually available to film.

## Assemble it on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and gather its recorded beginning, useful action and ending.
2. Add your own clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the sequence and the exact words, dates or sounds that must stay.
4. Review the draft for missing context and covered details.
5. Play the saved video before sharing it.

## A brief to adapt

> Tour these three objects in the order I explain them. Hold the maker’s mark when I mention it. Do not add dates or valuations I have not supplied.

## FAQ

**Do I need to show the entire collection?**
Choose a selection with a reason the viewer can follow. Make clear that the video is a selection when it is.

**Should I add a value to make the story interesting?**
Use a valuation only when it is supported and relevant. The object’s use or detail can explain the selection without one.
