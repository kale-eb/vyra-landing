**Choose one decision in the drawing that the footage or saved images can show. A changed composition or color choice gives the edit a clear beginning and result.**

These ideas are for illustrators filming their own material. Pick the one that fits the clips you can make or already have. Keep the useful explanation and give the finished result enough screen time to see it.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The composition you moved

Show the earlier composition, the change and the finished drawing. Keep the images aligned where possible so the difference is easy to see. Name the change in a brief voiceover or label.

### 2. One color that changed the mood

Place the earlier palette beside the final choice, then show the complete illustration. Keep the image treatment consistent. The edit should let viewers compare the color decision without a distracting effect.

### 3. A detail with its reference

Show your own reference material, the relevant drawn detail and the finished piece. Give the reference and drawing distinct labels. End with the full image so the small detail has a place.

## Put the edit together on iPhone

1. Choose one idea and collect the source clips that show its beginning and result.
2. Add those clips to a project in the [Vyra iPhone app](/ai-video-editor-app).
3. Describe the order, the important sound and the detail that must stay visible.
4. Review the draft for missing context, awkward cuts and text covering the subject.
5. Export the video and play the saved file before posting it.

## A brief to adapt

> Compare the old and new composition in matching frames, then show the finished illustration. Keep the colors consistent with the supplied images and use a short label for the moved subject.

## FAQ

**Can I make this from still images?**
Yes. Add the images to a project and set their order and duration. Hold comparison images long enough to see the change.

**How do I explain a subtle revision?**
Show aligned views and use one concrete label or sentence. Let the viewer see the difference while it is being explained.
