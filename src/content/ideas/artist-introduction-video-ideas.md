**Introduce yourself through one piece of work and a decision you can explain. Give a new viewer enough context to understand what you make.**

These ideas are for artists introducing their work to a new viewer. Choose the one your own recordings can support. Keep the explanation and the visible outcome connected.

## Three ideas to film

### 1. The piece I would start with

Show one finished piece, then the process detail that explains why it matters to you. Keep your own reason specific rather than listing every medium you use.

### 2. What I keep returning to

Choose a recurring subject visible across your own work and explain one example. Let the images support the pattern instead of claiming a history you have not shown.

### 3. The part of the process I enjoy

Show that action and describe what you notice while doing it. Finish with the result of the same piece so the introduction has an ending.

## FAQ

**Do I need to tell my whole biography?**
Choose the context that helps a new viewer understand this work. A concrete decision may explain more than a fast list of milestones.

**Should I show every piece I have made?**
Use the examples that support the introduction. Give each enough screen time for a viewer to see the detail you mention.
