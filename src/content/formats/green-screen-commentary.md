**A green screen commentary puts you in front of a screenshot, article, or clip while you react to it, with the source on screen the whole time.**

You don't need an actual green screen. A plain wall works.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | News takes, hot takes, explaining a post, myth-busting |
| Hook style | Point at the source and say your take in 2 seconds |
| Pacing | Fast, 6-10 cuts per 30 seconds |
| Caption style | Word-by-word or short phrases, away from the source text |
| Music | None or very faint |
| Shot list | One take on a plain wall. The source at full resolution. Zoomed crops of the key lines. |
| Common mistakes | Source text too small. You standing over the key line. Reading the whole thing out loud. |

## Structure

- 0-2s: source appears, you say the take
- 2-10s: what the source claims, one sentence
- 10-40s: your points, zooming into the relevant part each time
- 40-50s: your conclusion
- 50-55s: a question for the comments

## How to shoot it

- Stand against a plain, evenly lit wall.
- Keep your body to one side. Leave the other side for the source.
- Point and gesture at where the source will be.
- One take per point so each cut has a clean start.
- Save the source at native size, not a screenshot of a screenshot.

## How to edit it

- Remove the background and put the source full-bleed behind you.
- Zoom the source to the sentence you're on for each point.
- Captions go in the part of the frame that's not you or the key text.
- Cut every pause.
- Box or underline the key line of the source.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Remove my background and put the attached screenshot behind me, full frame. Each time I say "here" or "this part", zoom the screenshot into the sentence I'm talking about. Add word-by-word captions on the side I'm not standing on.

> Cut my commentary to 45 seconds with no pauses. Keep the article behind me the whole time and highlight the quoted line in yellow when I read it.

## Variations

- Clip reaction, a video plays behind you instead of an image.
- Duet style, source on the left, you on the right.
- Chart annotation, the source is a chart you mark up.

## FAQ

**Do I need a real green screen?**
No. A plain wall and even light is enough for the background removal. Avoid patterns and anything close to your hair color.

**Can I use someone else's post?**
Commentary on public posts is normal. Keep it attributed and add something the post doesn't say.

**Where do captions go?**
The side you're not on, below the key text. Never over the line you're pointing at.
