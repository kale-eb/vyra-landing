**A story time is you telling a personal story to camera, usually while doing something with your hands, with cuts and punch-ins instead of b-roll.**

No b-roll needed. The cut and your energy carry it.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Personal stories, awkward moments, "you won't believe what happened" |
| Hook style | A cliffhanger, "so I get a text from my landlord at 2am" |
| Pacing | Fast cuts, punch in on every reveal |
| Caption style | Word-by-word or short phrases, high contrast |
| Music | Faint or none |
| Shot list | One continuous take, usually with an activity (makeup, cooking, drawing). A punch-in framing. |
| Common mistakes | 20 seconds explaining who everyone is. Losing the thread. Flat delivery. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the cliffhanger line
- 3-10s: minimum context, two names max
- 10-90s: what happened, in order, a small reveal every 10 seconds
- 90-110s: the big reveal
- 110-120s: how it ended, one line

## How to shoot it

- Do something with your hands. It relaxes you and gives the frame movement.
- Frame from the chest up, activity visible at the bottom.
- Tell the story once out loud before recording.
- One take. Restarts kill it.
- Keep energy on the reveals.

## How to edit it

- Cut every pause and tangent.
- Punch in 15-20% on each reveal, back out on the next setup.
- Word-by-word captions if you talk fast, phrases if calm.
- A sound effect on the big reveal is enough.
- Keep the activity continuous across cuts so jump cuts look natural.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is a 6-minute story time. Cut it to 2 minutes. Remove pauses, tangents, and any part where I repeat myself. Zoom in slightly on every moment I say something surprising and zoom back out after. Captions word by word in bold white with a black outline.

> Open on the line "and then she said she knew" and then cut back to the start of the story.

## Variations

- Get ready with me story, told during makeup or an outfit.
- Cooking story, told while cooking.
- Car story, parked, very casual.

## FAQ

**Should I show the people in the story?**
No. Nicknames are enough. Keep it about what happened to you.

**Can it be long?**
Yes, if every 10 seconds has a small reveal. Cut anything that doesn't move the story.

**How does Vyra know where the reveals are?**
It transcribes the take. Quote the lines, or ask it to find "the surprising moments".
