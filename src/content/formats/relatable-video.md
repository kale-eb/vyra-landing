**A relatable video captures one small, specific, shared experience in a single scene, with on-screen text naming the situation so the viewer recognizes themselves within the first second.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Growing reach fast, niche communities, personality accounts |
| Hook style | On-screen text: "when the client says 'just one small change'" |
| Pacing | One or two shots, one beat, done |
| Caption style | Big text hook, held the whole time |
| Music | Trending audio or a single sound |
| Shot list | One shot of the situation. Optional reaction shot. |
| Common mistakes | Situation too general. Text too long to read. Going past the punchline. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: text hook visible on the first frame
- 1-6s: the situation plays out
- 6-10s: the reaction or the punchline
- End immediately

## How to shoot it

- Write the text hook first. If it isn't specific to one group, rewrite it.
- One location, one action, real objects.
- Perform small. Underplay it.
- Shoot 3-5 versions with slightly different reactions.
- Keep the phone still or on a very simple move.

## How to edit it

- Text on the first frame, top third, two lines max.
- Cut to the reaction exactly on the beat of the audio.
- End the video one frame after the punchline lands.
- No captions on speech unless there is speech.
- Loop it if the first frame and last frame can connect.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 9-second relatable video: put the text "when the client says 'just one small change'" at the top for the whole video, use the clip of me staring at the screen, then cut to the clip of me closing the laptop right on the beat of the attached sound. End immediately after.

> Try three versions with the three different reaction clips and the same hook text.

## Variations

- POV relatable: "POV:" prefix, camera as the viewer.
- Text-only relatable: static shot, all the story in the text.
- Series relatable: same character, different situation each day.
- Duet relatable: react to someone else's relatable.

## FAQ

**How specific should the situation be?**
Specific enough that one group says "this is literally me". If everyone relates, no one shares it.

**How long?**
Under 15 seconds. Shorter loops more and gets replays.

**Can Vyra cut on the beat of a sound?**
Yes. Attach the audio and ask for the cut to land on the beat or the drop.
