**A lessons learned video ties one real outcome, like a number or a failure, to two or three specific takeaways, with the outcome shown on screen.**

Showing the number is the whole point. "Be consistent" with no proof is a skip.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Founders, creators, freelancers, anyone with a result |
| Hook style | The result as a number, "I posted 100 videos. 3 things I'd change." |
| Pacing | One lesson per 15-20 seconds |
| Caption style | Phrase captions plus a card per lesson |
| Music | Light, neutral |
| Shot list | Talking head. Screenshot of the proof. One b-roll clip per lesson. |
| Common mistakes | Generic lessons. No proof. Too many lessons. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the outcome and how many lessons
- 3-8s: the proof on screen
- 8-70s: each lesson, what I did, what happened, what I'd do now
- 70-80s: the one that matters most
- 80-85s: what's next

## How to shoot it

- Only pick a lesson if you can name the moment you learned it.
- Record the proof screen first so you can point at it.
- One take per lesson.
- Talk like you're thinking it through, not lecturing.
- Film one b-roll clip per lesson, even a loose one.

## How to edit it

- Proof on screen in the first 5 seconds. Numbers shown, not just said.
- Numbered title card at the start of each lesson.
- B-roll on "what I did", back to your face on "what I'd do now".
- Punch in on the most important lesson.
- Captions throughout.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is a "3 things I learned from 100 posts" video. Cut it to 80 seconds. Put the analytics screenshot on screen when I say "a hundred videos", and add a numbered title card at the start of each lesson. Phrase captions throughout, light music under my voice.

> Reorder so the lesson about "hooks" goes first, and cut the intro so the first thing I say is the number.

## Variations

- Post-mortem, one thing that went wrong, three causes.
- Milestone recap, "what got me to 10k".
- Year in review, one lesson per quarter.

## FAQ

**How many lessons?**
Three. Two feels thin, four gets skipped.

**Do I have to show real numbers?**
Yes. Blur what's sensitive, keep the headline number.

**Can Vyra build the title cards and place the screenshot?**
Yes. Describe the card once and ask it to use that on each lesson and drop the screenshot where you say the number.
