**A speed paint is a full painting session compressed into 20 to 60 seconds by time remapping, so the viewer watches a blank surface become a finished piece in one continuous rush.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Showing skill fast, growing an art account, selling prints of the finished piece |
| Hook style | The finished piece for half a second, then jump to the blank canvas |
| Pacing | 20x to 80x speed with 2 or 3 brief slowdowns on the best strokes |
| Caption style | One title, optional single line at the reveal |
| Music | One track with a clear build; the reveal lands on the drop |
| Shot list | One locked overhead or 3/4 angle for the whole session. Optional second angle of your face for 3 clips. The finished piece held still for 4 seconds. |
| Common mistakes | Camera bumps mid-session. Speed so high the piece never reads. No slowdowns, so nothing feels earned. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: finished piece, one frame, then cut
- 1-4s: blank surface, first strokes at high speed
- 4-30s: block-in and mid-layers, steady fast speed
- 30-40s: one slowdown on a detail stroke, back to fast
- 40-50s: final details, speed ramps down
- 50-60s: reveal, held still, title or signature

## How to shoot it

- Lock the phone on a tripod or overhead arm. Do not touch it until you are done.
- Frame the whole surface plus a hand's width of margin so nothing crops.
- Use one constant light. Daylight that changes over four hours will flicker when sped up.
- Record in one continuous clip if storage allows, or stop only at natural breaks.
- Keep your head and shoulders out of the frame unless the format is the "artist plus canvas" variant.
- Film the finished piece separately with clean light for the reveal.

## How to edit it

- Time remap the full session to land at 40 to 50 seconds before the reveal.
- Pick 2 or 3 strokes worth watching and drop those to 2x or real time for a second each.
- Cut dead stretches (mixing, stepping back, phone checks) entirely rather than speeding through them.
- Put the reveal on the music drop or the last hit.
- One title in the first 2 seconds, nothing else on screen.
- Keep the last 4 seconds still. That is where saves and shares happen.

## Variations

- Reveal first: hold the finished piece for 2 seconds before the rewind.
- Artist plus canvas: second angle with your face, cut in 3 times.
- Layer speed paint: one clip per layer, each with its own slowdown.
- Progress speed paint: the same piece across several sessions, date stamps between them.

## FAQ

**How fast should the speed-up be?**
Fast enough that the piece finishes in about 40 seconds, slow enough that the viewer can read the shapes forming. Between 20x and 80x for most sessions.

**Do I need a second camera?**
No. One locked angle is the format. A face angle is a variation, not a requirement.

**Can Vyra handle a 3-hour file?**
Yes. It analyzes every scene on upload, so you can ask it to find the strokes worth slowing down instead of scrubbing three hours yourself.
