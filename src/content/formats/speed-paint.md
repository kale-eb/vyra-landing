**A speed paint is a whole painting session sped up to 20 to 60 seconds, so you watch a blank surface turn into the finished piece.**

Lock the phone and don't touch it for four hours. The hardest part is remembering that.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Showing skill fast, growing an art account, selling prints of the piece |
| Hook style | Finished piece for half a second, then the blank canvas |
| Pacing | 20x to 80x with 2 or 3 short slowdowns on the best strokes |
| Caption style | One title, maybe one line at the reveal |
| Music | One track with a build, reveal on the drop |
| Shot list | One locked overhead or 3/4 angle for the whole session. The finished piece held still for 4 seconds. |
| Common mistakes | Bumping the camera. So fast the piece never reads. No slowdowns. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: finished piece, one frame
- 1-4s: blank surface, first strokes fast
- 4-30s: block-in and mid layers, steady fast
- 30-40s: one slowdown on a detail, then fast again
- 40-50s: final details, slowing down
- 50-60s: reveal, held, title or signature

## How to shoot it

- Tripod or overhead arm. Don't touch it until you're done.
- Frame the whole surface plus a hand's width of margin.
- One constant light. Daylight over four hours flickers when sped up.
- Record in one clip if storage allows.
- Keep your head and shoulders out of frame.
- Film the finished piece separately in clean light.

## How to edit it

- Speed the session to land at 40-50 seconds before the reveal.
- Pick 2 or 3 strokes and drop those to real time for a second each.
- Cut the dead stretches (mixing, stepping back) instead of speeding through them.
- Reveal on the drop or the last hit.
- One title in the first 2 seconds.
- Last 4 seconds still. That's where the saves happen.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Turn this 3-hour painting session into a 45-second speed paint for Reels. Start with one frame of the finished piece, then jump to the blank canvas. Speed everything up to fit, but slow down to real time for one second on the three most visually interesting strokes. Cut out any stretch where I step away or mix paint. End on the finished piece held for 4 seconds with the title "forest" in the top third.

> Sync the speed ramps to the attached track so the reveal lands on the drop.

> Remove the section from 1:12:00 to 1:20:00 where the light changed, and match the color between the two halves.

## Variations

- Reveal first, hold the finished piece 2 seconds, then rewind.
- Artist plus canvas, a second angle with your face cut in 3 times.
- Progress speed paint, the same piece across sessions with date stamps.

## FAQ

**How fast?**
Fast enough to finish in about 40 seconds, slow enough that you can see the shapes forming. 20x to 80x usually.

**Do I need a second camera?**
No. One locked angle is the format.

**Can Vyra handle a 3-hour file?**
Yes. It analyzes every scene on upload, so you can ask it to find the strokes worth slowing down instead of scrubbing three hours.
