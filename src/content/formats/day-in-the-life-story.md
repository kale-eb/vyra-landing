**A day-in-the-life story is a chronological vlog compressed into under 90 seconds with a narrative voiceover that gives the day a point, so it reads as a story rather than a diary.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Creators, founders, artists, students, "what my job is actually like" |
| Hook style | Text or voiceover that frames the day: "the day I shipped the thing" |
| Pacing | 1.5-3 seconds per clip, faster mid-day, slower at night |
| Caption style | Phrase captions on voiceover, time-of-day labels |
| Music | One track the whole way, ducked under voiceover |
| Shot list | 20-40 clips across the day: wake up, commute, work close-ups, meals, one wide, one detail per location, the end of the day. |
| Common mistakes | Filming only the pretty parts. No voiceover, so no point. Too many clips of coffee. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: framing line, what today is about
- 3-15s: morning, fast
- 15-50s: the work, the thing the day is about, slower
- 50-70s: a small setback or moment of doubt
- 70-85s: evening, resolution
- 85-90s: one sentence of reflection

## How to shoot it

- Decide the point of the day before you film. "The day I..." not "a day".
- Film 3-5 seconds every time you change location or task.
- Get one close-up of hands doing the work at each stop.
- Film transitions: doors, walking, putting the phone down.
- Record the voiceover that night while it's fresh.

## How to edit it

- Write the voiceover first, then cut footage to it.
- Cut on movement. Door closes, cut. Cup lands, cut.
- Add small time labels ("7:40am") on location changes, not on every clip.
- Slow the cut rate for the work section, that's the content.
- Keep the music continuous. One track, ducked under voice.
- End on a quiet shot with no caption.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Build a 75-second day-in-the-life from today's clips in chronological order, cut to my voiceover. Keep the clips of me painting longer, 3-4 seconds, and make the morning and commute clips fast, 1-2 seconds. Add small time-of-day labels when the location changes. Music low under my voice.

## Variations

- Founder day: meetings, shipping, metrics, a small win.
- Artist day: studio, materials, progress, a finished piece.
- Silent day: no voiceover, on-screen text only.
- Two-days-compared: a good day vs a bad day, cross-cut.

## FAQ

**How many clips should I film?**
30-40. You'll use about 20. Short clips are easier to cut than long ones.

**Voiceover or on-screen text?**
Voiceover if you can write a point of view. Text if the footage speaks for itself and you want it quieter.

**Can Vyra order the clips by time automatically?**
Yes. It reads recording dates, so you can ask for a chronological cut and then adjust.
