**A day in the life story is a chronological vlog cut to under 90 seconds with a voiceover that gives the day a point.**

My YC day one video is one of these. The voiceover is what makes it a story instead of a diary.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Creators, founders, artists, students, "what my job is actually like" |
| Hook style | Text or voiceover that frames the day, "the day I shipped the thing" |
| Pacing | 1.5-3 seconds per clip, faster mid-day, slower at night |
| Caption style | Phrase captions on the voiceover, time labels on location changes |
| Music | One track, ducked under the voice |
| Shot list | 20-40 clips. Wake up, commute, work close-ups, meals, one wide and one detail per stop, the end of the day. |
| Common mistakes | Only the pretty parts. No voiceover. Too much coffee. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: what today is about
- 3-15s: morning, fast
- 15-50s: the work, slower
- 50-70s: a small setback
- 70-85s: evening
- 85-90s: one sentence looking back

## How to shoot it

- Decide the point of the day before you film. "The day I..." and not "a day".
- Film 3-5 seconds every time you change location or task.
- One close-up of hands doing the work at each stop.
- Film the transitions. Doors, walking, putting the phone down.
- Record the voiceover that night while it's fresh.

## How to edit it

- Write the voiceover first, then cut footage to it.
- Cut on movement. Door closes, cut.
- Small time labels ("7:40am") on location changes only.
- Slow down for the work section. That's the content.
- One track, ducked under the voice.
- End on a quiet shot with no caption.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Build a 75-second day-in-the-life from today's clips in chronological order, cut to my voiceover. Keep the clips of me painting longer, 3-4 seconds, and make the morning and commute clips fast, 1-2 seconds. Add small time-of-day labels when the location changes. Music low under my voice.

> Use the attached reference for the pacing and caption style. Match how they do the time labels.

> Swap the second coffee clip for the shot of the canvas drying, and cut on beats where you can.

## Variations

- Founder day, meetings, shipping, a small win.
- Artist day, studio, materials, a finished piece.
- Silent day, no voiceover, text only.

## FAQ

**How many clips should I film?**
30-40. You'll use about 20. Short clips are easier to cut.

**Voiceover or text?**
Voiceover if you have a point of view. Text if the footage speaks for itself.

**Can Vyra order the clips by time?**
Yes. It reads recording dates, so ask for a chronological cut and adjust from there.
