**A sketchbook flip-through turns pages on camera at a steady rhythm, holding on the strongest spreads a beat longer, and it works as a low-effort way to show a lot of work in under a minute.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Showing range, filling a posting gap, gift and crafts content, end-of-term recaps |
| Hook style | The best spread for one second, then the closed cover |
| Pacing | One page turn every 1-2 seconds, holds of 2-3 seconds on favorites |
| Caption style | Optional page labels, or none |
| Music | Gentle with a steady pulse; turns can land on beats |
| Shot list | Closed book, hands in. Every spread turned in order. Two or three close-ups of details. Closed book, hands out. |
| Common mistakes | Shadow of the phone on the page. Turning too fast to read anything. Uneven holds. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: best spread, still
- 1-3s: closed cover, hands come in
- 3-35s: every spread in order, turns on the beat
- 35-40s: two close-ups of details
- 40-45s: cover closes, hands out

## How to shoot it

- Overhead, book flat, phone directly above. Move the light to the side so the phone's shadow falls off the page.
- Turn pages with one hand from the bottom corner so the hand hides the least.
- Turn at a steady pace. Vyra can retime, but even turns look better than fixed ones.
- Shoot the close-ups separately, handheld, slowly.
- Do it twice. The second take is always calmer.

## How to edit it

- Cut so each turn starts on a beat. Hold favorites for a full extra beat.
- Skip weak pages by cutting between turns; the viewer will not notice.
- Speed the turning motion slightly if the take was slow.
- Close-ups go near the end, not the middle.
- One title with the sketchbook's name or the date range, optional.
- Music fade on the cover closing.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 40-second flip-through from this take. Open on one still of the flower spread, then the closed book. Cut so each page turn lands on the beat of the attached track. Hold the three spreads I marked in the transcript notes for an extra 2 seconds each. Skip pages 8 and 9. End on the book closing.

> Add a small caption in the top corner with the page's title when I say it out loud.

> Insert the two detail close-ups after the last spread, 2 seconds each.

## Variations

- Themed flip: only the pages on one subject.
- Commentary flip: a line of voiceover per spread.
- Gift flip: a handmade book being flipped by the person who received it.
- Progress flip: the same sketchbook at month one and month six.

## FAQ

**How many pages is too many?**
Above 25 turns the rhythm gets numbing. Cut the weak ones.

**Do I need to talk?**
No. Music and page turns are enough. Add voice if you want to explain one or two spreads.

**Can Vyra sync the turns to the music?**
Yes. Attach the track and ask for each turn on a beat; it will retime the gaps between turns.
