**A sketchbook flip-through turns pages on camera at a steady rhythm, holding a beat longer on the best spreads.**

Lowest effort way to show a lot of work. Do two takes. The second one is always calmer.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Showing range, filling a posting gap, end-of-term recaps |
| Hook style | The best spread for one second, then the closed cover |
| Pacing | A turn every 1-2 seconds, 2-3 second holds on favorites |
| Caption style | Optional page labels, or none |
| Music | Gentle with a steady pulse |
| Shot list | Closed book, hands in. Every spread in order. Two or three close-ups. Closed book, hands out. |
| Common mistakes | The phone's shadow on the page. Turning too fast. Uneven holds. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: best spread, still
- 1-3s: closed cover, hands come in
- 3-35s: every spread in order, turns on the beat
- 35-40s: two close-ups
- 40-45s: cover closes, hands out

## How to shoot it

- Overhead, book flat, phone directly above. Light from the side so the phone's shadow falls off the page.
- Turn from the bottom corner with one hand so it hides the least.
- Turn at a steady pace.
- Shoot the close-ups separately, handheld, slowly.
- Do it twice.

## How to edit it

- Each turn starts on a beat. Hold favorites an extra beat.
- Skip weak pages by cutting between turns. Nobody notices.
- Speed the turning motion a little if the take was slow.
- Close-ups near the end.
- One title with the sketchbook's name or date range, if you want.
- Fade the music on the cover closing.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 40-second flip-through from this take. Open on one still of the flower spread, then the closed book. Cut so each page turn lands on the beat of the attached track. Hold the three spreads I marked in the transcript notes for an extra 2 seconds each. Skip pages 8 and 9. End on the book closing.

> Add a small caption in the top corner with the page's title when I say it out loud.

> Insert the two detail close-ups after the last spread, 2 seconds each.

## Variations

- Themed flip, only the pages on one subject.
- Commentary flip, one line of voiceover per spread.
- Progress flip, the same sketchbook at month one and month six.

## FAQ

**How many pages is too many?**
Above 25 turns it gets numbing. Cut the weak ones.

**Do I need to talk?**
No. Music and page turns are enough.

**Can Vyra sync the turns to the music?**
Yes. Attach the track and ask for each turn on a beat. It retimes the gaps.
