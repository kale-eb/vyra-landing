**A talking head video is a single person speaking directly to camera, cut tight so every sentence earns its place, usually with captions and no b-roll.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Opinions, advice, explanations, personal updates |
| Hook style | Spoken claim or question in the first 2 seconds |
| Pacing | One idea per 5-8 seconds, no dead air |
| Caption style | 3-5 word phrases, centered below the mouth |
| Music | Optional low bed at -20 dB or none |
| Shot list | One medium close-up, eyes in upper third, shoulders in frame. Optional second angle (punch-in 15-20%) for cuts. Room tone for 5 seconds. |
| Common mistakes | Leaving pauses and "um"s in. Filming too wide so the face is small on a phone. Reading from notes below the lens. |

## Structure

- 0-2s: the hook, stated as a claim, not a greeting
- 2-8s: context, why this matters to the viewer
- 8-40s: the body, 2-4 points, each one a clean sentence block
- 40-55s: the payoff, the thing they can do today
- 55-60s: soft close, no "like and subscribe" speech

## How to shoot it

- Face a window, camera at eye level, lens an arm's length away.
- Frame from mid-chest up. Shoulders fully inside the frame on both sides.
- Record 2-3 takes of each point. Do not restart the whole video.
- Speak faster than feels natural. Cutting fixes slow, it cannot fix flat.
- Leave a 2-second pause before and after each take so cuts have room.
- Lock exposure and focus so cuts don't flicker.

## How to edit it

- Cut every pause longer than 0.3 seconds and every false start.
- Alternate the full frame and a 15-20% punch-in on sentence boundaries to hide jump cuts.
- Captions: 3-5 words per phrase, one style, no per-word bounce unless the format calls for it.
- Give one line in the video a larger, composed caption treatment. Only one.
- Keep original room audio. Add a music bed only if the delivery is calm.
- End on the last useful word, not on a fade.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Cut this 6-minute talking head down to the best 60 seconds for Reels. Remove every pause, "um", repeated take and the parts where I look at my notes. Keep my strongest take of each point. Add 3-5 word captions in white, centered below my mouth. No music.

> This is a talking head about pricing. Alternate between the full frame and a slight zoom-in at natural sentence breaks so the jump cuts feel intentional. Make the line "you are not charging enough" a big bold caption on its own.

> Match the caption style and cut rhythm of the reference video I attached. Keep my footage, my voice, and my order of points.

## Variations

- Punch-in talking head: two framings from one take, cut on every idea.
- Text-hook talking head: a bold on-screen title covers the first 2 seconds instead of a spoken hook.
- Walking talking head: same structure, handheld, outside, higher energy.
- Whisper talking head: quiet delivery, close mic, calm caption style.

## FAQ

**How long should a talking head be?**
30-60 seconds on TikTok and Reels, 60-90 seconds on Shorts, and as long as the idea needs on YouTube. Cut until nothing is skippable.

**Do I need two cameras?**
No. A single 4K take gives you enough resolution to punch in 20% and get a second angle in the edit.

**Should every word be captioned?**
Yes for short-form. Most viewers watch muted, and captions raise retention on the first 3 seconds.
