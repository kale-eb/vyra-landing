**A storytelling video is a short story with a setup, a turn, and an ending, told in voiceover or to camera and covered with footage from the story.**

My leaving-Brown video is one of these. Start in the middle, then go back.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Personal experiences, how the brand started, lessons, emotional stuff |
| Hook style | Start at the tense part, "the client had already paid and I had nothing" |
| Pacing | Slow open, faster middle, one held beat at the turn |
| Caption style | Phrase captions, one big line at the turn |
| Music | Builds and drops with the story |
| Shot list | 10-20 clips or photos of the places, objects, and people in the story. One to-camera take for the last line. |
| Common mistakes | Starting at the beginning. Saying feelings instead of showing moments. No clear turn. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the most tense moment, mid-story
- 3-15s: rewind to before it changed
- 15-50s: the problem gets worse, 2-3 specific beats
- 50-60s: the turn, held, music drops
- 60-85s: what happened after
- 85-95s: the line you want remembered, on your face

## How to shoot it

- Write the story in six sentences first.
- Record the voiceover in one go, close to the mic, sitting down.
- Use real footage and photos from the time if you have them. Real beats pretty.
- Shoot new b-roll of the places and objects in the story.
- Record one take of you saying the last line to camera.

## How to edit it

- Cut the voiceover tight. Keep a breath only at the turn.
- Footage goes on the nouns. Say "the studio", show the studio.
- Slow down at the turn. Hold one shot 3-4 seconds.
- Music quiet under the setup, builds through the problem, drops at the turn.
- One big caption on the line that matters. Normal captions everywhere else.
- End on your face.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is a voiceover about the time I almost quit painting. Build a 90-second vertical story. Open on the line "I had one week and no money", then go back to the beginning. Cover each part with the studio clips and old photos in the project. Music builds and drops out at the line "then the email came". Captions in 3-5 word phrases, make that one line big.

> Cut the to-camera ending onto the last 10 seconds so I'm on screen for the final sentence. Keep the music low under it.

> Use the pacing of the attached reference for the turn. Hold the shot longer and cut the music there.

## Variations

- Story time, to camera the whole way, no b-roll.
- Photo story, stills with slow zooms, voiceover only.
- Then and now, two timelines cross-cut.

## FAQ

**Do I need to be on camera?**
No. But one to-camera line at the end gets more follows.

**How do I find the hook?**
Take the worst or most surprising moment and start there. Tell the rest as a flashback.

**Can Vyra build this from a rambling voice memo?**
Yes. It transcribes the audio and can reorder it if you say what you want first, like "tension first, then rewind".
