**An explainer breaks down one idea or "why" question with a spoken argument and simple graphics on screen.**

Three ideas max. If you have five, that's two videos.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Concepts, industry topics, "why does X happen", how your product thinks |
| Hook style | The question, or a surprising fact |
| Pacing | 10-20 seconds per idea, graphic changes with each one |
| Caption style | Phrase captions, key terms as big text |
| Music | Neutral, low |
| Shot list | Face or voiceover. One diagram per idea. Real b-roll where it exists. A summary card. |
| Common mistakes | Too many ideas. Graphics that decorate instead of explain. No summary. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the question
- 3-10s: why it matters to them
- 10-80s: the explanation, 3 ideas, one graphic each
- 80-95s: the summary in one sentence
- 95-100s: what to do with it

## How to shoot it

- Write the explanation as three sentences before anything else.
- Record the voiceover close to the mic, calm.
- Sketch one simple diagram per idea. Boxes and arrows.
- Film b-roll of the real thing if there is one.
- Record the summary line to camera.

## How to edit it

- Graphic appears the moment the idea is introduced.
- Key terms as big text the first time you say them.
- Build diagrams piece by piece.
- Go back to face or b-roll between graphics.
- Summary card with the three ideas at the end.
- Captions throughout.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is an explainer on why short videos get more reach. Cut it to 90 seconds. When I introduce each of the three reasons, create a simple motion graphic that shows it. A bar chart for "completion rate", a loop arrow for "replays", and a share icon multiplying for "shares". Make the phrase "completion rate" big text the first time I say it. Phrase captions throughout, summary card with the three reasons at the end.

> Keep the graphics minimal, black text and one blue accent, matching the attached reference.

## Variations

- Whiteboard explainer, drawings appear as you talk.
- Data explainer, charts are the main visual.
- Myth buster, the claim, then why it's wrong.

## FAQ

**How many ideas?**
Three.

**Do I need a designer?**
No. Describe the graphic in plain words and Vyra builds it. Shapes, arrows, one accent color.

**Face or voiceover?**
Face for trust, voiceover for clarity. A lot of explainers use face just for the hook and the summary.
