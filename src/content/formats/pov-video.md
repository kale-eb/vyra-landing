**A POV video puts the camera where the viewer would be, so you talk to the lens like it's them, with a "POV:" caption setting the scene.**

Camera at their eye height, real room, real audio. Cuts break the spell, so keep them to one or two.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Comedy, roleplay, product moments, a day in your shoes |
| Hook style | "POV: you're the new hire and it's your first standup" |
| Pacing | One scene, real time, 1-3 cuts |
| Caption style | POV text held the whole video, speech captions optional |
| Music | Ambient or a trending sound |
| Shot list | Camera at the viewer's eye level. You talking to the lens. Hands entering from below for first person. |
| Common mistakes | Camera not at eye level. Looking off-lens. A scenario that needs explaining. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: POV text on frame
- 1-15s: the scene, talking to the lens
- 15-20s: the twist or the awkward beat
- end on the reaction

## How to shoot it

- Phone at the viewer's eye height. Seated viewer means seated height.
- Talk to the lens, never off to the side.
- For first person, hold the phone at chest height so your hands come in from the bottom.
- Real background. The actual office, the actual kitchen.
- Record the room sound.

## How to edit it

- POV text top center, first frame.
- As few cuts as possible.
- If you cut, cut on a blink or a head turn.
- Keep the real audio. One sound effect on the twist, max.
- End the moment the reaction lands.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 15-second POV video with the text "POV: you're the new hire and it's your first standup" at the top the whole time. Use the take where I look at the camera and ask "so what did you work on yesterday", cut once to the take where I wait awkwardly, and end there. Keep the room audio.

> Add small captions only for the spoken line, low so they don't cover the POV text.

## Variations

- First person, hands in frame, no face.
- Character POV, you play a character talking to them.
- Object POV, the camera is the coffee or the dog.

## FAQ

**Does it have to start with "POV:"?**
It's the convention and people read it instantly. Drop it only if the scene is obvious.

**Speech captions or not?**
If the line is the joke, caption it small and low. If your face is the joke, leave it clean.

**Can Vyra hold text for the whole video?**
Yes. Ask for text that stays on for the full length.
