**A POV video puts the camera in the viewer's position, so the creator or the scene addresses the lens as if it were the viewer, with a text caption starting "POV:" that sets the scenario.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Comedy, roleplay, immersive product moments, day-in-your-shoes |
| Hook style | "POV: you're the new hire and it's your first standup" |
| Pacing | One scene, natural time, 1-3 cuts |
| Caption style | POV text held the whole video, speech captions optional |
| Music | Ambient or trending sound |
| Shot list | Camera at eye level of the viewer. Creator addresses the lens. Hands entering the frame from below for first-person. |
| Common mistakes | Camera not at eye level. Creator looking off-lens. Scenario needs too much explanation. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: POV text on frame
- 1-15s: the scene plays, addressing the lens
- 15-20s: the twist or the awkward beat
- End on the reaction

## How to shoot it

- Mount the phone at the viewer's eye height. Sitting viewer means seated height.
- Talk to the lens, never to the side.
- For first-person, hold the phone at chest height and let your hands enter from the bottom.
- Keep the background real: the actual office, the actual kitchen.
- Record ambient sound; it sells the immersion.

## How to edit it

- POV text top center, first frame.
- Minimal cuts. The immersion breaks on every cut.
- If cutting, cut on a natural blink or head turn.
- Keep the real audio, add a sound only for the twist.
- End the moment the reaction lands.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 15-second POV video with the text "POV: you're the new hire and it's your first standup" at the top the whole time. Use the take where I look at the camera and ask "so what did you work on yesterday", cut once to the take where I wait awkwardly, and end there. Keep the real room audio.

> Add subtle captions only for the spoken line, placed low so they don't cover the POV text.

## Variations

- First-person POV: hands in frame, no face.
- Character POV: creator plays a character speaking to you.
- Object POV: camera is the product, the coffee, the dog.
- Series POV: same scenario, escalating each episode.

## FAQ

**Does the text have to start with "POV:"?**
It's the convention and it helps the viewer read the format instantly. Drop it only if the scene is unmistakable.

**Speech captions or not?**
Optional. If the line is the joke, caption it small and low. If the face is the joke, leave it clean.

**Can the agent hold text for the whole video?**
Yes. Ask for text that stays on for the full duration and it places it as a persistent layer.
