**A vlog is a first-person video diary. Talking to camera, b-roll of what is happening, and music, cut so the day has a beginning, a point, and an end.**

This is the format that made me build Vyra. Hours of footage, 90 seconds of video, whole nights of scrubbing.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Personality channels, creators, artists, travel, lifestyle |
| Hook style | Say what today is about in the first sentence |
| Pacing | Talking 5-15 seconds, b-roll 2-4 seconds |
| Caption style | Phrase captions on speech, none on b-roll |
| Music | One or two tracks, ducked under speech |
| Shot list | Talking to camera at each stop. B-roll of hands, food, places, walking. Doors and cars as transitions. A closing talk. |
| Common mistakes | 40 seconds of talking with no cut. No point to the day. Music over your voice. |

## Structure

- 0-5s: today's plan, to camera
- 5-20s: setting off, b-roll and a line
- 20-60s: the main thing, talking plus b-roll
- 60-75s: a small problem or surprise
- 75-85s: wrap up to camera
- 85-90s: last b-roll shot

## How to shoot it

- Say the plan into the camera first thing.
- Talk in short bursts, then film b-roll of what you just described.
- Camera at chest height, face in the upper third.
- Film every transition. Leaving, arriving.
- Record a closing thought before you stop for the day.

## How to edit it

- Keep only the talking that moves the day forward.
- Cover every mention with b-roll.
- Music 8-10 dB under speech.
- Cut b-roll on movement and on the music.
- Captions on speech only.
- Closing shot with no captions and no talking.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Cut today's vlog to 90 seconds. Keep my talking segments tight, only the sentences where I say what I'm doing or how it went, and cover everything I mention with the matching b-roll. Add phrase captions on my speech, a soft music bed that ducks under my voice, and end on the shot of the sunset with no captions.

> Use the "Vlog (Short)" template style for the pacing and caption look.

> Cut the part where I repeat the plan twice and move the coffee shop section before the studio section.

## Variations

- Silent vlog, no talking, text only.
- Studio vlog, one project.
- Weekly vlog, several days in one.

## FAQ

**Vlog editing takes me hours. Is that normal?**
You're scrubbing an hour of footage for 90 seconds. Vyra transcribes and analyzes everything on upload, so you can ask for "the part where I talk about the deadline" instead.

**Do I have to talk to camera?**
That's what makes it a vlog and not a montage. Three short moments are enough.

**How much footage per minute of vlog?**
About 10 to 1. A 90-second vlog comes from 15-20 minutes of clips.
