**A vlog is a first-person video diary that mixes talking to camera, b-roll of what is happening, and music, edited so the day has a beginning, a point, and an end.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Personality channels, creators, artists, travel, lifestyle |
| Hook style | Tell the viewer what today is about in the first sentence |
| Pacing | Talking segments 5-15 seconds, b-roll 2-4 seconds |
| Caption style | Phrase captions on speech, none on b-roll |
| Music | One or two tracks, ducked under speech |
| Shot list | Talking-to-camera segments at each location. B-roll of hands, food, places, walking. Transitions: doors, cars, walking. A closing talk. |
| Common mistakes | Talking for 40 seconds without a cut. No point to the day. Music fighting the voice. |

## Structure

- 0-5s: today's premise, to camera
- 5-20s: setting off, b-roll and a line
- 20-60s: the main activity, talking plus b-roll
- 60-75s: a small problem or surprise
- 75-85s: wrap-up to camera
- 85-90s: last b-roll shot

## How to shoot it

- Say the plan for the day into the camera first thing.
- Talk in short bursts, 10-20 seconds, then film b-roll of what you just described.
- Hold the camera at chest height, face in the upper third.
- Film every transition: leaving a place, arriving somewhere.
- Record a closing thought before you stop for the day.

## How to edit it

- Cut talking segments to only the sentences that move the day forward.
- Cover every mention with b-roll.
- Music under everything, ducked 8-10 dB under speech.
- Cut b-roll on movement and on the music.
- Captions on speech only.
- Keep the closing shot without captions or talking.

## Variations

- Silent vlog: no talking, text only.
- Studio vlog: process-driven, one project.
- Travel vlog: location-hopping, higher b-roll ratio.
- Weekly vlog: several days compressed.

## FAQ

**Why does vlog editing take so long?**
Because you're scrubbing an hour of footage to find 90 seconds. Vyra transcribes and analyzes everything on upload, so you can search "the part where I talk about the deadline" instead of scrubbing.

**Do I need to talk to camera?**
It's what makes it a vlog rather than a montage. Even three short talking moments are enough.

**How much footage per minute of vlog?**
Roughly 10 to 1. A 90-second vlog comes from 15-20 minutes of clips.
