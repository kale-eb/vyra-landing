**An unboxing video shows a product being opened and handled for the first time, with the creator's genuine first reaction and close-ups of every item that comes out of the box.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Product launches, gifts, subscription boxes, tech, affiliate content |
| Hook style | The best item held to camera, then back to the sealed box |
| Pacing | Fast for packaging, slow for the hero item |
| Caption style | Item labels, minimal speech captions |
| Music | Light, or ASMR with no music |
| Shot list | Top-down of the box. Hands opening. Each item held up. Detail macro of the hero item. Reaction face (optional). |
| Common mistakes | Ten seconds of tape peeling. Items too far from the lens. No sense of what the product is for. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: hero item to camera, or the reaction face
- 3-8s: the box, sealed, what it is
- 8-35s: opening, items one by one, each labeled
- 35-50s: hero item, detail, first impressions
- 50-60s: verdict in one line

## How to shoot it

- Top-down angle on a tripod for the opening. A second angle at 45 degrees for held items.
- Bright, soft light. Avoid reflections on packaging.
- Hold each item still for 2 seconds close to the lens.
- Say the name of each item out loud as you lift it.
- Record ambient sound cleanly if going the ASMR route.

## How to edit it

- Cut the packaging to 3-5 seconds total.
- One clip per item, labeled with a small text tag.
- Slow down or hold on the hero item.
- Speed up any fumbling 2x.
- Keep the real sounds of opening if the music is off.
- Verdict on your face or as a final text card.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Cut this unboxing to 55 seconds. Speed through the packaging, then show each item for 2-3 seconds with a small label naming it when I say its name. Slow down on the headphones and add a close-up. Keep the natural sound, no music.

> Start the video with the clip of me holding the headphones up to camera, then cut back to the sealed box.

## Variations

- ASMR unboxing: no talking, no music, sound only.
- Tech unboxing: item labels plus spec cards.
- Subscription box: monthly recurring format with a rating at the end.
- Gift unboxing: reaction-driven, face in frame.

## FAQ

**Should I talk or stay silent?**
Talk for reviews and affiliate content. Stay silent for ASMR and aesthetic accounts. Both work; pick one per channel.

**How do I get item labels on screen without a designer?**
Describe the label style once and ask Vyra to apply it at each moment you name an item.

**Should I show the price?**
If it's affiliate content, yes, and disclose it. Price cards raise saves.
