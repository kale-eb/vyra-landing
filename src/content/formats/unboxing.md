**An unboxing shows a product being opened for the first time, with your real first reaction and a close-up of each thing that comes out of the box.**

Nobody wants ten seconds of tape peeling. Cut the packaging to a few seconds and spend the time on the good item.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Launches, gifts, subscription boxes, tech, affiliate content |
| Hook style | The best item held to camera, then back to the sealed box |
| Pacing | Fast on packaging, slow on the hero item |
| Caption style | Item labels, minimal speech captions |
| Music | Light, or none for ASMR |
| Shot list | Top-down of the box. Hands opening. Each item held up. Macro of the hero item. Reaction face if you want. |
| Common mistakes | Long packaging. Items too far from the lens. No idea what the product is for. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: hero item to camera, or your reaction
- 3-8s: the sealed box, what it is
- 8-35s: opening, items one by one, labeled
- 35-50s: hero item, close, first impressions
- 50-60s: verdict in one line

## How to shoot it

- Top-down on a tripod for the opening. A second angle at 45 degrees for held items.
- Bright soft light. Watch for reflections on packaging.
- Hold each item still for 2 seconds, close to the lens.
- Say the name of each item as you lift it.
- Clean ambient sound if you're going ASMR.

## How to edit it

- Packaging down to 3-5 seconds total.
- One clip per item with a small text label.
- Slow down or hold on the hero item.
- Speed up fumbling 2x.
- Keep the real sounds if there's no music.
- Verdict on your face or a text card.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Cut this unboxing to 55 seconds. Speed through the packaging, then show each item for 2-3 seconds with a small label naming it when I say its name. Slow down on the headphones and add a close-up. Keep the natural sound, no music.

> Start with the clip of me holding the headphones up to camera, then cut back to the sealed box.

## Variations

- ASMR unboxing, no talking, no music.
- Tech unboxing, labels plus spec cards.
- Subscription box, same format every month with a rating at the end.

## FAQ

**Talk or stay quiet?**
Talk for reviews and affiliate stuff. Quiet for ASMR and aesthetic accounts. Pick one per channel.

**How do I get item labels without a designer?**
Describe the label once and ask Vyra to put it on each item when you say its name.

**Should I show the price?**
If it's affiliate, yes, and say so. Price cards get saves.
