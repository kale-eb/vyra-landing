**A before and after video shows the starting state, speeds through the process, and holds on the result.**

The whole thing rests on shooting the before and after from the exact same spot. Tape the tripod position.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Renovations, art, fitness, cleaning, design |
| Hook style | One frame of the after, then the before |
| Pacing | Slow before, very fast process, held after |
| Caption style | "Before" and "after" labels, one line of context |
| Music | Beat-driven, reveal on the drop |
| Shot list | Locked-off before. 10-30 process clips. Locked-off after from the same spot. Detail shots of the result. |
| Common mistakes | Different framing for before and after. Process too long. Reveal without a hold. |

## Structure

- 0-1s: one frame of the after (optional)
- 1-5s: the before, held, labeled
- 5-25s: the process, fast, on beats
- 25-30s: match cut from before to after
- 30-40s: details of the result
- 40-45s: one line on what it took

## How to shoot it

- Mark the tripod spot with tape. Shoot before and after from it.
- Same lens, same height, same light if you can.
- Film the process in 3-5 second bursts.
- Shoot the after wide, medium, and close.
- Get a slow push-in on the result.

## How to edit it

- Hold the before 3-4 seconds. Longer than feels right.
- Cut the process to the music, one clip per beat or half-beat.
- Match cut from before to after on the same frame.
- Land the reveal on the drop. Hold it 3 seconds, no caption.
- Details after the reveal.
- Optional split screen at the end.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 30-second transformation video. Hold the "before" clip for 4 seconds with a small "before" label, then cut the process clips fast on the beat, then match cut to the "after" clip on the drop and hold it for 3 seconds. Add the detail shots after.

> Put a one-frame flash of the after at the very start, then go to the before.

> Add a split screen of before and after for the last 3 seconds, before on the left.

## Variations

- Timelapse transformation, the process is one sped-up clip.
- Split screen the whole way.
- Multi-stage, three or four checkpoints with labels.

## FAQ

**How long should the before hold?**
3-4 seconds. People need to register it for the reveal to land.

**Does the process have to be on beat?**
No, but it holds people longer. Vyra can beat-sync that section.

**What if I forgot to film the before?**
Use a photo or the earliest shot you have, and label it honestly.
