**An art supplies review tests one product on camera, shows the result next to what you usually use, and gives a verdict in under a minute, with the swatch or stroke test doing most of the talking.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Affiliate income, answering the "what do you use" comments, search traffic on product names |
| Hook style | The verdict first ("this $6 brush replaced my $40 one") |
| Pacing | Quick: unbox 3s, test 20s, compare 10s, verdict 5s |
| Caption style | Product name on screen, phrase captions over speech |
| Music | Light, under the voice |
| Shot list | Product in hand. Overhead of the test: swatch, stroke, blend. Same test with your usual product, side by side. Your face for the verdict. Close-up of any flaw. |
| Common mistakes | Reviewing without a comparison. Hiding the price. Talking over the test instead of letting it show. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: verdict or the surprising claim, product in frame
- 3-8s: what it is, what it costs, why you tried it
- 8-30s: the test, overhead, real time
- 30-40s: side by side with your usual product
- 40-50s: one flaw, honestly
- 50-60s: verdict, would you buy it again

## How to shoot it

- Overhead for the test, same framing for both products so the comparison is fair.
- Same paper, same pressure, same light for both.
- Show the price tag or the receipt.
- Record the verdict to camera after the test, not before.
- Get a close-up of the failure if there is one. It is the most trusted shot in the video.

## How to edit it

- Verdict line first, cut from the end of the take.
- Test plays at real time; speed only the drying wait.
- Side-by-side as a split frame or a quick alternate.
- Product name and price as a persistent small caption.
- Phrase captions over the voice.
- End on your face, not the product.

## Variations

- Budget versus pro: two products, same test, price on screen.
- One-year review: the product after heavy use.
- Beginner kit review: what is worth it in a starter set.
- Unpopular opinion review: a beloved product that did not work for you.

## FAQ

**Do I need a comparison?**
Yes. A test without a reference means nothing to the viewer. Your usual product is the reference.

**Should I disclose affiliate links?**
Yes, on screen and in the caption. It also builds trust.

**Can Vyra build the split-screen comparison?**
Yes. Ask for the two test clips side by side and it will build the layout and align the timing.
