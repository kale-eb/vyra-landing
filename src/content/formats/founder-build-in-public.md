**A build-in-public video is a founder sharing a specific update, decision, or number from their company, mixing face, screen, and metrics so the audience follows the story of the product as it happens.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Early-stage startups, indie hackers, solo founders, launches |
| Hook style | The number or the decision: "we killed pricing tiers yesterday, here's why" |
| Pacing | Face 5-8 seconds, screen 3-5 seconds, alternate |
| Caption style | Phrase captions, big number cards |
| Music | Low, neutral |
| Shot list | Face take, handheld or desk. Screen recording of the product or dashboard. Metrics screenshot. Optional office or whiteboard. |
| Common mistakes | Vague updates ("big week"). Numbers spoken but not shown. Too polished. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: the update as a number or decision
- 3-10s: context in one sentence
- 10-50s: what happened, face and screen alternating
- 50-65s: what you learned or what's next
- 65-75s: ask for input

## How to shoot it

- Film the same day as the event. Freshness is the format.
- Handheld, walking or at your desk, no setup.
- Screen-record the actual thing: the feature, the chart, the message.
- Say numbers precisely. "1,240 users" beats "over a thousand".
- Record a version of the hook with the number first.

## How to edit it

- Number card on screen within 2 seconds.
- Cut to the screen recording when you describe the product.
- Metrics screenshot when you say the number, with a zoom.
- Keep it raw: jump cuts are fine, polish isn't the point.
- End on a question to the audience.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> This is a build-in-public update. Cut it to 60 seconds, show the Stripe screenshot with a zoom when I say "four thousand dollars", and cut to the product recording when I talk about the new feature. Big bold number card for the first 2 seconds that says "$4k MRR". Captions in phrases.

> Keep it feeling raw, jump cuts are fine, no transitions. Low neutral music.

## Variations

- Weekly update: fixed structure, same day every week.
- Launch day: hour-by-hour, higher energy.
- Post-mortem: what didn't work, honest.
- Revenue reveal: the number is the whole video.

## FAQ

**Should I show real revenue?**
Real numbers are why people follow. Round them if you must, but show the screen.

**How often?**
Weekly is the sweet spot. Daily works during a launch.

**Can Vyra keep a consistent look across weekly updates?**
Yes. Once you set a caption and card style, reference it in each new prompt or attach last week's video as the reference.
