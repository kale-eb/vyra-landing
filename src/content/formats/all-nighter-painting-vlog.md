**An all-nighter painting vlog packs one overnight session into a short that follows the clock, from setup to the finished piece at dawn.**

![5:48am, the finished triptych on the studio wall, from an all-nighter reel that reached 113K views](/newsletter/my-content-journey-as-an-artist-student-0-15k/04-Screenshot_2026-03-05_at_5.48.24_PM.png)

The timestamps tell the story. The 3am check-in where you look wrecked is the clip people watch for. Don't cut it.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Deadline stories, art school audiences, "why do I do this" content |
| Hook style | The time and the state of the canvas ("11:40 pm, it's blank, it's due at 9") |
| Pacing | A timestamp every 8-12 seconds, faster as the night goes, slow at dawn |
| Caption style | Timestamps as titles, phrases over the talking |
| Music | Upbeat, drops to quiet around 3am, lifts at dawn |
| Shot list | Locked canvas angle all night. Selfie check-ins every hour. The clock. Coffee, snacks, the floor. The window getting light. The finished piece in morning light. |
| Common mistakes | Only canvas, no you. Skipping the low point. Ending before dawn. |

## Structure

- 0-4s: the time, the blank canvas, the deadline
- 4-25s: 11pm to 1am, setup and block-in, quick timestamps
- 25-45s: 2am to 4am, the dip, music quieter, a tired check-in
- 45-60s: 4am to 6am, the push, fast canvas progress
- 60-75s: dawn, the window, the piece in real light
- 75-90s: one honest closing line, piece held still

## How to shoot it

- Lock one angle on the canvas and leave it running, or restart it at each check-in.
- Selfie check-in every hour. Say the time. Say how you feel. 20 seconds max.
- Film the small stuff. The clock, the coffee, the mess.
- Set an alarm for the window at dawn.
- Shoot the finished piece in morning light, not under the lamp.

## How to edit it

- Every chapter opens on a timestamp in the corner.
- Canvas footage sped up between check-ins. Check-ins at real time.
- Cut each check-in to the one sentence with the time and the mood.
- Music follows the night. Up, down, up.
- Captions on the talking clips only.
- Dawn plays slow, no speed-up, no cuts for at least 4 seconds.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Cut a 75-second all-nighter vlog from this footage. Follow the clock. Use my selfie check-ins in order and put a timestamp in the top left for each one. Speed up the canvas footage between check-ins so the painting visibly progresses. Keep the 3am check-in where I say "I think I'm going to cry" at real time. Music upbeat until 2am, quiet until 5, then lift at the window shot. End on the finished piece in the morning light held for 5 seconds.

> Add 3-5 word captions on the check-ins only, none over the canvas footage.

> Find the shot where the window starts to go blue and use it as the dawn transition.

## Variations

- Deadline vlog, the piece is due and you show the submission.
- Two-person all-nighter, alternating check-ins with a friend.
- Failed all-nighter, it didn't get done, and that's the story.

## FAQ

**How much footage do I need?**
The locked canvas angle running, plus 6-10 short check-ins.

**Should I show the low point?**
Yes. It's why people watch to the end.

**Can Vyra keep the check-ins in order?**
Yes. It reads the file timestamps and what you say on camera, so "put the check-ins in time order with a timestamp title" works.
