**A travel recap packs a trip into a music-driven sequence, in order by place or by day, with one held moment per location.**

More people, more reactions, fewer drone shots. The trip is the people.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Trips, weekends, friend-group travel |
| Hook style | The most recognizable shot of the place, with the title over it |
| Pacing | 1-2 seconds per clip, slower on the best views |
| Caption style | Destination title, optional location labels |
| Music | One track that fits the place |
| Shot list | Arrival. One wide, one medium, one detail per stop. Food. Friends laughing. Walking. A sunset or night shot. Leaving. |
| Common mistakes | Only landscapes. No people. No ending. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: iconic shot plus the title
- 3-15s: arrival and the first place
- 15-40s: places in order, one held shot each
- 40-50s: the best moment, slowed
- 50-58s: the last night or the leaving shot
- 58-60s: title again or a closing line

## How to shoot it

- Film the arrival. Plane window, the door, the first street.
- At each place, a wide, a medium with people, a detail.
- Film people reacting, not posing.
- One slow-mo clip per day.
- Film the leaving. Packed bag, the road out.

## How to edit it

- Group by location, keep the trip's order.
- Location label on the first clip of each place.
- Cut to the music, hold the best view 3 seconds.
- Slow-mo on one moment.
- People every 3-4 clips.
- Close on a wide and the title.

## Prompt it in Vyra

> Make a 50-second recap of the Lisbon trip from these clips, in the order they were shot, cut to the attached track. Put "Lisbon" as a title on the first clip and a small label with the neighborhood name when the location changes. Hold the viewpoint shot for 3 seconds and slow down the clip of everyone laughing at dinner.

> Match the pacing and label style of the attached reference reel. Keep the music ducked under the one clip where someone is talking.

> Replace any clip that's shaky or too dark.

## Variations

- Day by day, a title card per day.
- Food recap, every location is a meal.
- One second a day.

## FAQ

**How do I keep it from looking like everyone else's?**
More people, fewer landscapes.

**Voiceover?**
Optional. One line at the start and end gives it a story without making it a vlog.

**Can Vyra order the clips by when they were shot?**
Yes. It reads recording dates.
