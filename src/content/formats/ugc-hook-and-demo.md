**A UGC hook and demo is a creator-style ad. A problem or claim in the first 3 seconds, then the product doing the thing on camera, under 40 seconds.**

My "editors, are we cooked?" video is one. It's shot on a phone in my room and that's the point. Polished lighting reads as an ad.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Product ads, app launches, affiliate content, brand deals |
| Hook style | Problem first ("I was spending 4 hours on every edit") or claim first ("this replaced my editor") |
| Pacing | Hook 3s, demo 15-25s, result 5s, CTA 3s |
| Caption style | Bold phrases, hook text on screen |
| Music | Upbeat, low under your voice |
| Shot list | Selfie hook take. Screen recording or over-the-shoulder demo. The result. Product in hand. |
| Common mistakes | Too polished. Demo with no visible result. A hook that fits any product. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: hook to camera, text on screen
- 3-8s: the problem, one sentence
- 8-28s: demo, product doing the thing
- 28-33s: the result, shown
- 33-38s: one-line CTA

## How to shoot it

- Phone, handheld, your real space.
- Record the hook 5 different ways. The hook is what gets tested.
- Show the exact steps in the demo. Don't skip any.
- Shoot the result as its own shot.
- Keep takes short. Ads get cut from lots of short takes.

## How to edit it

- Hook text on screen in the first frame.
- Cut the demo to the actions. Remove all loading and waiting.
- Zoom into the screen or product on each key action.
- Captions on every word.
- Hold the result 2-3 seconds.
- Export 3-5 versions with different hooks and the same demo.

## Variations

- Problem, agitate, solve, with 5 extra seconds of agitation before the demo.
- Testimonial demo, a customer instead of you.
- Old way vs new way, split screen.

## FAQ

**Does it really have to look unpolished?**
Because polished looks like an ad and people skip ads. Good audio matters more than good lighting.

**How many hooks should I test?**
At least 3. Keep the demo identical so the hook is the only thing changing.

**Can Vyra make the variants?**
Yes. Ask for multiple versions from named hook takes and it builds them from the same timeline.
