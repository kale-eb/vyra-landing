**A UGC hook-and-demo video is a creator-style ad that opens with a relatable problem or bold claim, then shows the product solving it on camera in under 40 seconds, shot to look native to the feed rather than like an ad.**

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Product ads, app launches, affiliate content, creator partnerships |
| Hook style | Problem-first ("I was spending 4 hours on every edit") or claim-first ("this replaced my editor") |
| Pacing | Hook 3s, demo 15-25s, result 5s, CTA 3s |
| Caption style | Bold phrase captions, hook text on screen |
| Music | Trending or upbeat, low under voice |
| Shot list | Selfie hook take. Screen recording or over-the-shoulder demo. Result shot. Product in hand. |
| Common mistakes | Polished lighting that reads as an ad. Demo without a visible result. Hook that could apply to any product. |

## Structure

- 0-3s: hook, face to camera, text on screen
- 3-8s: the problem, one specific sentence
- 8-28s: demo, product on screen doing the thing
- 28-33s: the result, shown not described
- 33-38s: one-line CTA

## How to shoot it

- Shoot on a phone, handheld, in your real space.
- Record the hook 5 different ways. The hook is what gets tested.
- Demo: show the exact actions, no skipping steps.
- Capture the result as its own shot, the thing the viewer wants.
- Keep it under a minute of raw footage per take; ads are cut from many short takes.

## How to edit it

- Text hook on screen in the first frame, before you finish the sentence.
- Cut the demo to the actions, remove all waiting and loading.
- Zoom into the screen or product on each key action.
- Captions on every spoken word.
- Result shot held 2-3 seconds.
- Export 3-5 versions with different hooks from the same demo.

## Variations

- Problem-agitate-solve: extra 5 seconds of agitation before the demo.
- Testimonial demo: a customer instead of the creator.
- Comparison demo: old way vs new way, split.
- Unboxing-to-demo: starts with the package.

## FAQ

**Why does it need to look "not polished"?**
Native-looking video stops the scroll because it doesn't trigger ad-skipping. Good audio matters more than good lighting.

**How many hooks should I test?**
At least 3, ideally 5. Keep the demo identical so the hook is the only variable.

**Can Vyra generate the variants?**
Yes. Ask for multiple versions from named hook takes and it builds them from the same timeline.
