**A faceless channel lives or dies on two things: how fast you can cover a script with the right footage, and whether the captions carry the story when the viewer has the sound off. Vyra reads your narration, knows what is in every clip you uploaded, and cuts the two together from a written brief. It does not generate footage. You supply the b-roll, screen recordings, or product shots, and it does the placement.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Narration to video:
```
Use the voiceover file as the spine. Cut the b-roll clips over it so each clip matches what I am describing: when I say "morning routine" use the kitchen and coffee clips, when I say "the app" use the screen recording. Change shot every 3-5 seconds. 4-word captions, white, bold, lower third. No music.
```

Screen recording explainer:
```
Make a 60-second 9:16 explainer from the screen recording and the voiceover. Crop the screen to the part I am pointing at each time. Add a title card for the first 2 seconds that says "3 settings you should change". Word-by-word captions, yellow highlight on the word I am saying.
```

Daily batch:
```
Same style as yesterday's video: caption font, placement, cut rhythm, and end card. Build today's from the new voiceover and the clips in the "day 12" folder.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Record narration cleanly, one file per video. Upload it with your b-roll folder or screen recording.
2. Describe the structure: what plays under which part of the script, caption style, music or none.
3. Review the cut. Correct one thing per message: "swap the clip at 0:14 for the drone shot."
4. Lock the style and describe the next video as "same style."
5. Export 9:16 for Shorts and TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube, from the same edit.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Transcript-aware placement: the agent hears "the app" and cuts to the clip it identified as the app.
- Scene analysis on every clip, so "the drone shot over the harbor" finds the right file.
- Captions word-by-word or in phrases, with emphasis words, since faceless viewers often watch muted.
- Reference matching copies the rhythm and text style of a channel you want to sound like.
- Bring your own Claude or ChatGPT over MCP from $24/mo, or use the built-in AI.

## What it does not do

- It does not generate b-roll, stock footage, AI voices, or avatars. You bring the visuals and the voice.
- It does not write your script.

## FAQ

**Do I need to record narration first?**
It works best that way. The narration sets the timing and the agent cuts to it. You can also start from footage and add voiceover later.

**Can it use stock footage I already licensed?**
Yes. Upload it like any other clip. It gets analyzed the same way.

**Can I make a 10-minute YouTube video, not just Shorts?**
Yes. Describe the sections and the agent builds a long cut. Then ask for three Shorts from the strongest moments.
