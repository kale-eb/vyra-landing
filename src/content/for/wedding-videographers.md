**A wedding edit is finding forty perfect seconds inside eight hours. Vyra transcribes the vows and speeches, analyzes every clip, and cuts the moments you name to the couple's song, so the same-day teaser is a conversation, not a night at the desk.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Same-day teaser:
```
Cut a 60-second 16:9 teaser to the attached first-dance song. Open on the dress detail, then getting ready, the walk down the aisle on the first chorus, the kiss on the drop, reception energy at the end. Under the verse, use the line from the vows where he says "every version of you." Keep the vow audio clean over the music.
```

Highlight film:
```
Build a 4-minute highlight film. Structure: details and getting ready, ceremony with the vows as the spine, portraits, speeches with the two best lines from the best man and the maid of honor, first dance, party. Use the ceremony audio from the recorder, not the camera. Music ducked under every spoken line.
```

Multi-cam:
```
Sync the two ceremony angles by transcript and cut between them on the speaker. Wide during the vows, close on reactions.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the cards and the external audio. Vyra transcribes the speech and analyzes every clip.
2. Ask for the teaser with the song and the moments you want.
3. Correct specific beats: "the kiss should land on the drop."
4. Ask for the highlight film with the structure and the audio sources.
5. Export the teaser at 9:16 for the couple to post and the film at full resolution.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Transcript search across vows, speeches, and toasts.
- Multi-camera sync by transcript matching.
- Beat sync to the couple's song.
- Audio ducking and volume keyframes from a sentence.

## What it does not do

- It does not color grade to a LUT you have not described. Name the look or attach a reference.
- It does not fix bad audio. Record clean audio on the day.
