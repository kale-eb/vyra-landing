**Music content lives or dies on whether the cuts hit the beat. Vyra reads the BPM and onsets of your track, cuts your real footage to it, and lets you fix the moments that matter by saying which bar they should land on.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Release teaser:
```
Cut a 20-second 9:16 teaser from the show footage to the attached track, starting at the drop at 0:47. One cut per beat through the chorus, longer shots in the verse. Put the song title in bold white text for the first 2 seconds, then no text. End on the crowd shot.
```

Lyric clip:
```
Use the studio footage under the attached song from 1:10 to 1:40. Show each lyric line as it is sung, centered, bold, one line at a time. Keep my face visible when I am singing; cut to the hands on the keys during the instrumental bar.
```

Tour recap:
```
Build a 45-second recap of the tour footage to the attached track. Start slow with the drive and load-in, speed up through the shows, end on the last night crowd. Add city names as small lower-left labels when the location changes.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the footage and the track. Vyra transcribes any speech, analyzes every scene, and reads the audio rhythm.
2. Ask for a beat-synced first cut and name the section of the song it should use.
3. Correct specific beats: "the chorus should start on the wide shot."
4. Add text, a title, or lyric captions.
5. Export 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Beat sync from the actual audio: BPM, beat positions, and onsets, not a generic template.
- Footage search by content: "every shot with the drummer" or "the moment the crowd sings back."
- Speed ramps and time remapping for fast-slow-fast montage energy.
- Reference matching: attach a music video you like and say "match this cut rhythm."

## What it does not do

- It does not generate music or footage. Bring your own recording and your own clips.
- It does not license tracks. You own the rights to what you upload.

## FAQ

**Does it work with slow or off-grid songs?**
Yes. Say "cut on the downbeats only" or "every two bars" for slower tempos, and name a specific timestamp when a section should change.

**Can I sync to a stem or a demo instead of the master?**
Yes. Upload whatever version you have. Swap in the final master later and ask the agent to keep the cut points.

**Can the label or manager review?**
Export a draft and share it. The timeline stays editable for changes.
