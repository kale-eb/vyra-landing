## Your three most-used prompts

**Release teaser**
```
Cut a 20-second 9:16 teaser from the show footage to the attached track, starting at the drop at 0:47. One cut per beat through the chorus, longer shots in the verse. Song title in bold white for the first 2 seconds, then no text. End on the crowd shot.
```

**Lyric clip**
```
Use the studio footage under the attached song from 1:10 to 1:40. Show each lyric line as it's sung, centered, bold, one line at a time. Keep my face when I'm singing, cut to the hands on the keys during the instrumental bar.
```

**Tour recap**
```
Build a 45-second recap of the tour footage to the attached track. Start slow with the drive and load-in, speed up through the shows, end on the last night crowd. City names as small lower-left labels when the location changes.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the footage and the track. Vyra transcribes any speech, analyzes every scene, and reads the rhythm.
2. Ask for a beat-synced first cut and name the section of the song.
3. Fix specific beats. "The chorus should start on the wide shot."
4. Add text, a title, or lyric captions.
5. Export 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Beat sync from the actual audio. BPM, beat positions, onsets.
- Footage search by content. "Every shot with the drummer."
- Speed ramps for fast-slow-fast energy.
- Reference matching. Attach a music video you like and say "match this cut rhythm."

## What it does not do

- It doesn't generate music or footage.
- It doesn't license tracks. You own the rights to what you upload.

## FAQ

**Does it work with slow or off-grid songs?**
Yes. Say "cut on the downbeats only" or "every two bars", and name a timestamp when a section should change.

**Can I sync to a demo instead of the master?**
Yes. Swap in the final master later and ask it to keep the cut points.

**Can the label or manager review?**
Export a draft and share it. The timeline stays editable.
