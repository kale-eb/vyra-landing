**You have ten minutes to record and none to edit. Vyra takes a raw phone video, removes the pauses and restarts, adds captions and a screen recording where you point at the product, and exports it vertical. All from a few sentences.**

## What you make

- [Build-in-public updates](/formats/founder-build-in-public)
- [Product walkthroughs](/formats/app-walkthrough) with your face and screen
- [Talking head takes](/formats/talking-head)
- [Launch demos](/formats/product-demo)

## Your three most-used prompts

**Weekly update**
```
Cut this 6-minute phone recording into a 60-second 9:16 update. Keep the parts about the new feature and the metric, cut everything about the office move. Remove pauses over half a second and restarts. Word-by-word captions, bold white. No music.
```

**Face plus screen**
```
When I talk about the dashboard, show the screen recording as a picture-in-picture top right, about a third of the frame, for as long as I'm on that topic. Then back to just me.
```

**Launch demo**
```
Make a 45-second demo. Start on me saying the one-line pitch, then the screen recording cropped to what I'm clicking, then me for the ask at the end. Title card with the product name for the first 2 seconds and a lower third with my handle.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Record on your phone, screen-record the product, upload both.
2. Ask for the cut with the length, what to keep, and what to drop.
3. Fix one thing per message. Ask for a timestamped list of cuts if you want to check.
4. Add captions and the screen overlay.
5. Export 9:16 and post. Ask for 16:9 for LinkedIn if you need it.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Filler and restart removal with thresholds, so a rambling take gets tight without sounding chopped.
- Screen overlays triggered by what you say, no manual keyframes.
- One style reused across weekly updates. Describe it once.
- Works through Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor over MCP if that's already your setup.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't write your script or generate an avatar. You're on camera.
- It doesn't post to LinkedIn, X, or TikTok.

## FAQ

**I say "um" constantly. Can it fix that?**
Yes. "Remove every um, uh, and like used as filler" is a normal prompt. Add "keep like when I mean it."

**Can I drive it from Cursor or Claude Code?**
Yes, through MCP. Same prompts, your own assistant.

**How long from upload to export?**
Analysis runs once on upload. After that a short cut is a few prompts and a review.
