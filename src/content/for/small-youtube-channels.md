**If you shoot, edit, and publish alone, editing is the bottleneck. Vyra does the first 80 percent of the edit from a plain brief, and you finish the last 20 in the timeline.**

## What you make

- [Talking-to-camera videos](/formats/talking-head)
- [Tutorials](/formats/tutorial)
- [Explainers with cutaways](/formats/talking-head-b-roll)
- [Shorts](/formats/podcast-clip) from every upload

## Your three most-used prompts

**The weekly video**
```
Cut this 25-minute recording into an 8-minute video. Keep the best take of each point, remove pauses over half a second, filler words, and restarts. Title card for each section using my section names. No music.
```

**The Shorts**
```
Pull two 9:16 Shorts under 45 seconds from this edit. Pick moments that make sense without context. Word-by-word captions, bold white, and a 2-second text hook at the top of each.
```

**The consistency pass**
```
Apply the same caption style, title card style, and lower third as my last project to this one.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the recording plus any screen captures or b-roll.
2. Ask for the rough cut with a target length and your cut rules.
3. Watch it once. One correction per message.
4. Add captions, titles, and a lower third.
5. Ask for Shorts from the locked edit, then export everything.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- It does the tedious pass. Filler, pauses, retakes, with rules you set.
- One style, every video. Describe your caption and title look once.
- Shorts come from the same project, so you publish more without a second edit.
- Free to start, and the built-in AI needs no extra subscription.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't make thumbnails or write titles.
- It doesn't generate footage you didn't upload.

## FAQ

**I've never edited video. Is this harder than a template app?**
It's easier for talking videos. You describe what you want in sentences. There's no timeline to learn until you want it.

**It cut something I wanted to keep. Can I get it back?**
Give it the timestamp and say "put that back." Or drag it back yourself.

**Can I use my ChatGPT or Claude subscription instead of the built-in AI?**
Yes, through MCP. The built-in chat works without either.
