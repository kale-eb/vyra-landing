**A travel vlog is mostly a sorting problem: hundreds of clips, a handful worth keeping. Vyra analyzes every clip on upload, so you can say "build a 4-minute video of day two, open on the market, end on the sunset" and it picks the shots and cuts them.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Day recap:
```
Make a 60-second 9:16 recap of day three. Use the attached track and cut on its beats. Open on the wide drone-style shot of the coast, then favor clips with movement and faces, end on the sunset. No talking, no captions except a small location label in the first 2 seconds.
```

Chronological vlog:
```
Build a 5-minute travel vlog from these clips in time order. Keep all my talking-to-camera clips, cut pauses and restarts. Between them, use 2-3 second b-roll of the places I mention. Add a location title card when the city changes.
```

Fix a section:
```
The airport section is too long. Cut it to 10 seconds and get us to the city faster.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload at the end of each day or at the end of the trip. Clips are analyzed once.
2. Ask for a recap or a full vlog with a target length and an opening and closing shot.
3. Correct one thing per message: swap a shot, reorder a day, shorten a section.
4. Add music, captions, and location labels once the cut is right.
5. Export the long version for YouTube and the vertical recap for Reels or TikTok.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- It finds shots by what is in them. "Every clip with the blue boats" works without you tagging anything.
- It beat-syncs a montage to a track you upload, with cut density you set per section.
- It reframes horizontal clips to 9:16 with masks that follow the subject, so one shoot serves both platforms.
- It matches a reference travel edit's pacing and grade if you attach one and say which properties to copy.

## What it does not do

- It does not fix underexposed or blown-out clips beyond normal color correction.
- It does not add footage of places you did not visit.

## FAQ

**My footage is a mix of phone and camera. Does that matter?**
No. Upload both. Ask for a consistent grade if the color mismatch bothers you.

**Can it keep the trip in order?**
Yes. Say "in time order" and it will follow the recording dates.

**Can I edit while still traveling?**
Vyra runs in the browser. Upload when you have a stable connection; prompting after that is light.
