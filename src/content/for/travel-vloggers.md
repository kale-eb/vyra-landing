**A travel vlog is mostly a sorting problem. Hundreds of clips, a handful worth keeping. Vyra looks at every clip on upload, so you can say "build a 4-minute video of day two, open on the market, end on the sunset" and it picks the shots and cuts them.**

## Your three most-used prompts

**Day recap**
```
Make a 60-second 9:16 recap of day three. Use the attached track and cut on its beats. Open on the wide shot of the coast, then favor clips with movement and faces, end on the sunset. No talking, no captions except a small location label in the first 2 seconds.
```

**Chronological vlog**
```
Build a 5-minute travel vlog from these clips in time order. Keep all my talking-to-camera clips, cut pauses and restarts. Between them, use 2-3 second b-roll of the places I mention. Location title card when the city changes.
```

**Fix a section**
```
The airport section is too long. Cut it to 10 seconds and get us to the city faster.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload at the end of each day or at the end of the trip. Clips get analyzed once.
2. Ask for a recap or a full vlog with a length and an opening and closing shot.
3. Fix one thing per message. Swap a shot, reorder a day, shorten a section.
4. Add music, captions, and location labels once the cut is right.
5. Export the long version for YouTube and the vertical recap for Reels or TikTok.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- It finds shots by what's in them. "Every clip with the blue boats" works without tagging anything.
- It beat-syncs a montage to a track you upload, with cut density you set per section.
- It reframes horizontal clips to 9:16 with masks that follow the subject.
- It matches a reference travel edit's pacing and grade if you attach one and say what to copy.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't fix underexposed or blown-out clips beyond normal color correction.
- It doesn't add footage of places you didn't go.

## FAQ

**My footage is a mix of phone and camera. Does that matter?**
No. Upload both. Ask for a consistent grade if the mismatch bothers you.

**Can it keep the trip in order?**
Yes. Say "in time order" and it follows the recording dates.

**Can I edit while still traveling?**
Vyra runs in the browser. Upload when you have a stable connection. Prompting after that is light.
