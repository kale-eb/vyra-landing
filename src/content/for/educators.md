## Your three most-used prompts

**Lesson cleanup**
```
Trim this 55-minute recording to the lesson only. Remove the first 4 minutes of setup, every pause over 1 second, and the tangent about parking. Keep the Q&A at the end. Phrase captions, white, lower third, whole video. Title card at each section change using the section names I say out loud.
```

**Slide sync**
```
When I say "next slide" or "as you can see here", cut to the screen recording of the slides for as long as I'm on that slide, then back to me. Keep me small in the bottom right while the slide is up.
```

**Promo clips**
```
Find the three clearest 30-second explanations in this lesson, where I state a concept and give one example. Cut each as a 9:16 clip with word-by-word captions and a title card naming the concept.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the recording, your slides as a screen recording, and any demo clips.
2. Describe the lesson cut. What to remove, where slides go, caption style.
3. Fix one thing per message. "The slide at 12:40 should stay up longer."
4. Ask for clips for your landing page or social.
5. Export 16:9 for the course platform and 9:16 for promotion.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Transcript search across an hour. "The part where I define opportunity cost" finds it.
- Captions for every line, word-by-word or in phrases. That covers accessibility without a second pass.
- Masks keep you in a corner while slides take the frame.
- Section title cards and lower thirds from a description.
- Bring your own Claude or ChatGPT over MCP from $24/mo.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't generate slides, diagrams, or AI presenters. Record the real lesson.
- It doesn't host or sell your course.

## FAQ

**Can it handle a 90-minute recording?**
Yes. Long files get a searchable transcript and scene descriptions, so cutting by description works at any length.

**Can I fix a caption where it misheard a technical term?**
Yes. Say "at 14:20 the caption should read mitochondria."

**Can it remove my filler words without making me sound robotic?**
Say "remove filler words but keep natural pauses up to half a second."
