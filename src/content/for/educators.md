**Teaching video is long, unscripted, and full of pauses, tangents, and "let me find that slide." Vyra transcribes the whole recording, lets you cut by describing what to keep, and captions every word so the lesson is accessible without a separate pass. One 60-minute recording becomes a tight lesson plus a handful of clips for promotion.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Lesson cleanup:
```
Trim this 55-minute recording into the lesson only. Remove the first 4 minutes of setup, every pause over 1 second, and the tangent about the parking situation. Keep the Q&A at the end. Add phrase captions, white, lower third, for the whole video. Insert a title card at each section change using the section names I say out loud.
```

Slide sync:
```
When I say "next slide" or "as you can see here", cut to the screen recording of the slides for as long as I am on that slide, then back to me. Keep me small in the bottom right corner while the slide is showing.
```

Promo clips:
```
Find the three clearest 30-second explanations in this lesson, the ones where I state a concept and give one example. Cut each as a 9:16 clip with word-by-word captions and a title card naming the concept.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the recording, your slides as a screen recording, and any demo clips. Everything is transcribed and analyzed.
2. Describe the lesson cut: what to remove, where to insert slides, caption style.
3. Review and correct one thing per message: "the slide at 12:40 should stay on screen longer."
4. Ask for clips for your course landing page or social.
5. Export 16:9 for the course platform and 9:16 for promotion.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Transcript search across an hour: "the part where I define opportunity cost" finds it instantly.
- Captions for every line, word-by-word or in phrases, which covers accessibility requirements without extra work.
- Masks keep you visible in a corner while slides or screen recordings take the frame.
- Section title cards and lower thirds from a description, no template hunting.
- Bring your own Claude or ChatGPT over MCP from $24/mo if your institution already licenses one.

## What it does not do

- It does not generate slides, diagrams, or AI presenters. Record the real lesson.
- It does not host or sell your course. Export and upload to your platform.

## FAQ

**Can it handle a 90-minute recording?**
Yes. Long files are indexed with searchable transcripts and scene descriptions, so cutting by description works at any length.

**Can I correct a caption where it misheard a technical term?**
Yes. Say "at 14:20 the caption should read 'mitochondria'" and it is fixed in place.

**Can it remove my filler words without making me sound robotic?**
Say "remove filler words but keep natural pauses up to half a second." Numbers keep the pacing human.
