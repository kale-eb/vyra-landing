**Vlog editing takes hours because you have to watch everything you shot to decide what to keep. Vyra watches it for you: every clip is transcribed and scene-analyzed on upload, so you describe the day you want to show and it assembles it from your real footage.**

## What you make

- Daily and weekly vlogs: [/formats/vlog](/formats/vlog)
- Day in the life with a narrative arc: [/formats/day-in-the-life-story](/formats/day-in-the-life-story)
- Music-driven recaps: [/formats/montage](/formats/montage)
- Talking-to-camera segments with cutaways: [/formats/talking-head-b-roll](/formats/talking-head-b-roll)

## Your three most-used prompts

First assembly:
```
Build a 3-minute vlog from today's footage in the order it was shot. Keep every clip where I talk to camera but cut pauses and retakes. Between talking clips, use 2-4 second b-roll of what I was doing. Add a calm indie track at 30 percent under my voice, up to 70 percent when I am not talking.
```

Short version for Reels:
```
Make a 45-second 9:16 version. Open on the most energetic clip, no talking in the first 3 seconds, then my best two lines to camera, then a fast montage of the rest. Captions in 3-4 word phrases, white, lower third.
```

Fix pacing:
```
The middle section from 1:10 to 2:00 drags. Cut it to 25 seconds, keep the coffee shop and the studio, drop the walking shots.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Dump the whole day's clips in. Vyra transcribes anything spoken and describes every scene.
2. Ask for a first assembly in shooting order with a target length.
3. Watch it, then give one note at a time: reorder, tighten, swap a shot.
4. Ask for captions and music once the structure is right.
5. Export the long version, then ask for the vertical cut from the same project.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- It finds the b-roll for you. "Show the pastry while I talk about breakfast" works because it knows what is in each clip.
- It ducks music under speech automatically when you ask, so you are not keyframing volume by hand.
- It keeps original ambience where you tell it to, so a quiet walking shot can breathe.
- It matches a reference vlog's pacing and caption style if you attach one and say what to copy.

## What it does not do

- It does not stabilize badly shaken footage into something usable. Shoot steady.
- It does not generate footage of places you did not film.

## FAQ

**How do I stop it from making the vlog too fast?**
Give it a rhythm: "average shot length 3 seconds, hold talking clips as long as they need." Numbers beat adjectives.

**Can it keep the order I shot in?**
Yes. Say "in shooting order" and it will not restructure.

**What about music licensing?**
You upload the track. Vyra places and ducks it; licensing is on you.
