**A one-hour episode hides five good clips. Vyra transcribes the whole recording, so you can ask "find the five most quotable 45-second moments and make vertical clips with captions" and get them on the timeline instead of scrubbing for them.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Find clips:
```
Find the five strongest self-contained moments under 60 seconds where the guest makes a clear point or tells a story. List them with timestamps and a one-line summary before cutting anything.
```

Build a clip:
```
Make a 9:16 clip from 14:20 to 15:05. Show whoever is speaking, switch cameras on speaker change, not on a timer. Word-by-word captions, bold white with a thin black outline, centered above the lower third. Add a title at the top for the first 3 seconds: "why most creators quit at month three".
```

Clean the full episode:
```
Remove ums, uhs, and pauses over one second across the whole episode. Keep natural breathing room. Do not cut anything between 32:00 and 33:10, that silence is intentional. Give me a list of every cut with timestamps.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the recording, or each camera feed if you have more than one. Vyra transcribes and analyzes all of it.
2. Ask for a list of candidate moments before cutting. Approve or swap.
3. Build clips one at a time with the caption and camera rules you want.
4. Clean the full episode with thresholds for pauses and filler.
5. Export clips at 1080x1920 and the episode at up to 4K on AI Pro.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Transcript search across the whole episode: "every time we mention pricing" is one prompt.
- Speaker-aware camera switching when you have multiple feeds and tell it to cut on speaker change.
- Captions with names: "add a lower third with the guest's name the first time they speak."
- A timestamped change list on request, so you can verify a cleanup without re-listening.

## What it does not do

- It does not record or host the podcast. Bring the files.
- It does not clean up bad audio beyond basic level work. Record well.

## FAQ

**Can it handle two or three camera angles?**
Yes. Upload each feed and tell it how to switch: on speaker change, on reaction, or on a rule you give.

**How accurate is the transcript?**
Accurate enough to search and caption. Fix individual words by timestamp when needed.

**Can it make all clips match one style?**
Yes. Describe the style once, then say "same style" for each subsequent clip.
