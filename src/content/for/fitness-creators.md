## Your three most-used prompts

Routine:
```
Build a 60-second 9:16 routine from the session footage. Show each exercise for 8-10 seconds in this order: goblet squat, Romanian deadlift, split squat, plank. Put the exercise name and "3 x 10" as a label in the top left for each. Upbeat music, no captions.
```

Form tip:
```
Clean up this form tip to 30 seconds. Keep me talking to camera, and when I say "knees track over toes" cut to the side-angle clip of the squat for 4 seconds, then back. 3-5 word captions, bold, lower third.
```

Transformation:
```
Make a 20-second reel: the before photo for 3 seconds, then the after photo for 3 seconds, then the training clips cut to the beat of the attached track. Put "12 weeks" in large text over the first two shots.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the session clips, talking-head tips, and any photos. Vyra analyzes every scene.
2. Ask for the routine or the tip with the order you want.
3. Add labels, counters, or captions in one style.
4. Ask for a version with music for Reels and one without for the app or course.
5. Export.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Scene understanding: it can tell a squat clip from a deadlift clip.
- Labels and rep counters as motion graphics from a description.
- Beat sync for montages and hype reels.
- One caption style across a week of content: say "same as Monday."

## What it does not do

- It does not track reps or analyze your form. It edits what you filmed.
- It does not generate footage or a presenter.

## FAQ

**Can it mirror or flip a clip?**
Yes. Ask for a horizontal flip on a specific clip or the whole video.

**Can I keep the audio from the gym?**
Yes. Say "keep original audio" and it will leave the room sound, or duck music under it.

**Does it handle client footage with faces I want hidden?**
Ask for a blur or a crop on a specific person or region.
