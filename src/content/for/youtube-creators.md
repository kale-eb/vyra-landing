**Vyra is an AI video editor that edits your real footage from a plain-language description: it transcribes and analyzes everything you upload, so you can say "cut the best takes into a 10-minute video and pull three Shorts from it" and get a timeline you can still adjust by hand.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Rough cut of a long video:
```
Cut this 40-minute recording into a 12-minute YouTube video. Keep the best take of each point, remove pauses over half a second, retakes, and filler words. Keep the section order I filmed in. No captions, no music yet.
```

Pull Shorts from the long edit:
```
Find the three strongest self-contained moments under 50 seconds each. Make a 9:16 version of each with me centered, word-by-word captions in bold white, and a one-line title at the top for the first 2 seconds.
```

Chapters and lower thirds:
```
Add a title card at the start of each main section using the section names from the transcript. Add a lower third with my name and channel handle for the first 5 seconds. Simple slide in from the left.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the raw recording and any b-roll. Vyra transcribes every word and analyzes every scene while you wait.
2. Ask for a rough cut with a target length and cut rules. Watch it build on the timeline.
3. Give one correction per message: swap a take, tighten a section, fix a caption.
4. Ask for the Shorts once the long edit is locked, so they inherit the cleaned takes.
5. Export at 1440p on AI Starter or 4K on AI Pro, then export the 9:16 Shorts separately.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- It searches your footage by what was said and what is on screen, so "find every time I show the graph" works across an hour of tape.
- It removes filler and retakes with rules you set (pause length, keep last take), which is where most of a YouTube edit's time goes.
- It reframes 16:9 to 9:16 with masks that follow you, so Shorts come from the same project instead of a second edit.
- The full timeline editor is always there for the last 10 percent of tweaks.

## What it does not do

- It does not generate footage. Everything on the timeline is something you shot or uploaded.
- It does not upload to YouTube for you. You export and publish as usual.

## FAQ

**Can it handle a 2-hour recording?**
Yes. Upload is analyzed once; after that, prompts about specific moments resolve against the transcript and scene index rather than re-watching the file.

**Will it cut my jokes or pauses I meant to leave in?**
Only if you let it. Say "do not touch 4:10 to 4:25" or "allow pauses up to one second" and it respects that.

**Do I need an AI subscription?**
No. The built-in chat works on its own. If you already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor, you can connect it through MCP instead.
