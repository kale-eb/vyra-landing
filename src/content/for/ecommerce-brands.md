**Ecommerce video is a catalog problem. You have many SKUs, one style, and no time to hand-cut each one. Vyra edits real footage of your real products from a written brief, holds one caption and title style across every cut, and reframes each one for Reels, TikTok, and your product page without a second edit.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Product page demo:
```
Make a 30-second 9:16 demo of the ceramic mug from these clips. Open on the close-up of the glaze, then the pour, then the handle grip, then the shelf shot. No talking. Add 3-word captions naming each feature in white, lower third. Calm acoustic track at 40%. End on the product on a plain background for 2 seconds.
```

Hook-and-demo ad:
```
Cut a 25-second TikTok ad from the creator footage. Open on her line "I stopped buying candles after this", cut every pause and retake, then show the product in use for 8 seconds while she keeps talking, then back to her for the close. Word-by-word captions, bold, slightly above center. Keep the original audio.
```

Catalog batch:
```
Use the caption style, music level, and end card from the mug demo on the next five product folders. Same length, same structure: close-up, in use, on shelf, end card with the product name.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Shoot every product the same way: close-up, in use, on a plain surface, in the box. Upload one folder per SKU.
2. Write the first cut as a prompt and review it. Lock captions, music level, and end card.
3. Say "same style" for the remaining SKUs. Correct one thing per message if a cut is off.
4. Ask for a 1:1 version for the product page and a 9:16 for paid social from each locked cut.
5. Export and drop into your store and ad manager.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Style by description: captions, fonts, colors, logo position, end card, set once and reused across SKUs.
- Scene analysis finds the shot you mean. "The close-up of the stitching" works without scrubbing.
- Masks reframe 16:9 shoot footage to 9:16 and 1:1 while keeping the product centered.
- Reference matching copies the pacing and caption look of an ad that already converts for you.
- Bring your own Claude or ChatGPT over MCP if your team already pays for one, from $24/mo.

## What it does not do

- It does not generate product footage, AI models, or virtual try-ons. Shoot the real item.
- It does not run your ads or push to Shopify. Export and upload.

## FAQ

**Can it keep the product centered when it reframes to vertical?**
Yes. Say "keep the product centered in the 9:16 version" and the agent places a mask that follows the subject.

**Can we reuse a creator's clip across several product ads?**
Yes. Upload once, then describe each ad. The transcript is searchable, so "the part where she mentions shipping" finds the moment.

**Does it handle 50 SKUs?**
One project per SKU or one project with folders. Lock the style on the first cut and describe the rest as "same style", one message per product.
