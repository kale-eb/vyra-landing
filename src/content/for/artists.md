**Artists film everything and post almost none of it, because the edit is the bottleneck. Vyra takes the hours of overhead footage, keeps the moments where the piece visibly changes, speeds up the rest, and lands the reveal on the beat.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Speed paint:
```
Turn the overhead footage of this painting into a 30-second 9:16 speed paint to the attached track. Speed up the long stretches, slow to real time for the three moments where the biggest change happens, and end on the finished piece held for 3 seconds. Title at the start with the piece name and size. No captions.
```

Process with narration:
```
Build a 60-second process video from the overhead clips and my voiceover. Use my voice as the spine and cut the footage to match what I am describing. When I mention the preserved flowers, show the close-up of me placing them. Calm music at 20% under my voice.
```

Reveal:
```
Make a 15-second reveal: three seconds of the blank canvas, a fast timelapse of the whole process, then a slow pan across the finished piece for 5 seconds. Put the title and medium as small text in the bottom left during the pan.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the session footage, phone clips, and a photo of the finished piece. Vyra analyzes every scene.
2. Ask for the format you want and name the track.
3. Correct pacing: "hold the underpainting longer" or "faster through the background."
4. Add the title, medium, and dimensions as text.
5. Export 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 4:5 or 1:1 for the grid.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Time remapping from a sentence: speed ramps, slow moments, holds.
- Scene understanding across hours of similar-looking overhead footage.
- Beat sync so the reveal lands where the song does.
- One style across a body of work: attach a past reel and say "same treatment."

## What it does not do

- It does not generate art or alter your work. It edits what you filmed.
- It does not color correct unless asked. Say "keep the paint colors true."

## FAQ

**Will it keep the colors of my work accurate?**
Yes, by default. Ask for a grade only on the vlog clips if you want one.

**Can it handle a piece filmed across several days?**
Yes. Upload all the clips and say "in the order filmed." It uses the recording dates.
