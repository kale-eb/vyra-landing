**A listing video is a walkthrough cut to a rhythm, with the price, beds, and neighborhood on screen. Vyra edits your real walkthrough footage, so the home in the video is the home you're selling, and every label is one sentence away.**

## Your three most-used prompts

**Listing tour**
```
Cut this walkthrough into a 45-second 9:16 tour. Exterior, entry, kitchen, living room, primary bedroom, backyard. Hold each room 4-6 seconds, favor the shots with the most light. Label in the lower left for each room, title card at the start with address, price, beds and baths. Upbeat but calm music.
```

**Teaser**
```
Make a 15-second teaser from the same footage. The three most striking shots, one cut per beat of the attached track, and "Just listed" for the first 2 seconds. No other text.
```

**Market update**
```
Clean up this talking-head market update to 60 seconds. Remove pauses and restarts. When I say the median price, put that number on screen in large text for 3 seconds. 3-5 word captions throughout.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the walkthrough, exterior and drone clips, and your intro.
2. Ask for the tour with the room order you want.
3. Add the labels and the title card with listing facts.
4. Ask for a 15-second teaser and a 16:9 version for the MLS or YouTube.
5. Export and post.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- It knows which clip is the kitchen and which is the yard.
- Labels and title cards from a sentence, one style across listings.
- Beat sync for lifestyle and neighborhood reels.
- A reusable style. Attach your best listing video and say "match this."

## What it does not do

- It doesn't generate virtual tours, staging, or footage. Shoot the property.
- It doesn't post to Zillow, the MLS, or social.

## FAQ

**Can I use the same style for every listing?**
Yes. Keep one reference video and say "same as the Maple Street tour."

**Does it handle drone footage?**
Yes. Upload it with the rest and say where it goes, usually the open and the close.

**Can my assistant run it?**
Yes. Anyone who can describe the edit can drive it, in the app or from Claude or ChatGPT.
