**A listing video is a walkthrough cut to a rhythm, with the price, beds, and neighborhood on screen. Vyra edits your real walkthrough footage, so the home in the video is the home you are selling, and every label is one sentence away.**

## Your three most-used prompts

Listing tour:
```
Cut this walkthrough into a 45-second 9:16 tour. Order: exterior, entry, kitchen, living room, primary bedroom, backyard. Hold each room 4-6 seconds, favor the shots with the most light. Add a label in the lower left for each room and a title card at the start with the address, price, and beds and baths. Upbeat but calm music.
```

Teaser:
```
Make a 15-second teaser from the same footage: the three most striking shots, one cut on each beat of the attached track, and the words "Just listed" for the first 2 seconds. No other text.
```

Market update:
```
Clean up this talking-head market update to 60 seconds. Remove pauses and restarts. When I say the median price, put that number on screen in large text for 3 seconds. 3-5 word captions throughout.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Upload the walkthrough, exterior and drone clips, and your intro. Vyra analyzes every scene.
2. Ask for the tour with the room order you want.
3. Add the labels and the title card with listing facts.
4. Ask for a 15-second teaser and a 16:9 version for the MLS or YouTube.
5. Export and post.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- Scene understanding: it knows which clip is the kitchen and which is the yard.
- Labels and title cards from a sentence, kept in one style across listings.
- Beat sync for lifestyle and neighborhood reels.
- A reusable style: attach your best listing video and say "match this" for the next one.

## What it does not do

- It does not generate virtual tours, staging, or footage. Shoot the property.
- It does not post to Zillow, the MLS, or social platforms. Export and upload.

## FAQ

**Can I use the same style for every listing?**
Yes. Keep one reference video or a saved caption style and say "same as the Maple Street tour."

**Does it handle drone footage?**
Yes. Upload it with the rest and say where it should go, usually the open and the close.

**Can my assistant or marketing coordinator run it?**
Yes. Anyone who can describe the edit can drive it, in the app or from Claude or ChatGPT over MCP.
