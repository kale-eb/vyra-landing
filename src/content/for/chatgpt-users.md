## Your three most-used prompts

**Summarize, then cut**
```
List what's in my Vyra project and describe the footage in a few lines. Then cut a 60-second 9:16 video. Best takes, pauses over half a second removed, no captions yet. Read the timeline back when done.
```

**Script to edit**
```
Here's the script I wrote with you earlier. Find the take of each line in my footage, put them in script order, cut the rest. Where I stumbled, use the last clean take.
```

**Style match**
```
Describe the attached reference video's cut rhythm, caption style, and music behavior in three lines. Then apply those three things to my footage. Where my instructions and the reference disagree, follow mine.
```

## A typical workflow

1. Connect ChatGPT to Vyra through MCP.
2. Upload footage in Vyra. Transcripts and scene analysis get built once.
3. Ask ChatGPT to summarize and propose a structure. Approve it in text.
4. Go step by step. Rough cut, captions, music, overlays. Ask for frame captures to check.
5. Finish in the Vyra timeline if you want, then export.

## What Vyra does that matters for you

- The script you wrote in ChatGPT becomes the edit plan without switching tools.
- ChatGPT can search footage by what was said and what's on screen.
- Motion graphics, captions, and reference matching are all reachable from the chat.
- You pay for the editor and video understanding, not another model.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't make ChatGPT generate video. Everything comes from your uploads.
- It doesn't run without a Vyra plan. ChatGPT drives, Vyra is the editor.

## FAQ

**Does this work with the free ChatGPT tier?**
It works with any ChatGPT surface that supports MCP connections. Check your plan.

**Can I still use Vyra's built-in AI?**
Yes. The built-in chat doesn't need ChatGPT at all.

**ChatGPT says it made a change but I can't see it. Now what?**
Ask it to read the timeline back with timestamps and compare. Then tell it exactly what to do.
